######## *The Root Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-1-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of root condition – arisen roots are a condition, by way of root condition, for the associated aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness. At the moment of rebirth, arisen roots are a condition, by way of root condition, for the associated aggregates and for matter produced by kamma. (1) ^7-18-1

######## *The Object Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-2-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of object condition – one sees the arisen eye with insight as impermanent, as suffering, as not-self; one enjoys and delights in it; with that as object, lust arises, wrong view arises, doubt… restlessness… distress arises. The arisen ear… the nose… the tongue… the body… visible forms… sounds… odours… tastes… tangible objects… the physical base… one sees the arisen aggregates with insight as impermanent, as suffering, as not-self… distress arises. One sees a visible form with the divine eye, hears a sound with the divine ear element; the visible-form base is a condition, by way of object condition, for eye-consciousness… the tangible base for body-consciousness… arisen aggregates are a condition, by way of object condition, for knowledge of supernormal powers and for adverting. (1) ^7-18-2

A not-arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of object condition – one sees not-arisen visible forms… sounds… odours… tastes… tangible objects… not-arisen aggregates with insight as impermanent, as suffering, as not-self… distress arises. Not-arisen aggregates are a condition, by way of object condition, for knowledge of supernormal powers, knowledge of others' minds, knowledge of the future and adverting. (1) ^7-18-3

A state bound to arise is a condition for an arisen state by way of object condition – one sees the eye bound to arise… the body… visible forms… odours… tastes… tangible objects… the physical base… aggregates bound to arise with insight as impermanent, as suffering, as not-self… distress arises. Aggregates bound to arise are a condition, by way of object condition, for knowledge of supernormal powers, knowledge of others' minds, knowledge of the future and adverting. (1) ^7-18-4

######## *The Predominance Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-3-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of predominance condition – object-predominance, co-arising predominance. Object-predominance – giving the arisen eye weight, one enjoys and delights in it; giving it weight, lust arises, wrong view arises. The arisen ear… the nose… the tongue… the body… visible forms… sounds… odours… tastes… tangible objects… the physical base… giving the arisen aggregates weight, one enjoys and delights in them; giving them weight, lust arises…. Co-arising predominance – arisen predominance is a condition, by way of predominance condition, for the associated aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness. (1)
A not-arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of predominance condition. Object-predominance – not-arisen visible forms… sounds… odours… tastes… tangible objects… giving not-arisen aggregates weight, one enjoys and delights in them; giving them weight, lust arises, wrong view arises. (1)
A state bound to arise is a condition for an arisen state by way of predominance condition. Object-predominance – giving the eye bound to arise weight… the body… visible forms… tangible objects… the physical base… giving aggregates bound to arise weight, one enjoys and delights in them; giving them weight, lust arises, wrong view arises. (1) ^7-18-5

######## *The Co-arising Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-4-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of co-arising condition – one arisen aggregate is a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for three aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness… two aggregates are a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for two aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness. At the moment of rebirth, one arisen aggregate is a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for three aggregates and for matter produced by kamma… two aggregates are a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for two aggregates and for matter produced by kamma. The aggregates are a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for the physical base. The physical base is a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for the aggregates. One great element is a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for three great elements… two great elements… the great elements are a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for matter produced by consciousness, matter produced by kamma and derived matter. External… produced by nutriment… produced by temperature… among non-percipient beings, one great element… two great elements… the great elements are a condition, by way of co-arising condition, for matter produced by kamma and derived matter. (1) ^7-18-6

######## *The Mutuality Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-5-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of mutuality condition – one arisen aggregate is a condition, by way of mutuality condition, for three aggregates… two aggregates… At the moment of rebirth, one arisen aggregate is a condition, by way of mutuality condition, for three aggregates and for the physical base. Two aggregates… the aggregates are a condition, by way of mutuality condition, for the physical base. The physical base is a condition, by way of mutuality condition, for the aggregates. One great element… external… produced by nutriment… produced by temperature… among non-percipient beings, one great element is a condition, by way of mutuality condition, for three great elements… two great elements…. (1) ^7-18-7

######## *The Support Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-6-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of support condition – one arisen aggregate is a condition, by way of support condition, for three aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness… two aggregates… at the moment of rebirth… the aggregates for the physical base… the physical base for the aggregates… one great element… external… produced by nutriment… produced by temperature… among non-percipient beings, one great element… the great elements are a condition, by way of support condition, for matter produced by consciousness, matter produced by kamma and derived matter. The eye base is a condition, by way of support condition, for eye-consciousness… the body base for body-consciousness… the physical base is a condition, by way of support condition, for arisen aggregates. (1) ^7-18-8

######## *The Strong-Support Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-7-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of strong-support condition – object strong-support, natural strong-support… Natural strong-support – relying on the arisen temperature, one produces jhāna, produces insight… the path… higher knowledge… produces an attainment, fosters conceit, adopts a wrong view. Relying on the arisen food… lodging, one produces jhāna, produces insight… the path… higher knowledge… produces an attainment, fosters conceit, adopts a wrong view. Arisen temperature… food… lodging is a condition, by way of strong-support condition, for arisen faith… wisdom, for bodily pleasure, for bodily pain, for the path, for the attainment of fruition. (1)
A not-arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of strong-support condition – object strong-support, natural strong-support…. Natural strong-support – longing for not-arisen excellence of complexion, one gives a gift, undertakes the precepts, carries out the observance-day duties. Longing for not-arisen excellence of sound… odour… taste… tangible objects… not-arisen aggregates, one gives a gift, undertakes the precepts, carries out the observance-day duties. Not-arisen excellence of complexion… not-arisen aggregates are a condition, by way of strong-support condition, for arisen faith… wisdom, for bodily pleasure, for bodily pain, for the path, for the attainment of fruition. (1)
A state bound to arise is a condition for an arisen state by way of strong-support condition – object strong-support, natural strong-support…. Natural strong-support – longing for excellence of the eye bound to arise, one gives a gift, undertakes the precepts, carries out the observance-day duties. Longing for excellence of the ear bound to arise… the body… complexion… odour… taste… tangible objects… aggregates bound to arise, one gives a gift, undertakes the precepts, carries out the observance-day duties. Excellence of the eye bound to arise… the body… complexion… tangible objects… aggregates bound to arise are a condition, by way of strong-support condition, for arisen faith… wisdom, for bodily pleasure, for bodily pain, for the path, for the attainment of fruition. (1) ^7-18-9

######## *The Pre-arising Condition* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-8-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of pre-arising condition – pre-arising object, pre-arising physical base. Pre-arising object – one sees the eye… the physical base with insight as impermanent, as suffering, as not-self, enjoys and delights in it; with that as object, lust arises… distress arises; one sees a visible form with the divine eye, hears a sound with the divine ear element; the visible-form base is a condition, by way of pre-arising condition, for eye-consciousness… the tangible base for body-consciousness. Pre-arising physical base – the eye base is a condition, by way of pre-arising condition, for eye-consciousness… the physical base is a condition, by way of pre-arising condition, for arisen aggregates. (1) ^7-18-10

######## *The Jhāna Condition and the Rest* ^7-2-1-1-12-3-14-0

An arisen state is a condition for an arisen state by way of jhāna condition… by way of path condition… by way of association condition… by way of dissociation condition – co-arising, pre-arising, post-arising. Co-arising – arisen aggregates are a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for matter produced by consciousness. At the moment of rebirth, arisen aggregates are a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for matter produced by kamma. The aggregates for the physical base, and the physical base for the aggregates, are a condition, by way of dissociation condition. Pre-arising – the eye base is a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for eye-consciousness… the body base for body-consciousness… the physical base is a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for arisen aggregates. Co-arising – one arisen aggregate is a condition, by way of presence condition, for three aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness… two aggregates… at the moment of rebirth… one great element… external… produced by nutriment… produced by temperature… among non-percipient beings…. The eye base is a condition, by way of presence condition, for eye-consciousness… the body base for body-consciousness… the physical base is a condition, by way of presence condition, for arisen aggregates. Post-arising – arisen aggregates are a condition, by way of presence condition, for this body that arose earlier. Physical food is a condition, by way of presence condition, for this body. The material life faculty is a condition, by way of presence condition, for matter produced by kamma. (1) ^7-18-17
