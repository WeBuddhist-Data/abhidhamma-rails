######## *The Root Condition* ^7-2-1-1-9-3-1-0

Dependent on an inferior state, an inferior state arises because of the root condition – dependent on one inferior aggregate, three aggregates… dependent on two aggregates, two aggregates. (1)
Dependent on an inferior state, a middling state arises because of the root condition – dependent on the inferior aggregates, matter produced by consciousness. (2)
Dependent on an inferior state, inferior and middling states arise because of the root condition – dependent on one inferior aggregate, three aggregates and matter produced by consciousness… dependent on two aggregates…. (3) ^7-15-1

Dependent on a middling state, a middling state arises because of the root condition – dependent on one middling aggregate, three aggregates and matter produced by consciousness… dependent on two aggregates… at the moment of rebirth… dependent on the aggregates, the physical base; dependent on the physical base, the aggregates; one great element… dependent on the great elements, matter produced by consciousness, matter produced by kamma and derived matter. (1) ^7-15-2

Dependent on a superior state, a superior state arises because of the root condition… three. ^7-15-3

Dependent on a middling and a superior state, a middling state arises because of the root condition – dependent on the superior aggregates and the great elements, matter produced by consciousness. (1) ^7-15-4

Dependent on an inferior and a middling state, a middling state arises because of the root condition – dependent on the inferior aggregates and the great elements, matter produced by consciousness. (1)
(The Inferior Triad should be expanded in full in the same way as the Defiled Triad.)
The Inferior Triad is finished. ^7-15-5
