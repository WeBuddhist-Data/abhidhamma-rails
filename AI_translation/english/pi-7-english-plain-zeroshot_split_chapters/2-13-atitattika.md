######## *The Root Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-1-0

A present state is a condition for a present state by way of root condition – present roots are a condition, by way of root condition, for the associated aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness. At the moment of rebirth…. (1) ^7-19-1

######## *The Object Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-2-0

A past state is a condition for a present state by way of object condition – after giving a gift, undertaking the precepts and carrying out the observance-day duties… one reviews it, one reviews good deeds done in the past, having emerged from jhāna one reviews the jhāna. Noble ones, having emerged from the path, review the path, they review fruit, they review abandoned defilements, they review suppressed defilements, they know the defilements they habitually indulged in before. One sees the past eye with insight as impermanent, as suffering, as not-self… distress arises. The past ear… the nose… the tongue… the body… visible forms… sounds… odours… tastes… tangible objects… the physical base… one sees the past aggregates with insight as impermanent, as suffering, as not-self, enjoys and delights in them, with that as object lust arises, wrong view arises, doubt… restlessness… distress arises; the base of infinite space is a condition, by way of object condition, for the base of infinite consciousness. The base of nothingness is a condition, by way of object condition, for the base of neither perception nor non-perception. Past aggregates are a condition, by way of object condition, for knowledge of supernormal powers, knowledge of others' minds, knowledge of recollecting past lives, knowledge of how beings fare according to their kamma, and adverting. (1) ^7-19-2

A future state is a condition for a present state by way of object condition – the future eye… the physical base… one sees the future aggregates with insight as impermanent… distress arises. Future aggregates are a condition, by way of object condition, for knowledge of supernormal powers, knowledge of others' minds, knowledge of the future and adverting. (1)
A present state is a condition for a present state by way of object condition – the present eye… the body… visible forms… sounds… odours… tastes… tangible objects… the physical base… one sees the present aggregates with insight as impermanent… distress arises, one sees a visible form with the divine eye, hears a sound with the divine ear element, the visible-form base is a condition, by way of object condition, for eye-consciousness… the tangible base for body-consciousness… present aggregates are a condition, by way of object condition, for knowledge of supernormal powers and for adverting. (1) ^7-19-3

######## *The Predominance Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-3-0

A past state is a condition for a present state by way of predominance condition. Object-predominance – after giving a gift and undertaking the precepts… one reviews good deeds done in the past, giving them weight; having emerged from jhāna, one reviews the jhāna, giving it weight. Noble ones, having emerged from the path, review the path, giving it weight; they review fruit, giving it weight. The past eye… the body… visible forms… sounds… odours… tastes… tangible objects… the physical base… one enjoys and delights in the past aggregates, giving them weight; giving that weight, lust arises, wrong view arises. (1) A future state is a condition for a present state by way of predominance condition. Object-predominance – the future eye… the physical base… one enjoys and delights in the future aggregates, giving them weight; giving that weight, lust arises, wrong view arises. (1)
A present state is a condition for a present state by way of predominance condition – object-predominance, co-arising predominance. Object-predominance – the present eye… the physical base… one enjoys and delights in the present aggregates, giving them weight; giving that weight, lust arises, wrong view arises. Co-arising predominance – present predominance is a condition, by way of predominance condition, for the associated aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness. (1) ^7-19-4

######## *The Proximity Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-4-0

A past state is a condition for a present state by way of proximity condition – preceding past aggregates are a condition, by way of proximity condition, for following present aggregates. Conformity for change-of-lineage… conformity for cleansing… change-of-lineage for the path… cleansing for the path… the path for fruit… fruit for fruit… conformity for the attainment of fruition… for one emerging from cessation, the base of neither perception nor non-perception is a condition, by way of proximity condition, for the attainment of fruition. (1) ^7-19-5

A past state is a condition for a present state by way of contiguity condition (the same as the proximity condition). (1) ^7-19-6

######## *The Co-arising Condition and the Rest* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-6-0

A present state is a condition for a present state by way of co-arising condition… by way of mutuality condition… by way of support condition (abbreviated). (1) ^7-19-7

######## *The Strong-Support Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-7-0

A past state is a condition for a present state by way of strong-support condition – object strong-support, proximity strong-support, natural strong-support…. Natural strong-support – relying on past faith, one gives a gift, undertakes the precepts, carries out the observance-day duties, produces jhāna, produces insight… the path… higher knowledge… produces an attainment, fosters conceit, adopts a wrong view. Relying on past virtue… wisdom… lust… longing… bodily pleasure… bodily pain, one gives a gift, undertakes the precepts, carries out the observance-day duties… produces an attainment, kills a living being… causes a split in the monastic community. Past faith… wisdom, lust… longing, bodily pleasure… bodily pain… are a condition, by way of strong-support condition, for present faith… wisdom, for lust… longing… for the attainment of fruition. (1)
A future state is a condition for a present state by way of strong-support condition – object strong-support, natural strong-support…. Natural strong-support – longing for the future excellence of the eye… the excellence of the ear… the excellence of the nose, the excellence of the tongue… the excellence of the body… the excellence of complexion… the excellence of sound… the excellence of odour… the excellence of taste… the excellence of the tangible… longing… longing for the future aggregates, one gives a gift, undertakes the precepts, carries out the observance-day duties… Future excellence of the eye… of complexion… of the tangible… future aggregates are a condition, by way of strong-support condition, for present faith… wisdom, for bodily pleasure, for bodily pain, for the path and for the attainment of fruition. (1)
A present state is a condition for a present state by way of strong-support condition – object strong-support, natural strong-support…. Natural strong-support – relying on present climate, one produces jhāna, produces insight… relying on present food and lodging, one produces jhāna… produces an attainment. Present climate… food… lodging are a condition, by way of strong-support condition, for present faith… wisdom, for the bodily… for the attainment of fruition. (1) ^7-19-8

######## *The Pre-arising Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-8-0

A present state is a condition for a present state by way of pre-arising condition – pre-arising object, pre-arising physical base. Pre-arising object – the eye… one sees the physical base with insight as impermanent… distress arises, one sees a visible form with the divine eye, hears a sound with the divine ear element; the visible-form base is a condition, by way of pre-arising condition, for eye-consciousness… the tangible base for body-consciousness. Pre-arising physical base – the eye base for eye-consciousness… the body base for body-consciousness… the physical base is a condition, by way of pre-arising condition, for present aggregates. (1) ^7-19-9

######## *The Post-arising Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-9-0

A present state is a condition for a present state by way of post-arising condition – post-arising present aggregates are a condition, by way of post-arising condition, for this body that arose earlier. (1) ^7-19-10

######## *The Repetition Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-10-0

A past state is a condition for a present state by way of repetition condition – preceding past aggregates are a condition, by way of repetition condition, for following present aggregates. Conformity for change-of-lineage… conformity for cleansing… change-of-lineage for the path… cleansing is a condition, by way of repetition condition, for the path. (1) ^7-19-11

######## *The Kamma Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-11-0

A past state is a condition for a present state by way of kamma condition. From a different moment – past volition is a condition, by way of kamma condition, for present resultant aggregates and for matter produced by kamma. (1)
A present state is a condition for a present state by way of kamma condition – present volition is a condition, by way of kamma condition, for the associated aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness. At the moment of rebirth, present volition is a condition, by way of kamma condition, for the associated aggregates and for matter produced by kamma. (1) ^7-19-12

######## *The Result Condition* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-12-0

A present state is a condition for a present state by way of result condition – one present resultant aggregate is a condition, by way of result condition, for three aggregates and for matter produced by consciousness… two aggregates… at the moment of rebirth… aggregates are a condition, by way of result condition, for the physical base. (1) ^7-19-13

######## *The Nutriment Condition and the Rest* ^7-2-1-1-13-3-13-0

A present state is a condition for a present state by way of nutriment condition… by way of faculty condition… by way of jhāna condition… by way of path condition… by way of association condition… by way of dissociation condition – co-arising, pre-arising, post-arising. Co-arising – present aggregates are a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for matter produced by consciousness. At the moment of rebirth, present aggregates are a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for matter produced by kamma, aggregates are a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for the physical base, the physical base is a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for the aggregates. Pre-arising – the eye base for eye-consciousness… the body base for body-consciousness… the physical base is a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for present aggregates. Post-arising – present aggregates are a condition, by way of dissociation condition, for this body that arose earlier. (1) ^7-19-14
