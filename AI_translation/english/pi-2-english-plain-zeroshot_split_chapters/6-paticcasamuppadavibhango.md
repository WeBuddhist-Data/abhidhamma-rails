#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-6-1-0

With ignorance as condition, formations; with formations as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality and material form; with mentality and material form as condition, the six sense-fields; with the six sense-fields as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, craving; with craving as condition, clinging; with clinging as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair come to be. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-225

Therein, what is ignorance? Not knowing suffering, not knowing the origin of suffering, not knowing the cessation of suffering, not knowing the way leading to the cessation of suffering — this is called "ignorance".
Therein, what are the formations that have ignorance as condition? The meritorious formation, the demeritorious formation, the imperturbable formation, the bodily formation, the verbal formation, the mental formation.
Therein, what is the meritorious formation? Wholesome intention belonging to the sense sphere or the fine-material sphere, made of giving, made of virtue, made of meditative development — this is called "the meritorious formation".
Therein, what is the demeritorious formation? Unwholesome intention belonging to the sense sphere — this is called "the demeritorious formation".
Therein, what is the imperturbable formation? Wholesome intention belonging to the immaterial sphere — this is called "the imperturbable formation".
Therein, what is the bodily formation? Bodily intention is the bodily formation; verbal intention is the verbal formation; mental intention is the mental formation. These are called "the formations that have ignorance as condition". ^2-226

Therein, what is the consciousness that has formations as condition? Eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, mind-consciousness — this is called "the consciousness that has formations as condition". ^2-227

Therein, what are the mentality and material form that have consciousness as condition? There is mentality, there is material form. Therein, what is mentality? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations — this is called "mentality". Therein, what is material form? The four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements — this is called "material form". So this is mentality and this is material form. This is called "the mentality and material form that have consciousness as condition". ^2-228

Therein, what are the six sense-fields that have mentality and material form as condition? The eye sense-field, the ear sense-field, the nose sense-field, the tongue sense-field, the body sense-field, the mind sense-field — this is called "the six sense-fields that have mentality and material form as condition". ^2-229

Therein, what is the contact that has the six sense-fields as condition? Eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, mind-contact — this is called "the contact that has the six sense-fields as condition". ^2-230

Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Feeling born of eye-contact, feeling born of ear-contact, feeling born of nose-contact, feeling born of tongue-contact, feeling born of body-contact, feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition". ^2-231

Therein, what is the craving that has feeling as condition? Craving for sights, craving for sounds, craving for smells, craving for tastes, craving for tangible things, craving for mental objects — this is called "the craving that has feeling as condition". ^2-232

Therein, what is the clinging that has craving as condition? Clinging to sense pleasures, clinging to views, clinging to rules and observances, clinging to a doctrine of self — this is called "the clinging that has craving as condition". ^2-233

Therein, what is the existence that has clinging as condition? Existence is twofold: there is action-existence and there is rebirth-existence. Therein, what is action-existence? The meritorious formation, the demeritorious formation, the imperturbable formation — this is called "action-existence". All action that leads to existence is action-existence.
Therein, what is rebirth-existence? Sense-sphere existence, fine-material existence, immaterial existence, percipient existence, non-percipient existence, neither-percipient-nor-non-percipient existence, one-constituent existence, four-constituent existence, five-constituent existence — this is called "rebirth-existence". So this is action-existence and this is rebirth-existence. This is called "the existence that has clinging as condition". ^2-234

Therein, what is the birth that has existence as condition? The birth of the various beings in the various classes of beings, their being born, their descent, their coming forth, the appearance of the aggregates, the obtaining of the sense-fields — this is called "the birth that has existence as condition". ^2-235

Therein, what are the aging and death that have birth as condition? There is aging, there is death. Therein, what is aging? The aging of the various beings in the various classes of beings, their growing old, broken teeth, grey hair, wrinkled skin, the decline of the life span, the ripening of the faculties — this is called "aging".
Therein, what is death? The passing away of the various beings from the various classes of beings, their passing on, breakup, disappearance, dying, death, the completion of their time, the breakup of the aggregates, the laying down of the body, the cutting off of the life faculty — this is called "death". So this is aging and this is death. This is called "the aging and death that have birth as condition". ^2-236

Therein, what is sorrow? For one touched by loss of relatives, touched by loss of wealth, touched by loss through illness, touched by loss of virtue, or touched by loss of view; for one who meets with some misfortune or other, touched by some painful thing or other — the sorrow, sorrowing, state of sorrowing, inner sorrow, inner deep sorrow, the burning of the mind, grief, the dart of sorrow — this is called "sorrow". ^2-237

Therein, what is lamentation? For one touched by loss of relatives, touched by loss of wealth, touched by loss through illness, touched by loss of virtue, or touched by loss of view; for one who meets with some misfortune or other, touched by some painful thing or other — the crying, lamenting, act of crying, act of lamenting, state of crying, state of lamenting, wordy talk, babbling, wailing, the act of wailing, the state of wailing — this is called "lamentation". ^2-238

Therein, what is pain? Bodily discomfort, bodily pain, the uncomfortable painful experience born of body-contact, the uncomfortable painful feeling born of body-contact — this is called "pain". ^2-239

Therein, what is grief? Mental discomfort, mental pain, the uncomfortable painful experience born of mind-contact, the uncomfortable painful feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "grief". ^2-240

Therein, what is despair? For one touched by loss of relatives, touched by loss of wealth, touched by loss through illness, touched by loss of virtue, or touched by loss of view; for one who meets with some misfortune or other, touched by some painful thing or other — the distress, despair, state of distress, state of despair — this is called "despair". ^2-241

"Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering": thus there is the coming together of this entire mass of suffering, its meeting, its combination, its appearance. Therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering".
The Classification According to the Discourses is finished. ^2-242

On the occasion when, because unwholesome action has been done and accumulated, resultant ear-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sound as its object… nose-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a smell as its object… tongue-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a taste as its object… body-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by pain, with a tangible thing as its object… the mind element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… or a tangible thing as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with an unwholesome root as condition, a formation; with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-352

Which things are neutral? On the occasion when, because unwholesome action has been done and accumulated, the resultant mind-consciousness element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object… or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with an unwholesome root as condition, a formation; with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering. ^2-353

Therein, what is the formation that has an unwholesome root as condition? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called "the formation that has an unwholesome root as condition".
Therein, what is the consciousness that has the formation as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the consciousness that has the formation as condition".
Therein, what is the mentality that has consciousness as condition? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations — this is called "the mentality that has consciousness as condition".
Therein, what is the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition? The mind, mentality, mental activity… the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the sixth sense-field that has mentality as condition".
Therein, what is the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition? The contact, touching, being in contact, state of being in contact — this is called "the contact that has the sixth sense-field as condition".
Therein, what is the feeling that has contact as condition? Mental experience that is neither comfortable nor uncomfortable, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant experience born of mind-contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "the feeling that has contact as condition".
Therein, what is the decision that has feeling as condition? The decision of the mind, deciding, the state of being decided upon — this is called "the decision that has feeling as condition".
Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition".
Therein, what is the birth that has existence as condition? The birth of the various states, their being born, arising, coming forth, appearance — this is called "the birth that has existence as condition".
Therein, what are the aging and death that have birth as condition? There is aging, there is death. Therein, what is aging? The aging of the various states, their growing old, the decline of their life span — this is called aging. Therein, what is death? The destruction of the various states, their vanishing, breakup, disintegration, impermanence, disappearance — this is called "death". So this is aging and this is death. This is called "the aging and death that have birth as condition".
"Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering": thus there is the coming together of this entire mass of suffering, its meeting, its combination, its appearance. Therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering".
