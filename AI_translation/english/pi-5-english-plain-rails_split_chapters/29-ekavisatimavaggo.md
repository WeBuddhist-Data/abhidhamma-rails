##### (201) 2. Discussion of Not Being Separated ^5-29-2-1-0

Is an ordinary person not separated from the phenomena of the three realms? Yes. Is an ordinary person not separated from the contacts of the three realms … from the feelings of the three realms … perceptions … volitions … states of consciousness … faith … energy … mindfulness … concentration … from the wisdom of the three realms? That should not be said…
Is an ordinary person not separated from the actions of the three realms? Yes. At the moment when an ordinary person gives a robe, at that moment do they enter and dwell in the first jhāna … enter and dwell in the base of infinite space? That should not be said… At the moment when an ordinary person gives almsfood … gives lodging … gives medicine and requisites for the sick, at that moment do they enter and dwell in the fourth jhāna … enter and dwell in the base of neither perception nor non-perception? That should not be said… ^5-879

Should it not be said that "an ordinary person is not separated from the actions of the three realms"? Yes. Has an ordinary person fully understood the action that leads to the fine-material realm and the immaterial realm? That should not be said. In that case, an ordinary person is not separated from the actions of the three realms …
The Discussion of Not Being Separated is complete. ^5-880

##### (202) 3. Discussion of the Fetters ^5-29-3-1-0

Is there attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter? Yes. Is there attainment of arahantship without abandoning some identity view … without abandoning doubt … without abandoning clinging to rites and rituals … without abandoning lust … without abandoning hatred … without abandoning delusion … without abandoning fearlessness of wrongdoing? That should not be said…
Is there attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter? Yes. Is an arahant with lust, with hatred, with delusion, with conceit, with contempt, with rivalry, with despair, with defilements? That should not be said… But isn't it the case that an arahant is free from lust, free from hatred, free from delusion, free from conceit, free from contempt, free from rivalry, free from despair, free from defilements? Yes. If an arahant is free from lust … free from defilements, then surely you must not say: "There is attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter." ^5-881

Should it not be said that "there is attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter"? Yes. Does an arahant know everything within a Buddha's range? That should not be said. In that case, there is attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter.
The Discussion of the Fetters is complete. ^5-882

##### (203) 4. Discussion of Psychic Power ^5-29-4-1-0

Do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing? Yes. "Let the trees be permanently in leaf" — do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing for this? That should not be said… Let the trees be permanently in flower … let the trees be permanently in fruit … let there be permanent moonlight … let there be permanent safety … let there be permanent plenty … "let there be permanent well-being [variant: suvuṭṭhikaṃ (Syā.)]" — do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing for this? That should not be said…
Do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing? Yes. "Let the contact that has arisen not cease" — do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing for this? That should not be said…
Let the feeling that has arisen … perception … volition … consciousness … faith … energy … mindfulness … concentration … wisdom not cease — do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing for this? That should not be said…
Do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing? Yes. "Let material form be permanent" — do they have the power of wishing for this … feeling … perception … formations … "let consciousness be permanent" — do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing for this? That should not be said…
Do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing? Yes. "Let beings subject to birth not be born" — do they have … "let beings subject to ageing not age" … "let beings subject to sickness not fall sick" … "let beings subject to death not die" — do Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing for this? That should not be said… ^5-883

Should it not be said that "Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing"? Yes. But isn't it the case that venerable Pilindavaccha resolved that the palace of Seniya Bimbisāra, king of Magadha, was simply "gold," and it indeed became gold [Pārā. 621; Mahāva. 271]? Yes. If venerable Pilindavaccha resolved that the palace of Seniya Bimbisāra, king of Magadha, was simply gold, and it indeed became gold, then surely you must also say: "Buddhas or disciples have the power of wishing."
The Discussion of Psychic Power is complete. ^5-884

##### (206) 7. Discussion of Phenomena ^5-29-7-1-0

Are all phenomena fixed? Yes. Fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… Is there no unfixed group? That should not be said… But isn't it the case that there is an unfixed group? Yes. If there is an unfixed group, then surely you must not say: "All phenomena are fixed."
Are all phenomena fixed? Yes. But isn't it the case that the Blessed One declared three groups: the group fixed in wrongness, the group fixed in rightness, and the unfixed group [Dha. Sa., Matrix of Triads 15]? Yes. If the Blessed One declared three groups: the group fixed in wrongness, the group fixed in rightness, and the unfixed group, then surely you must not say: "All phenomena are fixed."
Is material form fixed in its character as material form? Yes. Fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… Is feeling … perception … are formations … is consciousness fixed in its character as consciousness? Yes. Fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… ^5-887

Should it not be said that "material form is fixed in its character as material form … feeling … perception … formations … consciousness is fixed in its character as consciousness"? Yes. Does material form become feeling … become perception … become formations … become consciousness; does feeling … perception … formations … does consciousness become material form … become feeling … become perception … become formations? That should not be said. In that case, material form is fixed in its character as material form, feeling … perception … formations … consciousness is fixed in its character as consciousness.
The Discussion of Phenomena is complete. ^5-888
