#### (53) 1. Discussion of the Fixed Course ^5-14-1-0

Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna, a protection, a shelter, a refuge, a final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said. …
Is the fixed course unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said. …
Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections, two shelters, two refuges, two final resorts, two imperishables, two deathless states, two nibbānas? That should not be said. …
Are there two nibbānas? Yes. Is there between the two nibbānas any higher and lower, any inferior and superior, any excess and deficiency, any boundary, or breaking, or dividing line, or interval? That should not be said. …
Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Are there some who enter the fixed course, obtain it, make it arise, make it fully arise, raise it up, fully raise it, produce it, fully produce it, generate it, fully generate it? Yes. Are there some who enter the unconditioned, obtain it, make it arise, make it fully arise, raise it up, fully raise it, produce it, fully produce it, generate it, fully generate it? That should not be said. … ^5-445

Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Is the path unconditioned? That should not be said. …
Is the path conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course conditioned? That should not be said. …
Is the fixed course of stream-entry unconditioned? Yes. Is the path of stream-entry unconditioned? That should not be said. …
Is the path of stream-entry conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of stream-entry conditioned? That should not be said. …
The fixed course of once-returning … the fixed course of non-returning … is the fixed course of arahantship unconditioned? Yes. Is the path of arahantship unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the path of arahantship conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of arahantship conditioned? That should not be said. …
Is the fixed course of stream-entry unconditioned … the fixed course of arahantship unconditioned, and nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there five unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there five unconditioned things? Yes. Are there five protections … or interval? That should not be said. …
Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of wrongness unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the fixed course of wrongness conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of rightness conditioned? That should not be said. … ^5-446

Should it not be said that "The fixed course is unconditioned"? Yes. When the fixed course has arisen and ceased, does one become not fixed? That should not be said. … In that case, the fixed course is unconditioned. When the fixed course of wrongness has arisen and ceased, does one become not fixed? That should not be said. … In that case, the fixed course of wrongness is unconditioned.
The Discussion of the Fixed Course is complete. ^5-447

##### (54) 2. Discussion of Dependent Origination ^5-14-2-1-0

Is dependent origination unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna, a protection, a shelter, a refuge, a final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said. … Is dependent origination unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections, two shelters, two refuges, two final resorts, two imperishables, two deathless states, two nibbānas? That should not be said. … Are there two nibbānas? Yes. Is there between the two nibbānas any higher and lower, any inferior and superior, any excess and deficiency, any boundary, or breaking, or dividing line, or interval? That should not be said. … ^5-448

Is dependent origination unconditioned? Yes. Is ignorance unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is ignorance conditioned? Yes. Is dependent origination conditioned? That should not be said. … Is dependent origination unconditioned? Yes. Are formations, with ignorance as condition, unconditioned? That should not be said. … Are formations, with ignorance as condition, conditioned? Yes. Is dependent origination conditioned? That should not be said. … Is dependent origination unconditioned? Yes. Is consciousness, with formations as condition, unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is consciousness, with formations as condition, conditioned? Yes. Is dependent origination conditioned? That should not be said. … Is dependent origination unconditioned? Yes. Is mentality and material form, with consciousness as condition, unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is mentality and material form, with consciousness as condition, conditioned? Yes. Is dependent origination conditioned? That should not be said. … Is dependent origination unconditioned? Yes. Are aging and death, with birth as condition, unconditioned? That should not be said. … Are aging and death, with birth as condition, conditioned? Yes. Is dependent origination conditioned? That should not be said. … ^5-449

Should it not be said that "Dependent origination is unconditioned"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, with birth as condition there is aging and death. Whether Tathāgatas (Buddhas) arise or do not arise, that element stands firm — the stability of things, the order of things, specific conditionality. A Tathāgata awakens to it and breaks through to it. Having awakened to it and broken through to it, they explain it, teach it, declare it, establish it, disclose it, analyse it and make it plain. And they say: ‘See!’ — with birth as condition, monks, there is aging and death. With becoming as condition, monks, there is birth … with ignorance as condition, monks, there are formations. Whether Tathāgatas arise or do not arise, that element stands firm … and they say: ‘See!’ — with ignorance as condition, monks, there are formations. So, monks, the suchness there, the non-falsity, the not-otherwiseness, the specific conditionality — this, monks, is called dependent origination" [Saṃ. Ni. 2.20]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case, dependent origination is unconditioned. ^5-450

Is the stability of things, the order of things, there in "with ignorance as condition, formations" unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections … or interval? That should not be said. …
Is the stability of things, the order of things, there in "with ignorance as condition, formations" unconditioned, is the stability of things, the order of things, there in "with formations as condition, consciousness" unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there three unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there three unconditioned things? Yes. Are there three protections … or interval? That should not be said. …
Is the stability of things, the order of things, there in "with ignorance as condition, formations" unconditioned, is the stability of things, the order of things, there in "with formations as condition, consciousness" unconditioned … is the stability of things, the order of things, there in "with birth as condition, aging and death" unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there twelve unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there twelve unconditioned things? Yes. Are there twelve protections, twelve shelters … or interval? That should not be said. …
The Discussion of Dependent Origination is complete. ^5-451

##### (55) 3. Discussion of the Truths ^5-14-3-1-0

Are the four truths unconditioned? Yes. Are there four protections, four shelters, four refuges, four final resorts, four imperishables, four deathless states, four nibbānas? That should not be said. … Are there four nibbānas? Yes. Is there among the four nibbānas any higher and lower, any inferior and superior, any excess and deficiency, any boundary, or breaking, or dividing line, or interval? That should not be said. …
Is the truth of suffering unconditioned? Yes. Is suffering unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of suffering unconditioned? Yes. Are bodily suffering, mental suffering, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief and despair unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of the origin unconditioned? Yes. Is the origin unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of the origin unconditioned? Yes. Are craving for sense pleasures, craving for continued existence and craving for non-existence unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of the path unconditioned? Yes. Is the path unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of the path unconditioned? Yes. Is right view … right concentration unconditioned? That should not be said. …
Is suffering conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of suffering conditioned? That should not be said. … Are bodily suffering, mental suffering, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief and despair conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of suffering conditioned? That should not be said. … Is the origin conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the origin conditioned? That should not be said. … Are craving for sense pleasures, craving for continued existence and craving for non-existence conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the origin conditioned? That should not be said. … Is the path conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the path conditioned? That should not be said. … Is right view … right concentration conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the path conditioned? That should not be said. … ^5-452

Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, and is cessation unconditioned? Yes. Is the truth of suffering unconditioned, and is suffering unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, and is cessation unconditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the origin unconditioned, and is the origin unconditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, and is cessation unconditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the path unconditioned, and is the path unconditioned? That should not be said. …
Is the truth of suffering unconditioned, while suffering is conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, while cessation is conditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of the origin unconditioned, while the origin is conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, while cessation is conditioned? That should not be said. … Is the truth of the path unconditioned, while the path is conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, while cessation is conditioned? That should not be said. … ^5-453

Should it not be said that "The four truths are unconditioned"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, there are these four things that are real, not false, not otherwise. What four? ‘This is suffering’ — monks, this is real, this is not false, this is not otherwise … ‘this is the origin of suffering’ … ‘this is the cessation of suffering’ … ‘this is the way leading to the cessation of suffering’ — this is real, this is not false, this is not otherwise. These, monks, are the four things that are real, not false, not otherwise" [Saṃ. Ni. 5.1090]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case, the four truths are unconditioned.
The Discussion of the Truths is complete. ^5-454

##### (56) 4. Discussion of the Formless States ^5-14-4-1-0

Is the base of infinite space unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna, a protection, a shelter, a refuge, a final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said. … Is the base of infinite space unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections … or interval? That should not be said. …
Is the base of infinite space unconditioned? Yes. Is the base of infinite space a becoming, a destination, an abode of beings, the round of rebirth, a mode of birth, a station of consciousness, an acquisition of individual existence? Yes. Is the unconditioned a becoming, a destination, an abode of beings, the round of rebirth, a mode of birth, a station of consciousness, an acquisition of individual existence? That should not be said. …
Is there action that leads to the base of infinite space? Yes. Is there action that leads to the unconditioned? That should not be said. … Are there beings who go to the base of infinite space? Yes. Are there beings who go to the unconditioned? That should not be said. …
In the base of infinite space, are beings born, do they age, die, pass away and reappear? Yes. In the unconditioned, are beings born, do they age, die, pass away and reappear? That should not be said. … In the base of infinite space, are there feeling, perception, formations and consciousness? Yes. In the unconditioned, are there feeling, perception, formations and consciousness? That should not be said. … Is the base of infinite space a four-aggregate existence? Yes. Is the unconditioned a four-aggregate existence? That should not be said. … ^5-455

Should it not be said that "The four formless states are unconditioned"? Yes. But isn't it the case that the Blessed One declared the four formless states to be imperturbable? Yes. If the Blessed One declared the four formless states to be imperturbable, then surely you must also say: "The four formless states are unconditioned."
The Discussion of the Formless States is complete. ^5-456

That should not be said. … Is the attainment of cessation unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections … or interval? That should not be said. …
Is the attainment of cessation unconditioned? Yes. Is cleansing, emergence from cessation, evident? Yes. Is cleansing, emergence from the unconditioned, evident? ##### (58) 6. Discussion of Space ^5-14-6-1-0

Is space unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna, a protection, a shelter, a refuge, a final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said. … Is space unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said. … Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections … or interval? That should not be said. …
Is space unconditioned? Yes. Are there some who make what is not space into space? Yes. Are there some who make the conditioned into the unconditioned? That should not be said. … Are there some who make space into what is not space? Yes. Are there some who make the unconditioned into the conditioned? That should not be said. …
In space, do birds go, do the moon and sun go, do the stars go; do people perform psychic power, move their arms, move their hands, throw clods, throw sticks, throw psychic power [variant: sattiṃ (?)], throw arrows? Yes. In the unconditioned, do birds go, do the moon and sun go, do the stars go; do people perform psychic power, move their arms, move their hands, throw clods, throw sticks, throw psychic power, throw arrows? That should not be said. … ^5-460

Do people build houses and build storerooms by enclosing space? Yes. Do people build houses and build storerooms by enclosing the unconditioned? That should not be said. …
When a well is being dug, does what is not space become space? Yes. Does the conditioned become the unconditioned? That should not be said. …
When an empty well is being filled, when an empty storeroom is being filled, when an empty pot is being filled, does space disappear? Yes. Does the unconditioned disappear? That should not be said. … ^5-461

Should it not be said that "Space is unconditioned"? Yes. Is space conditioned? That should not be said. … In that case, space is unconditioned.
The Discussion of Space is complete. ^5-462

##### (59) 7. Discussion of Whether Space Is Visible ^5-14-7-1-0

Is space visible? Yes. Is it visible form, the visible-form base, the visible-form element, blue, yellow, red, white, knowable by the eye; does it strike the eye, does it come into the range of the eye? That should not be said. …
Is space visible? Yes. Does eye-consciousness arise dependent on the eye and on space? That should not be said. …
Does eye-consciousness arise dependent on the eye and on space? Yes. "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on space" — is there really [variant: atthi (?)] such a discourse? There is not. "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on visible forms" [Ma. Ni. 1.400; 3.421; Saṃ. Ni. 4.60] — is there really such a discourse? Yes. If there really is the discourse "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on visible forms," then surely you must not say: "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on space." ^5-463

Should it not be said that "Space is visible"? Yes. But isn't it the case that one sees the gap between two trees, the gap between two posts, a keyhole, a window opening? Yes. If one sees the gap between two trees, the gap between two posts, a keyhole, a window opening, then surely you must also say: "Space is visible."
The Discussion of Whether Space Is Visible is complete. ^5-464

Its summary:
The fixed course is unconditioned; dependent origination is unconditioned; the four truths are unconditioned; the four formless states are unconditioned; the attainment of cessation is unconditioned; space is unconditioned; space is visible; the four great elements; the five faculties; and likewise bodily action. ^5-470
