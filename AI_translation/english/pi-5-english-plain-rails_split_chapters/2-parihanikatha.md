#### Falling Away: Proof from the Discourses ^5-2-3-0

Does an arahant fall away from arahantship? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"High and low are the ways [variant: paṭipādā (Aṭṭha.)] made known by the ascetic;
they do not go to the far shore twice, nor is this to be thought of as just once"? [Su. Ni. 719, Suttanipāta]
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "an arahant falls away from arahantship."
Does an arahant fall away from arahantship? Yes. Is there cutting of what has been cut? That should not be said.
Is there cutting of what has been cut? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"Free of craving, without grasping, one for whom no task is found;
for what has been cut there is no cutting; the snare of the flood has been removed"?
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "there is cutting of what has been cut." ^5-265

Does an arahant fall away from arahantship? Yes. Is there adding to what has been done? That should not be said.
Is there adding to what has been done? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"For that monk, rightly liberated, whose mind is at peace,
there is no adding to what has been done, and nothing left to do is found.
"Just as a solid rock is not shaken by the wind,
so visible forms, tastes, sounds, smells, and contacts, all of them,
"and mind-objects, desirable and undesirable, do not stir one who is steady.
Their consciousness stands firm and free, and they observe its passing"? [Mahāva. 244; A. Ni. 6.55]
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "there is adding to what has been done." ^5-266

Should it not be said that "an arahant falls away from arahantship"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, these five things lead to the falling away of a temporarily liberated monk. What five? Delight in work, delight in talk, delight in sleep, delight in company, and not reviewing their consciousness as it has been liberated. These, monks, are the five things that lead to the falling away of a temporarily liberated monk"? [A. Ni. 5.149] Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case, an arahant falls away from arahantship.
Does an arahant have delight in work? That should not be said.
Does an arahant have delight in work? Yes. Does an arahant have lust, sensual lust, obsession by sensual lust, the fetter of sensual lust, the flood of sensuality, the yoke of sensuality, the hindrance of sensual desire? That should not be said.
Does an arahant have delight in talk, does an arahant have delight in sleep, does an arahant have delight in company? That should not be said.
Does an arahant have delight in company? Yes. Does an arahant have lust, sensual lust, obsession by sensual lust, the fetter of sensual lust, the flood of sensuality, the yoke of sensuality, the hindrance of sensual desire? That should not be said. ^5-267

Does an arahant fall away from arahantship? Yes. When an arahant is falling away from arahantship, obsessed by what do they fall away? They fall away obsessed by lust. Dependent on what does obsession arise? It arises dependent on an underlying tendency. Does an arahant have underlying tendencies? That should not be said.
Does an arahant have underlying tendencies? Yes. Does an arahant have the underlying tendency to sensual lust, the tendency to aversion, the tendency to conceit, the tendency to views, the tendency to doubt, the tendency to lust for existence, the tendency to ignorance? That should not be said.
They fall away obsessed by hatred… they fall away obsessed by delusion… Dependent on what does obsession arise? It arises dependent on an underlying tendency. Does an arahant have underlying tendencies? That should not be said…
Does an arahant have underlying tendencies? Yes. Does an arahant have the underlying tendency to sensual lust… the tendency to ignorance? That should not be said.
Does an arahant fall away from arahantship? Yes. When an arahant is falling away from arahantship, what accumulates? Lust accumulates. Does identity view accumulate, does doubt accumulate, does clinging to rites and rituals accumulate? That should not be said. Hatred accumulates… delusion accumulates; does identity view accumulate, does doubt accumulate, does clinging to rites and rituals accumulate? That should not be said.
Does an arahant fall away from arahantship? Yes. Does an arahant build up? That should not be said. Does an arahant tear down? That should not be said. Does an arahant abandon? That should not be said. Does an arahant cling? That should not be said. Does an arahant scatter? That should not be said. Does an arahant bind? That should not be said. Does an arahant put out? That should not be said. Does an arahant kindle? That should not be said.
But isn't it the case that an arahant neither builds up nor tears down, but remains established, having torn down? Yes. If an arahant neither builds up nor tears down, but remains established, having torn down, then surely you must not say: "An arahant falls away from arahantship."
But isn't it the case that an arahant neither abandons nor clings, but remains established, having abandoned? Yes. If an arahant neither abandons nor clings, but remains established, having abandoned, then surely you must not say: "An arahant falls away from arahantship."
But isn't it the case that an arahant neither scatters nor binds, but remains established, having scattered? Yes. If an arahant neither scatters nor binds, but remains established, having scattered, then surely you must not say: "An arahant falls away from arahantship."
But isn't it the case that an arahant neither puts out nor kindles, but remains established, having put out? Yes. If an arahant neither puts out nor kindles, but remains established, having put out, then surely you must not say: "An arahant falls away from arahantship."
