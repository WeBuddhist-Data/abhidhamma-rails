### Discussion of "Some of It Exists" ^5-7-0

#### Discussion of Some of the Past and So On ^5-7-1-0

Does the past exist? Some of it exists, some of it does not. Has some of it ceased and some not ceased; has some of it gone and some not gone; has some of it vanished and some not vanished; has some of it completely vanished and some not completely vanished? That should not be said…
Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. Of past states whose result has not ripened, do some exist and some not exist? That should not be said… Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. Of past states whose result has ripened, do some exist and some not exist? That should not be said… Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. Of past states that have no result, do some exist and some not exist? That should not be said…
Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. What exists and what does not? Past states whose result has not ripened — these exist; past states whose result has ripened — these do not exist. Do past states whose result has not ripened — these — exist? Yes. Do past states whose result has ripened — these — exist? That should not be said… Do past states whose result has not ripened — these — exist? Yes. Do past states that have no result — these — exist [variant: atītā avipakkavipākā dhammā te natthīti (Ka.)]? That should not be said…
Do past states whose result has ripened — these — not exist? Yes. Do past states whose result has not ripened — these — not exist? That should not be said…
Do past states that have no result [variant: vipakkavipākā (Syā.), avipakkavipākā (Ka.)] — these — not exist? Yes. Do past states whose result has not ripened [variant: avipākā (Syā.)] — these — not exist? That should not be said…
Do past states whose result has not ripened — these — exist? Yes. But isn't it the case that past states whose result has not ripened have ceased? Yes. If past states whose result has not ripened have ceased, then surely you must not say: "Past states whose result has not ripened — these [variant: dhammā niruddhā te (Syā., Ka.)] exist."
Do past states whose result has not ripened, which have ceased — these — exist? Yes. Do past states whose result has ripened, which have ceased — these — exist? That should not be said… Do past states whose result has not ripened, which have ceased — these — exist? Yes. Do past states that have no result [variant: avipakkavipākā (Sī., Ka.)], which have ceased — these — exist? That should not be said…
Do past states whose result has ripened, which have ceased — these — not exist? Yes. Do past states whose result has not ripened, which have ceased — these — not exist? That should not be said…
Do past states that have no result [variant: vipakkavipākā (Syā.), avipakkavipākā (Ka.)], which have ceased — these — not exist? Yes. Do past states whose result has not ripened [variant: avipākā (Syā.)], which have ceased — these — not exist? That should not be said…
Do past states whose result has not ripened, which have ceased — these — exist? Yes. Do past states whose result has ripened, which have ceased — these — not exist? Yes. Past states that are partly ones whose result has ripened and partly ones whose result has not ripened, which have ceased — of these, do some exist and some not exist? That should not be said…
Should it not be said that "past states whose result has not ripened — these exist"? Yes. But isn't it the case that past states whose result has not ripened will ripen? Yes. If past states whose result has not ripened will ripen, then surely you must also say: "Past states whose result has not ripened — these exist."
On the grounds that past states whose result has not ripened will ripen, do they exist? Yes. On the grounds that they will ripen, are they present? That should not be said… On the grounds that they will ripen, are they present? Yes. On the grounds that present states will cease, do they not exist? That should not be said… ^5-299

#### Discussion of Some of the Future and So On ^5-7-2-0

Does the future exist? Some of it exists, some of it does not. Is some of it born and some unborn; is some of it arisen and some not arisen; is some of it produced and some not produced; is some of it manifested and some not manifested? That should not be said…
Does some of the future exist and some not exist? Yes. Of future states that are bound to arise, do some exist and some not exist? That should not be said… Does some of the future exist and some not exist? Yes. Of future states that are not bound to arise, do some exist and some not exist? That should not be said…
Does some of the future exist and some not exist? Yes. What exists, and what does not? Future states that are bound to arise — these exist; future states that are not bound to arise — these do not exist. Do future states that are bound to arise — these — exist? Yes. Do future states that are not bound to arise — these — exist? That should not be said… Do future states that are not bound to arise — these — not exist? Yes. Do future states that are bound to arise — these — not exist? That should not be said…
Do future states that are bound to arise — these — exist? Yes. But isn't it the case that future states that are bound to arise are unborn? Yes. If future states that are bound to arise are unborn, then surely you must not say: "Future states that are bound to arise — these exist."
Do future states that are bound to arise, which are unborn — these — exist? Yes. Do future states that are not bound to arise, which are unborn — these — exist? That should not be said… Do future states that are not bound to arise, which are unborn — these — not exist? Yes. Do future states that are bound to arise, which are unborn — these — not exist? That should not be said…
Should it not be said that "future states that are bound to arise — these exist"? Yes. But isn't it the case that future states that are bound to arise will arise? Yes. If future states that are bound to arise will arise, then surely you must also say: "Future states that are bound to arise — these exist."
On the grounds that future states that are bound to arise will arise, do they exist? Yes. On the grounds that they will arise, are they present? That should not be said… On the grounds that they will arise, are they present? Yes. On the grounds that present states will cease, do they not exist? That should not be said…
