#### (186) 1. Discussion of Abandoning Defilements ^5-27-1-0

Does one abandon past defilements? Yes. Does one make cease what has ceased, make go what has gone, destroy what is destroyed, make vanish what has vanished, make completely vanish what has completely vanished? Does one abandon future defilements? Yes. Does one keep the unborn unborn, keep the unarisen unarisen, keep the unproduced unproduced, keep the unmanifest unmanifest? That should not be said… Does one abandon future defilements? Yes. But isn't it the case that the future is unborn? Yes. If the future is unborn, then surely you must not say: "One abandons future defilements." Does one abandon future defilements? Yes. But isn't it the case that the future does not exist? Yes. Does one abandon present defilements? Yes. Does one who is impassioned abandon lust, one who is corrupted abandon hatred, one who is deluded abandon delusion, one who is defiled abandon defilements? That should not be said… Does one abandon lust by means of lust, hatred by means of hatred, delusion by means of delusion, defilements by means of defilements? That should not be said…
Is lust associated with consciousness, and is the path associated with consciousness? Yes. Do two states of consciousness come together? That should not be said…
Is lust unwholesome, and is the path wholesome? Yes. Do phenomena that are wholesome and unwholesome, blameworthy and blameless, inferior and superior, dark and bright and counterparts of each other, come face to face? That should not be said…
Do phenomena that are wholesome and unwholesome, blameworthy and blameless, inferior and superior, dark and bright and counterparts of each other, come face to face? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, there are these four things that are very far apart from each other. Which four? The sky and the earth, monks: this is the first pair that is very far apart … therefore the teaching of the good is far from the bad"? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "wholesome and unwholesome … come face to face." ^5-830

Should it not be said that "one abandons past defilements, abandons future defilements, abandons present defilements"? Yes. Is there no one who abandons defilements? That should not be said… In that case, one abandons past defilements, abandons future defilements, abandons present defilements.
The Discussion of Abandoning Defilements is complete. ^5-831
