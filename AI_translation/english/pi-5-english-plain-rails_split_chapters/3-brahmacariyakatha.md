#### Plain Discussion of the Holy Life ^5-3-1-0

Is the holy life not lived among the gods? Yes. Are all gods dull, deaf [variant: eḷamūgā (Syā.)], unintelligent, communicating only by hand signs, unable to understand the meaning of what is well spoken and what is badly spoken; do all gods have no confidence in the Buddha, no confidence in the teaching, no confidence in the Community; do they not attend on the Buddha, the Blessed One, not ask questions of the Buddha, the Blessed One, and are they not pleased when the Buddha, the Blessed One, has answered a question; do all gods possess the obstruction of action, the obstruction of defilements, the obstruction of results; are they without faith, without aspiration, lacking wisdom, incapable of entering the fixed course of rightness in wholesome states; are all gods killers of their mothers, killers of their fathers, killers of arahants, ones who shed a Buddha's blood, ones who split the Community; do all gods kill living beings, take what is not given, engage in sexual misconduct, speak falsely, speak divisively, speak harshly, engage in idle chatter; are they covetous, with minds full of ill will, holding wrong view? That should not be said…
But isn't it the case that there are gods who are not dull, not deaf, intelligent, not communicating only by hand signs, able to understand the meaning of what is well spoken and what is badly spoken; that there are gods who have confidence in the Buddha, confidence in the teaching, confidence in the Community, who attend on the Buddha, the Blessed One, ask questions of the Buddha, the Blessed One, and are pleased when the Buddha, the Blessed One, has answered a question; that there are gods who do not possess the obstruction of action, do not possess the obstruction of defilements, do not possess the obstruction of results, who have faith, have aspiration, are wise, capable of entering the fixed course of rightness in wholesome states; that there are gods who are not killers of their mothers, not killers of their fathers, not killers of arahants, not ones who shed a Buddha's blood, not ones who split the Community; that there are gods who do not kill living beings, do not take what is not given, do not engage in sexual misconduct, do not speak falsely, do not speak divisively, do not speak harshly, do not engage in idle chatter, are not covetous, have minds free of ill will, and hold right view? Yes.
If there are gods who are not dull, not deaf, intelligent, not communicating only by hand signs, able to understand the meaning of what is well spoken and what is badly spoken… if there are gods who have confidence in the Buddha… who hold right view, then surely you must not say: "The holy life is not lived among the gods." ^5-269
