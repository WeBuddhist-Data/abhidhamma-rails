#### (156) 1. Discussion of Restraining the Mind ^5-24-1-0

Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person restrain another person's mind, saying "Do not be impassioned," "do not be corrupted," "do not be deluded," "do not be defiled"? That should not be said… does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person restrain another person's arisen contact, saying "Do not cease"? That should not be said… does one person restrain another person's arisen feeling… arisen perception… arisen volition… arisen consciousness… arisen faith… arisen energy… arisen mindfulness… arisen concentration… arisen wisdom, saying "Do not cease"? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person abandon lust for the benefit of another… abandon hatred… abandon fearlessness of wrongdoing? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person develop the path for the benefit of another… cultivate a foundation of mindfulness… cultivate an awakening factor? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person, for the benefit of another, fully understand suffering, abandon its origin, realize its cessation, and develop the path? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Is one person the doer for another — is pleasure and pain made by another, with one person doing and another experiencing it? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"By oneself alone is the bad done; by oneself one is defiled;
by oneself the bad is left undone; by oneself alone one is purified;
purity and impurity are one's own affair; no one can purify another" [Dha. Pa. 165]?
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "one person restrains another person's mind." ^5-743

Should it not be said that "one person restrains another person's mind"? Yes. But aren't there those who have attained power, those who have achieved mastery? Yes. If there are those who have attained power, those who have achieved mastery, then surely you must also say: "One person restrains another person's mind."
The Discussion of Restraining the Mind is complete. ^5-744

##### (158) 3. Discussion of Giving Happiness ^5-24-3-1-0

Does one person give happiness to another? Yes. Does one person give suffering to another? That should not be said… does one person not give suffering to another? Yes. Does one person not give happiness to another? That should not be said… does one person give happiness to another? Yes. Should it not be said that "one person gives happiness to another"? Yes. But did the venerable Udāyī not say this: "Surely the Blessed One has removed many painful things from us; surely the Blessed One has brought many pleasant things to us; surely the Blessed One has removed many unwholesome states from us; surely the Blessed One has brought many wholesome states to us" [Ma. Ni. 2.148, in the Laṭukikopama]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case, one person gives happiness to another.

##### (160) 5. Discussion of Material Form as a Root ^5-24-5-1-0

Is material form a root? Yes. Is it the root non-greed? That should not be said… the root non-hate… the root non-delusion… the root greed… the root hatred… is it the root delusion? That should not be said…

Is non-greed a root that has an object, and does it have turning… aspiration? Yes. Is material form a root that has an object, and does it have turning… aspiration?
