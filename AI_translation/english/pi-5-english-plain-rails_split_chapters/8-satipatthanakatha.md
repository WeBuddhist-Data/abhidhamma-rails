### Discussion of the Foundations of Mindfulness ^5-8-0

Are all phenomena foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Are all phenomena mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness, the mindfulness factor of awakening, the one-way path, leading to destruction, leading to awakening, leading to dismantling, free of taints, not subject to fetters, not subject to ties, not subject to floods, not subject to yokes, not subject to hindrances, not wrongly grasped, not subject to clinging, not subject to defilement; are all phenomena recollection of the Buddha, recollection of the teaching, recollection of the Community, recollection of moral discipline, recollection of generosity, recollection of the deities, mindfulness of breathing, recollection of death, mindfulness directed to the body, recollection of peace? That should not be said…
Are all phenomena foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Is the eye base a foundation of mindfulness? That should not be said… Is the eye base a foundation of mindfulness? Yes. Is the eye base mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness, the mindfulness factor of awakening, the one-way path, leading to destruction, leading to awakening, leading to dismantling, free of taints, not subject to fetters… not subject to defilement; is the eye base recollection of the Buddha, recollection of the teaching, recollection of the Community, recollection of moral discipline, recollection of generosity, recollection of the deities, mindfulness of breathing, recollection of death, mindfulness directed to the body, recollection of peace? That should not be said… Is the ear base… the nose base… the tongue base… the body base… the visible-form base… the sound base… the smell base… the taste base… the tangible base… lust… hatred… delusion… conceit… view… doubt… sluggishness… restlessness… shamelessness… fearlessness of wrongdoing a foundation of mindfulness? That should not be said… Is fearlessness of wrongdoing a foundation of mindfulness? Yes. Is fearlessness of wrongdoing mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness… mindfulness directed to the body, recollection of peace? That should not be said…

Should it not be said that "all phenomena are foundations of mindfulness"? Yes. But isn't it the case that mindfulness is established with regard to all phenomena? Yes. If mindfulness is established with regard to all phenomena, then surely you must also say: "All phenomena are foundations of mindfulness."
Since mindfulness is established with regard to every phenomenon, are all phenomena foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Since contact is established with regard to every phenomenon, are all phenomena foundations of contact? That should not be said…
Since mindfulness is established with regard to every phenomenon, are all phenomena foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Since feeling is established… perception is established… volition is established… consciousness is established with regard to every phenomenon, are all phenomena foundations of consciousness? That should not be said…
Are all phenomena foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Do all beings have mindfulness established, possess mindfulness, are they possessed of mindfulness; is mindfulness present in all beings? That should not be said… ^5-302

Are all phenomena foundations of mindfulness? Yes. [Dī. Ni. 2.373; Ma. Ni. 1.106; Saṃ. Ni. 5.367] Is there really such a discourse? Yes? Are all phenomena the one-way path? That should not be said…
