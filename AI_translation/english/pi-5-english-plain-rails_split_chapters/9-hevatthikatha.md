### Discussion of "It Exists in a Certain Way" ^5-9-0

That should not be said… Is the thing that exists the same thing that does not exist? Yes. The Discussion of "It Exists in a Certain Way" is complete.
Its summary:
Being found, falling away, living the holy life, part by part;
full understanding, abandoning sensual lust, the doctrine that everything exists, the bases;
past and future, well divided [variant: atītānāgatesu bhāgo (Syā.)], all phenomena are foundations of mindfulness;
it exists in a certain way and in a certain way does not exist.
