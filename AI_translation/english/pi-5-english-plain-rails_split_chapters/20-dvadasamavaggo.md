##### (117) 2. Discussion of Action ^5-20-2-1-0

Does all volition have a result? Yes. Does resultant indeterminate volition of the sense sphere have a result? That should not be said… does all volition have a result? Yes. Does resultant indeterminate volition of the fine-material sphere, of the formless sphere, and not included in any sphere have a result? That should not be said…
Does all volition have a result? Yes. Does functional indeterminate volition of the sense sphere have a result? That should not be said… does all volition have a result? Yes. Does functional indeterminate volition of the fine-material sphere and of the formless sphere have a result? That should not be said… ^5-633

Is resultant indeterminate volition without result? Yes. If resultant indeterminate volition is without result, then surely you must not say: "All volition has a result."
Is functional indeterminate volition without result? Yes. If functional indeterminate volition is without result, then surely you must not say: "All volition has a result."
Is resultant indeterminate volition of the sense sphere, of the fine-material sphere, of the formless sphere, and not included in any sphere without result? Yes. Is functional indeterminate volition of the sense sphere, of the fine-material sphere and of the formless sphere without result? Yes. If functional indeterminate volition of the formless sphere is without result, then surely you must not say: "All volition has a result." ^5-634

Should it not be said that "all action has a result"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, I do not declare that intentional actions that have been done and accumulated come to an end without their results being experienced — and that either in this very life, or in the next rebirth [variant: upapajjaṃ (A. Ni. 10.17)], or on some later occasion" [A. Ni. 10.217]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case, all action has a result.
The Discussion of Action is complete. ^5-635

The Discussion of Sound as Result is complete. ^5-637
