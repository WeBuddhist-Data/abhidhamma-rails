#### Description of Nines of Persons ^4-1-9-0

What kind of person is a Fully Awakened Buddha? Here, someone, in teachings not heard before, awakens to the truths by themselves, and attains there all-knowing wisdom and mastery of the powers. This person is called a Fully Awakened Buddha.
What kind of person is a Solitary Buddha? Here, someone, in teachings not heard before, awakens to the truths by themselves, but does not attain there all-knowing wisdom, nor mastery of the powers. This person is called a Solitary Buddha.
What kind of person is one liberated both ways? Here, someone lives having touched the eight liberations with the body, and, having seen with wisdom, their taints are destroyed. This person is called one liberated both ways.
What kind of person is one liberated by wisdom? Here, someone does not live having touched the eight liberations with the body, yet, having seen with wisdom, their taints are destroyed. This person is called one liberated by wisdom.
What kind of person is a body-witness? Here, someone lives having touched the eight liberations with the body, and, having seen with wisdom, some of their taints are destroyed. This person is called a body-witness.
What kind of person is one attained to view? Here, someone really understands, "This is suffering" … really understands, "This is the way leading to the cessation of suffering". The teachings proclaimed by the Tathāgata have been clearly seen and examined by them with wisdom, and, having seen with wisdom, some of their taints are destroyed. This person is called one attained to view.
What kind of person is one liberated by faith? Here, someone really understands, "This is suffering" … really understands, "This is the way leading to the cessation of suffering". The teachings proclaimed by the Tathāgata have been clearly seen and examined by them with wisdom, and, having seen with wisdom, some of their taints are destroyed, but not in the way they are for one attained to view. This person is called one liberated by faith.
What kind of person is a follower by the teaching? The person who is practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry, in whom the wisdom faculty is predominant, and who cultivates the noble path that is carried by wisdom and led by wisdom. This person is called a follower by the teaching. A person practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry is a follower by the teaching; once established in the fruit, they are one attained to view.
What kind of person is a follower by faith? The person who is practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry, in whom the faith faculty is predominant, and who cultivates the noble path that is carried by faith and led by faith. This person is called a follower by faith. A person practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry is a follower by faith; once established in the fruit, they are one liberated by faith.
End of the exposition of nines. ^4-208
