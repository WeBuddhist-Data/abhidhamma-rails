#### Description of Tens of Persons ^4-1-10-0

For which five is the goal reached here? For one with at most seven more lives, one who goes from family to family, one with a single seed, a once-returner, and whoever is an arahant in this very life – for these five, the goal is reached here.
For which five is the goal reached after leaving here? For one who attains final release in the interval, one who attains final release on arrival, one who attains final release without effort, one who attains final release with effort, and one who goes upstream to the Highest heaven – for these five, the goal is reached after leaving here.
That is what the description of persons covers.
End of the exposition of tens.
The treatise, the Description of Persons, is complete. ^4-209
