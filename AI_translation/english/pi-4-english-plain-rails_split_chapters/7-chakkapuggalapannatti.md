#### Description of Sixes of Persons ^4-1-6-0

Of these, the person who, in teachings not heard before, awakens to the truths by themselves, and attains there all-knowing wisdom and mastery of the powers – this should be understood to mean a Fully Awakened Buddha.
Of these, the person who, in teachings not heard before, awakens to the truths by themselves, but does not attain there all-knowing wisdom or mastery of the powers – this should be understood to mean a Solitary Buddha.
Of these, the person who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, but makes an end of suffering in this very life [variant: dukkhassantaṃ karoti (Sī.); likewise further on] and attains the perfection of a disciple – this should be understood to mean Sāriputta and Moggallāna.
Of these, the person who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, but makes an end of suffering in this very life, though they do not attain the perfection of a disciple – this should be understood to mean the remaining arahants.
Of these, the person who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, and does not make an end of suffering in this very life, but is a non-returner who does not come back to this state of existence – this should be understood to mean a non-returner.
Of these, the person who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, and does not make an end of suffering in this very life, but comes back to this state of existence – this should be understood to mean stream-enterers and once-returners.
End of the exposition of sixes. ^4-202
