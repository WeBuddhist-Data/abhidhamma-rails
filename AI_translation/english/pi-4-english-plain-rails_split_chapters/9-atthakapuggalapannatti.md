#### Description of Eights of Persons ^4-1-8-0

In this context, what are the four persons who possess the path and the four persons who possess the fruit? A stream-enterer, and one practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry; a once-returner, and one practising to realize the fruit of once-returning; a non-returner, and one practising to realize the fruit of non-returning; an arahant, and one practising to realize the fruit of arahantship [variant: arahattāya (Syā., Ka.) A. Ni. 8.59]; these are the four persons who possess the path, and these are the four persons who possess the fruit.
End of the exposition of eights. ^4-207
