### Outline ^4-M-0

#### Outline of Single Types ^4-M-1-0

There are six descriptions – the description of the aggregates, the description of the bases, the description of the elements, the description of the truths, the description of the faculties, and the description of persons. ^4-M-1

What does the description of the aggregates cover? It covers the five aggregates – the aggregate of material form, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of formations, and the aggregate of consciousness; that is what the description of the aggregates covers. ^4-M-2

What does the description of the bases cover? It covers the twelve bases – the eye base, the visible-form base, the ear base, the sound base, the nose base, the smell base, the tongue base, the taste base, the body base, the tangible base, the mind base, and the mind-object base; that is what the description of the bases covers. ^4-M-3

What does the description of the elements cover? It covers the eighteen elements – the eye element, the visible-form element, the eye-consciousness element, the ear element, the sound element, the ear-consciousness element, the nose element, the smell element, the nose-consciousness element, the tongue element, the taste element, the tongue-consciousness element, the body element, the tangible element, the body-consciousness element, the mind-element, the mind-object element, and the mind-consciousness element; that is what the description of the elements covers. ^4-M-4

What does the description of the truths cover? It covers the four truths – the truth of suffering, the truth of its origin, the truth of its ending, and the truth of the path; that is what the description of the truths covers. ^4-M-5

What does the description of the faculties cover? It covers the twenty-two faculties – the eye faculty, the ear faculty, the nose faculty, the tongue faculty, the body faculty, the mind faculty, the femininity faculty, the masculinity faculty, the life faculty, the pleasure faculty, the suffering faculty, the joy faculty, the displeasure faculty, the equanimity faculty, the faith faculty, the energy faculty, the mindfulness faculty, the concentration faculty, the wisdom faculty, the faculty of "I shall know what is not yet known", the faculty of final knowledge, and the faculty of one who has fully known; that is what the description of the faculties covers. ^4-M-6

#### Outline of Sixes ^4-M-6-0

Six persons –
(1) There is someone who, in teachings not heard before, awakens to the truths by themselves, and attains there all-knowing wisdom and mastery of the powers [variant: phalesu (Pī.)]. There is someone who, in teachings not heard before, awakens to the truths by themselves, but does not attain there all-knowing wisdom or mastery of the powers. There is someone who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, but makes an end of suffering in this very life and attains the perfection of a disciple. There is someone who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, but makes an end of suffering in this very life, though they do not attain the perfection of a disciple. There is someone who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, and does not make an end of suffering in this very life, but is a non-returner who does not come back [variant: anāgantvā (Syā., Ka.) A. Ni. 4.171] to this state of existence. There is someone who, in teachings not heard before, does not awaken to the truths by themselves, and does not make an end of suffering in this very life, but is a returner [variant: sotāpannasakadāgāmī (Syā., Ka.)] who comes back to this state of existence.
