#### Description of Sevens of Persons ^4-1-7-0

And how is a person one who has sunk once and stays sunk? Here, someone possesses unwholesome states that are entirely black. In this way a person is one who has sunk once and stays sunk.
And how is a person one who comes up and sinks again? Here, someone comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good [variant: sāhu (Sī., Syā.); likewise in all three places], moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy [variant: viriyaṃ (Sī., Syā.)] in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." But their faith neither stays nor grows; it only declines. Their conscience neither stays nor grows; it only declines. Their moral fear neither stays nor grows; it only declines. Their energy neither stays nor grows; it only declines. Their wisdom neither stays nor grows; it only declines. In this way a person is one who comes up and sinks again.
And how is a person one who comes up and stays established? Here, someone comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Their faith neither declines nor grows; it stays established. Their conscience neither declines nor grows; it stays established. Their moral fear neither declines nor grows; it stays established. Their energy neither declines nor grows; it stays established. Their wisdom neither declines nor grows; it stays established. In this way a person is one who comes up and stays established.
And how is a person one who comes up, sees clearly and looks around? Here, someone comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the complete destruction of three fetters, they are a stream-enterer, no longer liable to fall into the lower realms, fixed in destiny, and headed for awakening. In this way a person is one who comes up, sees clearly and looks around.
And how is a person one who comes up and swims across? Here, someone comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the complete destruction of three fetters and the weakening of lust, hatred and delusion, they are a once-returner; coming back to this world only once more, they make an end of suffering. In this way a person is one who comes up and swims across.
And how is a person one who comes up and attains a foothold? Here, someone comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the complete destruction of the five lower fetters, they are reborn spontaneously; they attain final release there, and are not liable to return from that world. In this way a person is one who comes up and attains a foothold.
And how is a person one who comes up and is the brahmin who has crossed over, reached the far shore and stands on land? Here, someone comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the destruction of the taints, they have realized by direct knowledge themselves, in this very life, the liberation of mind and liberation by wisdom that is free of taints, and live having entered it. In this way a person is one who comes up and is the brahmin who has crossed over, reached the far shore and stands on land. ^4-203

What kind of person is one liberated both ways? Here, someone lives having touched the eight liberations with the body, and, having seen with wisdom, their taints are destroyed. This person is called one liberated both ways. ^4-204

What kind of person is one liberated by wisdom … a body-witness … one attained to view … one liberated by faith … a follower by the teaching …. ^4-205

What kind of person is a follower by faith? The person who is practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry, in whom the faith faculty is predominant, and who cultivates the noble path that is carried by faith and led by faith. This person is called a follower by faith. A person practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry is a follower by faith; once established in the fruit, they are one liberated by faith.
End of the exposition of sevens. ^4-206
