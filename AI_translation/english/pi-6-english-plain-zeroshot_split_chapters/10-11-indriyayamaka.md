At the moment of dissolution of the final consciousness associated with equanimity, for those possessing the final consciousness associated with joy, for beings without perception, the mind faculty will not arise there, and the equanimity faculty does not arise there either. (Based on the equanimity faculty.) ^6-10-401

For whomever and wherever the faith faculty does not arise, will the wisdom faculty… the mind faculty not arise for them there?
For everyone, at the moment of dissolution of consciousness, at the moment of arising of consciousness not associated with faith, the faith faculty does not arise there, but the mind faculty will arise there. At the moment of dissolution of the final consciousness, for beings without perception, the faith faculty does not arise there, and the mind faculty will not arise there either.
