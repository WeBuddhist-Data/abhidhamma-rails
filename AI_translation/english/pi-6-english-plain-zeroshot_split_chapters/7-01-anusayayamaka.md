There are seven underlying tendencies – the underlying tendency to sensual desire, to aversion, to conceit, to wrong view, to doubt, to desire for existence, to ignorance. ^6-7-1

#### The Section on the Place of Arising ^6-7-1-0

Where does the underlying tendency to sensual desire lie latent? In the sense sphere, in two kinds of feeling, the underlying tendency to sensual desire lies latent there.
Where does the underlying tendency to aversion lie latent? In painful feeling, the underlying tendency to aversion lies latent there.
Where does the underlying tendency to conceit lie latent? In the sense sphere, in two kinds of feeling, in the form sphere and in the formless sphere, the underlying tendency to conceit lies latent there.
Where does the underlying tendency to wrong view lie latent? In all phenomena included in personal existence, the underlying tendency to wrong view lies latent there.
Where does the underlying tendency to doubt lie latent? In all phenomena included in personal existence, the underlying tendency to doubt lies latent there.
Where does the underlying tendency to desire for existence lie latent? In the form sphere and in the formless sphere, the underlying tendency to desire for existence lies latent there.
