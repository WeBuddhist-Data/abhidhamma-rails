#### Description of Persons in Tens ^4-1-10-0

Which five reach the goal here? One with at most seven more lives, one who goes from family to family, one with a single seed, a once-returner, and one who becomes a worthy one (arahant) in this very life – these five reach the goal here.
Which five reach the goal after leaving here? One who attains final release in the interval, one who attains final release on arrival, one who attains final release without effort, one who attains final release with effort, and one who goes upstream to the highest heaven – these five reach the goal after leaving here.
This is the full extent of the description of persons.
The exposition of the tens.
The treatise "The Description of Persons" is complete. ^4-209
