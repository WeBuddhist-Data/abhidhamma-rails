#### Description of Persons in Sevens ^4-1-7-0

And how does a person sink once and stay sunk? Here a certain person possesses unwholesome states that are entirely black. This is how a person sinks once and stays sunk.
And how does a person come up and sink again? Here a certain person comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good [variant: sāhu (Sī., Syā.), and likewise in all three places], moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy [variant: viriyaṃ (Sī., Syā.)] in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." But that faith of theirs neither stays nor grows; it only declines. That conscience of theirs neither stays nor grows; it only declines. That moral fear of theirs neither stays nor grows; it only declines. That energy of theirs neither stays nor grows; it only declines. That wisdom of theirs neither stays nor grows; it only declines. This is how a person comes up and sinks again.
And how does a person come up and stay there? Here a certain person comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." That faith of theirs neither declines nor grows; it stays as it is. That conscience of theirs neither declines nor grows; it stays as it is. That moral fear of theirs neither declines nor grows; it stays as it is. That energy of theirs neither declines nor grows; it stays as it is. That wisdom of theirs neither declines nor grows; it stays as it is. This is how a person comes up and stays there.
And how does a person come up, see and look around? Here a certain person comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the complete destruction of the three fetters, they are a stream-enterer, no longer liable to fall into the lower realms, fixed in their course, headed for full awakening. This is how a person comes up, sees and looks around.
And how does a person come up and swim across? Here a certain person comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the complete destruction of the three fetters and the weakening of lust, hatred and delusion, they are a once-returner; coming back to this world only once more, they make an end of suffering. This is how a person comes up and swims across.
And how does a person come up and reach a foothold? Here a certain person comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the complete destruction of the five lower fetters, they are reborn spontaneously, attain final release there, and are not liable to return from that world. This is how a person comes up and reaches a foothold.
And how does a person come up, cross over, reach the far shore, and stand on dry land, a true brahmin? Here a certain person comes up, thinking: "Faith in wholesome states is good, conscience in wholesome states is good, moral fear in wholesome states is good, energy in wholesome states is good, wisdom in wholesome states is good." Through the destruction of the taints, they have realized for themselves by direct knowledge, here and now, the liberation of mind and liberation by wisdom that are free of taints, and have entered upon it and live in it. This is how a person comes up, crosses over, reaches the far shore, and stands on dry land, a true brahmin. ^4-203

What kind of person is one liberated in both ways? Here a certain person lives having touched the eight liberations with the body, and, having seen with wisdom, their taints are destroyed – this person is called "one liberated in both ways". ^4-204

What kind of person is one liberated by wisdom … a body-witness … one who has attained to view … one liberated by faith … a follower of the Teaching …. ^4-205

What kind of person is a follower by faith? A person practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry, in whom the faculty of faith is predominant, and who develops the noble path carried by faith and led by faith – this person is called "a follower by faith". A person practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry is a follower by faith; once established in the fruit, they are one liberated by faith.
