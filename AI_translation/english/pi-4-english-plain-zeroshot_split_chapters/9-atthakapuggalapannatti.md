#### Description of Persons in Eights ^4-1-8-0

And here, which are the four persons who possess a path and the four who possess a fruit? A stream-enterer, and one practising to realize the fruit of stream-entry; a once-returner, and one practising to realize the fruit of once-returning; a non-returner, and one practising to realize the fruit of non-returning; a worthy one (arahant), and one practising to realize the fruit of arahantship [variant: arahattāya (Syā., Ka.); A. Ni. 8.59]. These are the four persons who possess a path, and these are the four who possess a fruit.
