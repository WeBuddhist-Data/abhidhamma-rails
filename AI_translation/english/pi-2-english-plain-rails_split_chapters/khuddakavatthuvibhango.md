#### Explanation of Ones ^2-17-2-0

##### (1) Intoxication with birth ^2-17-2-1-0

Herein, what is intoxication with birth? Intoxication, becoming intoxicated, having become intoxicated; conceit, feeling conceited, having felt conceited; elation, feeling elated; holding oneself up like a banner; an air of self-importance; and a mental wish to seem outstanding, based on birth — this is called "intoxication with birth". ^2-843

##### (2-27) Intoxication with clan, and so on ^2-17-2-2-0

Herein, what is intoxication with clan? Based on clan... based on health... based on youth... based on life... based on gain... based on honor... based on respect... based on precedence... based on retinue... based on wealth... based on beauty... based on learning... based on quick understanding... based on seniority... based on being an alms-gatherer... based on not being despised... based on deportment... based on psychic power... based on fame... based on virtue... based on meditative absorption... based on craft... based on height... based on girth... based on bodily build... based on completeness — intoxication, becoming intoxicated, having become intoxicated; conceit, feeling conceited, having felt conceited; elation, feeling elated; holding oneself up like a banner; an air of self-importance; and a mental wish to seem outstanding — this is called "intoxication with completeness". ^2-844

##### (28) Intoxication ^2-17-2-3-0

##### (29) Negligence ^2-17-2-4-0

Herein, what is negligence? With respect to bodily misconduct, verbal misconduct, or mental misconduct, or the five strands of sense pleasure, letting the mind loose and repeatedly indulging it; with respect to developing wholesome qualities, acting carelessly, acting inconsistently, acting without steadiness, living in a slack way, giving up one's motivation, giving up one's sense of responsibility, not practicing, not developing, not cultivating repeatedly, lacking resolve, lacking application — this is negligence. Whatever such negligence, becoming negligent, having become negligent — this is called "negligence". ^2-846

##### (30) Obstinacy ^2-17-2-5-0

Herein, what is obstinacy? Whatever obstinacy, becoming obstinate, having become obstinate; hardness, harshness, rigidity of mind, lack of gentleness — this is called "obstinacy". ^2-847

##### (31) Contentiousness ^2-17-2-6-0

##### (32) Excessive desire ^2-17-2-7-0

Herein, what is excessive desire? Not being content with one's various robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicinal requisites for illness, or with the five strands of sense pleasure, wanting more — whatever such wish, resorting to wishing, excessive desire, passion, intense passion, the mind's intense passion — this is called "excessive desire". ^2-849

##### (33) Great desire ^2-17-2-8-0

Herein, what is great desire? Not being content with one's various robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicinal requisites for illness, or with the five strands of sense pleasure, wanting more — whatever such wish, resorting to wishing, great desire, passion, intense passion, the mind's intense passion — this is called "great desire". ^2-850

##### (34) Evil desire ^2-17-2-9-0

Herein, what is evil desire? Here someone without faith wishes, "let people know me as faithful"; being of poor conduct, wishes, "let people know me as virtuous"; having heard little, wishes, "let people know me as learned"; delighting in company, wishes, "let people know me as one who lives in seclusion"; being lazy, wishes, "let people know me as one whose energy is roused"; being forgetful, wishes, "let people know me as one whose mindfulness is established"; being unconcentrated, wishes, "let people know me as concentrated"; lacking wisdom, wishes, "let people know me as wise"; not having destroyed the taints, wishes, "let people know me as one who has destroyed the taints" — whatever such wish, resorting to wishing, evil desire, passion, intense passion, the mind's intense passion — this is called "evil desire". ^2-851
