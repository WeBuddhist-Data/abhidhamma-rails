#### Abhidhamma Classification ^2-14-1-0

There are five training rules: the training rule of abstention from killing living beings, the training rule of abstention from taking what is not given, the training rule of abstention from sexual misconduct, the training rule of abstention from false speech, the training rule of abstention from intoxicants that cause negligence. ^2-703

Herein, what is the training rule of abstention from killing living beings? At the time when a sense-sphere wholesome consciousness has arisen, accompanied by joy, joined with knowledge, in one who is abstaining from killing living beings — whatever at that time is the abstaining, avoiding, refraining, abstention, not doing, not committing, not transgressing the boundary, cutting off the bridge to it, from killing living beings: this is called "the training rule of abstention from killing living beings". The remaining states are joined with the abstention.

#### Questions and Answers ^2-14-2-0

There are five training rules: the training rule of abstention from killing living beings, the training rule of abstention from taking what is not given, the training rule of abstention from sexual misconduct, the training rule of abstention from false speech, the training rule of abstention from intoxicants that cause negligence. ^2-714

Of the five training rules, how many are wholesome, how many unwholesome, how many neutral... how many conflicted, how many peaceful? ^2-715

##### Triads ^2-14-2-1-0

They are only wholesome. They may be joined with pleasant feeling, or may be joined with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. They are things that produce a result. They are not clung to but able to be clung to. They are not defiled but open to defilement. They have applied and sustained thought. They may be accompanied by joy, may be accompanied by happiness, or may be accompanied by equanimity.
They are to be abandoned by neither seeing nor development. They are rooted in neither what is to be abandoned by seeing nor what is to be abandoned by development. They lead to accumulation. They are neither in training nor beyond training. They are limited. They have a limited object. They are middling. They are not fixed. They should not be said to have the path as object, as root, or as dominant factor. They may be arisen, or may be not yet arisen; they should not be said to be "bound to arise". They may be past, may be future, or may be present. They have a present object. They may be internal, may be external, or may be both internal and external. They have an external object. They are invisible and without resistance. ^2-716

##### Pairs ^2-14-2-2-0

They are not a root. They are rooted. They are joined with a root. They should not be said to be both a root and rooted; they are rooted but not themselves a root. They should not be said to be both a root and joined with a root; they are joined with a root but not themselves a root. They are not a root, but are rooted. They have conditions. They are conditioned. They are invisible. They offer no resistance. They are immaterial. They are worldly. They are knowable by some kinds of consciousness, not knowable by others.
They are not themselves a mental taint. They are subject to mental taints. They are dissociated from a mental taint. They should not be said to be both a mental taint and subject to mental taints; they are subject to mental taints but not themselves a mental taint. They should not be said to be both a mental taint and joined with a mental taint, or to be joined with a mental taint but not itself a mental taint. They are dissociated from a mental taint but still subject to it. They are not themselves a fetter... not themselves a tie... not themselves a flood... not themselves a yoke... not themselves a hindrance... not themselves wrong grasping... They have an object. They are not mind. They are mental factors. They are joined with mind. They are mingled with mind. They originate from mind. They co-arise with mind. They follow mind. They are mingled with mind and originating from mind. They are mingled with mind, originating from mind, and co-arising with mind. They are mingled with mind, originating from mind, and following mind. They are external. They are not derived. They are not clung to. They are not themselves clinging... not themselves a defilement.
They are not to be abandoned by seeing. They are not to be abandoned by development. They are not rooted in what is to be abandoned by seeing. They are not rooted in what is to be abandoned by development. They have applied thought. They have sustained thought. They may have joy, or may lack joy. They may be accompanied by joy, or may not be accompanied by joy. They may be accompanied by happiness, or may not be accompanied by happiness. They may be accompanied by equanimity, or may not be accompanied by equanimity. They belong to the sense sphere. They do not belong to the fine-material sphere. They do not belong to the immaterial sphere. They are included. They do not lead to liberation. They are not fixed. They are surpassable. They are peaceful.
