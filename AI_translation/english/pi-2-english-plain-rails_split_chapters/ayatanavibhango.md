Herein, what is the eye sense-field? The eye that, derived from the four great elements, is a sensitive surface, included in one's own being, invisible, and offering resistance; that eye — itself invisible and offering resistance — by which one has seen, sees, will see, or may see a visible form that is visible and offers resistance: this is the eye, this is the eye sense-field, this is the eye element, this is the eye faculty; this is the world, this is the door, this is the ocean; this is the bright, this is the field, this is the ground; this is the guide, this is the leader; this is the near shore, this is the empty village.
