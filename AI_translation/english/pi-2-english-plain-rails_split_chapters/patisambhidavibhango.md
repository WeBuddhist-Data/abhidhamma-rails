#### Discourse Classification ^2-15-1-0

##### Summary Section ^2-15-1-1-0

There are four analytical knowledges: the analytical knowledge of meaning, the analytical knowledge of principles, the analytical knowledge of language, the analytical knowledge of ready understanding. Knowledge of meaning is the analytical knowledge of meaning; knowledge of principles is the analytical knowledge of principles; knowledge of the language used to express those principles is the analytical knowledge of language; knowledge of these knowledges is the analytical knowledge of ready understanding. This is the summary section. ^2-718

##### Scripture Section ^2-15-1-6-0

There are four analytical knowledges: the analytical knowledge of meaning, the analytical knowledge of principles, the analytical knowledge of language, the analytical knowledge of ready understanding.
Herein, what is the analytical knowledge of principles? Here a monk knows the teaching — discourses, mixed prose-and-verse, expositions, verses, inspired utterances, "thus it was said" texts, birth stories, marvellous accounts, and question-and-answer texts. They are not fetters... they are not ties... they are not floods... they are not yokes... they are not hindrances... they are not wrong grasping... They have an object. They are not mind. They are mental factors. They are joined with mind. They are mingled with mind. They originate from mind. They co-arise with mind. They follow mind. They are mingled with and originate from mind. They are mingled with, originate from, and co-arise with mind. They are mingled with, originate from, and follow mind. They are external. They are not derived. They are not clung to.
They are not clinging... they are not defilements... they are not to be abandoned by seeing. They are not to be abandoned by development. They are not rooted in what is to be abandoned by seeing. They are not rooted in what is to be abandoned by development. Three analytical knowledges have applied thought; the analytical knowledge of meaning may have applied thought, or may be without applied thought. Three analytical knowledges have sustained thought; the analytical knowledge of meaning may have sustained thought, or may be without sustained thought. They may have joy, or may be without joy. They may be accompanied by joy, or may not be accompanied by joy. They may be accompanied by happiness, or may not be accompanied by happiness. They may be accompanied by equanimity, or may not be accompanied by equanimity. Three analytical knowledges belong to the sense sphere; the analytical knowledge of meaning may belong to the sense sphere, or may not belong to the sense sphere. They do not belong to the fine-material sphere. They do not belong to the immaterial sphere. Three analytical knowledges are included; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be included, or may not be included. Three analytical knowledges do not lead to liberation; the analytical knowledge of meaning may lead to liberation, or may not lead to liberation. Three analytical knowledges are not fixed; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be fixed, or may be not fixed. Three analytical knowledges are surpassable; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be surpassable, or may be unsurpassed. They are peaceful.
