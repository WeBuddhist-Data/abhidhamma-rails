### Analysis of the Aggregates ^2-1-0

#### Discourse Classification ^2-1-1-0

The five aggregates: the aggregate of material form, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of formations, the aggregate of consciousness. ^2-1

##### The Aggregate of Material Form ^2-1-1-1-0

Herein, what is the aggregate of material form? Whatever material form there is — past, future, or present; internal or external; coarse or subtle; inferior or superior; far or near — all of that, gathered and grouped together as one: this is called the aggregate of material form. ^2-2

Herein, what is past material form? Material form that has ceased, disappeared, changed, gone, completely vanished — having arisen and then passed away — belonging to the past, counted as belonging to the past, namely the four great elements and material form derived from the four great elements: this is called past material form.
Herein, what is future material form? Material form that is not born, has not come to be, has not been generated, has not been produced, has not been brought into being, has not appeared, has not arisen, has not co-arisen, has not come up, has not originated, belonging to the future, counted as belonging to the future, namely the four great elements and material form derived from the four great elements: this is called future material form.
Herein, what is present material form? Material form that is born, has come to be, has been generated, has been produced, has been brought into being, has appeared, has arisen, has co-arisen, has come up, has originated, belonging to the present, counted as belonging to the present, namely the four great elements and material form derived from the four great elements: this is called present material form. ^2-3

Herein, what is internal material form? Material form belonging internally, individually, exclusively, and personally, to this or that being, and clung to — namely the four great elements and material form derived from the four great elements: this is called internal material form.
Herein, what is external material form? Material form belonging internally, individually, exclusively, and personally, to this or that other being or other person, and clung to — namely the four great elements and material form derived from the four great elements: this is called external material form. ^2-4

Herein, what is coarse material form? The eye sense-field ... the tangible sense-field: this is called coarse material form.
Herein, what is subtle material form? The faculty of femininity ... solid food: this is called subtle material form. ^2-5

Herein, what is inferior material form? Material form that is looked down on, despised, scorned, treated with contempt, disregarded by this or that being — considered inferior, deemed inferior, regarded as inferior, unwished-for, unwanted, displeasing — namely sights, sounds, smells, tastes, and tangibles: this is called inferior material form.
Herein, what is superior material form? Material form that is not looked down on, not despised, not scorned, not treated with contempt, not disregarded, but is honored by this or that being — considered superior, deemed superior, regarded as superior, wished-for, wanted, pleasing — namely sights, sounds, smells, tastes, and tangibles: this is called superior material form. But material form should also be seen as inferior or superior only in comparison with this or that other material form. ^2-6

Herein, what is far material form? The faculty of femininity ... solid food, and whatever other material form there is that is not close by, not near at hand, far, not nearby: this is called far material form.
Herein, what is near material form? The eye sense-field ... the tangible sense-field, and whatever other material form there is that is close by, near at hand, not far, nearby: this is called near material form. But material form should also be seen as far or near only in comparison with this or that other material form. ^2-7

Herein, what is external feeling? Feeling belonging internally, individually, exclusively, and personally, to this or that other being or other person — namely pleasant feeling, painful feeling, and neutral feeling: this is called external feeling. ^2-10

Pleasant-and-painful feeling is inferior, neutral feeling is superior. The feeling of one not in an attainment is inferior, the feeling of one in an attainment is superior. Feeling with mental taints is inferior, feeling free of mental taints is superior. But feeling should also be seen as inferior or superior only in comparison with this or that other feeling. ^2-12

Herein, what is near feeling? Unwholesome feeling is near unwholesome feeling; wholesome feeling is near wholesome feeling; neutral feeling is near neutral feeling. Painful feeling is near painful feeling; pleasant feeling is near pleasant feeling; neutral feeling is near neutral feeling. The feeling of one not in an attainment is near the feeling of one not in an attainment; the feeling of one in an attainment is near the feeling of one in an attainment. Feeling with mental taints is near feeling with mental taints; feeling free of mental taints is near feeling free of mental taints. This is called near feeling. ##### The Aggregate of Material Form ^2-1-2-1-0

Herein, what is the aggregate of material form? As one kind, the aggregate of material form is: all material form is not a root, rootless, dissociated from a root, with conditions, conditioned, material form, worldly, subject to mental taints, bound by a fetter, tied, swept by a flood, yoked, subject to hindrance, wrongly grasped, able to be clung to, open to defilement, neutral, without an object, not a mental factor, dissociated from mind, neither a result nor something that produces a result, not defiled but open to defilement, without applied and sustained thought, not merely with sustained thought alone, without applied thought and without sustained thought, not accompanied by joy, not accompanied by happiness, not accompanied by equanimity, to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development, not rooted in what is to be abandoned by seeing or by development, neither leading to accumulation nor to dismantling, neither in training nor beyond training, limited, belonging to the sense sphere, not belonging to the fine-material sphere, not belonging to the immaterial sphere, included, not un-included, not fixed, not leading to liberation, arisen, cognizable by the six kinds of consciousness, impermanent, overcome by aging. This is how, as one kind, the aggregate of material form is described.
As two kinds, the aggregate of material form is: there is material form that is derived, and material form that is not derived. There is material form that is clung to, and material form that is not clung to. There is material form that is clung to and able to be clung to, and material form that is not clung to but able to be clung to. There is material form that is visible, and material form that is invisible. There is material form that offers resistance, and material form that does not. There is material form that is a faculty, and material form that is not a faculty. There is material form that is a great element, and material form that is not a great element. There is material form that is expression, and material form that is not expression. There is material form that originates from mind, and material form that does not. There is material form that co-arises with mind, and material form that does not. There is material form that follows mind, and material form that does not. There is material form that is internal, and material form that is external. There is material form that is coarse, and material form that is subtle. There is material form that is far, and material form that is near ... there is material form that is solid food, and material form that is not solid food. This is how, as two kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
(As it was analyzed in the section on material form, so it should be analyzed here.)
As three kinds, the aggregate of material form is: material form that is internal is derived; material form that is external is either derived or not derived. Material form that is internal is clung to; material form that is external is either clung to or not clung to. Material form that is internal is clung to and able to be clung to; material form that is external is either clung to and able to be clung to, or not clung to but able to be clung to ... material form that is internal is not solid food; material form that is external is either solid food or not solid food. This is how, as three kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As four kinds, the aggregate of material form is: material form that is derived is either clung to or not clung to; material form that is not derived is either clung to or not clung to. Material form that is derived is either clung to and able to be clung to, or not clung to but able to be clung to; material form that is not derived is likewise either clung to and able to be clung to, or not clung to but able to be clung to. Material form that is derived either offers resistance or does not; material form that is not derived either offers resistance or does not. Material form that is derived is either coarse or subtle; material form that is not derived is either coarse or subtle. Material form that is derived is either far or near; material form that is not derived is either far or near ... it is seen, heard, sensed, or cognized material form. This is how, as four kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As five kinds, the aggregate of material form is: the earth element, the water element, the fire element, the air element, and material form that is derived. This is how, as five kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As six kinds, the aggregate of material form is: material form cognizable by eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, mind-consciousness. This is how, as six kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As seven kinds, the aggregate of material form is: material form cognizable by eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, the mind element, the mind-consciousness element. This is how, as seven kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As eight kinds, the aggregate of material form is: material form cognizable by eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness; material form cognizable by body-consciousness is either accompanied by a pleasant contact or accompanied by a painful contact; material form cognizable by the mind element; material form cognizable by the mind-consciousness element. This is how, as eight kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As nine kinds, the aggregate of material form is: the eye faculty, the ear faculty, the nose faculty, the tongue faculty, the body faculty, the faculty of femininity, the faculty of masculinity, the life faculty, and material form that is not a faculty. This is how, as nine kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As ten kinds, the aggregate of material form is: the eye faculty, the ear faculty, the nose faculty, the tongue faculty, the body faculty, the faculty of femininity, the faculty of masculinity, the life faculty, and material form that is not a faculty, either offering resistance or not. This is how, as ten kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
As eleven kinds, the aggregate of material form is: the eye sense-field, the ear sense-field, the nose sense-field, the tongue sense-field, the body sense-field, the sight sense-field, the sense-field of sounds, the smell sense-field, the taste sense-field, the tangible sense-field, and whatever material form is invisible, without resistance, and included in the mental-object sense-field. This is how, as eleven kinds, the aggregate of material form is described.
This is called the aggregate of material form. ^2-33

##### The Aggregate of Feeling ^2-1-2-2-0

Herein, what is the aggregate of feeling? As one kind, the aggregate of feeling is: joined with contact.
As two kinds, the aggregate of feeling is: some is rooted, some is rootless.
As three kinds, the aggregate of feeling is: some is wholesome, some is unwholesome, some is neutral.
As four kinds, the aggregate of feeling is: some belongs to the sense sphere, some to the fine-material sphere, some to the immaterial sphere, some is not included in any sphere.
As five kinds, the aggregate of feeling is: some is the faculty of pleasure, some is the faculty of pain, some is the faculty of gladness, some is the faculty of sadness, some is the faculty of equanimity. This is how, as five kinds, the aggregate of feeling is described.
As six kinds, the aggregate of feeling is: feeling born of eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, mind-contact. This is how, as six kinds, the aggregate of feeling is described.
