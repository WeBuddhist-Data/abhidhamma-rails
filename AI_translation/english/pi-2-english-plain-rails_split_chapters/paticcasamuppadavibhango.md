### Analysis of Dependent Origination ^2-6-0

#### Discourse Classification ^2-6-1-0

Dependent on ignorance, formations come to be. Dependent on formations, consciousness comes to be. Dependent on consciousness, mind-and-matter comes to be. Dependent on mind-and-matter, the six sense-fields come to be. Dependent on the six sense-fields, contact comes to be. Dependent on contact, feeling comes to be. Dependent on feeling, craving comes to be. Dependent on craving, clinging comes to be. Dependent on clinging, existence comes to be. Dependent on existence, birth comes to be. Dependent on birth, aging-and-death comes to be, together with sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair. Thus does this entire mass of suffering come to be. ^2-225

Herein, what is ignorance? Not knowing suffering, not knowing the origin of suffering, not knowing the cessation of suffering, not knowing the way leading to the cessation of suffering: this is called "ignorance".
Herein, what are the formations dependent on ignorance? Merit-producing formations, demerit-producing formations, imperturbable formations, bodily formations, verbal formations, mental formations.
Herein, what is the merit-producing formation? Wholesome volition belonging to the sense sphere and the fine-material sphere, consisting of giving, of virtue, and of meditative development: this is called "the merit-producing formation".
Herein, what is the demerit-producing formation? Unwholesome volition belonging to the sense sphere: this is called "the demerit-producing formation".
Herein, what is the imperturbable formation? Wholesome volition belonging to the immaterial sphere: this is called "the imperturbable formation".
Herein, what is the bodily formation? Bodily volition is the bodily formation, verbal volition is the verbal formation, mental volition is the mental formation. These are called "the formations dependent on ignorance". ^2-226

Herein, what is the consciousness dependent on formations? Eye-consciousness, ear-consciousness, nose-consciousness, tongue-consciousness, body-consciousness, mind-consciousness: this is called "the consciousness dependent on formations". ^2-227

Herein, what is the mind-and-matter dependent on consciousness? There is mind, and there is matter. Herein, what is mind? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of formations: this is called "mind". Herein, what is matter? The four great elements, and material form derived from the four great elements: this is called "matter". Thus there is this mind, and this matter. This is called "the mind-and-matter dependent on consciousness". ^2-228

Herein, what are the six sense-fields dependent on mind-and-matter? The eye sense-field, the ear sense-field, the nose sense-field, the tongue sense-field, the body sense-field, the mind sense-field: this is called "the six sense-fields dependent on mind-and-matter". ^2-229

Herein, what is the contact dependent on the six sense-fields? Eye-contact, ear-contact, nose-contact, tongue-contact, body-contact, mind-contact: this is called "the contact dependent on the six sense-fields". ^2-230

Herein, what is the feeling dependent on contact? Feeling born of eye-contact, feeling born of ear-contact, feeling born of nose-contact, feeling born of tongue-contact, feeling born of body-contact, feeling born of mind-contact: this is called "the feeling dependent on contact". ^2-231

Herein, what is the craving dependent on feeling? Craving for sights, craving for sounds, craving for smells, craving for tastes, craving for tangible objects, craving for mental objects: this is called "the craving dependent on feeling". ^2-232

Herein, what is the clinging dependent on craving? Clinging to sense pleasures, clinging to views, clinging to rules and observances, clinging to a doctrine of self: this is called "the clinging dependent on craving". ^2-233

Herein, what is the existence dependent on clinging? Existence is of two kinds: there is existence as action, and there is existence as rebirth. Herein, what is existence as action? Merit-producing formations, demerit-producing formations, imperturbable formations: this is called "existence as action". All action that leads to existence is existence as action.
Herein, what is existence as rebirth? Existence in the sense sphere, existence in the fine-material sphere, existence in the immaterial sphere, existence with perception, existence without perception, existence with neither perception nor non-perception, existence with one aggregate, existence with four aggregates, existence with five aggregates: this is called "existence as rebirth". Thus there is this existence as action, and this existence as rebirth. This is called "the existence dependent on clinging". ^2-234

Herein, what is the birth dependent on existence? Whatever, for the various beings in the various orders of beings, is birth, coming to birth, descent into the womb, production, the appearance of the aggregates, the obtaining of the sense-fields: this is called "the birth dependent on existence". ^2-235

Herein, what is the aging-and-death dependent on birth? There is aging, and there is death. Herein, what is aging? Whatever, for the various beings in the various orders of beings, is aging, decrepitude, broken teeth, grey hair, wrinkled skin, the decline of the lifespan, the wearing out of the faculties: this is called "aging".
Herein, what is death? Whatever, for the various beings from the various orders of beings, is the passing away, the falling away, the breaking up, the disappearance, the dying, the death, the completion of time, the breaking up of the aggregates, the laying down of the body, the cutting off of the life faculty: this is called "death". Thus there is this aging, and this death. This is called "the aging-and-death dependent on birth". ^2-236

Herein, what is sorrow? For one touched by the loss of relatives, or touched by the loss of wealth, or touched by the loss of health, or touched by the loss of virtue, or touched by the loss of right view, or possessed of any other kind of loss, or touched by any other kind of painful state: whatever is sorrow, sorrowing, the state of sorrowing, inner sorrow, inner deep sorrow, the burning grief of the mind, grief, the dart of sorrow: this is called "sorrow". ^2-237

Herein, what is lamentation? For one touched by the loss of relatives, or touched by the loss of wealth, or touched by the loss of health, or touched by the loss of virtue, or touched by the loss of right view, or possessed of any other kind of loss, or touched by any other kind of painful state: whatever is crying out, lamenting, the state of crying out, the state of lamenting, wailing, weeping, lament, the utterance of laments, wailing speech, the state of wailing speech: this is called "lamentation". ^2-238

Herein, what is pain? Whatever bodily discomfort, bodily pain, the unpleasant, painful feeling born of bodily contact, the unpleasant, painful feeling that is felt and is born of bodily contact: this is called "pain". ^2-239

Herein, what is grief? Whatever mental discomfort, mental pain, the unpleasant, painful feeling born of mental contact, the unpleasant, painful feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called "grief". ^2-240

Herein, what is despair? For one touched by the loss of relatives, or touched by the loss of wealth, or touched by the loss of health, or touched by the loss of virtue, or touched by the loss of right view, or possessed of any other kind of loss, or touched by any other kind of painful state: whatever is distress, despair, the state of distress, the state of despair: this is called "despair". ^2-241

That "thus does this entire mass of suffering come to be" means: thus does this entire mass of suffering come together, meet, unite, and appear. That is why it is said, "thus does this entire mass of suffering come to be."

At the time when, as a result of wholesome sense-sphere action having been done and accumulated, a resultant eye-consciousness has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, having a sight as its object — at that time, dependent on the formation, consciousness comes to be. Dependent on consciousness, mentality comes to be. Dependent on mentality, the sixth sense-field comes to be. Dependent on the sixth sense-field, contact comes to be. Dependent on contact, feeling comes to be. Dependent on feeling, existence comes to be. Dependent on existence, birth comes to be. Dependent on birth, aging-and-death comes to be. Herein, what is the formation? Whatever is volition, intention, the state of intending: this is called "the formation".

Herein, what is the mentality dependent on consciousness? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of formations: this is called "the mentality dependent on consciousness".
Herein, what is the sixth sense-field dependent on mentality? Whatever is mind, mentality ... the corresponding eye-consciousness element: this is called "the sixth sense-field dependent on mentality".
Herein, what is the contact dependent on the sixth sense-field? Whatever is contact, touching, the act of touching, the state of touching: this is called "the contact dependent on the sixth sense-field".

Whatever is neither mental comfort nor mental discomfort, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mental contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called "the feeling dependent on contact".
Herein, what is the existence dependent on feeling? Herein, what is the formation dependent on an unwholesome root? Whatever is volition, intention, the state of intending: this is called "the formation dependent on an unwholesome root".
Herein, what is the consciousness dependent on the formation? Whatever is mind, mentality ... the corresponding mind-consciousness element: this is called "the consciousness dependent on the formation".
Herein, what is the mentality dependent on consciousness? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of formations: this is called "the mentality dependent on consciousness".
Herein, what is the sixth sense-field dependent on mentality? Whatever is mind, mentality ... the corresponding mind-consciousness element: this is called "the sixth sense-field dependent on mentality".
Herein, what is the contact dependent on the sixth sense-field? Whatever is contact, touching, the act of touching, the state of touching: this is called "the contact dependent on the sixth sense-field".
Herein, what is the feeling dependent on contact? Whatever is neither mental comfort nor mental discomfort, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mental contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called "the feeling dependent on contact".
Herein, what is the resolve dependent on feeling? Whatever is the mind's resolve, resolving, the state of being resolved: this is called "the resolve dependent on feeling".
Herein, what is the existence dependent on resolve? Apart from resolve, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of formations, the aggregate of consciousness: this is called "the existence dependent on resolve".
Herein, what is the birth dependent on existence? Whatever, of those various states, is birth, coming to birth, production, coming into being, appearance: this is called "the birth dependent on existence".
Herein, what is the aging-and-death dependent on birth? There is aging, and there is death. Herein, what is aging? Whatever, of those various states, is aging, decrepitude, the decline of the lifespan: this is called "aging". Herein, what is death? Whatever, of those various states, is destruction, decay, breaking up, disintegration, impermanence, disappearance: this is called "death". Thus there is this aging, and this death. This is called "the aging-and-death dependent on birth".
That "thus does this entire mass of suffering come to be" means: thus does this entire mass of suffering come together, meet, unite, and appear. That is why it is said, "thus does this entire mass of suffering come to be."
