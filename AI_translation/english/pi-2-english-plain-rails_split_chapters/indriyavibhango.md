Herein, what is the eye faculty? The eye that, derived from the four great elements, is a sensitive surface... the empty village: this is called "the eye faculty".
Herein, what is the ear faculty... the nose faculty... the tongue faculty... the body faculty? The body that, derived from the four great elements, is a sensitive surface... the empty village: this is called "the body faculty".
Herein, what is the mind faculty? As one kind, the mind faculty is: joined with contact. As two kinds, the mind faculty is: it may be rooted, or it may be rootless. As three kinds, the mind faculty is: it may be wholesome, unwholesome, or neutral. As four kinds, the mind faculty is: it may belong to the sense sphere, the fine-material sphere, the immaterial sphere, or be unincluded. As five kinds, the mind faculty is: it may be joined with the faculty of pleasure, joined with the faculty of pain, joined with the faculty of joy, joined with the faculty of displeasure, or joined with the faculty of equanimity. As six kinds, the mind faculty is: eye-consciousness... mind-consciousness. This is how, as six kinds, the mind faculty is described.
As seven kinds, the mind faculty is: eye-consciousness... body-consciousness, the mind element, the mind-consciousness element. This is how, as seven kinds, the mind faculty is described.
As eight kinds, the mind faculty is: eye-consciousness... body-consciousness, which may be accompanied by pleasure or accompanied by pain, the mind element, the mind-consciousness element. This is how, as eight kinds, the mind faculty is described.
As nine kinds, the mind faculty is: eye-consciousness... body-consciousness, the mind element, the mind-consciousness element, each of which may be wholesome, unwholesome, or neutral. This is how, as nine kinds, the mind faculty is described.
As ten kinds, the mind faculty is: eye-consciousness... body-consciousness, which may be accompanied by pleasure or accompanied by pain, the mind element, the mind-consciousness element, each of which may be wholesome, unwholesome, or neutral. This is how, as ten kinds, the mind faculty is described... this is how, in many ways, the mind faculty is described. This is called "the mind faculty".
Herein, what is the faculty of femininity? Whatever, in a woman, is the feminine feature, the feminine mark, the feminine behavior, the feminine manner, the feminine condition, the feminine nature: this is called "the faculty of femininity".
Herein, what is the faculty of masculinity? Whatever, in a man, is the masculine feature, the masculine mark, the masculine behavior, the masculine manner, the masculine condition, the masculine nature: this is called "the faculty of masculinity".
Herein, what is the life faculty? As two kinds, the life faculty is: it may be the material life faculty, or it may be the immaterial life faculty.
Herein, what is the material life faculty? Whatever, of those material states, is the lifespan, the persistence, the continuance, the sustaining, the behaving, the carrying-on, the preserving, the life, the life faculty: this is called "the material life faculty".
Herein, what is the immaterial life faculty? Whatever, of those immaterial states, is the lifespan, the persistence, the continuance, the sustaining, the behaving, the carrying-on, the preserving, the life, the life faculty: this is called "the immaterial life faculty". This is called "the life faculty".
Herein, what is the faculty of pleasure? Whatever bodily comfort, bodily pleasure, the pleasant, happy feeling born of bodily contact, the pleasant, happy feeling that is felt and is born of bodily contact: this is called "the faculty of pleasure".
Herein, what is the faculty of pain? Whatever bodily discomfort, bodily pain, the unpleasant, painful feeling born of bodily contact, the unpleasant, painful feeling that is felt and is born of bodily contact: this is called "the faculty of pain".
Herein, what is the faculty of joy? Whatever mental comfort, mental pleasure, the pleasant, happy feeling born of mental contact, the pleasant, happy feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called "the faculty of joy".
Herein, what is the faculty of displeasure? Whatever mental discomfort, mental pain, the unpleasant, painful feeling born of mental contact, the unpleasant, painful feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called "the faculty of displeasure".
Herein, what is the faculty of equanimity? Whatever is neither mental comfort nor mental discomfort, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling born of mental contact, the neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called "the faculty of equanimity".
Herein, what is the faculty of faith? Whatever faith, believing, trusting, confidence, faith, the faculty of faith, the power of faith: this is called "the faculty of faith".
Herein, what is the faculty of energy? Whatever mental arousing of energy, launching out, exertion, endeavoring, striving, zeal, vigor, steadfastness, persistence without slackening, not giving up the desire to act, not putting down the task, taking up the task, energy, the faculty of energy, the power of energy: this is called "the faculty of energy".
Herein, what is the faculty of mindfulness? Whatever mindfulness, recollection, remembering, memory, keeping in mind, not floating away, not forgetting, mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness: this is called "the faculty of mindfulness".
Herein, what is the faculty of concentration? Whatever steadiness of mind, its stability, steadfastness, non-distraction, non-wavering, an undistracted state of mind, calm, the faculty of concentration, the power of concentration, right concentration: this is called "the faculty of concentration".
Herein, what is the faculty of wisdom? The wisdom, understanding... non-delusion, investigation of states, right view: this is called "the faculty of wisdom".
Herein, what is the faculty of "I shall come to know the unknown"? The wisdom, understanding... non-delusion, investigation of states, right view, the enlightenment factor of investigation of states, a path factor, included in the path, arising for the direct realization of those states not yet known, not yet seen, not yet attained, not yet understood, not yet directly realized: this is called "the faculty of 'I shall come to know the unknown'".
Herein, what is the faculty of full knowledge? The wisdom, understanding... non-delusion, investigation of states, right view, the enlightenment factor of investigation of states, a path factor, included in the path, arising for the direct realization of those states already known, already seen, already attained, already understood, already directly realized: this is called the faculty of full knowledge.
Herein, what is the faculty of one who has fully known? The wisdom, understanding... non-delusion, investigation of states, right view, the enlightenment factor of investigation of states, a path factor, included in the path, arising for one who has fully known, in relation to those states already seen, already attained, already understood, already directly realized: this is called "the faculty of one who has fully known".
