#### Discourse Classification ^2-3-1-0

The six elements: the earth element, the water element, the fire element, the air element, the space element, the consciousness element. ^2-172

Herein, what is the earth element? The earth element is twofold: there is the internal, there is the external. Herein, what is the internal earth element? Whatever, belonging internally and individually, is hard, of a solid nature, hardness, a state of hardness, and clung to internally — namely, head hair, body hair, nails, teeth, skin, flesh, sinews, bones, bone marrow, kidneys, heart, liver, membrane, spleen, lungs, intestines, mesentery, stomach contents, feces, or whatever else there is that, belonging internally and individually, is hard, of a solid nature, hardness, a state of hardness, and clung to internally: this is called 'the internal earth element'.
Herein, what is the external earth element? Whatever, external, is hard, of a solid nature, hardness, a state of hardness, and not clung to — namely, iron, copper, tin, lead, silver, pearl, gem, beryl, conch shell, stone, coral, silver, gold, ruby, cat's-eye, grass, wood, gravel, pebbles, earth, rock, mountain, or whatever else there is that, external, is hard, of a solid nature, hardness, a state of hardness, and not clung to: this is called 'the external earth element'. Both the internal earth element and the external earth element, gathered and grouped together as one: this is called 'the earth element'. ^2-173

Herein, what is the water element? The water element is twofold: there is the internal, there is the external. Herein, what is the internal water element? Whatever, belonging internally and individually, is liquid, of a watery nature, moisture, a moist state, a binding together of material form, and clung to internally — namely, bile, phlegm, pus, blood, sweat, fat, tears, grease, saliva, snot, oil-of-the-joints, urine, or whatever else there is that, belonging internally and individually, is liquid, of a watery nature, moisture, a moist state, a binding together of material form, and clung to internally: this is called 'the internal water element'.
Herein, what is the external water element? Whatever, external, is liquid, of a watery nature, moisture, a moist state, a binding together of material form, and not clung to — namely, the sap of roots, the sap of stems, the sap of bark, the sap of leaves, the sap of flowers, the sap of fruits, milk, curd, ghee, butter, oil, honey, molasses, water in the ground, or water in the sky, or whatever else there is that, external, is liquid, of a watery nature, moisture, a moist state, a binding together of material form, and not clung to: this is called 'the external water element'. Both the internal water element and the external water element, gathered and grouped together as one: this is called 'the water element'. ^2-174

Herein, what is the fire element? The fire element is twofold: there is the internal, there is the external. Herein, what is the internal fire element? Whatever, belonging internally and individually, is fire, of a fiery nature, warmth, a warm state, heat, a heated state, and clung to internally — namely, that by which one is warmed, that by which one ages, that by which one is consumed with fever, and that by which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted is properly digested, or whatever else there is that, belonging internally and individually, is fire, of a fiery nature, warmth, a warm state, heat, a heated state, and clung to internally: this is called 'the internal fire element'.
Herein, what is the external fire element? Whatever, external, is fire, of a fiery nature, warmth, a warm state, heat, a heated state, and not clung to — namely, fire from wood, fire from straw, fire from grass, fire from cow-dung, fire from husks, fire from rubbish, fire from a thunderbolt, the heat of fire, the heat of the sun, the heat of a heap of wood, the heat of a heap of grass, the heat of a heap of grain, the heat of a heap of goods, or whatever else there is that, external, is fire, of a fiery nature, warmth, a warm state, heat, a heated state, and not clung to: this is called 'the external fire element'. Both the internal fire element and the external fire element, gathered and grouped together as one: this is called 'the fire element'. ^2-175

Herein, what is the air element? The air element is twofold: there is the internal, there is the external. Herein, what is the internal air element? Whatever, belonging internally and individually, is air, of an airy nature, a rigidity of material form, and clung to internally — namely, upward-moving winds, downward-moving winds, winds in the belly, winds in the bowels, winds that course through the limbs, winds that feel like knife-cuts, winds that feel like razor-cuts, winds that feel like a piercing, in-breath and out-breath, or whatever else there is that, belonging internally and individually, is air, of an airy nature, a rigidity of material form, and clung to internally: this is called 'the internal air element'.
Herein, what is the external air element? Whatever, external, is air, of an airy nature, a rigidity of material form, and not clung to — namely, easterly winds, westerly winds, northerly winds, southerly winds, dusty winds, dustless winds, cold winds, hot winds, gentle winds, strong winds, storm winds, whirlwinds, winds from wings, winds from a garuda bird, winds from a palm-leaf fan, winds from a hand-fan, or whatever else there is that, external, is air, of an airy nature, a rigidity of material form, and not clung to: this is called 'the external air element'. Both the internal air element and the external air element, gathered and grouped together as one: this is called 'the air element'. ^2-176

Herein, what is the space element? The space element is twofold: there is the internal, there is the external. Herein, what is the internal space element? Whatever, belonging internally and individually, is space, of a spatial nature, an empty space, a state of emptiness, an opening, a state of being an opening, not touched by flesh and blood, and clung to internally — namely, the hole of the ear, the hole of the nose, the door of the mouth, that by which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted is swallowed, that in which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted comes to rest, and that by which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted passes out from below, or whatever else there is that, belonging internally and individually, is space, of a spatial nature, an empty space, a state of emptiness, an opening, a state of being an opening, not touched by flesh and blood, and clung to internally: this is called 'the internal space element'.
Herein, what is the external space element? Whatever, external, is space, of a spatial nature, an empty space, a state of emptiness, an opening, a state of being an opening, not touched by the four great elements, and not clung to: this is called 'the external space element'. Both the internal space element and the external space element, gathered and grouped together as one: this is called 'the space element'. ^2-177

Herein, what is the consciousness element? The eye-consciousness element, the ear-consciousness element, the nose-consciousness element, the tongue-consciousness element, the body-consciousness element, the mind-consciousness element: this is called 'the consciousness element'.
These are the six elements. ^2-178

Another six elements: the pleasure element, the pain element, the gladness element, the sadness element, the equanimity element, the ignorance element. ^2-179

Herein, what is the pleasure element? Whatever is bodily ease, bodily pleasure, the pleasant, pleasurable feeling born of bodily contact, the pleasant, pleasurable feeling that is felt and is born of bodily contact: this is called 'the pleasure element'.
Herein, what is the pain element? Whatever is bodily discomfort, bodily pain, the unpleasant, painful feeling born of bodily contact, the unpleasant, painful feeling that is felt and is born of bodily contact: this is called 'the pain element'.
Herein, what is the gladness element? Whatever is mental ease, mental pleasure, the pleasant, pleasurable feeling born of mental contact, the pleasant, pleasurable feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called 'the gladness element'.
Herein, what is the sadness element? Whatever is mental discomfort, mental pain, the unpleasant, painful feeling born of mental contact, the unpleasant, painful feeling that is felt and is born of mental contact: this is called 'the sadness element'.
Herein, what is the equanimity element? Whatever is mental, neither ease nor discomfort, the neutral feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant, born of mental contact, felt and born of mental contact: this is called 'the equanimity element'.
Herein, what is the ignorance element? Whatever is not-knowing, not-seeing, non-penetration, non-understanding, non-awakening, non-realization, failure to grasp, failure to fully grasp, failure to examine, failure to reflect, an act done without direct seeing, dullness of mind, foolishness, lack of clear comprehension, delusion, great delusion, complete delusion, ignorance, the flood of ignorance, the yoke of ignorance, the underlying tendency of ignorance, the obsession of ignorance, the bolt of ignorance, delusion, the root of the unwholesome: this is called 'the ignorance element'.
These are the six elements. ^2-180

Another six elements: the sense-pleasure element, the element of ill will, the element of cruelty, the element of renunciation, the element of goodwill, the element of harmlessness. ^2-181

Herein, what is the sense-pleasure element? Thinking, thought, intention connected with sense-pleasure, fixing, focusing, the directing of the mind, wrong intention: this is called the sense-pleasure element. Taking the Avīci hell as the lower limit and the Paranimmitavasavatti gods as the upper limit, whatever aggregates, elements, sense-fields, material form, feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness belong there, are included there, within that range: this is called 'the sense-pleasure element'.
Herein, what is the element of ill will? Thinking, thought... wrong intention connected with ill will: this is called 'the element of ill will'. Or, with regard to the ten grounds for resentment, resentment of mind, aversion, irritation, opposition, anger, being angered, being greatly angered, hatred, hostility, great hostility, malice of mind, ill will of mind, wrath, becoming wrathful, the state of being wrathful, hatred, becoming hateful, the state of being hateful, malice, becoming malicious, opposition, hostility, fury, ferocity, and displeasure of mind: this is called 'the element of ill will'.
Herein, what is the element of cruelty? Thinking, thought... wrong intention connected with cruelty: this is called 'the element of cruelty'. Herein, someone harms beings with the hand, with a clod, with a stick, with a weapon, with a rope, or by some other means; whatever such harming, harassing, injuring, cruelty, hurting, offending, and injuring of others there is: this is called 'the element of cruelty'.
Herein, what is the element of renunciation? Thinking, thought... right intention connected with renunciation: this is called 'the element of renunciation'. All wholesome states, too, are 'the element of renunciation'.
Herein, what is the element of goodwill? Thinking, thought... right intention connected with goodwill: this is called 'the element of goodwill'. Whatever loving-kindness there is toward beings, the practice of loving-kindness, the state of practicing loving-kindness, the mind's release through loving-kindness: this is called 'the element of goodwill'.
Herein, what is the element of harmlessness? Thinking, thought, intention, fixing, focusing, the directing of the mind, right intention connected with harmlessness: this is called 'the element of harmlessness'. Whatever compassion there is toward beings, the practice of compassion, the state of practicing compassion, the mind's release through compassion: this is called 'the element of harmlessness'.
These are the six elements.
Thus these three sets of six, gathered and grouped together as one, make eighteen elements.

The eighteen elements: the eye element, the sight element, the eye-consciousness element, the ear element, the sound element, the ear-consciousness element, the nose element, the smell element, the nose-consciousness element, the tongue element, the taste element, the tongue-consciousness element, the body element, the tangible element, the body-consciousness element, the mind element, the mental-object element, the mind-consciousness element. ^2-183

Herein, what is the eye element? The eye that, derived from the four great elements, is a sensitive surface... this is the empty village. This is called 'the eye element'.

Herein, what is the mental-object element? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of formations, whatever material form there is that is invisible and without resistance and included in the mental-object sense-field, and the unconditioned element.

Herein, what is the unconditioned element? The destruction of desire, the destruction of hatred, the destruction of delusion: this is called 'the unconditioned element'. This is called 'the mental-object element'.
Herein, what is the mind-consciousness element? Immediately after the eye-consciousness element has arisen and ceased, the mind element arises; immediately after the mind element has arisen and ceased, there arises consciousness, mind, mental activity... the corresponding mind-consciousness element; immediately after the ear-consciousness element... the nose-consciousness element... the tongue-consciousness element... the body-consciousness element has arisen and ceased, the mind element arises; immediately after the mind element, too, has arisen and ceased, there arises consciousness, mind, mental activity... the corresponding mind-consciousness element; or, in dependence on the mind and mental objects, there arises consciousness, mind, mental activity, heart, the bright, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, the corresponding mind-consciousness element: this is called 'the mind-consciousness element'.
