### The Chapter of Explanations ^1-4-0

#### Explanation of the Sets of Three ^1-4-1-0

What are wholesome states? The wholesome mind of the four planes – these are wholesome states. ^1-1384

What are unwholesome states? The twelve arisings of unwholesome mind – these are unwholesome states. ^1-1385

What are indeterminate states? The results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are indeterminate states. ^1-1386

What are states associated with pleasant feeling? The four arisings of mind accompanied by joy from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, four from the unwholesome mind, five from the results of the wholesome mind of the sense sphere and from the functional mind, the three absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the three world-transcending absorptions in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome and resultant), except the pleasant feeling arisen here – these are states associated with pleasant feeling. ^1-1387

What are states associated with painful feeling? The two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness, body-consciousness accompanied by pain, except the painful feeling arisen here – these are states associated with painful feeling. ^1-1388

What are states associated with feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant? The four arisings of mind accompanied by equanimity from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, six from the unwholesome mind, ten from the results of the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, six from the results of the unwholesome mind, six from the functional mind, the fourth absorption of the fine-material sphere (wholesome, resultant and functional), the four minds of the immaterial sphere (wholesome, resultant and functional), the fourth world-transcending absorption (wholesome and resultant), except the feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant arisen here – these are states associated with feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant. The three feelings, material form and Nibbāna – these are not to be counted among states associated with pleasant feeling, nor among states associated with painful feeling, nor among states associated with feeling that is neither painful nor pleasant. ^1-1389

What are states that are results? The results of the four planes – these are states that are results. ^1-1390

What are states that produce results? The wholesome mind of the four planes, the unwholesome mind – these are states that produce results. ^1-1391

What are states that neither are results nor produce results? The indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states that neither are results nor produce results. ^1-1392

What are states produced by past action and subject to clinging? The results of the three planes and whatever material form is due to past action – these are states produced by past action and subject to clinging. ^1-1393

What are states not produced by past action but subject to clinging? The wholesome mind of the three planes, the unwholesome mind, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes and whatever material form is not due to past action – these are states not produced by past action but subject to clinging. ^1-1394

What are states neither produced by past action nor subject to clinging? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence, the four fruits of the contemplative life and Nibbāna – these are states neither produced by past action nor subject to clinging. ^1-1395

What are states that are defiled and subject to defilement? The twelve arisings of unwholesome mind – these are states that are defiled and subject to defilement. ^1-1396

What are states that are undefiled but subject to defilement? The wholesome mind of the three planes, the results of the three planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes and all material form – these are states that are undefiled but subject to defilement. ^1-1397

What are states that are undefiled and not subject to defilement? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence, the four fruits of the contemplative life and Nibbāna – these are states that are undefiled and not subject to defilement. ^1-1398

What are states with applied thought and sustained thought? The wholesome mind of the sense sphere, the unwholesome mind, eleven arisings of mind from the results of the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, two from the results of the unwholesome mind, eleven from the functional mind, the first absorption of the fine-material sphere (wholesome, resultant and functional), the first world-transcending absorption (wholesome and resultant), except the applied thought and sustained thought arisen here – these are states with applied thought and sustained thought. ^1-1399

What are states without applied thought but with sustained thought only? The second absorption of the fine-material sphere in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the second world-transcending absorption in the fivefold method (wholesome and resultant), except the sustained thought arisen here, and applied thought – these are states without applied thought but with sustained thought only. ^1-1400

What are states without applied thought and without sustained thought? The two sets of five kinds of sense consciousness, the three absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the three in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the four immaterial attainments (wholesome, resultant and functional), the three world-transcending absorptions in the fourfold method and the three in the fivefold method (wholesome and resultant), the sustained thought arisen in the second absorption [variant: dutiyajjhāne (Sī.)] of the fivefold method, material form and Nibbāna – these are states without applied thought and without sustained thought. Sustained thought arisen together with applied thought is not to be counted among states with applied thought and sustained thought, nor among states without applied thought but with sustained thought only, nor among states without applied thought and without sustained thought. ^1-1401

What are states accompanied by rapture? The four arisings of mind accompanied by joy from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, four from the unwholesome mind, five from the results of the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, five from the functional mind, the two absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the three in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the two world-transcending absorptions in the fourfold method and the three in the fivefold method (wholesome and resultant), except the rapture arisen here – these are states accompanied by rapture. ^1-1402

What are states accompanied by happiness? The four arisings of mind accompanied by joy from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, four from the unwholesome mind, six from the results of the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, five from the functional mind, the three absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the three world-transcending absorptions in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome and resultant), except the happiness arisen here – these are states accompanied by happiness. ^1-1403

What are states accompanied by equanimity? The four arisings of mind accompanied by equanimity from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, six from the unwholesome mind, ten from the results of the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, six from the results of the unwholesome mind, six from the functional mind, the fourth absorption of the fine-material sphere (wholesome, resultant and functional), the four immaterial attainments (wholesome, resultant and functional), the fourth world-transcending absorption (wholesome and resultant), except the equanimity arisen here – these are states accompanied by equanimity. Rapture does not belong to states accompanied by rapture; it belongs to states accompanied by happiness; it does not belong to states accompanied by equanimity. Happiness does not belong to states accompanied by happiness; it sometimes belongs to states accompanied by rapture; it does not belong to states accompanied by equanimity; and sometimes it is not to be counted among states accompanied by rapture. The two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness, body-consciousness accompanied by pain, the feeling that is equanimity, material form and Nibbāna – these are not to be counted among states accompanied by rapture, nor among states accompanied by happiness, nor among states accompanied by equanimity. ^1-1404

What are states to be abandoned by seeing? The four arisings of mind associated with wrong view, the arising of mind accompanied by doubt – these are states to be abandoned by seeing. ^1-1405

What are states to be abandoned by development? The arising of mind accompanied by restlessness – these are states to be abandoned by development. The four arisings of mind accompanied by greed and dissociated from wrong view, the two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness – these are sometimes states to be abandoned by seeing, sometimes states to be abandoned by development. ^1-1406

What are states to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development? The wholesome mind of the four planes, the results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development. ^1-1407

What are states whose roots are to be abandoned by seeing? The four arisings of mind associated with wrong view, the arising of mind accompanied by doubt, except the delusion arisen here – these are states whose roots are to be abandoned by seeing. ^1-1408

What are states whose roots are to be abandoned by development? The arising of mind accompanied by restlessness, except the delusion arisen here – these are states whose roots are to be abandoned by development. The four arisings of mind accompanied by greed and dissociated from wrong view, the two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness – these are sometimes states whose roots are to be abandoned by seeing, sometimes states whose roots are to be abandoned by development. ^1-1409

What are states whose roots are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development? The delusion accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the wholesome mind of the four planes, the results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states whose roots are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development. ^1-1410

What are states leading to accumulation? The wholesome mind of the three planes, the unwholesome mind – these are states leading to accumulation. ^1-1411

What are states leading away from accumulation? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence – these are states leading away from accumulation. ^1-1412

What are states leading neither to nor away from accumulation? The results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states leading neither to nor away from accumulation. ^1-1413

What are states of one in training? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence, and the three lower fruits of the contemplative life – these are states of one in training. ^1-1414

What are states of one beyond training? The highest fruit, the fruit of arahantship – these are states of one beyond training. ^1-1415

What are states of one neither in training nor beyond training? The wholesome mind of the three planes, the unwholesome mind, the results of the three planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states of one neither in training nor beyond training. ^1-1416

What are limited states? The wholesome mind of the sense sphere, the unwholesome mind, all the results of the sense sphere, the indeterminate functional mind of the sense sphere and all material form – these are limited states. ^1-1417

What are exalted states? States of the fine-material sphere, states of the immaterial sphere, wholesome and indeterminate – these are exalted states. ^1-1418

What are immeasurable states? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence, the four fruits of the contemplative life and Nibbāna – these are immeasurable states. ^1-1419

What are states with a limited object? All the results of the sense sphere, the functional mind element, the functional mind-consciousness element without roots and accompanied by joy – these are states with a limited object. ^1-1420

What are states with an exalted object? The base of infinite consciousness, the base of neither perception nor non-perception – these are states with an exalted object. ^1-1421

What are states with an immeasurable object? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence and the four fruits of the contemplative life – these are states with an immeasurable object. The four arisings of mind dissociated from knowledge from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, the four arisings of mind dissociated from knowledge from the functional mind, every unwholesome mind – these are sometimes states with a limited object, sometimes states with an exalted object, not states with an immeasurable object, and sometimes not to be counted among states with a limited object, nor among states with an exalted object. The four arisings of mind associated with knowledge from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, the four arisings of mind associated with knowledge from the functional mind, the fourth absorption of the fine-material sphere (wholesome and functional), the functional mind-consciousness element without roots and accompanied by equanimity – these are sometimes states with a limited object, sometimes states with an exalted object, sometimes states with an immeasurable object, and sometimes not to be counted among states with a limited object, nor among states with an exalted object, nor among states with an immeasurable object. The three absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the result of the fourth absorption, the base of infinite space, the base of nothingness – these are not to be counted among states with a limited object, nor among states with an exalted object, nor among states with an immeasurable object. Material form and Nibbāna are states without an object. ^1-1422

What are inferior states? The twelve arisings of unwholesome mind – these are inferior states. ^1-1423

What are middling states? The wholesome mind of the three planes, the results of the three planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes and all material form – these are middling states. ^1-1424

What are superior states? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence, the four fruits of the contemplative life and Nibbāna – these are superior states. ^1-1425

What are states fixed in wrongness? The four arisings of mind associated with wrong view, the two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness – these are sometimes states fixed in wrongness, sometimes states that are not fixed. ^1-1426

What are states fixed in rightness? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence – these are states fixed in rightness. ^1-1427

What are states that are not fixed? The four arisings of mind accompanied by greed and dissociated from wrong view, the arising of mind accompanied by doubt, the arising of mind accompanied by restlessness, the wholesome mind of the three planes, the results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states that are not fixed. ^1-1428

What are states with the path as their object? The four arisings of mind associated with knowledge from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, the four arisings of mind associated with knowledge from the functional mind – these are sometimes states with the path as their object, not states with the path as their root; sometimes states with the path as their dominant factor, and sometimes not to be counted among states with the path as their object, nor among states with the path as their dominant factor. The four noble paths are not states with the path as their object, but states with the path as their root; sometimes states with the path as their dominant factor, and sometimes not to be counted among states with the path as their dominant factor. The fourth absorption of the fine-material sphere (wholesome and functional), the functional mind-consciousness element without roots and accompanied by equanimity – these are sometimes states with the path as their object; not states with the path as their root, not states with the path as their dominant factor; and sometimes not to be counted among states with the path as their object. The four arisings of mind dissociated from knowledge from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, every unwholesome mind, all the results of the sense sphere, six arisings of mind from the functional mind, the three absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the result of the fourth absorption, the four immaterial attainments (wholesome, resultant and functional), and the four fruits of the contemplative life – these are not to be counted among states with the path as their object, nor among states with the path as their root, nor among states with the path as their dominant factor. Material form and Nibbāna are states without an object. ^1-1429

What are arisen states? The results of the four planes and whatever material form is due to past action – these are sometimes arisen states, sometimes states that are bound to arise; they are not to be counted among states that have not arisen. The wholesome mind of the four planes, the unwholesome mind, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes and whatever material form is not due to past action – these are sometimes arisen states, sometimes states that have not arisen; they are not to be counted among states that are bound to arise. Nibbāna is not to be counted among arisen states, nor among states that have not arisen, nor among states that are bound to arise. ^1-1430

Except Nibbāna, all states are sometimes past states, sometimes future states, sometimes present states. Nibbāna is not to be counted among past states, nor among future states, nor among present states. ^1-1431

What are states with a past object? The base of infinite consciousness, the base of neither perception nor non-perception – these are states with a past object. ^1-1432

There are no states that are invariably states with a future object. ^1-1433

What are states with a present object? The two sets of five kinds of sense consciousness and the three mind elements – these are states with a present object. Ten arisings of mind from the results of the wholesome mind of the sense sphere, the mind-consciousness element accompanied by equanimity from the results of the unwholesome mind, the functional mind-consciousness element without roots and accompanied by joy – these are sometimes states with a past object, sometimes states with a future object, sometimes states with a present object. The wholesome mind of the sense sphere, the unwholesome mind, nine arisings of mind from the functional mind, the fourth absorption of the fine-material sphere (wholesome and functional) – these are sometimes states with a past object, sometimes states with a future object, sometimes states with a present object; and sometimes not to be counted among states with a past object, nor among states with a future object, nor among states with a present object. The three absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the result of the fourth absorption, the base of infinite space, the base of nothingness, the four paths not included in the spheres of existence and the four fruits of the contemplative life – these are not to be counted among states with a past object, nor among states with a future object, nor among states with a present object. Material form and Nibbāna are states without an object. ^1-1434

Except material form not bound up with the faculties and Nibbāna, all states are sometimes one's own states, sometimes others' states, sometimes states both one's own and others'. Material form not bound up with the faculties and Nibbāna are others' states. ^1-1435

What are states with one's own states as object? The base of infinite consciousness, the base of neither perception nor non-perception – these are states with one's own states as object. ^1-1436

What are states with others' states as object? The three absorptions of the fine-material sphere in the fourfold method and the four in the fivefold method (wholesome, resultant and functional), the result of the fourth absorption, the base of infinite space, the four paths not included in the spheres of existence and the four fruits of the contemplative life – these are states with others' states as object. Except material form, all the wholesome, unwholesome and indeterminate states of the sense sphere, the fourth absorption of the fine-material sphere (wholesome and functional) – these are sometimes states with one's own states as object, sometimes states with others' states as object, sometimes states with both one's own and others' states as object. The base of nothingness is not to be counted among states with one's own states as object, nor among states with others' states as object, nor among states with both one's own and others' states as object. Material form and Nibbāna are states without an object. ^1-1437

What are states that are visible and make impact? The visible-form base – these are states that are visible and make impact. ^1-1438

What are states that are invisible but make impact? The eye base… the touch base – these are states that are invisible but make impact. ^1-1439

What are states that are invisible and make no impact? #### Explanation of the Pairs ^1-4-2-0

##### The Group on Roots ^1-4-2-1-0

What are states that are roots? The three wholesome roots, the three unwholesome roots, the three indeterminate roots. Non-greed as a wholesome root and non-hate as a wholesome root arise in the wholesome minds of the four planes. Non-delusion as a wholesome root arises in the wholesome minds of the four planes, except the four arisings of mind dissociated from knowledge from the wholesome mind of the sense sphere.
Greed arises in the eight arisings of mind accompanied by greed. Hate arises in the two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness. Delusion arises in all unwholesome minds.
Non-greed as a resultant root and non-hate as a resultant root arise in the results of the four planes, except the arisings of mind without roots from the results of the sense sphere. Non-delusion as a resultant root arises in the results of the four planes, except the arisings of mind without roots from the results of the sense sphere, and except the four arisings of mind dissociated from knowledge.
Non-greed as a functional root and non-hate as a functional root arise in the functional minds of the three planes, except the arisings of mind without roots from the functional mind of the sense sphere. Non-delusion as a functional root arises in the functional minds of the three planes, except the arisings of mind without roots from the functional mind of the sense sphere, and except the four arisings of mind dissociated from knowledge – these are states that are roots. ^1-1441

What are states that are not roots? Except the roots, the wholesome mind of the four planes, the unwholesome mind, the results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states that are not roots. ^1-1442

What are states with roots? Except the delusion accompanied by doubt and the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the remaining unwholesome mind, the wholesome mind of the four planes, the results of the four planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the results of the sense sphere, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the functional mind of the sense sphere – these are states with roots. ^1-1443

What are states without roots? The delusion accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the two sets of five kinds of sense consciousness, the three mind elements, the five mind-consciousness elements without roots, material form and Nibbāna – these are states without roots. ^1-1444

What are states associated with roots? Except the delusion accompanied by doubt and the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the remaining unwholesome mind, the wholesome mind of the four planes, the results of the four planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the results of the sense sphere, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the functional mind of the sense sphere – these are states associated with roots. ^1-1445

What are states dissociated from roots? The delusion accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the two sets of five kinds of sense consciousness, the three mind elements, the five mind-consciousness elements without roots, material form and Nibbāna – these are states dissociated from roots. ^1-1446

What are states that are both roots and have roots? Where two or three roots arise together – these are states that are both roots and have roots. ^1-1447

What are states that have roots but are not roots? The wholesome mind of the four planes, the unwholesome mind, the results of the four planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the results of the sense sphere, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the functional mind of the sense sphere, except the roots arisen here – these are states that have roots but are not roots. States without roots are not to be counted – among states that are both roots and have roots, nor among states that have roots but are not roots. ^1-1448

What are states that are both roots and associated with roots? Where two or three roots arise together – these are states that are both roots and associated with roots. ^1-1449

What are states that are associated with roots but are not roots? The wholesome mind of the four planes, the unwholesome mind, the results of the four planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the results of the sense sphere, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the functional mind of the sense sphere, except the roots arisen here – these are states that are associated with roots but are not roots. States dissociated from roots are not to be counted – among states that are both roots and associated with roots, nor among states that are associated with roots but are not roots. ^1-1450

What are states that are not roots but have roots? The wholesome mind of the four planes, the unwholesome mind, the results of the four planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the results of the sense sphere, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes except the arisings of mind without roots from the functional mind of the sense sphere, except the roots arisen here – these are states that are not roots but have roots. ^1-1451

What are states that are not roots and are without roots? The two sets of five kinds of sense consciousness, the three mind elements, the five mind-consciousness elements without roots, material form and Nibbāna – these are states that are not roots and are without roots. States that are roots are not to be counted – among states that are not roots but have roots, nor among states that are not roots and are without roots. ^1-1452

##### The Group on Taints ^1-4-2-3-0

What are states that are taints? The four taints – the taint of sensual desire, the taint of existence, the taint of views, the taint of ignorance. The taint of sensual desire arises in the eight arisings of mind accompanied by greed, the taint of existence arises in the four arisings of mind accompanied by greed and dissociated from wrong view, the taint of views arises in the four arisings of mind associated with wrong view. The taint of ignorance arises in all unwholesome minds – these are states that are taints. ^1-1465

What are states that are not taints? Except the taints, the remaining unwholesome mind, the wholesome mind of the four planes, the results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states that are not taints. ^1-1466

What are states subject to taints? The wholesome mind of the three planes, the unwholesome mind, the results of the three planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes and all material form – these are states subject to taints. ^1-1467

What are states free of taints? The four paths not included in the spheres of existence, the four fruits of the contemplative life and Nibbāna – these are states free of taints. ^1-1468

What are states associated with taints? The two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness, except the delusion arisen here; except the delusion accompanied by doubt and the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the remaining unwholesome mind – these are states associated with taints. ^1-1469

What are states dissociated from taints? The delusion arisen in the two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness, the delusion accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the wholesome mind of the four planes, the results of the four planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes, material form and Nibbāna – these are states dissociated from taints. ^1-1470

What are states that are both taints and subject to taints? Those same taints are states that are both taints and subject to taints. ^1-1471

What are states that are subject to taints but are not taints? Except the taints, the remaining unwholesome mind, the wholesome mind of the three planes, the results of the three planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes and all material form – these are states that are subject to taints but are not taints. States free of taints are not to be counted – among states that are both taints and subject to taints, nor among states that are subject to taints but are not taints. ^1-1472

What are states that are both taints and associated with taints? Where two or three taints arise together – these are states that are both taints and associated with taints. ^1-1473

What are states that are associated with taints but are not taints? Except the taints, the remaining unwholesome mind – these are states that are associated with taints but are not taints. States dissociated from taints are not to be counted – among states that are both taints and associated with taints, nor among states that are associated with taints but are not taints. ^1-1474

What are states dissociated from taints but subject to taints? The delusion arisen in the two arisings of mind accompanied by sadness, the delusion accompanied by doubt, the delusion accompanied by restlessness, the wholesome mind of the three planes, the results of the three planes, the indeterminate functional mind of the three planes and all material form – these are states dissociated from taints but subject to taints. ^1-1475
