#### Summary of the Discourse Pairs ^1-3-3-0

What are states that share in true knowledge? The states associated with true knowledge – these are states that share in true knowledge. ^1-1303

What are states that share in ignorance? The states associated with ignorance – these are states that share in ignorance. ^1-1304

What are states like lightning? Wisdom in the three lower noble paths – these are states like lightning. ^1-1305

What are states like a diamond? Wisdom in the highest path, the path of arahantship – these are states like a diamond. ^1-1306

What are foolish states? Shamelessness and fearlessness of wrongdoing – these are foolish states. All unwholesome states are foolish. ^1-1307

What are wise states? Moral shame and fear of wrongdoing – these are wise states. All wholesome states are wise. ^1-1308

What are dark states? Shamelessness and fearlessness of wrongdoing – these are dark states. All unwholesome states are dark. ^1-1309

What are bright states? Moral shame and fear of wrongdoing – these are bright states? All wholesome states are bright. ^1-1310

What are states that cause torment? Bodily misconduct, verbal misconduct, mental misconduct – these are states that cause torment. All unwholesome states cause torment. ^1-1311

What are states that do not cause torment? Good bodily conduct, good verbal conduct, good mental conduct – these are states that do not cause torment. All wholesome states are states that do not cause torment. ^1-1312

What are states that are names? Whatever reckoning, label, designation, usage, name, naming, appellation, expression, term or utterance there is for each of those states – these are states that are names. All states are pathways for names. ^1-1313

What are states that are expressions? Whatever reckoning, label, designation, usage, name, naming, appellation, expression, term or utterance there is for each of those states – these are states that are expressions. All states are pathways for expressions. ^1-1314

What are states that are designations? Whatever reckoning, label, designation, usage, name, naming, appellation, expression, term or utterance there is for each of those states – these are states that are designations. All states are pathways for designations. ^1-1315

Therein, what is mentality? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness, and the unconditioned element – this is called mentality. ^1-1316

Therein, what is material form? The four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements – this is called material form. ^1-1317

Therein, what is ignorance? Whatever lack of knowledge there is, lack of vision… the bar of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root – this is called ignorance. ^1-1318

Therein, what is craving for continued existence? Whatever, with regard to states of existence, is desire for existence… holding on to existence – this is called craving for continued existence. ^1-1319

Therein, what is the view of continued existence? "The self and the world will exist", whatever view of this kind there is, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the view of continued existence. ^1-1320

Therein, what is the view of non-existence? "The self and the world will not exist", whatever view of this kind there is, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the view of non-existence. ^1-1321

Therein, what is the eternalist view? "The self and the world are eternal", whatever view of this kind there is, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the eternalist view. ^1-1322

Therein, what is the annihilationist view? "The self and the world will be annihilated", whatever view of this kind there is, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the annihilationist view. ^1-1323

Therein, what is the view that there is an end? "The self and the world have an end", whatever view of this kind there is, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the view that there is an end. ^1-1324

Therein, what is the view that there is no end? "The self and the world have no end", whatever view of this kind there is, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the view that there is no end. ^1-1325

Therein, what is the view concerning the past? Whatever view arises concerning the past, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the view concerning the past. ^1-1326

Therein, what is the view concerning the future? Whatever view arises concerning the future, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called the view concerning the future. ^1-1327

Therein, what is shamelessness? Not being ashamed of what one should be ashamed of, not being ashamed of engaging in bad, unwholesome states – this is called shamelessness. ^1-1328

Therein, what is fearlessness of wrongdoing? Not fearing what one should fear, not fearing engaging in bad, unwholesome states – this is called fearlessness of wrongdoing. ^1-1329

Therein, what is moral shame? Being ashamed of what one should be ashamed of, being ashamed of engaging in bad, unwholesome states – this is called moral shame. ^1-1330

Therein, what is fear of wrongdoing? Fearing what one should fear, fearing engaging in bad, unwholesome states – this is called fear of wrongdoing. ^1-1331

Therein, what is being hard to correct? When one is spoken to in accordance with the Teaching, intractability, unruliness, being hard to correct, taking things the opposite way, delight in opposing, disregard, disregarding, disrespect, lack of deference – this is called being hard to correct. ^1-1332

Therein, what is having bad friends? Those persons who are without faith, immoral, of little learning, stingy and unwise: whatever associating with them there is, frequenting them, keeping company with them, resorting to them, closely resorting to them, devotion to them, close devotion to them, leaning towards them – this is called having bad friends. ^1-1333

Therein, what is being easy to correct? When one is spoken to in accordance with the Teaching, tractability, compliance, being easy to correct, not taking things the opposite way, not delighting in opposing, respect, regard, regarding, deference – this is called being easy to correct. ^1-1334

Therein, what is having good friends? Those persons who have faith, are moral, of much learning, generous and wise: whatever associating with them there is, frequenting them, keeping company with them, resorting to them, closely resorting to them, devotion to them, close devotion to them, leaning towards them – this is called having good friends. ^1-1335

Therein, what is skill regarding offences? The five classes of offences are offences, and the seven classes of offences are offences. Whatever skill regarding offences there is with respect to those offences, wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill regarding offences. ^1-1336

Therein, what is skill in emerging from offences? Whatever skill in emerging from those offences there is, wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in emerging from offences. ^1-1337

Therein, what is skill in meditative attainments? There is meditative attainment with applied thought and sustained thought, there is meditative attainment without applied thought but with sustained thought only, there is meditative attainment without applied thought and without sustained thought. Whatever skill in meditative attainments there is with respect to those attainments, wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in meditative attainments. ^1-1338

Therein, what is skill in emerging from meditative attainments? Whatever skill in emerging from those attainments there is, wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in emerging from meditative attainments. ^1-1339

Therein, what is skill in the elements? The eighteen elements: the eye element, the visible-form element, the eye-consciousness element, the ear element, the sound element, the ear-consciousness element, the nose element, the smell element, the nose-consciousness element, the tongue element, the taste element, the tongue-consciousness element, the body element, the touch element, the body-consciousness element, the mind element, the element of mental objects, the mind-consciousness element. Whatever skill in the elements there is with respect to those elements, wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in the elements. ^1-1340

Therein, what is skill in attention? Whatever skill in attention there is with respect to those elements, wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in attention. ^1-1341

Therein, what is skill in the sense bases? The twelve sense bases – the eye base, the visible-form base, the ear base, the sound base, the nose base, the smell base, the tongue base, the taste base, the body base, the touch base, the mind base, the mental-object base. Whatever skill in the sense bases there is with respect to those sense bases, wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in the sense bases. ^1-1342

Therein, what is skill in dependent origination? With ignorance as condition there are mental formations, with mental formations as condition there is consciousness, with consciousness as condition there are mentality and material form, with mentality and material form as condition there are the six sense bases, with the six sense bases as condition there is contact, with contact as condition there is feeling, with feeling as condition there is craving, with craving as condition there is clinging, with clinging as condition there is existence, with existence as condition there is birth, with birth as condition ageing and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness and despair come to be; thus there is the arising of this whole mass of suffering. Whatever wisdom there is in this, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in dependent origination. ^1-1343

Therein, what is skill in what is possible? Whatever states are roots and conditions of whatever states for their arising [variant: upādāya (Sī., Ka.)], that in each case is what is possible; whatever wisdom there is in this, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in what is possible. ^1-1344

Therein, what is skill in what is impossible? Whatever states are not roots and not conditions of whatever states for their arising, that in each case is what is impossible; whatever wisdom there is in this, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called skill in what is impossible. ^1-1345

Therein, what is straightforwardness? Whatever is straightforwardness, freedom from deviousness, freedom from crookedness, freedom from twistedness – this is called straightforwardness. ^1-1346

Therein, what is mildness? Whatever is pliancy, softness, freedom from hardness, freedom from rigidity, humility of mind – this is called mildness. ^1-1347

Therein, what is patience? Whatever is patience, forbearance, endurance, non-ferocity, non-abusiveness, contentment of mind – this is called patience. ^1-1348

Therein, what is gentleness? Whatever is non-transgression by body, non-transgression by speech, non-transgression by body and speech – this is called gentleness. All restraint by morality is gentleness. ^1-1349

Therein, what is courtesy? Having given up the kind of speech that is rough, harsh, hurtful to others, offensive to others, bordering on anger and not conducive to concentration, one speaks the kind of speech that is blameless, pleasing to the ear, lovable, going to the heart, polite, liked by many and pleasing to many; whatever smooth speech there is in this, courteous speech, non-harsh speech – this is called courtesy. ^1-1350

Therein, what is hospitality? There are two kinds of hospitality – hospitality with material things and hospitality with the Teaching. Here someone is hospitable, through hospitality with material things or through hospitality with the Teaching – this is called hospitality. ^1-1351

Therein, what is not guarding the doors of the senses? Here someone, on seeing a visible form with the eye, grasps at its general features and grasps at its details. Since, if he leaves the faculty of the eye unrestrained, bad, unwholesome states of covetousness and sadness might flow in upon him, he does not practise its restraint, he does not guard the faculty of the eye, he does not undertake the restraint of the faculty of the eye. On hearing a sound with the ear… on smelling a smell with the nose… on tasting a taste with the tongue… on touching a touch with the body… on cognising a mental object with the mind, he grasps at its general features and grasps at its details. Since, if he leaves the faculty of mind unrestrained, bad, unwholesome states of covetousness and sadness might flow in upon him, he does not practise its restraint, he does not guard the faculty of mind, he does not undertake the restraint of the faculty of mind. Whatever non-guarding, non-protection, non-watching, non-restraint there is of these six faculties – this is called not guarding the doors of the senses. ^1-1352

Therein, what is not knowing moderation in eating? Here someone, without reflecting and unwisely, takes food for amusement, for intoxication, for adornment, for beautification. Whatever there is in this of not being content, not knowing moderation and lack of reflection in eating – this is called not knowing moderation in eating. ^1-1353

Therein, what is guarding the doors of the senses? Here someone, on seeing a visible form with the eye, does not grasp at its general features and does not grasp at its details. Since, if he leaves the faculty of the eye unrestrained, bad, unwholesome states of covetousness and sadness might flow in upon him, he practises its restraint, he guards the faculty of the eye, he undertakes the restraint of the faculty of the eye. On hearing a sound with the ear… on smelling a smell with the nose… on tasting a taste with the tongue… on touching a touch with the body… on cognising a mental object with the mind, he does not grasp at its general features and does not grasp at its details. Since, if he leaves the faculty of mind unrestrained, bad, unwholesome states of covetousness and sadness might flow in upon him, he practises its restraint, he guards the faculty of mind, he undertakes the restraint of the faculty of mind. Whatever guarding, protection, watching, restraint there is of these six faculties – this is called guarding the doors of the senses. ^1-1354

Therein, what is knowing moderation in eating? Here someone, reflecting wisely, takes food – not for amusement, not for intoxication, not for adornment, not for beautification, but only for the support and maintenance of this body, for ending discomfort, for assisting the holy life, thinking, "Thus I shall put an end to old feeling and not give rise to new feeling, and I shall have continued life, blamelessness and living at ease." Whatever there is in this of being content, knowing moderation and reflection in eating – this is called knowing moderation in eating. ^1-1355

Therein, what is forgetfulness? Whatever is lack of mindfulness, lack of recollection, lack of recall, lack of mindfulness, not remembering, not keeping in mind, floating away, forgetting – this is called forgetfulness. ^1-1356

Therein, what is lack of clear awareness? Whatever lack of knowledge there is, lack of vision… the bar of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root – this is called lack of clear awareness. ^1-1357

Therein, what is mindfulness? Whatever is mindfulness, recollection, recall, mindfulness, remembering, keeping in mind, not floating away, not forgetting, mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness – this is called mindfulness. ^1-1358

Therein, what is clear awareness? Whatever is wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called clear awareness. ^1-1359

Therein, what is the power of reflection? Whatever is wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called the power of reflection. ^1-1360

Therein, what is the power of development? Whatever is practice, development and repeated practice of wholesome states – this is called the power of development. The seven factors of awakening too are the power of development. ^1-1361

Therein, what is calm? Whatever is the stability of mind… right concentration – this is called calm. ^1-1362

Therein, what is insight? Whatever is wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called insight. ^1-1363

Therein, what is the sign of calm? Whatever is the stability of mind… right concentration – this is called the sign of calm. ^1-1364

Therein, what is the sign of exertion? Whatever is the mental arousing of energy… right effort – this is called the sign of exertion. ^1-1365

Therein, what is exertion? Whatever is the mental arousing of energy… right effort – this is called exertion. ^1-1366

Therein, what is non-distraction? Whatever is the stability of mind… right concentration – this is called non-distraction. ^1-1367

Therein, what is failure in morality? Whatever is transgression by body, transgression by speech, transgression by body and speech – this is called failure in morality. All immorality is failure in morality. ^1-1368

Therein, what is failure in view? "There is nothing given, there is nothing offered, there is nothing sacrificed, there is no fruit or result of good and bad actions, this world does not exist, the other world does not exist, there is no mother, there is no father, there are no beings spontaneously reborn, there are in the world no ascetics and brahmins who have gone rightly and practise rightly and who, having realised this world and the other world by their own direct knowledge, make them known"; whatever view of this kind there is, holding views… a distorted grasp – this is called failure in view. All wrong view is failure in view. ^1-1369

Therein, what is accomplishment in morality? Whatever is non-transgression by body, non-transgression by speech, non-transgression by body and speech – this is called accomplishment in morality. All restraint by morality is accomplishment in morality. ^1-1370

Therein, what is accomplishment in view? "There is what is given, there is what is offered, there is what is sacrificed, there is fruit and result of good and bad actions, there is this world, there is the other world, there is mother, there is father, there are beings spontaneously reborn, there are in the world ascetics and brahmins who have gone rightly and practise rightly and who, having realised this world and the other world by their own direct knowledge, make them known"; whatever wisdom of this kind there is, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view – this is called accomplishment in view. All right view is accomplishment in view. ^1-1371

Therein, what is purification of morality? Whatever is non-transgression by body, non-transgression by speech, non-transgression by body and speech – this is called purification of morality. All restraint by morality is purification of morality. ^1-1372

Therein, what is purification of view? Knowledge of the ownership of action, knowledge in conformity with the truths, the knowledge of one who possesses the path, the knowledge of one who possesses the fruit. ^1-1373

"Purification of view" – whatever is wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of states, right view. ^1-1374

"And effort in keeping with one's view" – whatever is the mental arousing of energy… right effort. ^1-1375

"A sense of urgency" – fear of birth, fear of ageing, fear of sickness, fear of death. "The things that should stir it" [variant: saṃvejanīyaṃ (Sī.)] – birth, ageing, sickness, death. ^1-1376

"And the wise effort of one so stirred" – here a bhikkhu, for the non-arising of bad, unwholesome states that have not yet arisen, generates desire, makes an effort, arouses energy, exerts the mind and strives; for the abandoning of bad, unwholesome states that have arisen, he generates desire, makes an effort, arouses energy, exerts the mind and strives; for the arising of wholesome states that have not yet arisen, he generates desire, makes an effort, arouses energy, exerts the mind and strives; for the continuance, non-decline, increase, abundance, development and fulfilment of wholesome states that have arisen, he generates desire, makes an effort, arouses energy, exerts the mind and strives. ^1-1377

"Not being content with wholesome states" – whatever wish for more there is in one who is not content in the development of wholesome states. ^1-1378

"Not holding back in effort" – whatever there is, in the development of wholesome states, of acting carefully, acting persistently, acting without stopping, not being sluggish, not putting down desire, not putting down the burden, practice, development and repeated practice. ^1-1379

"True knowledge" – the three kinds of true knowledge – knowledge of the recollection of past lives is true knowledge, knowledge of the passing away and reappearance of beings is true knowledge, knowledge of the destruction of the taints is true knowledge. ^1-1380

"Liberation" – the two kinds of liberation – the release of the mind [variant: cittassa ca (Sī., Syā.)], and Nibbāna. ^1-1381

"Knowledge of destruction" – the knowledge of one who possesses the path. ^1-1382

"Knowledge of non-arising" – the knowledge of one who possesses the fruit.
The chapter of summaries is complete. ^1-1383
