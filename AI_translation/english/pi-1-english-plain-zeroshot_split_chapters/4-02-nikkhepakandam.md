Therein, what is the hindrance of sensual desire? Whatever, with regard to sensual pleasures, is sensual desire, sensual lust, sensual delight, sensual craving, sensual affection, sensual fever, sensual infatuation, holding on to sensual pleasures – this is called the hindrance of sensual desire. ^1-1159

Therein, what is the hindrance of ill will? Annoyance arises at the thought "He has done me harm", annoyance arises at the thought "He is doing me harm", annoyance arises at the thought "He will do me harm", annoyance arises at the thought "He has done harm to someone dear and pleasing to me… he is doing harm… he will do harm", annoyance arises at the thought "He has done good to someone not dear and not pleasing to me… he is doing good… he will do good", or annoyance arises without reason. Whatever annoyance of mind of this kind there is, resentment, aversion, antagonism, irritation, exasperation, fury, hate, hatred, intense hatred, malice of mind, mental hatred, anger, being angry, the state of being angry, hate, hating, the state of hating, malice, being malicious, the state of being malicious, hostility, antagonism, fierceness, abusiveness, discontent of mind – this is called the hindrance of ill will. ^1-1160

Therein, what is the hindrance of sloth and torpor? There is sloth, and there is torpor. ^1-1161

Therein, what is sloth? Whatever is indisposition of mind, unworkability, drooping, sagging, sluggishness, being sluggish, the state of being sluggish, sloth, being slothful, the slothful state of mind – this is called sloth. ^1-1162

Therein, what is torpor? Whatever is indisposition of the mental body, unworkability, enveloping, full enveloping, inner confinement, torpor, sleep, drowsiness, sleep, sleeping, the state of being asleep – this is called torpor. Therein, what is the hindrance of restlessness and remorse? There is restlessness, and there is remorse. ^1-1164

Therein, what is restlessness? Whatever is restlessness of mind, disquiet, distraction of the mind, agitation of mind – this is called restlessness. ^1-1165

Therein, what is remorse? Perceiving what is not allowable as allowable, perceiving what is allowable as not allowable, perceiving what is blameless as blameworthy, perceiving what is blameworthy as blameless. Whatever remorse of this kind there is, being remorseful, the state of being remorseful, regret of mind, mental scratching – this is called remorse. So this restlessness and this remorse – this is called the hindrance of restlessness and remorse. ^1-1166

Therein, what is the hindrance of doubt?
