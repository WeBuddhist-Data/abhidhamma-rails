For those in the Pure Abodes while the second consciousness is occurring, and for non-percipient beings, wholesome phenomena have not arisen there, but indeterminate phenomena have ceased there. For those being reborn in the Pure Abodes, wholesome phenomena have not arisen there, and indeterminate phenomena have not ceased there either.
(b) And for whomever and wherever indeterminate phenomena have not ceased, have wholesome phenomena not arisen for them there? Yes. ^6-9-185

For those being reborn in the Pure Abodes, unwholesome phenomena have not arisen there, and indeterminate phenomena have not ceased there either.
