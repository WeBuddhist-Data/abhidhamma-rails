#### The Section on Terms ^6-6-1-0

##### (a) Outline ^6-6-1-1-0

There are three formations – the bodily formation, the verbal formation, the mental formation. In-and-out breathing is the bodily formation; thought and holding the thought are the verbal formation; perception and feeling are the mental formation. Apart from thought and holding the thought, all phenomena associated with consciousness are the mental formation. ^6-6-1
