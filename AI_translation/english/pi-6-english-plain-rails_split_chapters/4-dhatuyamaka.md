#### The Section on Clarifying the Terms ^6-4-6-0

##### (a) Positive ^6-4-6-1-0

(a) Is the eye the eye element?
The divine eye and the eye of wisdom are eyes, but not the eye element. The eye element is both the eye and the eye element.
(b) Is the eye element the eye? Yes.
(a) Is the ear the ear element?
The divine ear and the stream of craving are "ear" (sota), but not the ear element. The ear element is both the ear and the ear element.
(b) Is the ear element the ear? Yes.
(a) Is the nose the nose element? Yes.
(b) Is the nose element the nose? Yes. (The tongue too is like the nose element.)
(a) Is the body the body element?
Apart from the body element, any other body [variant: avaseso kāyo kāyo (Syā.)] is a body, but not the body element. The body element is both the body and the body element.
(b) Is the body element the body? Yes.
(a) Is material form the visible-form element?
Apart from the visible-form element, any other material form is material form, but not the visible-form element. The visible-form element is both material form and the visible-form element.
(b) Is the visible-form element material form? Yes. (Sound is like the nose.)
(a) Is scent the scent element?
The scent of virtue, the scent of concentration and the scent of wisdom are scents, but not the scent element. The scent element is both scent and the scent element.
(b) Is the scent element scent? Yes.
(a) Is taste the taste element?
The taste of meaning, the taste of the Dhamma and the taste of liberation are tastes, but not the taste element. The taste element is both taste and the taste element.
(b) Is the taste element taste? Yes. (The tangible is like the nose.)
(a) Is eye-consciousness the eye-consciousness element? Yes.
(b) Is the eye-consciousness element eye-consciousness? Yes.
Ear-consciousness… nose-consciousness… tongue-consciousness… body-consciousness…
(a) Is the mind the mind-element?
Apart from the mind-element, any other mind is a mind, but not the mind-element. The mind-element is both the mind and the mind-element.
(b) Is the mind-element the mind? Yes.
(a) Is mind-consciousness the mind-consciousness element? Yes.
(b) Is the mind-consciousness element mind-consciousness? Yes.
(a) Is a phenomenon the mind-object element?
Apart from the mind-object element, any other phenomenon is a phenomenon, but not the mind-object element. The mind-object element is both a phenomenon and the mind-object element.
(b) Is the mind-object element a phenomenon? Yes. ^6-4-10

#### The Section on Occurrence: 1. Arising ^6-4-10-0

#### (1) Present ^6-4-11-0

##### (a) Positive: Person ^6-4-11-1-0

(a) For whomever the eye element arises, does the ear element arise for them?
For those being reborn with eyes but without ears, the eye element arises, but the ear element does not arise. For those being reborn with eyes and ears, the eye element arises, and the ear element arises too.
(b) And for whomever the ear element arises, does the eye element arise for them?
For those being reborn with ears but without eyes, the ear element arises, but the eye element does not arise. For those being reborn with ears and eyes, the ear element arises, and the eye element arises too.
(a) For whomever the eye element arises, does the nose element arise for them?
For those being reborn with eyes but without a nose, the eye element arises, but the nose element does not arise. For those being reborn with eyes and a nose, the eye element arises, and the nose element arises too.
(b) And for whomever the nose element arises, does the eye element arise for them?
For those being reborn with a nose but without eyes, the nose element arises, but the eye element does not arise. For those being reborn with a nose and eyes, the nose element arises, and the eye element arises too.
(a) For whomever the eye element arises, does the visible-form element arise for them? Yes.
(b) And for whomever the visible-form element arises, does the eye element arise for them?
For those being reborn with material form but without eyes, the visible-form element arises, but the eye element does not arise. For those being reborn with eyes, the visible-form element arises, and the eye element arises too.
(a) For whomever the eye element arises, does the mind-consciousness element arise for them? Yes.
(b) And for whomever the mind-consciousness element arises, does the eye element arise for them?
For those being reborn with mind but without eyes, the mind-consciousness element arises, but the eye element does not arise. For those being reborn with eyes, the mind-consciousness element arises, and the eye element arises too.
(a) For whomever the eye element arises, does the mind-object element arise for them? Yes.
(b) And for whomever the mind-object element arises, does the eye element arise for them?
For those being reborn without eyes, the mind-object element arises, but the eye element does not arise. For those being reborn with eyes, the mind-object element arises, and the eye element arises too.
