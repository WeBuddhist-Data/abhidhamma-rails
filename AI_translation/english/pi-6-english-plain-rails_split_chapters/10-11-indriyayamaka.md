For everyone, at the moment of dissolution of consciousness, at the moment of arising of consciousness not associated with equanimity, the equanimity faculty does not arise there, but the mind faculty will arise there. At the moment of dissolution of the final consciousness associated with equanimity, for those possessing the final consciousness associated with joy, for non-percipient beings, the equanimity faculty does not arise there, and the mind faculty will not arise there either.
And for whomever and wherever the mind faculty will not arise, does the equanimity faculty not arise for them there?
At the moment of arising of the final consciousness associated with equanimity, the mind faculty will not arise there, but the equanimity faculty does arise there. At the moment of dissolution of the final consciousness associated with equanimity, for those possessing the final consciousness associated with joy, for non-percipient beings, the mind faculty will not arise there, and the equanimity faculty does not arise there either.
