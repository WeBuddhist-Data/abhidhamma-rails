#### The Section on Clarifying the Terms ^6-3-7-0

##### (a) Positive ^6-3-7-1-0

(a) Is the eye the eye base?
The divine eye and the eye of wisdom are eyes, but not the eye base. The eye base is both the eye and the eye base.
(b) Is the eye base the eye? Yes.
(a) Is the ear the ear base?
The divine ear and the stream of craving are "ear" (sota), but not the ear base. The ear base is both the ear and the ear base.
(b) Is the ear base the ear? Yes.
(a) Is the nose the nose base? Yes.
(b) Is the nose base the nose? Yes.
(a) Is the tongue the tongue base? Yes.
(b) Is the tongue base the tongue? Yes.
(a) Is the body the body base?
Apart from the body base, any other body is a body, but not the body base. The body base is both the body and the body base.
(b) Is the body base the body? Yes.
(a) Is material form the visible-form base?
Apart from the visible-form base, any other material form is material form, but not the visible-form base. The visible-form base is both material form and the visible-form base.
(b) Is the visible-form base material form? Yes.
(a) Is sound the sound base? Yes.
(b) Is the sound base sound? Yes.
(a) Is scent the scent base?
The scent of virtue, the scent of concentration and the scent of wisdom are scents, but not the scent base. The scent base is both scent and the scent base.
(b) Is the scent base scent? Yes.
(a) Is taste the taste base?
The taste of meaning, the taste of the Dhamma and the taste of liberation are tastes, but not the taste base. The taste base is both taste and the taste base.
(b) Is the taste base taste? Yes.
(a) Is the tangible the tangible base? Yes.
(b) Is the tangible base the tangible? Yes.
(a) Is the mind the mind base? Yes.
(b) Is the mind base the mind? Yes.
(a) Is a phenomenon the mind-object base?
Apart from the mind-object base, any other phenomenon is a phenomenon, but not the mind-object base. The mind-object base is both a phenomenon and the mind-object base.
(b) Is the mind-object base a phenomenon? Yes. ^6-3-10
