#### (156) 1. Discussion of Restraining ^5-24-1-0

Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person restrain another person's mind, saying "Do not be impassioned", "Do not be corrupted", "Do not be deluded", "Do not be defiled"? That should not be said… Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person restrain another person's contact that has arisen, saying "Let it not cease"? That should not be said… Does one person restrain another person's feeling that has arisen… perception that has arisen… intention that has arisen… mind that has arisen… faith that has arisen… energy that has arisen… mindfulness that has arisen… concentration that has arisen… wisdom that has arisen, saying "Let it not cease"? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person abandon greed for the sake of another… abandon hatred… abandon lack of moral fear for the sake of another? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person develop the path for the sake of another… develop a foundation of mindfulness… develop a factor of awakening for the sake of another? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Does one person, for the sake of another, fully understand suffering, abandon its origin, realize its cessation and develop the path? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. Is one the doer for another; is pleasure and pain made by another; does one person do and another experience? That should not be said…
Does one person restrain another person's mind? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"By oneself alone is evil done, by oneself is one defiled;
by oneself is evil left undone, by oneself alone is one purified;
purity and impurity are one's own affair — no one can purify another" [Dha. Pa. 165]?
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "one person restrains another person's mind." ^5-743

Should it not be said that "one person restrains another person's mind"? Yes. But aren't there those who have attained power, and aren't there those who have achieved mastery? Yes. If there are those who have attained power and those who have achieved mastery, then surely you must also say: "One person restrains another person's mind."
The Discussion of Restraining is finished. ^5-744
