### Discussion of "Some of It Exists" ^5-7-0

#### Discussion of Some of the Past and So On ^5-7-1-0

Does the past exist? Some of it exists, some of it does not exist. Then has some of it ceased and some not ceased; has some gone and some not gone; has some vanished and some not vanished; has some completely vanished and some not completely vanished? That should not be said…
Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. Do some past states whose result has not yet ripened exist, and some not exist? That should not be said… Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. Do some past states whose result has ripened exist, and some not exist? That should not be said… Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. Do some past states that have no result exist, and some not exist? That should not be said…
Does some of the past exist and some not exist? Yes. What exists and what does not exist? Past states whose result has not yet ripened — these exist; past states whose result has ripened — these do not exist. Past states whose result has not yet ripened — do these exist? Yes. Past states whose result has ripened — do these exist? That should not be said… Past states whose result has not yet ripened — do these exist? Yes. Past states that have no result — do these exist [variant: atītā avipakkavipākā dhammā te natthīti (Ka.)]? That should not be said…
Past states whose result has ripened — do these not exist? Yes. Past states whose result has not yet ripened — do these not exist? That should not be said…
Past states that have no result [variant: vipakkavipākā (Syā.), avipakkavipākā (Ka.)] — do these not exist? Yes. Past states whose result has not yet ripened [variant: avipākā (Syā.)] — do these not exist? That should not be said…
Past states whose result has not yet ripened — do these exist? Yes. But haven't past states whose result has not yet ripened ceased? Yes. If past states whose result has not yet ripened have ceased, then surely you must not say: "Past states whose result has not yet ripened — these [variant: dhammā niruddhā te (Syā., Ka.)] exist."
Past states whose result has not yet ripened, which have ceased — do these exist? Yes. Past states whose result has ripened, which have ceased — do these exist? That should not be said… Past states whose result has not yet ripened, which have ceased — do these exist? Yes. Past states that have no result [variant: avipakkavipākā (Sī., Ka.)], which have ceased — do these exist? That should not be said…
Past states whose result has ripened, which have ceased — do these not exist? Yes. Past states whose result has not yet ripened, which have ceased — do these not exist? That should not be said…
Past states that have no result [variant: vipakkavipākā (Syā.), avipakkavipākā (Ka.)], which have ceased — do these not exist? Yes. Past states whose result has not yet ripened [variant: avipākā (Syā.)], which have ceased — do these not exist? That should not be said…
Past states whose result has not yet ripened, which have ceased — do these exist? Yes. Past states whose result has ripened, which have ceased — do these not exist? Yes. Past states whose result has partly ripened and partly not yet ripened, which have ceased — do some of these exist and some not exist? That should not be said…
Should it not be said that "past states whose result has not yet ripened — these exist"? Yes. But won't past states whose result has not yet ripened ripen? Yes. If past states whose result has not yet ripened will ripen, then surely you must also say: "Past states whose result has not yet ripened — these exist."
On the grounds that past states whose result has not yet ripened will ripen, do they exist? Yes. On the grounds that they will ripen, are they present? That should not be said… On the grounds that they will ripen, are they present? Yes. On the grounds that present states will cease, do they not exist? That should not be said… ^5-299

#### Discussion of Some of the Future and So On ^5-7-2-0

Does the future exist? Some of it exists, some of it does not exist. Then is some of it born and some unborn; has some arisen and some not arisen; has some been produced and some not produced; has some become manifest and some not become manifest? That should not be said…
Does some of the future exist and some not exist? Yes. Do some future states that are bound to arise exist, and some not exist? That should not be said… Does some of the future exist and some not exist? Yes. Do some future states that are not bound to arise exist, and some not exist? That should not be said…
Does some of the future exist and some not exist? Yes. What exists and what does not exist? Future states that are bound to arise — these exist; future states that are not bound to arise — these do not exist. Future states that are bound to arise — do these exist? Yes. Future states that are not bound to arise — do these exist? That should not be said… Future states that are not bound to arise — do these not exist? Yes. Future states that are bound to arise — do these not exist? That should not be said…
Future states that are bound to arise — do these exist? Yes. But aren't future states that are bound to arise unborn? Yes. If future states that are bound to arise are unborn, then surely you must not say: "Future states that are bound to arise — these exist."
Future states that are bound to arise, which are unborn — do these exist? Yes. Future states that are not bound to arise, which are unborn — do these exist? That should not be said… Future states that are not bound to arise, which are unborn — do these not exist? Yes. Future states that are bound to arise, which are unborn — do these not exist? That should not be said…
Should it not be said that "future states that are bound to arise — these exist"? Yes. But won't future states that are bound to arise arise? Yes. If future states that are bound to arise will arise, then surely you must also say: "Future states that are bound to arise — these exist."
On the grounds that future states that are bound to arise will arise, do they exist? Yes. On the grounds that they will arise, are they present? That should not be said… On the grounds that they will arise, are they present? Yes. On the grounds that present states will cease, do they not exist? That should not be said…
