##### (112) 7. Discussion of the Strength of Psychic Power ^5-19-5-1-0

Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon (world-age)? Yes. Is that life span made by psychic power, that destination made by psychic power, that acquisition of individual existence made by psychic power? That should not be said…
Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon? Yes. Could one live on for a past aeon, live on for a future aeon? That should not be said… Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon? Yes. Could one live on for two aeons, live on for three aeons, live on for four aeons? That should not be said… Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon? Yes. Would one live on while there is life and a remainder of life, or would one live on while there is no life and no remainder of life? One would live on while there is life and a remainder of life. If one would live on while there is life and a remainder of life, then surely you must not say: "One endowed with the strength of psychic power could live on for an aeon." Would one live on while there is no life and no remainder of life — would one who has died live on, would one who has passed away live on? That should not be said… ^5-621

Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon? Yes. Is it possible by psychic power to hold up contact that has arisen, saying "Let it not cease"? That should not be said… Is it possible by psychic power to hold up feeling that has arisen… perception that has arisen… intention that has arisen… a mind that has arisen… faith that has arisen… energy that has arisen… mindfulness that has arisen… concentration that has arisen… wisdom that has arisen, saying "Let it not cease"? That should not be said…
Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon? Yes. Is it possible by psychic power to hold up material form, saying "Let material form be permanent"? That should not be said… Is it possible by psychic power to hold up feeling… perception… mental formations… consciousness, saying "Let consciousness be permanent"? That should not be said…
Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon? Yes. Is it possible by psychic power to hold up beings, saying "Let beings who are subject to birth not be born"? That should not be said… saying "Let beings who are subject to ageing not age"… saying "Let beings who are subject to sickness not fall sick"… is it possible by psychic power to hold up beings, saying "Let beings who are subject to death not die"? That should not be said… ^5-622

Should it not be said that "one endowed with the strength of psychic power could live on for an aeon"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Ānanda, whoever has developed and cultivated the four bases of psychic power, made them a vehicle, made them a basis, established them, consolidated them and properly undertaken them could, if they wished, live on for an aeon or for the remainder of an aeon" [D. Ni. 2.166; S. Ni. 5.822; Udā. 51]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case one endowed with the strength of psychic power could live on for an aeon. ^5-623

Could one endowed with the strength of psychic power live on for an aeon? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, for four things there is no guarantor — no ascetic or brahmin, god, Māra or Brahmā, or anyone in the world. Which four? For what is subject to ageing — ‘Let it not age’ — there is no guarantor — no ascetic or brahmin, god, Māra or Brahmā, or anyone in the world. For what is subject to sickness — ‘Let it not fall sick’… for what is subject to death — ‘Let it not die’… And as for those evil actions done in the past, which are defiling, which lead to renewed existence [variant: ponobhavikāni (Sī., Pī.)], which are fraught with distress [variant: nissārāni (Sī., Pī., Ka.), dukkhudrayāni (Syā.)], which have suffering as their result and lead to future birth, ageing and death — for their result — ‘Let it not come about’ — there is no guarantor — no ascetic or brahmin, god, Māra or Brahmā, or anyone in the world. For these four things, monks, there is no guarantor — no ascetic or brahmin, god, Māra or Brahmā, or anyone in the world" [A. Ni. 4.182]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "one endowed with the strength of psychic power could live on for an aeon."
