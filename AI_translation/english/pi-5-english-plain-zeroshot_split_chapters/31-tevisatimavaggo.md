### The Twenty-Third Chapter ^5-31-0

#### (218) 1. Discussion of a Shared Intention ^5-31-1-0

May sexual intercourse be engaged in with a shared intention? Yes. With a shared intention, should one become a non-ascetic, become a non-monk, become one cut off at the root, become one who has incurred defeat (expulsion from the monastic order)? That should not be said… May sexual intercourse be engaged in with a shared intention? Yes. With a shared intention, may one kill a living being, take what is not given, speak falsely, speak divisively, speak harshly, engage in idle chatter, break into houses, carry off plunder, rob a single house, lie in ambush on the highway, go to another's wife, destroy a village, destroy a town? That should not be said…
The Discussion of a Shared Intention is finished. ^5-908

#### The Twenty-Third Chapter ^5-31-3-0

##### (220-4) 3-7. Discussion of Acting at Will through Sovereign Power and So On ^5-31-3-1-0

Does the bodhisatta (a Buddha-to-be) go to the lower world because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. Does the bodhisatta, because of acting at will through sovereign power, go to hell, go to the Sañjīva hell, go to the Kālasutta hell, go to the Tāpana hell, go to the Mahātāpana hell [variant: patāpanaṃ (Sī., Syā., Kaṃ., Pī.)], go to the Saṅghātaka hell, go to the Roruva hell… go to the Avīci hell? That should not be said…
Does the bodhisatta go to the lower world because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. "The bodhisatta goes to the lower world because of acting at will through sovereign power" — is there really such a discourse? There is not. If there really is no discourse "The bodhisatta goes to the lower world because of acting at will through sovereign power," then surely you must not say: "The bodhisatta goes to the lower world because of acting at will through sovereign power." ^5-910

Does the bodhisatta enter a womb because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. Would the bodhisatta, because of acting at will through sovereign power, be reborn in hell, be reborn in the animal realm? That should not be said…
Does the bodhisatta enter a womb because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. Does the bodhisatta have psychic power? That should not be said… Does the bodhisatta have psychic power? Yes. Has the bodhisatta developed the basis of psychic power consisting of desire… the basis of psychic power consisting of energy… the basis of psychic power consisting of mind… the basis of psychic power consisting of investigation? That should not be said…
Does the bodhisatta enter a womb because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. "The bodhisatta enters a womb because of acting at will through sovereign power" — is there really such a discourse? There is not. If there really is no discourse "The bodhisatta enters a womb because of acting at will through sovereign power," then surely you must not say: "The bodhisatta enters a womb because of acting at will through sovereign power." ^5-911

Did the bodhisatta practise severe austerities because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. Did the bodhisatta, because of acting at will through sovereign power, adopt the view "The world is eternal"… "The world is not eternal"… "The world is finite"… "The world is infinite"… "The soul is the same as the body"… "The soul is one thing and the body another"… "The Tathāgata (the Buddha) exists after death"… "The Tathāgata does not exist after death"… "The Tathāgata both exists and does not exist after death"… did he adopt the view "The Tathāgata neither exists nor does not exist after death"? That should not be said…
Did the bodhisatta practise severe austerities because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. "The bodhisatta practised severe austerities because of acting at will through sovereign power" — is there really such a discourse? There is not. If there really is no discourse "The bodhisatta practised severe austerities because of acting at will through sovereign power," then surely you must not say: "The bodhisatta practised severe austerities because of acting at will through sovereign power." ^5-912

Did the bodhisatta, because of acting at will through sovereign power, practise another kind of austerity [variant: amaraṃ tapaṃ (S. Ni. 1.137)] and acknowledge another teacher? Yes. Did the bodhisatta, because of acting at will through sovereign power, adopt the view "The world is eternal"… did he adopt the view "The Tathāgata neither exists nor does not exist after death"? That should not be said… ^5-913

Did the bodhisatta acknowledge another teacher because of acting at will through sovereign power? Yes. "The bodhisatta acknowledged another teacher because of acting at will through sovereign power" — is there really such a discourse? There is not. If there really is no discourse "The bodhisatta acknowledged another teacher because of acting at will through sovereign power," then surely you must not say: "The bodhisatta acknowledged another teacher because of acting at will through sovereign power."
The Discussion of Acting at Will through Sovereign Power is finished. ^5-914

#### The Twenty-Third Chapter ^5-31-5-0

##### (226) 9. Discussion of the Not Fully Formed ^5-31-5-1-0

Is material form not fully formed? Yes. Is material form not impermanent, not conditioned, not dependently arisen, not subject to destruction, not subject to passing away, not subject to fading, not subject to cessation, not subject to change? That should not be said… But isn't material form impermanent, conditioned, dependently arisen, subject to destruction, subject to passing away, subject to fading, subject to cessation, subject to change? Yes. If material form is impermanent, conditioned… subject to change, then surely you must not say: "Material form is not fully formed."
Is only suffering fully formed? Yes. But hasn't the Blessed One said that what is impermanent is suffering [S. Ni. 3.15], and that "material form is impermanent"? Yes. If the Blessed One has said that what is impermanent is suffering, and that "material form is impermanent," then surely you must not say: "Only suffering is fully formed."… ^5-917

Is feeling… perception… are mental formations… is consciousness… the eye base… the base of mental objects… the eye element… the element of mental objects… the eye faculty… the faculty of one who has final knowledge not fully formed? Yes. Is the faculty of one who has final knowledge not impermanent… not subject to change? That should not be said… But isn't the faculty of one who has final knowledge impermanent, conditioned… subject to change? Yes. If the faculty of one who has final knowledge is impermanent, conditioned, dependently arisen, subject to destruction, subject to passing away, subject to fading, subject to cessation, subject to change, then surely you must not say: "The faculty of one who has final knowledge is not fully formed."
Is only suffering fully formed? Yes. But hasn't the Blessed One said that what is impermanent is suffering, and that "the faculty of one who has final knowledge is impermanent"? Yes. If the Blessed One has said that what is impermanent is suffering, and that "the faculty of one who has final knowledge is impermanent," then surely you must not say: "Only suffering is fully formed."
The Discussion of the Not Fully Formed is finished.
