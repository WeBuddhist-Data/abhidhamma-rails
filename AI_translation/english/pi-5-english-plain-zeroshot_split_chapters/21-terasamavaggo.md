#### (126) 1. Discussion of the One Who Remains for an Aeon ^5-21-1-0

Would one who remains for an aeon (world-age) remain for a whole aeon? Yes. Does the aeon come into being, and does a Buddha arise in the world? That should not be said… Would one who remains for an aeon remain for a whole aeon? Yes. Does the aeon come into being, and is the Community split? That should not be said… Would one who remains for an aeon remain for a whole aeon? Yes. Does the aeon come into being, and does one who remains for an aeon do the action that makes one remain for an aeon? That should not be said… Would one who remains for an aeon remain for a whole aeon? Yes. Does the aeon come into being, and does the person who remains for an aeon die? That should not be said… ^5-654

Would one who remains for an aeon remain for a whole aeon? Yes. Would they remain for a past aeon, remain for a future aeon? That should not be said… Would one who remains for an aeon remain for a whole aeon? Yes. Would they remain for two aeons… remain for three aeons… remain for four aeons? That should not be said… ^5-655

Would one who remains for an aeon remain for a whole aeon? Yes. When the aeon is burning up, where does the one who remains for an aeon go? They go to another world system. Do they go having died, or do they go through the air? They go having died. Is the action that makes one remain for an aeon one that ripens in some later existence? That should not be said… [“They go through the air” (?) — for this is just the other school's assertion, as in the ninth line of Kathā. 621-623] Do they go through the air? Yes [“They go through the air” (?) — for this is just the other school's assertion, as in the ninth line of Kathā. 621-623]. Does one who remains for an aeon have psychic power? That should not be said… Does one who remains for an aeon have psychic power? Yes. Has the one who remains for an aeon developed the basis of psychic power consisting of desire, developed the basis of psychic power consisting of energy, developed the basis of psychic power consisting of mind, developed the basis of psychic power consisting of investigation? That should not be said… ^5-656

Should it not be said that "one who remains for an aeon would remain for a whole aeon"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"Bound for the lower realms, bound for hell, remaining there for an aeon, is the splitter of the Community;
delighting in faction, standing in what is not the Dhamma, he falls away from security from bondage;
having split a Community that was in harmony [variant: bhinditvā (Sī., Ka.)], he is tormented in hell for an aeon" [Cūḷava. 354; A. Ni. 10.39; Itivu. 18]?
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case one who remains for an aeon would remain for a whole aeon.
The Discussion of the One Who Remains for an Aeon is finished. ^5-657

##### (127) 2. Discussion of Obtaining the Wholesome ^5-21-2-1-0

Would one who remains for an aeon (world-age) not obtain a wholesome mind? Yes. Would one who remains for an aeon give a gift? Yes. If one who remains for an aeon would give a gift, then surely you must not say: "One who remains for an aeon would not obtain a wholesome mind."
Would one who remains for an aeon not obtain a wholesome mind? Yes. Would one who remains for an aeon give robes… give almsfood… give lodgings… give medicines and requisites for the sick… give food to chew… give meals… give drink… pay homage to a shrine… offer a garland at a shrine… offer scents… offer ointments… walk around a shrine keeping it on their right [variant: padakkhiṇaṃ (Pī.)]? Yes. If one who remains for an aeon would walk around a shrine keeping it on their right, then surely you must not say: "One who remains for an aeon would not obtain a wholesome mind."… ^5-658

Would one who remains for an aeon obtain a wholesome mind? Yes. Would they obtain a wholesome mind for emerging from there? Yes. Would they obtain a wholesome mind belonging to the realm of form… belonging to the formless realm… a world-transcending wholesome mind? That should not be said…
The Discussion of Obtaining the Wholesome is finished. ^5-659

##### (128) 3. Discussion of the One Engaged in a Deed with Immediate Result ^5-21-3-1-0

Would a person engaged in a deed with immediate result enter the fixed course of rightness? Yes. Would they enter both the fixed course of wrongness and the fixed course of rightness? That should not be said… Would a person engaged in a deed with immediate result enter the fixed course of rightness? Yes. But hasn't that action been undertaken, remorse been aroused and regret been produced? Yes. If that action has been undertaken, remorse has been aroused and regret has been produced, then surely you must not say: "A person engaged in a deed with immediate result would enter the fixed course of rightness." ^5-660

Is a person engaged in a deed with immediate result incapable of entering the fixed course of rightness? Yes. Have they deprived their mother of life… deprived their father of life… deprived a worthy one (arahant) of life… with a corrupt mind shed the blood of the Tathāgata (the Buddha)… split the Community? That should not be said…
Is a person engaged in a deed with immediate result, having withdrawn from that action, dispelled the remorse and removed the regret [variant: paṭivinodetvā (Sī., Pī., Ka.)], incapable of entering the fixed course of rightness? Yes. Have they deprived their mother of life… deprived their father of life… split the Community? That should not be said…
Is a person engaged in a deed with immediate result, having withdrawn from that action, dispelled the remorse and removed the regret, incapable of entering the fixed course of rightness? Yes. But hasn't that action been withdrawn from, the remorse dispelled and the regret removed? Yes. If that action has been withdrawn from, the remorse dispelled and the regret removed, then surely you must not say: "A person engaged in a deed with immediate result, having withdrawn from that action, dispelled the remorse and removed the regret, is incapable of entering the fixed course of rightness." ^5-661

Would a person engaged in a deed with immediate result enter the fixed course of rightness? Yes. But wasn't that person engaged in that action? Yes. If that person was engaged in that action, then surely you must not say: "A person engaged in a deed with immediate result would enter the fixed course of rightness."
The Discussion of the One Engaged in a Deed with Immediate Result is finished. ^5-662

##### (129) 4. Discussion of the Fixed Course for One Whose Course Is Fixed ^5-21-4-1-0

Does one whose course is fixed enter the fixed course? Yes. Does one whose course is fixed in wrongness enter the fixed course of rightness, and does one whose course is fixed in rightness enter the fixed course of wrongness? That should not be said…
Does one whose course is fixed enter the fixed course? Yes. Having first developed the path, does one afterwards enter the fixed course? That should not be said… Having first developed the path of stream-entry, does one afterwards enter the fixed course of stream-entry? That should not be said… Having first developed the path of once-returning… of non-returning… of arahantship (the state of a worthy one), does one afterwards enter the fixed course of arahantship? That should not be said…
Having first developed a foundation of mindfulness… a right striving… a basis of psychic power… a faculty… a power… a factor of awakening, does one afterwards enter the fixed course? That should not be said… ^5-663

Should it not be said that "one whose course is fixed enters the fixed course"? Yes. Is it possible for the bodhisatta (a Buddha-to-be) not to break through to the Dhamma in that birth? That should not be said. In that case one whose course is fixed enters the fixed course.
The Discussion of the Fixed Course for One Whose Course Is Fixed is finished. ^5-664

##### (130) 5. Discussion of the One Who Is Hindered ^5-21-5-1-0

Does one who is hindered abandon a hindrance? Yes. Does one who is impassioned abandon greed, one who is corrupted abandon hatred, one who is deluded abandon delusion, one who is defiled abandon the defilements? That should not be said… Does one abandon greed by means of greed, hatred by means of hatred, delusion by means of delusion, the defilements by means of the defilements? That should not be said…
Is greed associated with the mind, and is the path associated with the mind? Yes. Is there a coming together of two contacts… of two states of mind? That should not be said… Is greed unwholesome, and is the path wholesome? Yes. Do wholesome and unwholesome, blameworthy and blameless, inferior and superior, dark and bright states with their counterparts come face to face? That should not be said… ^5-665

Do wholesome and unwholesome, blameworthy and blameless, inferior and superior, dark and bright states with their counterparts come face to face? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, there are these four things that are very far apart. Which four? The sky, monks, and the earth — this is the first thing that is very far apart… therefore the Dhamma of the good is far from the bad"? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "wholesome and unwholesome… come face to face."
Does one who is hindered abandon a hindrance? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "When the mind is thus concentrated, purified, cleansed, without blemish, free from impurities, pliable, workable, steady and attained to imperturbability, they direct the mind to the knowledge of the destruction of the taints"? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "one who is hindered abandons a hindrance."… ^5-666

Should it not be said that "one who is hindered abandons a hindrance"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "For one who knows thus, who sees thus, the mind becomes liberated from the taint of sensuality… the mind becomes liberated from the taint of ignorance"? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case one who is hindered abandons a hindrance.
The Discussion of the One Who Is Hindered is finished. ^5-667

##### (132) 7. Discussion of "One in a Meditative Attainment Relishes It" ^5-21-7-1-0

Does one in a meditative attainment relish it, and does the longing for meditative absorption (jhāna) have meditative absorption as its object? Yes. ##### (133) 8. Discussion of Greed for the Unpleasant ^5-21-8-1-0

Is there greed for the unpleasant? Yes. Do beings delight in pain; are there some who wish for pain, long for it, seek it, search for it, look for it, and remain holding on to pain? That should not be said… But don't beings delight in pleasure; aren't there some who wish for pleasure, long for it, seek it, search for it, look for it, and remain holding on to pleasure? Yes. If beings delight in pleasure, and there are some who wish for pleasure, long for it, seek it, search for it, look for it, and remain holding on to pleasure, then surely you must not say: "There is greed for the unpleasant."
Is there greed for the unpleasant? Yes. Does the underlying tendency to greed lie latent in painful feeling, and does the underlying tendency to aversion lie latent in pleasant feeling? That should not be said… But doesn't the underlying tendency to greed lie latent in pleasant feeling, and the underlying tendency to aversion lie latent in painful feeling? Yes. If the underlying tendency to greed lies latent in pleasant feeling, and the underlying tendency to aversion lies latent in painful feeling, then surely you must not say: "There is greed for the unpleasant." ^5-674

Should it not be said that "there is greed for the unpleasant"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Being thus caught up in favouring and opposing, whatever feeling they feel — pleasant, painful or neither-painful-nor-pleasant — they delight in that feeling, welcome it and remain holding on to it" [M. Ni. 3.409, in the Greater Discourse on the Destruction of Craving]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case there is greed for the unpleasant.
The Discussion of Greed for the Unpleasant is finished. ^5-675

One who remains for an aeon would remain for a whole aeon; one who remains for an aeon would not obtain a wholesome mind; a person engaged in a deed with immediate result would enter the fixed course of rightness; one whose course is fixed enters the fixed course; one who is hindered abandons a hindrance; one who is face to face abandons a fetter; the longing for meditative absorption; greed for the unpleasant; craving for mental objects is indeterminate; craving for mental objects is not the origin of suffering. ^5-685
