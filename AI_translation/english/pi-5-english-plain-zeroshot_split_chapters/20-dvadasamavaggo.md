##### (117) 2. Discussion of Action ^5-20-2-1-0

Does all intention have a result? Yes. Does intention of the sense-desire realm that is functional and indeterminate have a result? That should not be said… Does all intention have a result? Yes. Does intention that is a result and indeterminate have no result? Yes. If intention that is a result and indeterminate has no result, then surely you must not say: "All intention has a result."
Does intention that is functional and indeterminate have no result? Yes. If intention that is functional and indeterminate has no result, then surely you must not say: "All intention has a result."

Should it not be said that "all action has a result"? Yes.
