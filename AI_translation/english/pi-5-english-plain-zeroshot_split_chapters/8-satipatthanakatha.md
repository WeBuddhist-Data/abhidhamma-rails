### Discussion of the Foundations of Mindfulness ^5-8-0

Are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Are all things mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness, the mindfulness factor of awakening, the one-way path, leading to destruction, leading to awakening, leading to dismantling, free of taints, not subject to the fetters, not subject to the knots, not subject to the floods, not subject to the bonds, not subject to the hindrances, not seized upon, not subject to clinging, not subject to defilement; are all things recollection of the Buddha, recollection of the Dhamma, recollection of the Community, recollection of virtue, recollection of generosity, recollection of the deities, mindfulness of breathing, recollection of death, mindfulness directed to the body, recollection of peace? That should not be said…
Are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Is the eye base a foundation of mindfulness? That should not be said… Is the eye base a foundation of mindfulness? Yes. Is the eye base mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness, the mindfulness factor of awakening, the one-way path, leading to destruction, leading to awakening, leading to dismantling, free of taints, not subject to the fetters… not subject to defilement; is the eye base recollection of the Buddha, recollection of the Dhamma, recollection of the Community, recollection of virtue, recollection of generosity, recollection of the deities, mindfulness of breathing, recollection of death, mindfulness directed to the body, recollection of peace? That should not be said… Is the ear base… the nose base… the tongue base… the body base… the visible-form base… the sound base… the smell base… the taste base… the touch base… greed… hatred… delusion… conceit… wrong view… doubt… sluggishness… restlessness… shamelessness… lack of moral fear a foundation of mindfulness? That should not be said… Is lack of moral fear a foundation of mindfulness? Yes. Is lack of moral fear mindfulness, the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, right mindfulness… mindfulness directed to the body, recollection of peace? That should not be said…
Is mindfulness a foundation of mindfulness, and is it mindfulness? Yes. Is the eye base a foundation of mindfulness, and is it mindfulness? That should not be said… Is mindfulness a foundation of mindfulness, and is it mindfulness? Yes. Is the ear base… the body base… the visible-form base… the touch base… greed… hatred… delusion… lack of moral fear a foundation of mindfulness, and is it mindfulness? That should not be said…
Is the eye base a foundation of mindfulness, but not mindfulness? Yes. Is mindfulness a foundation of mindfulness, but not mindfulness? That should not be said… Is the ear base… the body base… the visible-form base… the touch base… greed… hatred… delusion… lack of moral fear a foundation of mindfulness, but not mindfulness? Yes. Is mindfulness a foundation of mindfulness, but not mindfulness? That should not be said… ^5-301

Should it not be said that "all things are foundations of mindfulness"? Yes. But doesn't mindfulness become established with regard to all things? Yes. If mindfulness becomes established with regard to all things, then surely you must also say: "All things are foundations of mindfulness."
Since mindfulness becomes established with regard to every thing, are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Since contact becomes established with regard to every thing, are all things foundations of contact? That should not be said…
Since mindfulness becomes established with regard to every thing, are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Since feeling becomes established with regard to every thing… perception becomes established… intention becomes established… mind becomes established with regard to every thing, are all things foundations of mind? That should not be said…
Are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Are all beings ones whose mindfulness is established, endowed with mindfulness, possessed of mindfulness; is mindfulness present in all beings? That should not be said… ^5-302

Are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, they do not partake of the deathless who do not partake of mindfulness directed to the body. Monks, they partake of the deathless who partake of mindfulness directed to the body" [A. Ni. 1.600]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. Do all beings partake of, obtain, pursue, develop and cultivate mindfulness directed to the body? That should not be said…
Are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, this is the one-way path for the purification of beings, for the overcoming of sorrow and lamentation, for the disappearance of pain and grief, for the attainment of the true method, for the realization of nibbāna (final release) — namely, the four foundations of mindfulness" [D. Ni. 2.373; M. Ni. 1.106; S. Ni. 5.367]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. Are all things the one-way path? That should not be said…
Are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, with the appearance of a wheel-turning king (a universal monarch), seven treasures appear. Which seven? The wheel treasure appears, the elephant treasure appears, the horse treasure… the jewel treasure… the woman treasure… the householder treasure… the adviser treasure appears. Monks, with the appearance of a wheel-turning king, these seven treasures appear.
"Monks, with the appearance of the Tathāgata (the Buddha), the worthy one (arahant), the Fully Awakened One, seven treasures of the factors of awakening appear. Which seven? The treasure of the mindfulness factor of awakening appears, the treasure of the investigation-of-states factor of awakening appears, the treasure of the energy factor of awakening appears, the treasure of the joy factor of awakening appears, the treasure of the tranquillity factor of awakening appears, the treasure of the concentration factor of awakening appears, the treasure of the equanimity factor of awakening appears. Monks, with the appearance of the Tathāgata, the worthy one, the Fully Awakened One, these seven treasures of the factors of awakening appear" [S. Ni. 5.223]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. With the appearance of the Tathāgata, the worthy one, the Fully Awakened One, do all things become simply the treasure of the mindfulness factor of awakening? That should not be said… Are all things foundations of mindfulness? Yes. Are all things right strivings… bases of psychic power… faculties… powers… factors of awakening? That should not be said…
The Discussion of the Foundations of Mindfulness is finished. ^5-303
