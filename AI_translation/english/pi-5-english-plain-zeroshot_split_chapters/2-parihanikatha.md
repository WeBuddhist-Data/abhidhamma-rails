Does a temporarily liberated worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. Does a worthy one who is not temporarily liberated fall away from arahantship [variant: na parihāyatīti (Ka.)]? That should not be said.
Does a worthy one who is not temporarily liberated not fall away from arahantship? Yes. Does a temporarily liberated worthy one not fall away from arahantship? That should not be said.
Greed has been abandoned by the temporarily liberated worthy one — does the temporarily liberated worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. Greed has been abandoned by the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated — does the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated fall away from arahantship? That should not be said.
Hatred has been abandoned by the temporarily liberated worthy one… lack of moral fear has been abandoned — does the temporarily liberated worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. Lack of moral fear has been abandoned by the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated — does the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated fall away from arahantship? That should not be said.
The temporarily liberated worthy one has developed the path for the abandoning of greed — does the temporarily liberated worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. The worthy one who is not temporarily liberated has developed the path for the abandoning of greed — does the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated fall away from arahantship? That should not be said.
The temporarily liberated worthy one has developed the foundations of mindfulness for the abandoning of greed… developed the right strivings… developed the bases of psychic power… developed the faculties… developed the powers… developed the factors of awakening — does the temporarily liberated worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. The worthy one who is not temporarily liberated has developed the foundations of mindfulness for the abandoning of greed… developed the factors of awakening — does the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated fall away from arahantship? That should not be said.
The temporarily liberated worthy one has developed the path for the abandoning of hatred… for the abandoning of lack of moral fear… developed the factors of awakening — does the temporarily liberated worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. The worthy one who is not temporarily liberated has developed the path for the abandoning of lack of moral fear… developed the factors of awakening — does the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated fall away from arahantship? That should not be said…
The temporarily liberated worthy one is free from greed, free from hatred, free from delusion, one who has done what had to be done, who has laid down the load, who has reached their own goal, whose fetters of existence are utterly destroyed, who is completely liberated through final knowledge, who has lifted the cross-bar, filled in the moat, pulled out the pillar, who has no bolt, a noble one, whose banner is lowered, whose burden is put down, who is unfettered, who has won complete victory; by whom suffering has been fully understood, its origin abandoned, its cessation realized, the path developed; by whom what is to be directly known has been directly known, what is to be fully understood has been fully understood, what is to be abandoned has been abandoned, what is to be developed has been developed, what is to be realized has been realized — does the temporarily liberated worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. The worthy one who is not temporarily liberated is free from greed, free from hatred, free from delusion… and what is to be realized has been realized — does the worthy one who is not temporarily liberated fall away from arahantship? That should not be said. ^5-262

#### Falling Away: Proof from the Discourses ^5-2-3-0

Does a worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"High and low are the ways of practice [variant: paṭipādā (Aṭṭha.)] made known by the ascetic;
they do not go to the far shore twice, yet this is not to be thought of as reached only once" [Su. Ni. 719, in the Sutta Nipāta]?
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "a worthy one falls away from arahantship."
Does a worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. Is there anything still to be cut for one who has cut? That should not be said.
Is there anything still to be cut for one who has cut? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"One who is free from craving, without grasping, for whom there is nothing left to do —
for one who has cut, there is nothing still to be cut; the snare of the floods has been removed"?
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "there is something still to be cut for one who has cut." ^5-265

Does a worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. Is there any adding to what has been done? That should not be said.
Is there any adding to what has been done? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say:
"For that monk, rightly liberated, whose mind is at peace,
there is no adding to what has been done, and nothing remains to be done.
"As a rock that is one solid mass is not shaken by the wind,
so visible forms, tastes, sounds, smells and touches, all of them,
"and things desirable and undesirable, do not stir one who is steady;
the mind stands firm and set free, and they observe its passing away" [Mahāva. 244; A. Ni. 6.55]?
Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "there is adding to what has been done." ^5-266

Should it not be said that "a worthy one falls away from arahantship"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, these five things lead to the falling away of a temporarily liberated monk. Which five? Delight in activity, delight in talk, delight in sleep, delight in company, and not reviewing the mind according to how it has been liberated. These, monks, are the five things that lead to the falling away of a temporarily liberated monk" [A. Ni. 5.149]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case, a worthy one falls away from arahantship.
Does a worthy one have delight in activity? That should not be said.
Does a worthy one have delight in activity? Yes. Does a worthy one have greed, sensual greed, obsession by sensual greed, the fetter of sensual greed, the flood of sensuality, the bond of sensuality, the hindrance of sensual desire? That should not be said.
Does a worthy one have delight in talk, does a worthy one have delight in sleep, does a worthy one have delight in company? That should not be said.
Does a worthy one have delight in company? Yes. Does a worthy one have greed, sensual greed, obsession by sensual greed, the fetter of sensual greed, the flood of sensuality, the bond of sensuality, the hindrance of sensual desire? That should not be said. ^5-267

Does a worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. When a worthy one falls away from arahantship, obsessed by what do they fall away? They fall away obsessed by greed. Dependent on what does the obsession arise? It arises dependent on an underlying tendency. Does a worthy one have underlying tendencies? That should not be said.
Does a worthy one have underlying tendencies? Yes. Does a worthy one have the underlying tendency to sensual greed, the underlying tendency to aversion, the underlying tendency to conceit, the underlying tendency to views, the underlying tendency to doubt, the underlying tendency to greed for existence, the underlying tendency to ignorance? That should not be said.
They fall away obsessed by hatred… they fall away obsessed by delusion… Dependent on what does the obsession arise? It arises dependent on an underlying tendency. Does a worthy one have underlying tendencies? That should not be said…
Does a worthy one have underlying tendencies? Yes. Does a worthy one have the underlying tendency to sensual greed… the underlying tendency to ignorance? That should not be said.
Does a worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. When a worthy one falls away from arahantship, what increases in them? Greed increases. Does identity view increase, does doubt increase, does clinging to rules and rituals increase? That should not be said. Hatred increases… delusion increases — does identity view increase, does doubt increase, does clinging to rules and rituals increase? That should not be said.
Does a worthy one fall away from arahantship? Yes. Does a worthy one build up? That should not be said. Does a worthy one tear down? That should not be said. Does a worthy one abandon? That should not be said. Does a worthy one cling? That should not be said. Does a worthy one scatter? That should not be said. Does a worthy one bind together? That should not be said. Does a worthy one put out? That should not be said. Does a worthy one kindle? That should not be said.
But doesn't a worthy one neither build up nor tear down, but stand having torn down? Yes. If a worthy one neither builds up nor tears down, but stands having torn down, then surely you must not say: "A worthy one falls away from arahantship."
But doesn't a worthy one neither abandon nor cling, but stand having abandoned? Yes. If a worthy one neither abandons nor clings, but stands having abandoned, then surely you must not say: "A worthy one falls away from arahantship."
But doesn't a worthy one neither scatter nor bind together, but stand having scattered? Yes. If a worthy one neither scatters nor binds together, but stands having scattered, then surely you must not say: "A worthy one falls away from arahantship."
But doesn't a worthy one neither put out nor kindle, but stand having put out? Yes. If a worthy one neither puts out nor kindles, but stands having put out, then surely you must not say: "A worthy one falls away from arahantship."
The Discussion of Falling Away is finished. ^5-268
