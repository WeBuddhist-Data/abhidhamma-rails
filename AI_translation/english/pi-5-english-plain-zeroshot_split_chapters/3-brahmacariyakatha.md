#### Plain Discussion of the Holy Life ^5-3-1-0

Is there no living of the holy life (the life of spiritual training) among the gods? Yes. Are all gods dull, deaf and dumb [variant: eḷamūgā (Syā.)], unintelligent, communicating by hand signs, unable to understand the meaning of what is well spoken and badly spoken; are all gods without confidence in the Buddha, without confidence in the Dhamma, without confidence in the Community; do they not attend on the Buddha, the Blessed One, not ask the Buddha, the Blessed One, questions, and are they not pleased when the Buddha, the Blessed One, has answered a question; are all gods burdened with the obstruction of action, burdened with the obstruction of defilements, burdened with the obstruction of results, without faith, without wholesome desire, lacking wisdom, incapable of entering the fixed course of rightness in wholesome states; are all gods killers of their mother, killers of their father, killers of worthy ones (arahants), shedders of a Buddha's blood, splitters of the Community; are all gods ones who kill living beings, take what is not given, engage in sexual misconduct, speak falsely, speak divisively, speak harshly, engage in idle chatter, are covetous, have minds full of ill will, and hold wrong view? That should not be said…
But aren't there gods who are not dull, not deaf and dumb, intelligent, not communicating by hand signs, able to understand the meaning of what is well spoken and badly spoken; aren't there gods who have confidence in the Buddha, confidence in the Dhamma, confidence in the Community, who attend on the Buddha, the Blessed One, ask the Buddha, the Blessed One, questions, and are pleased when the Buddha, the Blessed One, has answered a question; aren't there gods who are not burdened with the obstruction of action, not burdened with the obstruction of defilements, not burdened with the obstruction of results, who have faith, have wholesome desire, are wise, capable of entering the fixed course of rightness in wholesome states; aren't there gods who are not killers of their mother, not killers of their father, not killers of worthy ones, not shedders of a Buddha's blood, not splitters of the Community; aren't there gods who do not kill living beings, do not take what is not given, do not engage in sexual misconduct, do not speak falsely, do not speak divisively, do not speak harshly, do not engage in idle chatter, are not covetous, have minds free of ill will, and hold right view? Yes.
If there are gods who are not dull, not deaf and dumb, intelligent, not communicating by hand signs, able to understand the meaning of what is well spoken and badly spoken… there are gods who have confidence in the Buddha… who hold right view, then surely you must not say: "There is no living of the holy life among the gods." ^5-269
