#### (53) 1. Discussion of the Fixed Course ^5-14-1-0

Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna (final release), the protection, the shelter, the refuge, the final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said…
Is the fixed course unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said…
Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections, two shelters, two refuges, two final resorts, two imperishables, two deathless states, two nibbānas? That should not be said…
Are there two nibbānas? Yes. Is there between the two nibbānas any difference of high and low, of inferior and superior, of better and worse, or any boundary, division, dividing line or gap? That should not be said…
Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Are there some who enter the fixed course, obtain it, make it arise, make it fully arise, raise it up, fully raise it up, produce it, fully produce it, generate it, fully generate it? Yes. Are there some who enter the unconditioned, obtain it, make it arise, make it fully arise, raise it up, fully raise it up, produce it, fully produce it, generate it, fully generate it? That should not be said… ^5-445

Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Is the path unconditioned? That should not be said…
Is the path conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course conditioned? That should not be said…
Is the fixed course of stream-entry unconditioned? Yes. Is the path of stream-entry unconditioned? That should not be said…
Is the path of stream-entry conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of stream-entry conditioned? That should not be said…
Is the fixed course of once-returning… the fixed course of non-returning… the fixed course of arahantship (the state of a worthy one) unconditioned? Yes. Is the path of arahantship unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the path of arahantship conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of arahantship conditioned? That should not be said…
Is the fixed course of stream-entry unconditioned… is the fixed course of arahantship unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there five unconditioned things? That should not be said… Are there five unconditioned things? Yes. Are there five protections… or any gap? That should not be said…
Is the fixed course unconditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of wrongness unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the fixed course of wrongness conditioned? Yes. Is the fixed course of rightness conditioned? That should not be said… ^5-446

Should it not be said that "the fixed course is unconditioned"? Yes. When the fixed course has arisen and ceased, does one become one whose course is not fixed? That should not be said… In that case the fixed course is unconditioned. When the fixed course of wrongness has arisen and ceased, does one become one whose course is not fixed? That should not be said… In that case the fixed course of wrongness is unconditioned.
The Discussion of the Fixed Course is finished. ^5-447

##### (54) 2. Discussion of Dependent Arising ^5-14-2-1-0

Is dependent arising unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna (final release), the protection, the shelter, the refuge, the final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said… Is dependent arising unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said… Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections, two shelters, two refuges, two final resorts, two imperishables, two deathless states, two nibbānas? That should not be said… Are there two nibbānas? Yes. Is there between the two nibbānas any difference of high and low, of inferior and superior, of better and worse, or any boundary, division, dividing line or gap? That should not be said… ^5-448

Is dependent arising unconditioned? Yes. Is ignorance unconditioned? That should not be said… Is ignorance conditioned? Yes. Is dependent arising conditioned? That should not be said… Is dependent arising unconditioned? Yes. Are the formations that have ignorance as their condition unconditioned? That should not be said… Are the formations that have ignorance as their condition conditioned? Yes. Is dependent arising conditioned? That should not be said… Is dependent arising unconditioned? Yes. Is the consciousness that has formations as its condition unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the consciousness that has formations as its condition conditioned? Yes. Is dependent arising conditioned? That should not be said… Is dependent arising unconditioned? Yes. Is the name-and-form (mind and body) that has consciousness as its condition unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the name-and-form that has consciousness as its condition conditioned? Yes. Is dependent arising conditioned? That should not be said… Is dependent arising unconditioned? Yes. Are the ageing and death that have birth as their condition unconditioned? That should not be said… Are the ageing and death that have birth as their condition conditioned? Yes. Is dependent arising conditioned? That should not be said… ^5-449

Should it not be said that "dependent arising is unconditioned"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, with birth as condition there is ageing and death. Whether Tathāgatas (Buddhas) arise or do not arise, that element stands firm — the stability of things, the fixed order of things, specific conditionality. The Tathāgata awakens to it and breaks through to it. Having awakened to it and broken through to it, he explains it, teaches it, makes it known, establishes it, discloses it, analyses it and makes it plain. And he says: ‘See!’ — with birth as condition, monks, there is ageing and death. With existence as condition, monks, there is birth… with ignorance as condition, monks, there are formations. Whether Tathāgatas arise or do not arise, that element stands firm… and he says: ‘See!’ — with ignorance as condition, monks, there are formations. So, monks, the reality in this, the non-falsity, the not-otherwiseness, the specific conditionality — this, monks, is called dependent arising" [S. Ni. 2.20]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case dependent arising is unconditioned. ^5-450

The stability of things, the fixed order of things in "with ignorance as condition there are formations" — is that unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said… Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections… or any gap? That should not be said…
The stability of things, the fixed order of things in "with ignorance as condition there are formations" — is that unconditioned; the stability of things, the fixed order of things in "with formations as condition there is consciousness" — is that unconditioned; and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there three unconditioned things? That should not be said… Are there three unconditioned things? Yes. Are there three protections… or any gap? That should not be said…
The stability of things, the fixed order of things in "with ignorance as condition there are formations" — is that unconditioned; the stability of things, the fixed order of things in "with formations as condition there is consciousness" — is that unconditioned… the stability of things, the fixed order of things in "with birth as condition there is ageing and death" — is that unconditioned; and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there twelve unconditioned things? That should not be said… Are there twelve unconditioned things? Yes. Are there twelve protections, twelve shelters… or any gap? That should not be said…
The Discussion of Dependent Arising is finished. ^5-451

##### (55) 3. Discussion of the Truths ^5-14-3-1-0

Are the four truths unconditioned? Yes. Are there four protections, four shelters, four refuges, four final resorts, four imperishables, four deathless states, four nibbānas (final releases)? That should not be said… Are there four nibbānas? Yes. Is there among the four nibbānas any difference of high and low, of inferior and superior, of better and worse, or any boundary, division, dividing line or gap? That should not be said…
Is the truth of suffering unconditioned? Yes. Is suffering unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of suffering unconditioned? Yes. Are bodily suffering, mental suffering, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief and despair unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of the origin unconditioned? Yes. Is the origin unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of the origin unconditioned? Yes. Are craving for sensual pleasures, craving for existence and craving for non-existence unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of the path unconditioned? Yes. Is the path unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of the path unconditioned? Yes. Is right view… right concentration unconditioned? That should not be said…
Is suffering conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of suffering conditioned? That should not be said… Are bodily suffering, mental suffering, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief and despair conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of suffering conditioned? That should not be said… Is the origin conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the origin conditioned? That should not be said… Are craving for sensual pleasures, craving for existence and craving for non-existence conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the origin conditioned? That should not be said… Is the path conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the path conditioned? That should not be said… Is right view… right concentration conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the path conditioned? That should not be said… ^5-452

Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, and is cessation unconditioned? Yes. Is the truth of suffering unconditioned, and is suffering unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, and is cessation unconditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the origin unconditioned, and is the origin unconditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, and is cessation unconditioned? Yes. Is the truth of the path unconditioned, and is the path unconditioned? That should not be said…
Is the truth of suffering unconditioned, while suffering is conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, while cessation is conditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of the origin unconditioned, while the origin is conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, while cessation is conditioned? That should not be said… Is the truth of the path unconditioned, while the path is conditioned? Yes. Is the truth of cessation unconditioned, while cessation is conditioned? That should not be said… ^5-453

Should it not be said that "the four truths are unconditioned"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, there are these four things that are real, not unreal, not otherwise. Which four? ‘This is suffering,’ monks — this is real, this is not unreal, this is not otherwise… ‘This is the origin of suffering’… ‘This is the cessation of suffering’… ‘This is the way leading to the cessation of suffering’ — this is real, this is not unreal, this is not otherwise. These, monks, are the four things that are real, not unreal, not otherwise" [S. Ni. 5.1090]? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case the four truths are unconditioned.
The Discussion of the Truths is finished. ^5-454

Is the base of the infinity of space unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna (final release), the protection, the shelter, the refuge, the final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said… Is the base of the infinity of space unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said… Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Are there two protections… or any gap? That should not be said…
Is the base of the infinity of space unconditioned? Yes. Is the base of the infinity of space an existence, a destination, an abode of beings, a round of rebirth, a mode of birth, a station of consciousness, an acquisition of individual existence? Yes. Is the unconditioned an existence, a destination, an abode of beings, a round of rebirth, a mode of birth, a station of consciousness, an acquisition of individual existence? That should not be said…
Is there action that leads to rebirth in the base of the infinity of space? Yes. Is there action that leads to rebirth in the unconditioned? That should not be said… In the base of the infinity of space, are there feeling, perception, mental formations and consciousness? Yes. In the unconditioned, are there feeling, perception, mental formations and consciousness? That should not be said… Is the base of the infinity of space a four-aggregate existence? Yes. Is the unconditioned a four-aggregate existence? That should not be said… ^5-455

Should it not be said that "the four formless states are unconditioned"? Yes. But hasn't the Blessed One declared the four formless states to be imperturbable? Yes. If the Blessed One has declared the four formless states to be imperturbable, then surely you must also say: "The four formless states are unconditioned."

##### (58) 6. Discussion of Space ^5-14-6-1-0

Is space unconditioned? Yes. Is it nibbāna (final release), the protection, the shelter, the refuge, the final resort, the imperishable, the deathless? That should not be said… Is space unconditioned, and is nibbāna unconditioned? Yes. Are there two unconditioned things? That should not be said… Are there two unconditioned things? Yes. Is space unconditioned? Yes. Are there some who make what is not space into space? Yes. Are there some who make the conditioned into the unconditioned? That should not be said… Are there some who make space into what is not space? Yes. Are there some who make the unconditioned into the conditioned? That should not be said…
In space do birds go, do the moon and sun go, do the stars go, do people perform feats of psychic power, move their arms, move their hands, throw clods, throw sticks, project psychic power [variant: sattiṃ (?)], shoot arrows? That should not be said… ^5-460

Do people build houses and build storerooms by enclosing space? Yes. Do people build houses and build storerooms by enclosing the unconditioned? That should not be said…
When a well is being dug, does what is not space become space? Yes. Does the conditioned become unconditioned? That should not be said…
When an empty well is being filled, when an empty storeroom is being filled, when an empty pot is being filled, does space disappear? Yes. Does the unconditioned disappear? That should not be said… ^5-461

Should it not be said that "space is unconditioned"? Yes. Is space conditioned? That should not be said… In that case space is unconditioned.

##### (59) 7. Discussion of "Space Is Visible" ^5-14-7-1-0

Is space visible? Yes. Is it a visible form, the visible-form base, the visible-form element, blue, yellow, red, white, cognizable by the eye; does it strike the eye, does it come within range of the eye? That should not be said…
Is space visible? Yes. Does eye-consciousness arise dependent on the eye and on space? That should not be said…
Does eye-consciousness arise dependent on the eye and on space? Yes. "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on space" — is there really [variant: atthi (?)] such a discourse? There is not. "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on visible forms" [M. Ni. 1.400; 3.421; S. Ni. 4.60] — is there really such a discourse? Yes. If there really is the discourse "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on visible forms," then surely you must not say: "Eye-consciousness arises dependent on the eye and on space." ^5-463

Should it not be said that "space is visible"? Yes. But doesn't one see the gap between two trees, the gap between two posts, a keyhole, a window opening? Yes. If one sees the gap between two trees, the gap between two posts, a keyhole, a window opening, then surely you must also say: "Space is visible."
The Discussion of "Space Is Visible" is finished. ^5-464
