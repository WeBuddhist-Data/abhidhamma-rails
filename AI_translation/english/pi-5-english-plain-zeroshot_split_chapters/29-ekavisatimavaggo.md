### The Twenty-First Chapter ^5-29-0

#### (200) 1. Discussion of the Teaching ^5-29-1-0

Has the teaching been made new? Yes. Have the foundations of mindfulness been made new? That should not be said… Has the teaching been made new? Yes. Have the right strivings… the bases of psychic power… the faculties… the powers… the factors of awakening been made new? That should not be said… Has what was earlier unwholesome later been made wholesome? That should not be said… Has what was earlier accompanied by taints… subject to the fetters, subject to the knots, subject to the floods, subject to the bonds, subject to the hindrances, seized upon, subject to clinging… subject to defilement later been made not subject to defilement? That should not be said…
Is there anyone who makes the teaching of the Tathāgata (the Buddha) new? Yes. Is there anyone who makes the foundations of mindfulness new? That should not be said… Is there anyone who makes the right strivings… the bases of psychic power… the faculties… the powers… the factors of awakening new? That should not be said… Is there anyone who makes what was earlier unwholesome later wholesome? That should not be said… Is there anyone who makes what was earlier accompanied by taints… subject to defilement later not subject to defilement? That should not be said…
Is it possible to make the teaching of the Tathāgata new again? Yes. Is it possible to make the foundations of mindfulness new again? That should not be said… Is it possible to make the right strivings… the bases of psychic power… the faculties… the powers… the factors of awakening new again? That should not be said… Is it possible to make what was earlier unwholesome later wholesome? That should not be said… Is it possible to make what was earlier accompanied by taints… subject to defilement later not subject to defilement? That should not be said…
The Discussion of the Teaching is finished. ^5-878

##### (201) 2. Discussion of Not Being Separated ^5-29-2-1-0

Is an ordinary person not separated from the states of the three realms? Yes. Is an ordinary person not separated from the contacts… the feelings… perceptions… intentions… states of mind… kinds of faith… energy… mindfulness… concentration… the wisdom of the three realms? That should not be said…
Is an ordinary person not separated from the actions of the three realms? Yes. At the moment an ordinary person gives a robe, do they at that moment enter and dwell in the first meditative absorption (jhāna)… enter and dwell in the base of the infinity of space? That should not be said… At the moment an ordinary person gives almsfood… gives lodgings… gives medicines and requisites for the sick, do they at that moment enter and dwell in the fourth meditative absorption… enter and dwell in the base of neither perception nor non-perception? That should not be said… ^5-879

Should it not be said that "an ordinary person is not separated from the actions of the three realms"? Yes. Has an ordinary person fully understood the action that leads to rebirth in the realm of form and in the formless realm? That should not be said. In that case an ordinary person is not separated from the actions of the three realms…
The Discussion of Not Being Separated is finished. ^5-880

##### (202) 3. Discussion of the Fetters ^5-29-3-1-0

Is there attainment of arahantship (the state of a worthy one) without abandoning some fetter? Yes. Is there attainment of arahantship without abandoning identity view… without abandoning doubt… without abandoning clinging to rules and rituals… without abandoning greed… without abandoning hatred… without abandoning delusion… without abandoning lack of moral fear? That should not be said…
Is there attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter? Yes. Is a worthy one (arahant) one who has greed, hatred, delusion, conceit, contempt, rivalry, despair and defilements? That should not be said… But isn't a worthy one free from greed, free from hatred, free from delusion, free from conceit, free from contempt, free from rivalry, free from despair, free from defilements? Yes. If a worthy one is free from greed… free from defilements, then surely you must not say: "There is attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter." ^5-881

Should it not be said that "there is attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter"? Yes. Does a worthy one know the whole domain of a Buddha? That should not be said. In that case there is attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter.
The Discussion of the Fetters is finished. ^5-882

##### (203) 4. Discussion of Psychic Power ^5-29-4-1-0

Do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing (making things happen as they wish)? Yes. "May the trees always be in leaf" — do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing this? That should not be said… May the trees always be in flower… may the trees always be in fruit… may it always be moonlight… may it always be safe… may there always be plenty of food… "may there always be well-being [variant: suvuṭṭhikaṃ (Syā.)]" — do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing this? That should not be said…
Do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing? Yes. "May contact that has arisen not cease" — do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing this? That should not be said…
May feeling that has arisen… perception… intention… mind… faith… energy… mindfulness… concentration… wisdom not cease — do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing this? That should not be said…
Do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing? Yes. "May material form be permanent" — do they have the psychic power of wishing this… feeling… perception… mental formations… "may consciousness be permanent" — do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing this? That should not be said…
Do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing? Yes. "May beings who are subject to birth not be born" — do they have… "may beings who are subject to ageing not age"… "may beings who are subject to sickness not fall sick"… "may beings who are subject to death not die" — do Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing this? That should not be said… ^5-883

Should it not be said that "Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing"? Yes. But didn't the venerable Pilindavaccha resolve that the palace of Seniya Bimbisāra, king of Magadha, was "gold", and wasn't it indeed gold [Pārā. 621; Mahāva. 271]? Yes. If the venerable Pilindavaccha resolved that the palace of Seniya Bimbisāra, king of Magadha, was gold, and it was indeed gold, then surely you must also say: "Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing."
The Discussion of Psychic Power is finished. ^5-884

##### (205) 6. Discussion of All Directions ^5-29-6-1-0

Do Buddhas live in all directions? Yes. Does a Buddha live in the eastern direction? That should not be said… Does a Buddha live in the eastern direction? Yes. What is that Blessed One's name, what is his birth, what is his clan, what are the names of that Blessed One's mother and father, what are the names of that Blessed One's pair of chief disciples, what is the name of that Blessed One's attendant, what kind of robe does he wear, what kind of bowl does he carry, in which village or town or city or kingdom or country is he? That should not be said…
Does a Buddha live in the southern direction… in the western direction… in the northern direction… in the direction below? That should not be said… Does a Buddha live in the direction below? Yes. What is that Blessed One's name… or country is he? That should not be said…
Does a Buddha live in the direction above? That should not be said… Does a Buddha live in the direction above? Yes. Does he live in the realm of the gods of the Four Great Kings… among the Thirty-three gods… among the Yāma gods… among the Tusita gods… among the gods who delight in creating… among the gods who wield power over the creations of others… in the Brahmā world? That should not be said…
The Discussion of All Directions is finished. ^5-886

##### (206) 7. Discussion of States ^5-29-7-1-0

Are all states fixed? Yes. Fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… Is there no group that is not fixed? That should not be said… But isn't there a group that is not fixed? Yes. If there is a group that is not fixed, then surely you must not say: "All states are fixed."
Are all states fixed? Yes. But hasn't the Blessed One declared three groups — the group fixed in wrongness, the group fixed in rightness, and the group that is not fixed [Dha. Sa., Triad Matrix 15]? Yes. If the Blessed One has declared three groups — the group fixed in wrongness, the group fixed in rightness, and the group that is not fixed — then surely you must not say: "All states are fixed."
Is material form fixed in the sense of being material form? Yes. Is it fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… Is feeling… perception… are mental formations… is consciousness fixed in the sense of being consciousness? Yes. Is it fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… ^5-887

Should it not be said that material form is fixed in the sense of being material form… feeling… perception… mental formations… consciousness is fixed in the sense of being consciousness? Yes. Does material form become feeling… become perception… become mental formations… become consciousness; does feeling… perception… do mental formations… does consciousness become material form… become feeling… become perception… become mental formations? That should not be said. In that case material form is fixed in the sense of being material form, feeling… perception… mental formations… consciousness is fixed in the sense of being consciousness.
The Discussion of States is finished. ^5-888

##### (207) 8. Discussion of Action ^5-29-8-1-0

Are all actions fixed? Yes. Fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… Is there no group that is not fixed? That should not be said… But isn't there a group that is not fixed? Yes. If there is a group that is not fixed, then surely you must not say: "All actions are fixed."
Are all actions fixed? Yes. But hasn't the Blessed One declared three groups — the group fixed in wrongness, the group fixed in rightness, and the group that is not fixed? Yes. If the Blessed One has declared three groups — the group fixed in wrongness, the group fixed in rightness, and the group that is not fixed — then surely you must not say: "All actions are fixed." ^5-889

Is action to be experienced in this very life fixed in the sense of being experienced in this very life? Yes. Is it fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… Is action to be experienced in the next rebirth… is action to be experienced on some later occasion fixed in the sense of being experienced on some later occasion? Yes. Is it fixed in wrongness? That should not be said… Fixed in rightness? That should not be said… ^5-890

Should it not be said that action to be experienced in this very life is fixed in the sense of being experienced in this very life, action to be experienced in the next rebirth… action to be experienced on some later occasion is fixed in the sense of being experienced on some later occasion? Yes. Does action to be experienced in this very life become action to be experienced in the next rebirth, become action to be experienced on some later occasion… does action to be experienced in the next rebirth become action to be experienced in this very life, become action to be experienced on some later occasion… does action to be experienced on some later occasion become action to be experienced in this very life, become action to be experienced in the next rebirth? That should not be said. In that case action to be experienced in this very life is fixed in the sense of being experienced in this very life, action to be experienced in the next rebirth… action to be experienced on some later occasion is fixed in the sense of being experienced on some later occasion.
The Discussion of Action is finished.
The Twenty-First Chapter.
Here is its summary:
The teaching has been made new; there is someone who makes the teaching of the Tathāgata new; it is possible to make the teaching of the Tathāgata new again; an ordinary person is not separated from the states of the three realms; there is attainment of arahantship without abandoning some fetter; Buddhas or disciples have the psychic power of wishing; Buddhas are inferior or superior to other Buddhas; Buddhas live in all directions; all states are fixed; all actions are fixed. ^5-891
