#### Proof from the Discourses ^5-6-2-0

Should it not be said that "past and future things are aggregates, elements and sense bases, and yet these do not exist"? Yes. But did the Blessed One not say: "Monks, there are these three pathways of language, pathways of naming, pathways of designation… wise…"? Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "past and future things are aggregates, elements and sense bases, and yet these do not exist."

Is there really such a discourse? Yes. In that case it should not be said that "past and future things are aggregates, elements and sense bases, and yet these do not exist."
