Katamaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ āpodhātu? Yaṃ āpo āpogataṃ sineho sinehagataṃ bandhanattaṃ rūpassa – idaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ āpodhātu. ^1-726

Katamaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ na āpodhātu? Cakkhāyatanaṃ…pe… kabaḷīkāro āhāro – idaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ na āpodhātu. ^1-727

Katamaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ kabaḷīkāro āhāro? Odano kummāso sattu maccho maṃsaṃ khīraṃ dadhi sappi navanītaṃ telaṃ madhu phāṇitaṃ, yaṃ vā panaññampi atthi rūpaṃ yamhi yamhi janapade tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ mukhāsiyaṃ dantavikhādanaṃ galajjhoharaṇīyaṃ kucchivitthambhanaṃ, yāya ojāya sattā yāpenti – idaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ kabaḷīkāro āhāro. ^1-742

Katamaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ na kabaḷīkāro āhāro? Cakkhāyatanaṃ…pe… rūpassa aniccatā – idaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ na kabaḷīkāro āhāro.
