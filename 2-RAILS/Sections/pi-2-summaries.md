## Khandhavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-1|↑↑↑]]

Khandhavibhaṅgo vibhaṅgappakaraṇassa ādibhūto. Bhagavā catusaccadaso nātho catudhā dhammasaṅgaṇiṃ pakāsayitvā tasseva samanantaraṃ aṭṭhārasannaṃ khandhādivibhaṅgānaṃ vasena vibhaṅgaṃ desesi. Tattha 'khandhā'ti paricchinnadhammanidassanaṃ; khandhasaddo ca rāsimhi, guṇe, paṇṇattiyaṃ, ruḷhiyanti sambahulesu ṭhānesu dissati. Yathā mahāsamudde udakassa pamāṇaṃ gahetuṃ na sukaraṃ, atha kho 'mahāudakakkhandho'tveva saṅkhyaṃ gacchati, evaṃ idha rāsito khandho adhippeto — piṇḍaṭṭho pūgaṭṭho ghaṭaṭṭho rāsaṭṭho. Tasmā 'rāsilakkhaṇā khandhā'ti veditabbā; koṭṭhāsalakkhaṇātipi vattuṃ vaṭṭati. Iti rūpakkhandhoti rūparāsi rūpakoṭṭhāso, vedanākkhandhoti vedanārāsi vedanākoṭṭhāsoti iminā nayena sesānampi attho veditabbo. Pañcāti gaṇanaparicchedo, tena na tato heṭṭhā na uddhanti dasseti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-1]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-5]]

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ

[[#^toc-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīyaṃ nāma khandhānaṃ suttantanayena vibhajanaṃ. Ettāvatā sammāsambuddho ekādasasu okāsesu — atītānāgatapaccuppanna-ajjhattabahiddhā-oḷārikasukhuma-hīnapaṇīta-dūresantikavasena — vibhattaṃ rūpavedanāsaññāsaṅkhāraviññāṇarāsiṃ paripiṇḍetvā pañca khandhe dassesi. Apicettha sabbampi catusamuṭṭhānikaṃ rūpaṃ rūpakkhandho; ekūnanavuticittehi sahajātā vedanā vedanākkhandho, saññā saññākkhandho, phassādayo dhammā saṅkhārakkhandho, ekūnanavuti cittāni viññāṇakkhandhoti evampi pañcasu khandhesu dhammaparicchedo veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-6]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-7]]

#### Rūpakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandhaniddese 'tatthā'ti tesu pañcasu khandhesu; 'katamo'ti kathetukamyatāpucchā; 'rūpakkhandho'ti pucchitadhammanidassanaṃ. 'Yaṃ kiñcī'ti anavasesapariyādānaṃ, 'rūpa'nti atippasaṅganiyamanaṃ — evaṃ padadvayenāpi rūpassa anavaseso pariggaho kato hoti. Kenaṭṭhena rūpanti? Ruppanaṭṭhena rūpaṃ — sītuṇhādivasena vikāraṃ āpajjanato. Tattha atītaṃ rūpaṃ niruddhaṃ vigataṃ vipariṇataṃ atthaṅgataṃ; anāgataṃ ajātaṃ abhūtaṃ anuppannaṃ; paccuppannaṃ jātaṃ bhūtaṃ uppannaṃ samuṭṭhitaṃ. Sabbattha cattāro mahābhūtā catunnañca mahābhūtānaṃ upādāyarūpanti rūpassa paricchedo veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-8]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-9]]

#### Vedanākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Vedanākkhandhaniddese 'yā kāci vedanā'ti catubhūmikavedanaṃ pariyādiyati; sukhādukkhaadukkhamasukhāti atītādivasena niddiṭṭhavedanaṃ sabhāvato dassetuṃ vuttaṃ. Tattha sukhā vedanā atthi kāyikā atthi cetasikā; tathā dukkhā. Sabbāpi kāyikā kāmāvacarā, tathā cetasikā dukkhā; cetasikā sukhā tebhūmikā; adukkhamasukhā catubhūmikā. Tassā atītādibhāvo santativasena ca khaṇādivasena ca veditabbo — ekavīthiekajavanaekasamāpattipariyāpannā paccuppannā, tato pubbe atītā, pacchā anāgatā. Oḷārikasukhumaniddese akusalā sadarathaṭṭhena dukkhavipākaṭṭhena ca oḷārikā, kusalā niddarathaṭṭhena sukhavipākaṭṭhena ca sukhumā, abyākatā nirussāhaṭṭhena avipākaṭṭhena ca sukhumā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-42]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-43]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-44]]

#### Saññākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhaniddese 'yā kāci saññā'ti catubhūmikasaññaṃ pariyādiyati; cakkhusamphassajā saññātiādīni atītādivasena niddiṭṭhasaññaṃ sabhāvato dassetuṃ vuttāni. Tattha cakkhusamphassato cakkhusamphassasmiṃ vā jātā cakkhusamphassajā nāma, sesāsupi eseva nayo. Purimā pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāva; manosamphassajā hadayavatthukāpi avatthukāpi. Oḷārikadukaniddese 'paṭighasamphassajā'ti sappaṭighe cakkhupasādādayo vatthuṃ katvā sappaṭighe rūpādayo ārabbha uppanno phasso paṭighasamphasso nāma, tato tasmiṃ vā jātā paṭighasamphassajā nāma. Cakkhuviññāṇañhi 'mano' nāma; tena sahajāto phasso manosamphasso, tasmā vā jātāti manosamphassajā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-59]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-60]]

#### Saṅkhārakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese 'ye keci saṅkhārā'ti catubhūmikasaṅkhāre pariyādiyati. 'Cetanā'ti heṭṭhimakoṭiyā padhānasaṅkhāravasena vuttaṃ — heṭṭhimakoṭiyā hi antamaso cakkhuviññāṇena saddhiṃ pāḷiyaṃ āgatā cattāro saṅkhārā uppajjanti, tesu cetanā padhānā āyūhanaṭṭhena pākaṭattā, tasmā ayameva gahitā; taṃsampayuttasaṅkhārā pana tāya gahitāya gahitāva honti. Idhāpi purimā pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāva, manosamphassajā hadayavatthukāpi avatthukāpi; sabbā catubhūmikacetanā. Sesaṃ vedanākkhandhasadisameva.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-64]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-65]]

#### Viññāṇakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Viññāṇakkhandhaniddese 'yaṃ kiñci viññāṇa'nti catubhūmakaviññāṇaṃ pariyādiyati; cakkhuviññāṇantiādīni atītādivasena niddiṭṭhaviññāṇaṃ sabhāvato dassetuṃ vuttāni. Tattha cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāneva; manoviññāṇaṃ hadayavatthukampi avatthukampi. Sabbaṃ catubhūmakaviññāṇaṃ. Sesaṃ vedanākkhandhasadisamevāti. Ayaṃ viññāṇakkhandhaniddeso.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-66]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-67]]

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ nāma tesaṃyeva khandhānaṃ abhidhammanayena vibhajanaṃ. Satthā imasmiṃ abhidhammabhājanīye khandhaṃ bhājento tike gahetvā dukesu pakkhipi, duke gahetvā tikesu pakkhipi, tike ca duke ca ubhatovaḍḍhananīhārena āhari, avasāne sattavidhādivasena bahuvidhena dassesi. Iti dukamūlako, tikamūlako, ubhatovaḍḍhitako, bahuvidhavāroti cattāro mahāvārā. Kasmā evaṃ bahudhā? Puggalajjhāsayena ceva desanāvilāsena ca — ye devaputtā tike ādāya dukesu pakkhipitvā kathiyamānaṃ paṭivijjhituṃ sakkonti, tesaṃ sappāyavasena tathā desesi; sammāsambuddho ca attano mahāvisayatāya yathā yathā icchati tathā tathā desetuṃ sakkoti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-120]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-121]]

#### Rūpakkhandho (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye rūpakkhandhaniddeso heṭṭhā dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ rūpakaṇḍe vitthāritanayeneva veditabbo — tattha ekavidhādivasena rūpassa vibhāgo vitthārito, tasmā idha na puna vitthārito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-116]]

#### Vedanākkhandho (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Vedanākkhandhaniddese 'ekavidhenā'ti ekakoṭṭhāsena; 'phassasampayutto'ti phassena sampayutto — sabbāpi catubhūmikavedanā. Sahetukaduke sahetukā catubhūmikavedanā, ahetukā kāmāvacarāva. Ayaṃ vedanākkhandho ekavidhena phassasampayuttato dassito, duvidhena sahetukāhetukato, tividhena jātito, catubbidhena bhūmantarato, pañcavidhena indriyato, chabbidhena vatthuto. Tattha sukhindriyadukkhindriyāni kāyappasādavatthukāni kāmāvacarāneva; somanassindriyaṃ chaṭṭhavatthukaṃ vā avatthukaṃ vā tebhūmakaṃ; domanassindriyaṃ chaṭṭhavatthukaṃ kāmāvacaraṃ; upekkhindriyaṃ catuppasādavatthukaṃ chaṭṭhavatthukaṃ avatthukañca catubhūmakaṃ. Sattavidhena manosamphassajā bhedato, aṭṭhavidhena kāyasamphassajā bhedato, navavidhadasavidhesu manoviññāṇadhātusamphassajā kusalādivasena tidhā bhinnā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-117]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-118]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-119]]

Vedanākkhandhaniddesassa pariyosāne cha vedanā cakkhusamphassajātiādivasena sampayuttapaccayavasena vuttā. Catubhūmikavedanā ca cakkhudvārādīsu tīhi ākārehi labbhati — upanissayavasena, samatikkamavasena, bhāvanāvasena. Manodvāre pana tīhipi ākārehi labbhanti: ekacco hi jātijarābyādhimaraṇaṃ bhayato disvā 'na kho sakkā nipajjitvā niddāyantena jātiādīhi muccituṃ, āditova vīriyaṃ kātuṃ sīlaṃ sodhetuṃ vaṭṭatī'ti cintetvā paṭipajjitvā arahattaṃ pāpuṇāti; tattha parikammavedanā kāmāvacarā, aṭṭhasamāpattivedanā rūpāvacarārūpāvacarā, maggaphalavedanā lokuttarāti. Aparo dhammārammaṇaṃ 'kiṃ nissita'nti pariggaṇhanto cattāri mahābhūtāni upādārūpañca 'rūpa'nti, tadārammaṇe dhamme 'arūpa'nti pariggaṇhitvā, sappaccayaṃ nāmarūpaṃ pariggaṇhitvā tīṇi lakkhaṇāni āropetvā vipassanāpaṭipāṭiyā saṅkhāre sammasitvā arahattaṃ pāpuṇāti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-137]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-139]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-140]]

#### Saññā-Saṅkhāra-Viññāṇakkhandhā (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhādayopi vedanākkhandhe vuttena upāyena veditabbā. Kevalañhi saññākkhandhaniddese tikesu vedanāttikapītittikāpi labbhanti, dukesu ca sukhasahagatadukādayopi. Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese phassassāpi saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannattā 'phassasampayutto'ti avatvā 'cittasampayutto'ti vuttaṃ; dukesu cettha hetudukādayopi labbhanti, tikā saññākkhandhasadisā eva. Viññāṇakkhandhaniddese cakkhusamphassajādibhāvaṃ avatvā 'cakkhuviññāṇa'ntiādi vuttaṃ — na hi sakkā viññāṇaṃ 'manosamphassaja'nti niddisituṃ; sesaṃ saññākkhandhe vuttasadisameva. Imesaṃ tiṇṇampi khandhānaṃ niddeseyeva vedanākkhandhaniddesato atirekatikadukā laddhā, tesaṃ vasena vārappabhedo veditabbo.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchane pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ "katikusalā? Katiakusalā? Katiabyākatā"tiādinā nayena yaṃ labbhati yañca na labbhati taṃ sabbaṃ pucchitvā, vissajjane "rūpakkhandho abyākato"tiādinā nayena yaṃ labbhati tadeva uddhaṭaṃ. Ārammaṇattikesu cattāro khandhā pañcapaṇṇāsa kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha rajjantassa dussantassa muyhantassa saṃvarantassa sammasantassa ca parittārammaṇā honti, sattavīsati rūpārūpāvacaradhamme ārabbha mahaggatārammaṇā, maggaphalanibbānāni paccavekkhantassa appamāṇārammaṇā, paññattiṃ paccavekkhaṇakāle navattabbārammaṇā. Tathā attano khandhadhātuāyatanāni ārabbha ajjhattārammaṇā, paresaṃ ārabbha bahiddhārammaṇā. ## Āyatanavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-2|↑↑↑]]

Khandhavibhaṅgassa anantaro dutiyo āyatanavibhaṅgo, yattha bhagavā suttantabhājanīyaṃ tāva dassento dvādasāyatanāni cakkhāyatanaṃ rūpāyatanantiādimāha. Tattha avisesato āyatanato, āyānaṃ tananato, āyatassa ca nayanato āyatananti veditabbaṃ; apica nivāsaṭṭhānaṭṭhena, ākaraṭṭhena, samosaraṇaṭṭhānaṭṭhena, sañjātidesaṭṭhena, kāraṇaṭṭhena cāti imehi kāraṇehi ete dhammā 'āyatanaṃ āyatana'nti vuccanti — cakkhādīsu hi te te cittacetasikā dhammā nivasanti tadāyattavuttitāya, tattha ākiṇṇā taṃnissitattā tadārammaṇattā ca, tattheva samosaranti vatthudvārārammaṇavasena, tattheva uppajjanti, tesañca abhāve na hontīti. Tasmā cakkhu ca taṃ āyatanañcāti cakkhāyatanaṃ…pe… dhammā ca te āyatanañcāti dhammāyatananti veditabbaṃ. Cakkhādayopi ca kho dhammā eva; evaṃ sati kasmā dhammāyatanamicceva avatvā dvādasa vuttānīti ce? Channaṃ viññāṇakāyānaṃ uppattidvārārammaṇavavatthānato — cakkhuviññāṇavīthipariyāpannassa hi viññāṇakāyassa cakkhāyatanameva uppattidvāraṃ, rūpāyatanameva ārammaṇaṃ, tathā itarāni itaresaṃ; chaṭṭhassa pana bhavaṅgamanasaṅkhāto manāyatanekadesova uppattidvāraṃ, asādhāraṇañca dhammāyatanaṃ ārammaṇanti.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Āyatana)

[[#^toc-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Imasmiṃ āyatanānaṃ suttantabhājanīye pāḷimuttakena nayena atthalakkhaṇatāvatvakamasaṅkhepavitthāradaṭṭhabbavasena vinicchayo viññātabbo. Tato tesaṃ vipassitabbākāraṃ dassetuṃ cakkhuṃ aniccantiādi āraddhaṃ — cakkhu hutvā abhāvaṭṭhena aniccaṃ, paṭipīḷanaṭṭhena dukkhaṃ, avasavattanaṭṭhena anattā, vibhavagatikato pakatibhāvavijahanato ca vipariṇāmadhammaṃ; rūpā aniccātiādīsupi eseva nayo. Ettha ca tathāgatena dvādasannaṃ āyatanānaṃ anattalakkhaṇaṃ dassitaṃ. Sammāsambuddho hi anattalakkhaṇaṃ dassento aniccena vā dasseti, dukkhena vā, aniccadukkhehi vā; idha panassa aniccadukkhehi dassitattā 'aniccaṃ dukkhaṃ anattā'ti vuttaṃ. Kasmā? Aniccadukkhānaṃ pākaṭattā, anattalakkhaṇassa ca apākaṭattā duppaṭivijjhattā duddīpanattā — aniccadukkhalakkhaṇāni hi tathāgatānaṃ uppādā vā anuppādā vā paññāyanti, anattalakkhaṇaṃ pana buddhuppādeyeva paññāyati. Saṅkhepato panettha dasāyatanāni kāmāvacarāni, dve tebhūmakāni; sabbesu ca sammasanacārova kathitoti veditabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Āyatana)

[[#^toc-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye yathā heṭṭhā suttantabhājanīye vipassakānaṃ upakāratthāya 'cakkhāyatanaṃ rūpāyatana'nti yugalato āyatanāni vuttāni, tathā avatvā ajjhattikabāhirānaṃ sabbākārato sabhāvadassanatthaṃ 'cakkhāyatanaṃ sotāyatana'nti evaṃ ajjhattikabāhiravavatthānanayena vuttāni; tesaṃ niddesavāre 'tattha katamaṃ cakkhāyatana'ntiādīni heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva veditabbāni. ### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Āyatana)

[[#^toc-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Idhāpi pañhāpucchake yaṃ labbhati yañca na labbhati, taṃ sabbaṃ pucchitvā labbhamānavaseneva vissajjanaṃ vuttaṃ; na kevalañca idha, sabbesupi pañhāpucchakesu eseva nayo. Idha pana dasannaṃ āyatanānaṃ rūpabhāvena abyākatatā veditabbā; dvinnaṃ āyatanānaṃ pana khandhavibhaṅge catunnaṃ khandhānaṃ viya kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Kevalañhi cattāro khandhā sappaccayāva saṅkhatāva, dhammāyatanaṃ pana 'siyā appaccayaṃ, siyā asaṅkhata'nti āgataṃ. Ārammaṇattikesu ca anārammaṇaṃ sukhumarūpasaṅkhātaṃ dhammāyatanaṃ navattabbakoṭṭhāsaṃ bhajati, tañca kho anārammaṇattā, na parittādibhāvena navattabbadhammārammaṇattāti ayamettha viseso. Sesaṃ tādisameva — dvāyatanāpi hi cattāro khandhā viya pañcapaṇṇāsa kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha rajjantassa dussantassa muyhantassa saṃvarantassa sammasantassa paccavekkhantassa ca parittārammaṇāti sabbaṃ khandhesu vuttasadisameva.

## Dhātuvibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-3|↑↑↑]]

Āyatanavibhaṅgassa anantaro tatiyo dhātuvibhaṅgo. Tattha avisesena vidahati, dhīyate, vidhānaṃ, vidhīyate etāya, ettha vā dhīyatīti dhātu. Lokiyā hi dhātuyo kāraṇabhāvena vavatthitā hutvā suvaṇṇarajatādidhātuyo viya suvaṇṇarajatādiṃ anekappakāraṃ saṃsāradukkhaṃ vidahanti; bhārahārehi ca bhāro viya sattehi dhīyante dhārīyanti. Apica yathā titthiyānaṃ attā nāma sabhāvato natthi, na evametā; etā pana attano sabhāvaṃ dhārentīti dhātuyo. Yathā ca loke vicittā haritālamanosilādayo silāvayavā dhātuyoti vuccanti, evametāpi ñāṇañeyyāvayavattā vicittā dhātuyo viya dhātuyo; yathā ca sarīrasaṅkhātassa samudāyassa avayavabhūtesu rasasoṇitādīsu aññamaññaṃ visabhāgalakkhaṇaparicchinnesu dhātusamaññā, evaṃ pañcakkhandhasaṅkhātassa attabhāvassa avayavesu cakkhādīsu dhātusamaññā veditabbā. Apica dhātūti nijjīvamattassetaṃ adhivacanaṃ; tathā hi bhagavā 'cha dhāturo ayaṃ, bhikkhu, puriso'tiādīsu jīvasaññāsamūhanatthaṃ dhātudesanaṃ akāsi — santi hi sattā vijānanasabhāve viññāṇe jīvasaññino, tesaṃ tassā anekattaṃ aniccatañca pakāsetvā dīgharattānusayitaṃ jīvasaññaṃ samūhanitukāmena, veneyyajjhāsayavasena ca bhagavatā dhātuyo pakāsitā.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Dhātu)

[[#^toc-3-1|↑↑↑]]

Imasmiṃ dhātūnaṃ suttantabhājanīye bhagavā sabbā dhātuyo chahi chahi dhātūhi saṅkhipitvā tīhi chakkehi dassento cha dhātuyotiādimāha. Tattha chāti gaṇanaparicchedo, dhātuyoti paricchinnadhammanidassanaṃ; dhātvaṭṭho nāma sabhāvaṭṭho, sabhāvaṭṭho nāma suññataṭṭho, suññataṭṭho nāma nissattaṭṭho. Iti pathavīdhātūti patiṭṭhānadhātu, āpodhātūti ābandhanadhātu, tejodhātūti paripācanadhātu, vāyodhātūti vitthambhanadhātu, ākāsadhātūti asamphuṭṭhadhātu, viññāṇadhātūti vijānanadhātu. Iminā kiṃ dassitaṃ hoti? Dhātumanasikāro. Imasmiṃ pana dhātumanasikāre kammaṃ katvā vipassanaṃ paṭṭhapetvā uttamatthaṃ arahattaṃ pāpuṇitukāmena catupārisuddhisīlaṃ sodhetabbaṃ, dasa pubbapalibodhā chinditabbā, ācariyassa santike sattavidhena uggahakosallena dasavidhena ca manasikārakosallena kammaṭṭhānaṃ uggaṇhitabbaṃ, tato pañcaṅgasamannāgate senāsane vivittokāsagatena vaṇṇasaṇṭhānadisokāsaparicchedavasena kesādīsu ekeko koṭṭhāso 'acetano abyākato suñño nissatto'ti manasikātabbo. Tiṇṇaṃ pana chakkānaṃ vasena aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo pariggahitāva honti; iti imasmiṃ suttantabhājanīye soḷasa dhātuyo kāmāvacarā, dve tebhūmikāti evamettha sammasanacārova kathitoti veditabbo.

## Saccavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-4|↑↑↑]]

Dhātuvibhaṅgassa anantaro catuttho saccavibhaṅgo. Tattha cattārīti gaṇanaparicchedo, ariyasaccānīti paricchinnadhammanidassanaṃ. Kasmā ariyasaccānīti vuccanti? Yasmā etāni buddhādayo ariyā paṭivijjhanti; apica ariyassa saccānītipi ariyasaccāni, sadevake hi loke tathāgato ariyo; atha vā etesaṃ abhisambuddhattā ariyabhāvasiddhitopi ariyasaccāni; apica kho ariyāni tathāni avitathāni avisaṃvādakāni saccānītipi ariyasaccāni. Ko cettha saccaṭṭho? Yo paññācakkhunā upaparikkhamānānaṃ māyāva viparītako, marīcīva visaṃvādako, titthiyānaṃ attāva anupalabbhasabhāvo ca na hoti; atha kho bādhanapabhavasantiniyyānappakārena tacchāviparītabhūtabhāvena ariyañāṇassa gocaro hotiyeva — esa aggilakkhaṇaṃ viya, lokapakati viya ca tacchāviparītabhūtabhāvo saccaṭṭhoti veditabbo.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Sacca)

[[#^toc-4-1|↑↑↑]]

Saccānaṃ suttantabhājanīye 'dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhasamudayaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasacca'nti uddesavāro. Tattha vibhāgato, nibbacanalakkhaṇādippabhedato, atthatthuddhārato, anūnādhikato, kamato, saccañāṇakiccato, antogadhānaṃ pabhedato, upamāto, catukkato, suññatekavidhādīhi, sabhāgavisabhāgato ca viññunā vinicchayo veditabbo. Lakkhaṇādippabhedato hi bādhanalakkhaṇaṃ dukkhasaccaṃ santāpanarasaṃ pavattipaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, pabhavalakkhaṇaṃ samudayasaccaṃ anupacchedakaraṇarasaṃ palibodhapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, santilakkhaṇaṃ nirodhasaccaṃ accutirasaṃ animittapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ, niyyānalakkhaṇaṃ maggasaccaṃ kilesappahānakaraṇarasaṃ vuṭṭhānapaccupaṭṭhānaṃ. Kamato pana oḷārikattā sabbasattasādhāraṇattā ca suviññeyyanti dukkhasaccaṃ paṭhamaṃ vuttaṃ, tasseva hetudassanatthaṃ tadanantaraṃ samudayasaccaṃ, hetunirodhā phalanirodhoti ñāpanatthaṃ tato nirodhasaccaṃ, tadadhigamupāyadassanatthaṃ ante maggasaccaṃ; bhavasukhassādagadhitānaṃ vā sattānaṃ saṃvegajananatthaṃ paṭhamaṃ dukkhamāha, saṃviggamānasānaṃ dukkhanissaraṇagavesīnaṃ assāsajananatthaṃ nirodhaṃ, tato nirodhasampāpakaṃ magganti.

#### Dukkhasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Idāni saṅkhepato uddiṭṭhāni dukkhādīni vibhajitvā dassetuṃ 'tattha katamaṃ dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ? Jātipi dukkhā'ti niddesavāro āraddho. Tattha ayaṃ mātikā — jāti veditabbā, jātiyā dukkhaṭṭho veditabbo; jarā, maraṇaṃ, soko, paridevo, dukkhaṃ, domanassaṃ, upāyāso, appiyasampayogo, piyavippayogo veditabbo, tesañca dukkhaṭṭho; icchā veditabbā, icchāya dukkhaṭṭho; khandhā veditabbā, khandhānaṃ dukkhaṭṭho veditabbo. Idañhi dukkhaṃ nāma anekaṃ nānappakāraṃ, seyyathidaṃ — dukkhadukkhaṃ, vipariṇāmadukkhaṃ, saṅkhāradukkhaṃ, paṭicchannadukkhaṃ, appaṭicchannadukkhaṃ, pariyāyadukkhaṃ, nippariyāyadukkhanti. Tattha pariyāyadukkhaṃ nippariyāyadukkhanti imasmiṃ padadvaye ṭhatvā dukkhaṃ ariyasaccaṃ kathetabbaṃ. Ariyasaccañca nāmetaṃ pāḷiyaṃ saṅkhepatopi āgacchati vitthāratopi; saṅkhepato āgataṭṭhāne saṅkhepenapi vitthārenapi kathetuṃ vaṭṭati, vitthārato āgataṭṭhāne pana vitthāreneva, na saṅkhepena. Taṃ idaṃ imasmiṃ ṭhāne vitthārena āgatanti vitthāreneva kathetabbaṃ; tasmā jātiādīni 'yā tesaṃ tesaṃ sattānaṃ tamhi tamhi sattanikāye jāti sañjātī'tiādinā padabhājanīyassa vasena veditabbāni.

#### Samudayasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Samudayasaccaniddese yāyaṃ taṇhāti yā ayaṃ taṇhā. Ponobbhavikāti punabbhavakaraṇaṃ punobbhavo, punobbhavo sīlamassāti ponobbhavikā; apica punabbhavaṃ deti, punabbhavāya saṃvattati, punappunaṃ bhave nibbattetīti ponobbhavikā. Abhinandanasaṅkhātena nandirāgena sahagatāti nandirāgasahagatā, nandirāgena saddhiṃ atthato ekattameva gatāti vuttaṃ hoti. Tatratatrābhinandinīti yatra yatra attabhāvo tatratatrābhinandinī, rūpādīsu vā ārammaṇesu tatratatrābhinandinī. Kāmataṇhāti kāme taṇhā, pañcakāmaguṇikarāgassetaṃ adhivacanaṃ; bhave taṇhā bhavataṇhā, bhavapatthanāvasena uppannassa sassatadiṭṭhisahagatassa rūpārūpabhavarāgassetaṃ nāmaṃ. Idāni tassā taṇhāya vatthuṃ vitthārato dassetuṃ 'sā kho panesā'tiādi vuttaṃ; tattha uppajjatīti jāyati, nivisatīti punappunaṃ pavattivasena patiṭṭhahati, yaṃ loke piyarūpaṃ sātarūpanti yaṃ lokasmiṃ piyasabhāvañceva madhurasabhāvañca. Lokasmiñhi cakkhādīsu mamattena abhiniviṭṭhā sattā attano cakkhuṃ suvaṇṇavimāne ugghāṭitamaṇisīhapañjaraṃ viya maññanti, sotaṃ rajatapanāḷikaṃ viya, ghānaṃ vaṭṭetvā ṭhapitaharitālavaṭṭiṃ viya, jivhaṃ rattakambalapaṭalaṃ viya, kāyaṃ suvaṇṇatoraṇaṃ viya, manaṃ aññesaṃ manena asadisaṃ uḷāraṃ maññantīti evaṃ tesu tesu vatthūsu taṇhāya uppatti nivesanañca veditabbaṃ.

#### Nirodhasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Nirodhasaccaniddese 'yo tasseva dukkhassā'ti vattabbe yasmā samudayanirodheneva dukkhaṃ nirujjhati no aññathā, tasmā taṃ dukkhanirodhaṃ samudayanirodhena dassento 'yo tassāyeva taṇhāyā'tiādimāha. Sīhasamānavuttino hi tathāgatā; te dukkhaṃ nirodhentā dukkhanirodhañca dassentā hetumhi paṭipajjanti, na phale; suvānavuttino pana aññatitthiyā, te attakilamathānuyogena phale paṭipajjanti, na hetumhi. Tattha asesavirāganirodho cāgo paṭinissaggo mutti anālayoti sabbāni nibbānavevacanāneva — nibbānañhi āgamma taṇhā asesā virajjati nirujjhati, cajiyati paṭinissajjiyati muccati na alliyati. Ekameva hi nibbānaṃ; nāmāni panassa sabbasaṅkhatānaṃ nāmapaṭipakkhavasena anekāni, seyyathidaṃ — rāgakkhayo dosakkhayo mohakkhayo taṇhākkhayo anuppādo appavattaṃ animittaṃ ajātaṃ ajaraṃ amataṃ asokantiādīni. Yathā ca puriso khette jātaṃ tittaalābuvalliṃ aggato paṭṭhāya mūlaṃ pariyesitvā chindeyya, sā milāyitvā appavattiṃ gaccheyya, tato tasmiṃ khette tittaalābu niruddhāti vucceyya; yathā ca aṭavito core ānetvā nagarassa dakkhiṇadvāre ghāteyyuṃ, tato aṭaviyaṃ corā matāti vucceyyuṃ — evameva cakkhādīsu taṇhā ariyamaggena mūlacchinnā nibbānaṃ āgamma appavattiṃ gacchati, evaṃ gatā ca tesu vatthūsu na paññāyatīti.

#### Maggasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Maggasaccaniddese ayamevāti aññamaggapaṭikkhepanatthaṃ niyamanaṃ. Ariyoti taṃtaṃmaggavajjhehi kilesehi ārakattā ariyabhāvakarattā ariyaphalapaṭilābhakarattā ca ariyo. Aṭṭhaṅgāni assāti aṭṭhaṅgiko — svāyaṃ caturaṅgikā viya senā, pañcaṅgikaṃ viya tūriyaṃ aṅgamattameva hoti, aṅgavinimutto natthi. Nibbānatthikehi maggīyati, nibbānaṃ vā maggati, kilese vā mārento gacchatīti maggo. Tattha sammā dassanalakkhaṇā sammādiṭṭhi, sammā abhiniropanalakkhaṇo sammāsaṅkappo, sammā pariggahalakkhaṇā sammāvācā, sammā samuṭṭhāpanalakkhaṇo sammākammanto, sammā vodānalakkhaṇo sammāājīvo, sammā paggahalakkhaṇo sammāvāyāmo, sammā upaṭṭhānalakkhaṇā sammāsati, sammā samādhānalakkhaṇo sammāsamādhi. So kho panesa maggo sammādiṭṭhisaṅkappānaṃ vijjāya sesadhammānaṃ caraṇena saṅgahitattā vijjā ceva caraṇañca, tesaṃ dvinnaṃ vipassanāyānena itaresaṃ samathayānena saṅgahitattā samatho ceva vipassanā ca, paññāsīlasamādhikkhandhehi adhipaññāadhisīlaadhicittasikkhāhi ca saṅgahitattā khandhattayañceva sikkhattayañca; yena samannāgato ariyasāvako vijjācaraṇasampanno hutvā vipassanāyānena kāmasukhallikānuyogaṃ samathayānena attakilamathānuyoganti antadvayaṃ parivajjetvā majjhimapaṭipadaṃ paṭipanno amataṃ nibbānaṃ sacchikaroti.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Sacca)

[[#^toc-4-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye 'ariyasaccānī'ti avatvā nippadesato paccayasaṅkhātaṃ samudayaṃ dassetuṃ 'cattāri saccānī'ti vuttaṃ. Ariyasaccānīti hi vutte avasesā ca kilesā, avasesā ca akusalā dhammā, tīṇi ca kusalamūlāni sāsavāni, avasesā ca sāsavā kusalā dhammā na saṅgayhanti; na ca kevalaṃ taṇhāva dukkhaṃ samudāneti, imepi avasesā kilesādayo paccayā samudānentiyeva. Niddesavāre ca nesaṃ paṭhamaṃ dukkhaṃ aniddisitvā tasseva dukkhassa sukhaniddesatthaṃ dukkhasamudayo niddiṭṭho; nirodhasaccaṃ yathāvuttassa samudayassa pahānavasena pañcahākārehi niddiṭṭhaṃ; maggasaccaṃ paṭhamajjhānikasotāpattimaggavasena dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ vibhattassa desanānayassa mukhamattameva dassentena niddiṭṭhaṃ. Yasmā pana na kevalaṃ aṭṭhaṅgiko maggova paṭipadā, puggalajjhāsayavasena pañcaṅgikopi maggo paṭipadā evāti tassa nayassa dassanatthaṃ pañcaṅgikavāropi niddiṭṭho; yasmā ca sampayuttakā atirekapaññāsadhammāpi paṭipadā eva, tasmā tatiyo sabbasaṅgāhikavāropi niddiṭṭho. Evamidaṃ tividhamahāvāraṃ pañcadasakoṭṭhāsaṃ saṭṭhinayasahassapaṭimaṇḍitaṃ abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ nāma niddiṭṭhanti veditabbaṃ; pāḷi pana saṅkhepena āgatā.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Sacca)

[[#^toc-4-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake catunnampi saccānaṃ khandhavibhaṅge vuttanayānusāreneva kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana samudayasaccaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme assādentassa parittārammaṇaṃ hoti, mahaggatadhamme assādentassa mahaggatārammaṇaṃ, paññattiṃ assādentassa navattabbārammaṇaṃ; dukkhasaccaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha uppannaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ, rūpārūpāvacaradhamme ārabbha uppattikāle mahaggatārammaṇaṃ, nava lokuttaradhamme paccavekkhaṇakāle appamāṇārammaṇaṃ, paññattiṃ paccavekkhaṇakāle navattabbārammaṇaṃ. Maggasaccaṃ sahajātahetuvasena sabbadāpi maggahetukaṃ vīriyaṃ vā vīmaṃsaṃ vā jeṭṭhakaṃ katvā maggabhāvanākāle maggādhipati hoti; dukkhasaccaṃ ariyānaṃ maggapaccavekkhaṇakāle maggārammaṇaṃ, maggaṃ garuṃ katvā paccavekkhaṇakāle maggādhipati. Iti imasmiṃ pañhāpucchake dve saccāni lokiyāni honti, dve lokuttarāni; yathā ca imasmiṃ, evaṃ purimesupi dvīsu — sammāsambuddhena hi tīsupi suttantabhājanīyādīsu lokiyalokuttarāneva saccāni kathitāni. Evamayaṃ saccavibhaṅgopi teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvāva bhājetvā dassitoti.

## Indriyavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-5|↑↑↑]]

Saccavibhaṅgassa anantaro pañcamo indriyavibhaṅgo. Tattha bāvīsatīti gaṇanaparicchedo, indriyānīti paricchinnadhammanidassanaṃ. Cakkhudvāre indaṭṭhaṃ kāretīti cakkhundriyaṃ; eteneva nayena sotaghānajivhākāyadvāresu tāni cattāri, vijānanalakkhaṇe manindriyaṃ, itthipurisabhāvesu itthindriyaṃ purisindriyañca, anupālanalakkhaṇe jīvitindriyaṃ, sukhadukkhasomanassadomanassupekkhālakkhaṇesu pañca vedanindriyāni, adhimokkhapaggahupaṭṭhānāvikkhepadassanalakkhaṇesu saddhāvīriyasatisamādhipaññindriyāni, avasāne ca tīṇi lokuttarindriyānīti veditabbāni. Idha suttantabhājanīyaṃ nāma na gahitaṃ; kasmā? Suttante imāya paṭipāṭiyā bāvīsatiyā indriyānaṃ anāgatattā — suttantasmiñhi katthaci dve indriyāni kathitāni, katthaci tīṇi, katthaci pañca, evaṃ pana nirantaraṃ dvāvīsati āgatāni nāma natthi. Ko panesa indriyaṭṭho nāma? Indaliṅgaṭṭho indadesitaṭṭho indadiṭṭhaṭṭho indasiṭṭhaṭṭho indajuṭṭhaṭṭhoti so sabbopi idha yathāyogaṃ yujjati; bhagavā hi sammāsambuddho paramissariyabhāvato indo, kammasañjanitāni ca indriyāni kusalākusalakammaṃ ulliṅgenti. Apica ādhipaccasaṅkhātena issariyaṭṭhenāpi etāni indriyāni — cakkhuviññāṇādippavattiyañhi cakkhādīnaṃ siddhamādhipaccaṃ, tasmiṃ tikkhe tikkhattā mande ca mandattāti. Atthato lakkhaṇādīhi kamato bhedābhedā kiccā bhūmito cāti chahākārehi cettha vinicchayo veditabbo.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye paṭhamaṃ bāvīsati indriyāni uddisitvā "tattha katamaṃ cakkhundriya"nti niddesavāro āraddho. Tattha "yaṃ cakkhu catunnaṃ mahābhūtānaṃ upādāya pasādo"tiādi sabbaṃ dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ padabhājane vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Vīriyindriyasamādhindriyaniddesādīsu ca sammāvāyāmo micchāvāyāmo sammāsamādhi micchāsamādhīti ādīni na vuttāni; kasmā? Sabbasaṅgāhakattā — sabbasaṅgāhakāni hi idha indriyāni kathitāni. Desanākkamo cettha evaṃ veditabbo: ajjhattadhammaṃ pariññāya ariyabhūmipaṭilābho hotīti attabhāvapariyāpannāni cakkhundriyādīni paṭhamaṃ desitāni; yaṃ dhammaṃ upādāya itthīti vā purisoti vā saṅkhaṃ gacchati, tassa nidassanatthaṃ tato itthindriyaṃ purisindriyañca; so duvidhopi jīvitindriyapaṭibaddhavuttīti ñāpanatthaṃ tato jīvitindriyaṃ; yaṃ kiñci vedayitaṃ sabbaṃ taṃ sukhadukkhanti ñāpanatthaṃ tato sukhindriyādīni; taṃnirodhatthaṃ pana ete dhammā bhāvetabbāti paṭipattidassanatthaṃ tato saddhādīni; paṭipattiyā amoghabhāvadassanatthaṃ tato anaññātaññassāmītindriyaṃ, tasseva phalattā tato aññindriyaṃ, ante ca paramassāsabhūtaṃ aññātāvindriyaṃ desitaṃ. Bhedo cettha jīvitindriyasseva — taṃ hi rūpajīvitindriyaṃ arūpajīvitindriyanti duvidhaṃ hoti, sesānaṃ abhedo. Kiccato ca cakkhundriyassa attano tikkhamandādibhāvena cakkhuviññāṇādidhammānaṃ attākārānuvattāpanaṃ kiccaṃ, manindriyassa sahajātadhammānaṃ attano vasavattāpanaṃ, jīvitindriyassa sahajātadhammānupālanaṃ, saddhādīnaṃ paṭipakkhābhibhavanaṃ, aññātāvindriyassa sabbakiccesu ussukkappahānanti. Bhūmito pana dasa indriyāni lokiyāni kāmāvacarāneva, tīṇi lokuttarāni, nava lokiyalokuttaramissakānīti niddesavāre siddhaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake sabbesampi indriyānaṃ kusalākusalābyākatādivibhāgo pāḷinayānusāreneva veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana "sattindriyā anārammaṇā"ti cakkhusotaghānajivhākāyaitthipurisindriyāni sandhāya vuttaṃ; jīvitindriyaṃ pana arūpamissakattā idha anābhaṭaṃ. "Dvindriyā"ti sukhadukkhadvayaṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ — taṃ hi ekantaparittārammaṇaṃ. Domanassindriyaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha pavattikāle parittārammaṇaṃ hoti, rūpāvacarārūpāvacare ārabbha pavattikāle mahaggatārammaṇaṃ, paññattiṃ ārabbha pavattikāle navattabbārammaṇaṃ. "Navindriyā siyā parittārammaṇā"ti manindriyajīvitindriyasomanassindriyaupekkhindriyāni ceva saddhādipañcakañca sandhāya vuttaṃ. "Cattāri indriyānī"ti sukhadukkhadomanassaaññātāvindriyāni — tāni hi maggārammaṇattike na bhajanti; maggahetukanti ca sahajātahetuṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ, vīriyavīmaṃsājeṭṭhakakāle siyā maggādhipati, chandacittajeṭṭhakakāle siyā navattabbā. "Dasindriyā siyā uppannā, siyā uppādino"ti satta rūpindriyāni tīṇi ca vipākindriyāni sandhāya vuttaṃ; "tīṇindriyāni bahiddhārammaṇānī"ti tīṇi lokuttarindriyāni. Iti imasmimpi pañhāpucchake dasindriyāni kāmāvacarāni, tīṇi lokuttarāni, nava lokiyalokuttaramissakāneva kathitāni; ayampi abhidhammabhājanīyena saddhiṃ ekaparicchedova hoti. Evamayaṃ indriyavibhaṅgo dveparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā bhājetvā dassitoti.

## Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-6|↑↑↑]]

Indriyavibhaṅgassa anantaro chaṭṭho paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅgo. Porāṇā āhu — "saccaṃ satto paṭisandhi, paccayākārameva ca; duddasā caturo dhammā, desetuñca sudukkarā"ti; tasmā vibhajjavādimaṇḍalaṃ otaritvā ācariye anabbhācikkhantena sakasamayaṃ avokkamantena parasamayaṃ anāyūhantena suttaṃ appaṭibāhantena vinayaṃ anulomentena mahāpadese olokentena cāyaṃ atthasaṃvaṇṇanā kātabbā. Desanābhedato hi vallihārakānaṃ catunnaṃ purisānaṃ valliggahaṇaṃ viya ādito vā majjhato vā paṭṭhāya yāva pariyosānaṃ, tathā pariyosānato vā majjhato vā paṭṭhāya yāva ādīti catubbidhā paṭiccasamuppādadesanā. Kasmā evaṃ deseti? Paṭiccasamuppādassa samantabhaddakattā, sayañca desanāvilāsappattattā — samantabhaddako hi paṭiccasamuppādo tato tato ñāyappaṭivedhāya saṃvattatiyeva. Kasmā cettha avijjā ādito vuttā? Na akāraṇattā — "āsavasamudayā avijjāsamudayo"ti hissa kāraṇaṃ vuttaṃ; atha kho vaṭṭakathāya sīsabhāvato. Bhagavā hi vaṭṭakathaṃ kathento avijjaṃ vā bhavataṇhaṃ vā sīsaṃ katvā kathesi, duggatigāmino kammassa visesahetubhūtattā avijjāya, sugatigāmino ca bhavataṇhāya. Avijjādīheva ca kāraṇehi ayaṃ kevalo dukkhakkhandho samudeti, na issaranimmānādīhīti.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭicca)

[[#^toc-6-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā"tiādinā nayena dvādasaṅgiko paccayākāro nānācittavasena vibhatto. Tattha desanābhedato, atthalakkhaṇekavidhādito, aṅgānañca vavatthānāti vinicchayo viññātabbo. Atthato tāva pūretuṃ ayuttaṭṭhena kāyaduccaritādi avindiyaṃ nāma, taṃ vindatīti avijjā; tabbiparītato kāyasucaritādi vindiyaṃ nāma, taṃ na vindatītipi avijjā; khandhānaṃ rāsaṭṭhaṃ, āyatanānaṃ āyatanaṭṭhaṃ, dhātūnaṃ suññaṭṭhaṃ, saccānaṃ tathaṭṭhaṃ, indriyānaṃ ādhipateyyaṭṭhaṃ aviditaṃ karotītipi avijjā. Yaṃ paṭicca phalameti so paccayo; upakārakaṭṭho paccayaṭṭho. Saṅkhatamabhisaṅkharontīti saṅkhārā; vijānātīti viññāṇaṃ, namatīti nāmaṃ, ruppatīti rūpaṃ, āye tanoti āyatañca nayatīti āyatanaṃ, phusatīti phasso, vedayatīti vedanā, paritassatīti taṇhā, upādiyatīti upādānaṃ, bhavati bhāvayati cāti bhavo, jananaṃ jāti, jīraṇaṃ jarā, maranti etenāti maraṇaṃ. Lakkhaṇādito ca aññāṇalakkhaṇā avijjā sammohanarasā chādanapaccupaṭṭhānā āsavapadaṭṭhānā, abhisaṅkharaṇalakkhaṇā saṅkhārā āyūhanarasā cetanāpaccupaṭṭhānā avijjāpadaṭṭhānā, vijānanalakkhaṇaṃ viññāṇaṃ pubbaṅgamarasaṃ paṭisandhipaccupaṭṭhānaṃ saṅkhārapadaṭṭhānanti eteneva nayena sesaṅgānipi veditabbāni. "Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hotī"ti ettha kevalassāti asammissassa sakalassa vā, dukkhakkhandhassāti dukkhasamūhassa — na sattassa, na sukhasubhādīnaṃ; samudayoti nibbatti.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭicca)

[[#^toc-6-2|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye niggaṇṭhiṃ nijjaṭaṃ paccayākāraṃ nānācittavasena dassetvā, yasmā na kevalaṃ ayaṃ paccayākāro nānācittesuyeva hoti, ekacittepi hotiyeva, tasmā abhidhammabhājanīyavasena ekacittakkhaṇikaṃ paccayākāraṃ nānappakārato dassetuṃ "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro"tiādinā nayena mātikā ṭhapitā. Vuttañhi — "avijjādīhi mūlehi, nava mūlapadā nava; nayā tattha catukkāni, vārabhedañca dīpaye"ti. Avijjāsaṅkhāraviññāṇanāmachaṭṭhāyatanaphassavedanātaṇhāupādānappabhedehi hi navahi mūlapadehi nava nayā honti; ekekasmiṃ naye paccayacatukkaṃ hetucatukkaṃ sampayuttacatukkaṃ aññamaññacatukkanti cattāri catukkāni, iti chattiṃsa catukkāni; ekekena ca catukkena catunnaṃ vārānaṃ saṅgahitattā ekekasmiṃ naye soḷasa vārāti catucattālīsādhikaṃ vārasataṃ hotīti veditabbaṃ. Ekacittakkhaṇikattā cettha suttantabhājanīye viya "saṅkhārā"ti avatvā "saṅkhāro"ti vuttaṃ, ekacittakkhaṇe bahūnaṃ cetanānaṃ abhāvato.

#### Paccayacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Sabbapaṭhame avijjāmūlake naye paccayacatukke cattāro vārā. Paṭhamo nāmarūpaṭṭhāne nāmassa, saḷāyatanaṭṭhāne ca chaṭṭhāyatanassa vuttattā aparipuṇṇaaṅgadvayayutto dvādasaṅgikavāro nāma; dutiyo nāmarūpaṭṭhāne nāmasseva vuttattā, saḷāyatanaṭṭhāne ca na kassaci vuttattā aparipuṇṇaekaṅgayutto ekādasaṅgikavāro nāma; tatiyo saḷāyatanaṭṭhāne chaṭṭhāyatanassa vuttattā paripuṇṇaekaṅgayutto dvādasaṅgikavāro nāma; catuttho pana paripuṇṇadvādasaṅgikoyeva. Aṭṭhakathāyaṃ vuttaṃ — "paṭhamo sabbasaṅgāhikaṭṭhena, dutiyo paccayavisesaṭṭhena, tatiyo gabbhaseyyakasattānaṃ vasena, catuttho opapātikasattānaṃ vasena gahito"ti; tathā aparipuṇṇaparipuṇṇāyatanavasena, rūpabhavakāmabhavavasena ca. Visesato paṭhamakā dve vārā arūpabhave paccayākāradassanatthaṃ vuttā, tatiyo rūpabhave, catuttho kāmabhave. Tattha "yaṃ yattha aññathā vuttaṃ, avuttañcāpi yaṃ yahiṃ; yaṃ yathā paccayo yassa, taṃ sabbamupalakkhaye"ti gāthāya nayo veditabbo. Saṅkhārassa hi avijjā sampayuttadhammasādhāraṇehi sahajātaaññamaññanissayasampayuttaatthiavigatapaccayehi chahi hetupaccayena cāti sattadhā paccayo; yasmā pana parato hetucatukkādīni tīṇi catukkāni avigatasampayuttaaññamaññapaccayavasena vuttāni, tasmā idha tāni apanetvā avasesānaṃ vasena catudhāva paccayoti veditabbo.

#### Hetucatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Hetucatukke "avijjāpaccayā"ti ettāvatā sahajātādipaccayavasena sādhāraṇato saṅkhārassa avijjā paccayoti dassetvā, puna "avijjāhetuko"ti eteneva visesato avigatapaccayatā dassitā — avijjā hetu assāti avijjāhetuko, avijjā assa sahavattanato yāva bhaṅgā pavattikā gamikāti vuttaṃ hoti. "Saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ saṅkhārahetuka"ntiādīsupi eseva nayo. Kasmā pana bhavādīsu hetukaggahaṇaṃ na katanti? Avigatapaccayaniyamābhāvato abhāvato ca avigatapaccayassa. Yāva hi upādānaṃ tāva jātijarāmaraṇānaṃ anupalabbhanato upādānaṃ bhavassa na niyamato avigatapaccayo hoti; jātiyāpi jarāmaraṇakkhaṇe abhāvato avigatapaccayabhāvo na sambhavati. Upanissayapaccayeneva pana bhavo jātiyā, jāti ca jarāmaraṇassa paccayoti. "Bhavapaccayā jātī"ti ettha ca ṭhapetvā jātijarāmaraṇāni avaseso bhavo jātiyā paccayoti veditabbo, jātiādīnaṃ jātiyā appaccayattā; avuttopi pana so yuttito gahetabbo. Viññāṇādīnaṃ pana avigatapaccayabhāvasambhavato "viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ viññāṇahetuka"ntiādivacanaṃ katanti esa hetucatukke viseso.

#### Sampayuttacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Sampayuttacatukkepi "avijjāpaccayā"ti ettāvatā sahajātādipaccayavasena saṅkhārassa avijjāpaccayataṃ dassetvā, puna "avijjāsampayutto"ti sampayuttapaccayatā dassitā; sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Yasmā pana arūpīnaṃ dhammānaṃ rūpadhammehi sampayogo natthi, tasmā "viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpa"ntiādīsu tatiyacatutthavārapadesu "viññāṇasampayuttaṃ nāma"ntiādinā nayena yaṃ labbhati, tadeva gahitanti esa sampayuttacatukke viseso. Yathā cettha, evaṃ "etenevānusārena, sabbametaṃ nayaṃ ito; viseso yo ca taṃ jaññā, catukkesu paresupī"ti vuttanayena sesacatukkesupi viseso veditabbo.

#### Aññamaññacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-4|↑↑↑]]

Aññamaññacatukkepi "avijjāpaccayā"ti sahajātādipaccayavasena saṅkhārassa avijjāpaccayataṃ dassetvā, "saṅkhārapaccayāpi avijjā"ti aññamaññapaccayatā dassitā; sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Yasmā pana bhavo nippadeso, upādānaṃ sappadesaṃ, sappadesadhammo ca nippadesadhammassa paccayo hoti, na nippadesadhammo sappadesadhammassa, tasmā ettha "bhavapaccayāpi upādāna"nti na vuttaṃ; heṭṭhā vā desanāya pacchinnattā evaṃ na vuttaṃ. Yasmā ca nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ atthi, saḷāyatanapaccayā pana ekacittakkhaṇe nāmarūpaṃ natthi yassa saḷāyatanaṃ aññamaññapaccayo bhaveyya, tasmā catutthavāre "chaṭṭhāyatanapaccayāpi nāmarūpa"nti yaṃ labbhati tadeva gahitanti esa aññamaññacatukke viseso. Ettāvatā avijjāmūlakanayamātikā niṭṭhitā.

#### Saṅkhārādimūlakanayamātikā

[[#^toc-6-2-5|↑↑↑]]

Idāni "saṅkhārapaccayā avijjā"ti saṅkhāramūlako nayo āraddho. Tatthāpi yathā avijjāmūlake evaṃ cattāri catukkāni soḷasa ca vārā veditabbā; paṭhamacatukke pana paṭhamavārameva dassetvā desanā saṃkhittā, yathā cettha evaṃ viññāṇamūlakādīsupi. Sabbesu hi saṅkhāramūlakādīsu aṭṭhasu nayesu "saṅkhārapaccayā avijjā"tiādinā nayena sahajātādipaccayavasena avijjāya saṅkhārādipaccayataṃ dassetvā, puna "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā"tiādinā nayena ekacittakkhaṇepi paccayākāracakkassa pavatti dassitā. Kasmā pana bhavamūlakā jātijarāmaraṇamūlakā vā nayā na vuttā? Na hi bhavapaccayā avijjā na hoti; apubbassa pana aññassa vattabbassa avijjāpaccayassa abhāvato, bhavaggahaṇena ca avijjāya saṅgahitattā — "bhavapaccayā avijjā"ti vuccamāne "avijjāpaccayā avijjā"tipi vuttaṃ siyā, na ca ekacittakkhaṇe avijjā avijjāya paccayo nāma hoti. Jātijarāmaraṇamūlakāpi nayā tattha pacchinnattāva na gahitā; apica bhave jātijarāmaraṇānipi antogadhāni, na cetāni ekacittakkhaṇe avijjāya paccayā hontīti.

#### Akusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Akusalaniddese yasmā idha mātikā "avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro"ti akusaladhammavasena nikkhittā, tasmā nikkhepapaṭipāṭiyāva paṭhamaṃ kusalaṃ anāmasitvā "katame dhammā akusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Yasmā ekacittakkhaṇe taṇhāya ca kāmupādānassa ca sambhavo natthi, tasmā yaṃ ettha taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ labbhati, tadeva dassetuṃ "diṭṭhi diṭṭhigata"ntiādi vuttaṃ. Bhavaniddese ca upādānassa saṅkhārakkhandhe saṅgahitattā "ṭhapetvā upādānaṃ vedanākkhandho saññākkhandho saṅkhārakkhandho viññāṇakkhandho"ti vuttaṃ; itarathā hi upādānasseva upādānapaccayattaṃ āpajjeyya. Jātiādiniddesesu ca arūpadhammānaṃ jātiādibhāvato "khaṇḍiccaṃ pāliccaṃ valittacatā cuti cavanatā"ti na vuttaṃ. Dutiyavāre "ṭhapetvā phassa"nti phassassa nāmato nīharaṇatthaṃ vuttaṃ. Tatiyavāre cittasamuṭṭhānarūpe pavattamāne tenupatthaddhānaṃ cakkhāyatanādīnaṃ upacitattā "cakkhāyatanassa upacayo"tiādi vuttaṃ. Catutthavāre mahābhūtarūpapaccayā cakkhāyatanādīni, hadayarūpapaccayā chaṭṭhāyatanaṃ, nāmapaccayā ca pacchājātasahajātādivasena sabbāni pavattantīti "nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ cakkhāyatana"ntiādi vuttaṃ. Sesākusalacittesu ca diṭṭhivippayuttesu taṇhāpaccayā upādānassa abhāvā upādānaṭṭhāne daḷhanipātinā adhimokkhena padaṃ pūritaṃ; domanassasahagatesu vedanāpaccayā taṇhāya abhāvā taṇhāṭṭhāne balavakilesena paṭighena; vicikicchāsampayutte sanniṭṭhānābhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne vicikicchāya, upādānaṭṭhānaṃ pana parihīnameva; uddhaccasampayutte taṇhāṭṭhāne uddhaccena, upādānaṭṭhāne ca adhimokkheneva. Adhimuccanavasena hi adhimokkho; adhimuccati vā tena ārammaṇe cittaṃ nibbicikicchatāya sanniṭṭhānaṃ gacchatītipi adhimokkho.

#### Kusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-7|↑↑↑]]

Idāni imināva nayena kusalacittādīsupi paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ "katame dhammā kusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Yathā pana akusale paṭhamaṃ mātikaṃ nikkhipitvā pacchā niddeso kato, na tathā idha; kasmā? Appanāvāre nānattasambhavato — lokiyakusalādīsu hi tesaṃ dhammānaṃ dukkhasaccapariyāpannattā "evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassā"ti appanā hoti, lokuttarakusalādīsu pana "evametesaṃ dhammāna"nti. Tasmā ettha sādhāraṇato mātikaṃ ṭhapetuṃ na sakkāti pāṭiyekkaṃ tesaṃ tesaṃ kusalādīnaṃ mātikaṃ uddisitvāva niddeso kato. Tattha yasmā ekacittakkhaṇe kusalasaṅkhārena saddhiṃ avijjā natthi, tasmā taṃ avatvā, avijjā viya akusalānaṃ kusalānaṃ mūlato kusalamūlaṃ vuttaṃ; taṇhupādānānañca abhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne taṇhā viya ārammaṇe ajjhogāḷho pasādo, upādānaṭṭhāne ca upādānaṃ viya daḷhanipātī adhimokkho vutto. Sesaṃ heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

#### Abyākataniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-8|↑↑↑]]

Abyākataṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe āgatapaṭipāṭiyāva vibhattaṃ. Sabbavāresu cettha avijjāmūlakā nayā parihīnā; kasmā? Avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbassa abhāvato — kusalacittesu hi avijjāṭṭhāne ṭhapetabbaṃ kusalamūlaṃ atthi, cakkhuviññāṇādīsu natthi; sahetukesu pana kiñcāpi atthi, evaṃ santepi idha pacchinnattā na gahitaṃ. Visesato panettha cakkhuviññāṇādīsu taṇhāṭṭhānaṃ upādānaṭṭhānañca parihīnaṃ, taṇhāṭṭhānārahassa balavadhammassa abhāvā adhimokkharahitattā ca; sesāhetukesu taṇhāṭṭhānameva parihīnaṃ; sahetukesu pana pasādasabbhāvato taṇhāṭṭhāne pasādena padaṃ pūritaṃ. Evamettha kusalākusalavipākesu cakkhuviññāṇādīsu saṅkhāraviññāṇanāmachaṭṭhāyatanaphassavedanāmūlakā cha cha nayā, sesāhetukesu adhimokkhamūlakena saddhiṃ satta satta, sahetukesu pasādamūlakena saddhiṃ aṭṭha aṭṭha nayā veditabbā. Cakkhuviññāṇādīsu ca catunnampi catukkānaṃ ādivārova vutto; tatiyacatutthavārā asambhavatoyeva na vuttā — rūpamissakā hi te, na ca cakkhuviññāṇādīni rūpaṃ samuṭṭhāpenti. Arūpāvacaravipākasmiṃ pana vāradvayameva labbhati.

#### Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-9|↑↑↑]]

Idāni aparena pariyāyena ekacittakkhaṇe paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ puna "katame dhammā kusalā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha "avijjāpaccayā"ti upanissayapaccayataṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ; teneva niddesavāre "tattha katamā avijjā"ti avibhajitvā "tattha katamo avijjāpaccayā saṅkhāro"ti vibhattaṃ — kusalacetanāsaṅkhāto hi saṅkhāroyeva tasmiṃ samaye cittena sahajāto hoti, na avijjā. Tattha lokiyakusalassa heṭṭhā suttantabhājanīye vuttanayeneva avijjā paccayo hoti; yasmā pana appahīnāvijjo avijjāya pahānatthaṃ lokuttaraṃ bhāveti, tasmā tassāpi samatikkamavasena paccayo hoti — avijjāvatoyeva hi kusalāyūhanaṃ hoti, na itarassa. Tebhūmakakusale ca sammohavasenapi samatikkamabhāvanāvasenapi āyūhanaṃ labbhati, lokuttare samucchedabhāvanāvasena. Ayaṃ pana viseso — yathā heṭṭhā ekekakusale catunnaṃ catukkānaṃ vasena nava soḷasakā laddhā, tathā idha na labbhanti; avijjāya avigatasampayuttaaññamaññapaccayābhāvato upanissayavasena panettha paṭhamacatukkameva labbhati, tampi paṭhamavārameva dassetvā saṃkhittaṃ.

#### Kusalamūlakavipākaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-10|↑↑↑]]

Idāni abyākatesupi apareneva nayena paccayākāraṃ dassetuṃ "katame dhammā abyākatā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Tattha "kusalamūlapaccayā"ti idampi upanissayapaccayataṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ — kusalavipākassa hi kusalamūlaṃ, akusalavipākassa ca akusalamūlaṃ upanissayapaccayo hoti; nānākkhaṇikakammapaccaye pana vattabbameva natthi, tasmā esa upanissayapaccayena ceva nānākkhaṇikakammapaccayena ca paccayo hoti. Teneva niddesavāre "tattha katamaṃ kusalamūla"nti avibhajitvā "tattha katamo kusalamūlapaccayā saṅkhāro"ti vibhattaṃ; akusalavipākepi eseva nayo, tenevettha akusalamūlakavipākaniddesopi iminā saddhiṃ ekaparicchedova hoti. Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddese viya ca imasmimpi vipākaniddese paṭhamaṃ paccayacatukkameva labbhati, tampi paṭhamavāraṃ dassetvā saṃkhittaṃ. Kiriyādhammānaṃ pana yasmā neva avijjā na kusalākusalamūlāni upanissayapaccayataṃ labhanti, tasmā kiriyavasena paccayākāro na vutto. Evamayaṃ paccayākāro suttantaabhidhammabhājanīyavasena dveparivaṭṭameva nīharitvā bhājetvā dassito hoti.

## Satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-7|↑↑↑]]

Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅgānantaraṃ satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha "cattāro"ti gaṇanaparicchedo; tena na tato heṭṭhā na uddhanti satipaṭṭhānaparicchedo dīpito. Satipaṭṭhānasaddo pana tīsu atthesu dissati – satigocare, tidhā paṭipannesu sāvakesu satthuno paṭighānunayavītivattatāyaṃ, satiyañca; idha pana "sati ca sā paṭṭhānañcā"ti satiyeva adhippetā, ārammaṇabhedena pana tāsaṃ satīnaṃ bahuttā bahuvacanaṃ kataṃ. Cattārova ca vuttā anūnā anadhikā veneyyahitattā – taṇhācaritadiṭṭhicaritasamathayānikavipassanāyānikānaṃ mandatikkhabhedena dvidhā pavattānaṃ yathānurūpaṃ visuddhimaggadassanato – subhasukhaniccaattavipallāsānañca pahānatthaṃ, caturoghayogāsavaganthupādānaagatippahānatthaṃ catubbidhāhārapariññatthañca. Aṭṭhakathānayena pana saraṇavasena ekattasamosaraṇavasena ca ekameva satipaṭṭhānaṃ, ārammaṇavasena cattāro; yathā hi catudvāre nagare catūhi disāhi āgacchantā sakasakadisāya uṭṭhānakaṃ bhaṇḍaṃ gahetvā ekaṃyeva nagaraṃ pavisanti, evaṃ cuddasavidhaṃ kāyānupassanaṃ, navavidhaṃ vedanānupassanaṃ, soḷasavidhaṃ cittānupassanaṃ, pañcavidhaṃ dhammānupassanaṃ bhāvetvā tadanubhāvanibbattena ariyamaggena ekaṃyeva nibbānaṃ osaranti.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Satipaṭṭhāna)

[[#^toc-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Uddesavāre "idha bhikkhū"ti ālapanaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ aggaparisabhāvato paṭipattiyā ca bhikkhubhāvadassanato kataṃ; yo hi imaṃ paṭipattiṃ paṭipajjati, so devo vā hotu manusso vā, bhikkhūti saṅkhaṃ gacchatiyeva. Ajjhattaṃ kāyeti attano kāye, bahiddhāti parassa kāye, ajjhattabahiddhāti kālena attano kālena parassa kāye; ajjhattabahiddhā ghaṭitārammaṇaṃ nāma natthi, paguṇakammaṭṭhānassa pana aparāparaṃ sañcaraṇakālo ettha kathito. "Kāye kāyānupassī"ti dutiyaṃ kāyaggahaṇaṃ asammissato vavatthānaghanavinibbhogādidassanatthaṃ kataṃ – rathasambhārānupassako viya aṅgapaccaṅgasamūhānupassī, nagarāvayavānupassako viya kesalomādisamūhānupassī hoti; na hettha yathāvuttasamūhavinimutto kāyo vā itthī vā puriso vā añño vā koci dhammo dissati. Ātāpīti tīsu bhavesu kilesānaṃ ātāpanato ātāpoti laddhanāmena vīriyena, sampajānoti pariggāhakena ñāṇena, satimāti pariggāhikāya satiyā samannāgato; anātāpino hi antosaṅkhepo antarāyakaro hoti, asampajāno upāyapariggahe sammuyhati, muṭṭhassati ca asamattho hotīti etehi vinā kammaṭṭhānaṃ na sampajjati. "Vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassa"nti pahānaṅgaṃ vuttaṃ; abhijjhāgahaṇena kāmacchando, domanassaggahaṇena byāpādo saṅgahaṃ gacchatīti balavanīvaraṇadvayadassanena nīvaraṇappahānaṃ vuttaṃ hoti. Vedanācittadhammānupassanuddesesupi ajjhattādīni eteneva nayena veditabbāni; tā ca vedanā lokiyāva, tathā cittaṃ dhammā ca.

#### Kāyānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Niddesavāre bhagavā cheko vilīvakārako viya ekaṃ mahāveḷuṃ labhitvā nānāupakaraṇāni karonto ajjhattabahiddhābhedena tividhaṃ kāyapariggahaṃ vitthārento "uddhaṃ pādatalā adho kesamatthakā tacapariyantaṃ pūraṃ nānappakārassa asucino"ti dvattiṃsākārakammaṭṭhānaṃ dassesi. Ācariyena cettha vacasā manasā vaṇṇato saṇṭhānato disato okāsato paricchedatoti sattadhā uggahakosallaṃ, anupubbato nātisīghato nātisaṇikato vikkhepapaṭibāhanato paṇṇattisamatikkamanato anupubbamuñcanato lakkhaṇato tayo ca suttantāti dasadhā manasikārakosallaṃ ācikkhitabbaṃ. Koṭṭhāsesu paguṇesu tīsu mukhesu ekena mukhena kammaṭṭhānaṃ vimuccati – vaṇṇato manasikaroto nīlapītalohitodātavasena cattāri vaṇṇakasiṇāni, paṭikūlato manasikaroto paṭikūlārammaṇaṃ paṭhamajjhānaṃ uppajjati, dhātuto pana "acetanā abyākatā suññā"ti manasikaroto kesādīnaṃ acetanabhāvadassanena nāmarūpavavatthānaṃ hoti; tato sahetusappaccayaṃ nāmarūpaṃ passanto tīsu addhāsu kaṅkhaṃ vitaritvā ñātapariññāya cūḷasotāpanno hutvā vipassanāpaṭipāṭiyā arahattaṃ gaṇhāti. Vinicchayo cettha nimittato saṭṭhisataṃ, lakkhaṇato aṭṭhavīsatisataṃ, dhātuto aṭṭhavīsatisataṃ, suññato channavuti suññatāti evaṃ veditabbo. "Sveva kāyo loko"ti yasmiṃ kāye kāyānupassī viharati, sveva lujjanapalujjanaṭṭhena loko vutto. Imasmiṃ pana pabbe samathavipassanā ubhayampi kathitaṃ.

#### Vedanānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Vedanānupassanāniddese "sukhaṃ vedanaṃ vedayāmī"ti pajānanaṃ na uttānaseyyakadārakānaṃ viya sattūpaladdhiavijahanakaṃ jānanaṃ adhippetaṃ, atha kho "ko vedayati, kassa vedanā, kiṃ kāraṇā vedanā"ti sampajānavediyanaṃ; na hi koci satto vā puggalo vā vedayati, vatthuṃ pana ārammaṇaṃ katvā vedanāva vedayati, taṃ vedanāpavattiṃ upādāya "ahaṃ vedayāmī"ti vohāramattaṃ hoti – yathā taṃ cittalapabbate balavavedanaṃ adhivāsetvā sahapaṭisambhidāhi arahattaṃ patto thero. Iti bhagavā rūpakammaṭṭhānaṃ kathetvā arūpakammaṭṭhānaṃ kathento vedanāvasena kathesi; tividho hi arūpe abhiniveso phassavasena vedanāvasena cittavasenāti, tesu ca yassa yaṃ pākaṭaṃ, so phassapañcamake dhamme pariggaṇhitvā vatthunissitabhāvaṃ ñatvā nāmarūpaṃ passati, "avijjādihetukā"ti paccayaṃ vavatthapetvā tilakkhaṇaṃ āropetvā arahatte patiṭṭhāti. Vedanāvasena pana kathitaṃ pākaṭaṃ hoti vedanānaṃ uppattipākaṭatāya; adukkhamasukhā pana duddīpanā, sā sukhadukkhānaṃ apagame sātāsātapaṭikkhepavasena majjhattākārabhūtāti nayato gaṇhantasseva pākaṭā hoti – piṭṭhipāsāṇassa orabhāge aparabhāge ca padaṃ disvā majjhe apassantopi "iminā padesena gato"ti nayato jānanto migaluddako viya. Sāmisā nirāmisā cettha cha gehassita cha nekkhammassitavasena veditabbā. Imasmiṃ pabbe suddhavipassanāva kathitā.

#### Cittānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Cittānupassanāniddese sarāganti aṭṭhavidhaṃ lobhasahagataṃ, vītarāganti lokiyakusalābyākataṃ; yasmā panidaṃ sammasanaṃ, na dhammasamodhānaṃ, tasmā ettha ekapadepi lokuttaraṃ na labbhati. Sadosanti duvidhaṃ domanassasahagataṃ, vītadosanti lokiyakusalābyākataṃ. Samohanti vicikicchāsahagatañceva uddhaccasahagatañcāti duvidhaṃ; yasmā pana moho sabbākusalesu uppajjati, tasmā imasmiṃyeva duke dvādasākusalacittāni pariyādiṇṇāni. Saṃkhittanti thinamiddhānupatitaṃ saṅkuṭitacittaṃ, vikkhittanti uddhaccasahagataṃ pasaṭacittaṃ. Mahaggatanti rūpāvacaraṃ arūpāvacarañca, amahaggatanti kāmāvacaraṃ; sauttaranti kāmāvacaraṃ, anuttaranti rūpārūpāvacaraṃ, tatrāpi sauttaraṃ rūpāvacaraṃ, anuttaraṃ arūpāvacarameva. Samāhitanti yassa appanāsamādhi upacārasamādhi vā atthi, asamāhitanti ubhayasamādhivirahitaṃ. Vimuttanti tadaṅgavikkhambhanavimuttīhi vinimuttaṃ; samucchedapaṭippassaddhinissaraṇavimuttīnaṃ pana idha okāsova natthi. Imasmimpi pabbe suddhavipassanāva kathitā.

#### Dhammānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Yasmā kāyānupassanāya rūpakkhandhapariggaho, vedanānupassanāya vedanākkhandhapariggaho, cittānupassanāya viññāṇakkhandhapariggahova kathito, tasmā idāni sampayuttadhammasīsena saññāsaṅkhārakkhandhapariggahampi kathetuṃ dhammānupassanā dassitā. Nīvaraṇapabbe santaṃ abhiṇhasamudācāravasena, asantaṃ asamudācāravasena vā pahīnattā vā avijjamānaṃ; tattha subhanimitte ayonisomanasikārena kāmacchandassa, paṭighanimitte byāpādassa, aratitandivijambhikābhattasammadacetasolīnattesu thinamiddhassa, cetaso avūpasame uddhaccakukkuccassa, vicikicchāṭhānīyesu dhammesu vicikicchāya uppādo; asubhanimitte pana, mettāya cetovimuttiyā, ārambhanikkamaparakkamadhātūsu, cetaso vūpasame, kusalākusalādidhammesu ca yonisomanasikārena tesaṃ pahānaṃ. Cha cha dhammā ca ekamekassa pahānāya saṃvattanti, sabbattha kalyāṇamittatā sappāyakathā ca antogadhā; āyatiṃ anuppādo pana kāmacchandathinamiddhauddhaccānaṃ arahattamaggena, byāpādakukkuccānaṃ anāgāmimaggena, vicikicchāya sotāpattimaggena hoti. Bojjhaṅgapabbe santaṃ paṭilābhavasena, asantaṃ appaṭilābhavasena; satisambojjhaṅgassa cattāro, dhammavicayassa satta, vīriyassa ekādasa, pītiyā ekādasa, passaddhiyā satta, samādhissa ekādasa, upekkhāya pañca dhammā uppādāya saṃvattanti, sabbesañca bhāvanāpāripūri arahattamaggena hoti; visesato cettha vatthuvisadakiriyā indriyasamattapaṭipādanā ca vuttā – saddhāpaññānaṃ samādhivīriyānañca samatā pasatthā, sati pana loṇadhūpanaṃ viya sabbabyañjanesu sabbattha icchitabbā. Imesu dvīsu pabbesu suddhavipassanāva kathitā. Ime pana cattāro satipaṭṭhānā pubbabhāge nānācittesu labbhanti, lokuttaramaggakkhaṇe pana ekāva maggasampayuttā sati catukiccasādhanaṭṭhena cattāri nāmāni labhatīti ekacitteyeva labbhanti.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Satipaṭṭhāna)

[[#^toc-7-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye lokuttarasatipaṭṭhānavasena desanāya āraddhattā yathā kāyādiārammaṇesu lokiyasatipaṭṭhānesu tanti ṭhapitā, na tathā idha; atha kho sabbānipi kāyānupassanādīni satipaṭṭhānāni dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ vibhattassa desanānayassa mukhamattameva dassentena niddiṭṭhāni. Nayabhedo panettha evaṃ veditabbo – kāyānupassanāya sotāpattimagge jhānābhinivese suddhikapaṭipadā, suddhikasuññatā, suññatapaṭipadā, suddhikaappaṇihitaṃ, appaṇihitapaṭipadāti imesu pañcasu ṭhānesu dvinnaṃ dvinnaṃ catukkapañcakanayānaṃ vasena dasa nayā; evaṃ vīsatiyā abhinivesesu dve nayasatāni, tāni catūhi adhipatīhi catuguṇitāni aṭṭha satāni, iti suddhikāni dve sādhipatīni aṭṭhāti nayasahassaṃ. Tathā vedanānupassanādīsu suddhikasatipaṭṭhāne cāti sotāpattimagge pañca nayasahassāni; yathā ca sotāpattimagge, evaṃ sesamaggesūti kusale vīsati nayasahassāni, suññatāppaṇihitānimittabhedesu tato tiguṇe vipāke saṭṭhi nayasahassāni. Iti kusale pañcannaṃ vipāke pañcannanti dasannaṃ niddesavārānaṃ vasena dasappabhedo asītinayasahassapatimaṇḍito abhidhammabhājanīyaniddeso.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Satipaṭṭhāna)

[[#^toc-7-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva satipaṭṭhānānaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana sabbānipi etāni appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattanato appamāṇārammaṇāneva, na maggārammaṇāni; sahajātahetuvasena pana maggahetukāni; vīriyaṃ vā vīmaṃsaṃ vā jeṭṭhakaṃ katvā maggabhāvanākāle maggādhipatīni; chandacittajeṭṭhakāya pana maggabhāvanāya phalakāle ca navattabbāni; atītādīsu ekārammaṇabhāvenapi navattabbāni; nibbānassa bahiddhādhammattā bahiddhārammaṇāni nāma honti. Evaṃ imasmiṃ pañhāpucchake nibbattitalokuttarāneva satipaṭṭhānāni kathitāni; suttantabhājanīyasmiṃyeva hi sammāsambuddhena lokiyalokuttaramissakā satipaṭṭhānā kathitā, abhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakesu pana lokuttarāyeva. Evamayaṃ satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgopi teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā bhājetvā dassito.

## Sammappadhānavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-8|↑↑↑]]

Satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgānantaraṃ sammappadhānavibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha "cattāro"ti gaṇanaparicchedo; tena na tato heṭṭhā na uddhanti sammappadhānaparicchedo dīpito. Sammappadhānāti kāraṇappadhānā upāyappadhānā yonisopadhānā. Ayaṃ pana sammappadhānakathā duvidhā – lokiyā lokuttarā ca; lokiyā sabbapubbabhāge hoti, kassapasaṃyuttapariyāyena veditabbā, lokuttaramaggakkhaṇe pana ekameva vīriyaṃ catukiccasādhanavasena "anuppannānaṃ pāpakānaṃ anuppādāyā"tiādīni cattāri nāmāni labhati. Iti suttantabhājanīye lokiyalokuttaramissakā, abhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakesu pana lokuttarāyeva sammappadhānā kathitāti ayampi vibhaṅgo teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā bhājetvā dassito.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Sammappadhāna)

[[#^toc-8-1|↑↑↑]]

Uddesavāre anuppannānanti anibbattānaṃ, pāpakānanti lāmakānaṃ, akusalānaṃ dhammānanti akosallasambhūtānaṃ; chandaṃ janetīti kattukamyatāsaṅkhātaṃ kusalacchandaṃ uppādeti, vāyamatīti payogaṃ parakkamaṃ karoti, vīriyaṃ ārabhatīti kāyikacetasikaṃ vīriyaṃ karoti, cittaṃ paggaṇhātīti sahajātavīriyena cittaṃ ukkhipati, padahatīti padhānavīriyaṃ karoti; etāni cattāri padāni āsevanābhāvanābahulīkammasātaccakiriyāhi yojetabbāni. Niddese "chando"ti na taṇhāchando na diṭṭhichando, atha kho kattukamyatākusaladhammacchando adhippeto; janeti sañjaneti uṭṭhapeti samuṭṭhapeti nibbatteti abhinibbattetīti padāni chandaṃ karontassa satataṃ karaṇaṃ, antarāyena patitassa puna ukkhipanaṃ, pabandhaṭṭhitiṃ pāpanaṃ, pākaṭakaraṇaṃ, alīnavuttitāya abhimukhaṃ nibbattanañca dassenti. Vinicchaye pana "anuppannā"ti asamudācāravasena vā ananubhūtārammaṇavasena vā veditabbā – vattaganthadhutaṅgasamāpattivipassanānavakammabrahmalokāgamanānaṃ aññatarena hi kilesā okāsaṃ na labhanti, tesu vissaṭṭhesu ayonisomanasikārañceva sativossaggañca āgamma uppajjanti. "Uppanna"ñca catubbidhaṃ – vattamānuppannaṃ, bhutvā vigatuppannaṃ, okāsakatuppannaṃ, bhūmiladdhuppannanti; aparampi catubbidhaṃ – samudācāruppannaṃ, ārammaṇādhigahituppannaṃ, avikkhambhituppannaṃ, asamugghātituppannanti. Tesu purimaṃ catukkaṃ na maggavajjhaṃ, pacchimaṃ maggavajjhaṃ; maggo ca ye kilese pajahati, te atītā vā anāgatā vā paccuppannā vāti na vattabbā – chinnassa ambarukkhassa yāni phalāni nibbatteyyuṃ tāni ajātāneva na jāyanti, evaṃ khandhānaṃ pariññātattā te kilesā anibbattāva na nibbattanti.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Sammappadhāna)

[[#^toc-8-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye sabbānipi sammappadhānāni dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ vibhattassa desanānayassa mukhamattameva dassentena niddiṭṭhāni. Nayabhedo panettha – paṭhamasammappadhāne sotāpattimagge jhānābhinivese suddhikapaṭipadā, suddhikasuññatā, suññatapaṭipadā, suddhikaappaṇihitaṃ, appaṇihitapaṭipadāti pañcasu ṭhānesu dvinnaṃ dvinnaṃ catukkapañcakanayānaṃ vasena dasa nayā; evaṃ vīsatiyā abhinivesesu dve nayasatāni, tāni catūhi adhipatīhi catugguṇitāni aṭṭha satāni, iti suddhikāni dve sādhipatīni aṭṭhāti nayasahassaṃ. Tathā dutiyasammappadhānādīsu suddhikasammappadhāne cāti sotāpattimagge pañca nayasahassāni; yathā ca sotāpattimagge evaṃ sesamaggesūti kusalavaseneva vīsati nayasahassāni. **Vipāke pana sammappadhānehi kattabbakiccaṃ natthīti vipākavāro na gahito** – iminā satipaṭṭhānabojjhaṅgavibhaṅgehi ayaṃ viseso veditabbo. Sammappadhānāni cettha nibbattitalokuttarāneva kathitāni.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Sammappadhāna)

[[#^toc-8-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva sammappadhānānaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana sabbānipi etāni appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattanato appamāṇārammaṇāneva, na maggārammaṇāni; sahajātahetuvasena pana maggahetukāni; vīmaṃsaṃ jeṭṭhakaṃ katvā maggabhāvanākāle maggādhipatīni; chandacittajeṭṭhikāya maggabhāvanāya na vattabbāni maggādhipatīnīti; **vīriyajeṭṭhikāya pana aññassa vīriyassa abhāvā na vattabbāni maggādhipatīnīti vā na maggādhipatīnīti vā** – sammappadhānaṃ hi sayameva vīriyaṃ, tasmā aññaṃ vīriyaṃ adhipati hutvā na labbhati. Atītādīsu ekārammaṇabhāvenapi na vattabbāni; nibbānassa pana bahiddhādhammattā bahiddhārammaṇāni nāma honti. Evaṃ imasmiṃ pañhāpucchake nibbattitalokuttarāneva sammappadhānāni kathitāni.

## Iddhipādavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-9|↑↑↑]]

Sammappadhānavibhaṅgānantaraṃ iddhipādavibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha "cattāro"ti gaṇanaparicchedo. Ijjhatīti iddhi, samijjhati nipphajjatīti attho; ijjhanti vā etāya sattā iddhā vuddhā ukkaṃsagatā hontītipi iddhi. Iddhipādasaddo ca dvīhi atthehi veditabbo – paṭhamenatthena iddhi eva pādo iddhipādo, iddhikoṭṭhāsoti attho; dutiyenatthena iddhiyā pādoti iddhipādo, pādoti patiṭṭhā adhigamupāyoti attho; uparūparivisesasaṅkhātaṃ hi iddhiṃ pajjanti pāpuṇanti etenāti pādo. Suttantabhājanīye lokiyalokuttaramissakā, abhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakesu pana lokuttarāyeva iddhipādā kathitāti ayampi vibhaṅgo teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā dassito.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Iddhipāda)

[[#^toc-9-1|↑↑↑]]

Chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgatanti ettha chandahetuko chandādhiko vā samādhi chandasamādhi – kattukamyatāchandaṃ adhipatiṃ karitvā paṭiladdhasamādhissetaṃ adhivacanaṃ; padhānabhūtā saṅkhārā padhānasaṅkhārā – catukiccasādhakassa sammappadhānavīriyassetaṃ adhivacanaṃ; iddhipādoti tesaṃ adhiṭṭhānaṭṭhena pādabhūto sesacittacetasikarāsi. Yatheva chandaṃ adhipatiṃ karitvā paṭiladdhasamādhi chandasamādhīti vutto, evaṃ vīriyaṃ cittaṃ vīmaṃsañca adhipatiṃ karitvā paṭiladdhā vīriyacittavīmaṃsasamādhīti vuccanti; **sesaiddhipādaniddesesupi imināva nayena attho veditabbo**, tasmā ekāva ayaṃ vaṇṇanā catunnampi iddhipādānaṃ sādhāraṇā. Ettāvatā catunnaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ matthakappattaṃ kammaṭṭhānaṃ kathitaṃ – eko hi chandaṃ avassayati, eko vīriyaṃ, eko cittaṃ, eko paññaṃ; yathā ṭhānantaraṃ patthentesu catūsu amaccaputtesu eko upaṭṭhānaṃ eko sūrabhāvaṃ eko jātiṃ eko mantaṃ avassāya sabbepi ṭhānantarāni pāpuṇiṃsu, evaṃ chandadhurena raṭṭhapālatthero, vīriyadhurena soṇatthero, cittadhurena sambhūtatthero, vīmaṃsādhurena mogharājatthero lokuttaradhammaṃ nibbattesuṃ. Abhedato tayo chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasaṅkhātā dhammā iddhīpi honti iddhipādāpi, sesā sampayuttakā cattāro khandhā iddhipādāyeva; bhedato pana chando iddhi, chandadhurena bhāvitā cattāro khandhā chandiddhipādo. Pubbabhāgo ca iddhipādo nāma, paṭilābho iddhi nāma – paṭhamajjhānaparikammaṃ iddhipādo, paṭhamajjhānaṃ iddhi; sotāpattimaggassa vipassanā iddhipādo, sotāpattimaggo iddhi. Iddhipi iddhipādopi nipphannā tilakkhaṇabbhāhatāti sanniṭṭhānaṃ kataṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Iddhipāda)

[[#^toc-9-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ uttānatthameva; nayā panettha gaṇetabbā. "Chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṃ iddhipādaṃ bhāvetī"ti vuttaṭṭhāne lokuttarāni cattāri nayasahassāni vibhattāni; vīriyasamādhiādīsupi eseva nayo. Tathā uttaracūḷabhājanīye chandiddhipāde cattāri nayasahassāni, vīriyacittavīmaṃsiddhipādesu cattāri cattārīti sabbānipi aṭṭhannaṃ catukkānaṃ vasena dvattiṃsa nayasahassāni vibhattāni. Uttaracūḷabhājanīye ca "chandoyeva chandiddhipādo, vīriyameva, cittameva, vīmaṃsāva vīmaṃsiddhipādo"ti kathitaṃ; tena "iddhipādo sampayuttakānaṃ catunnaṃ khandhānaṃyeva adhivacana"nti ekaṃsagāho na gahetabbo. Evametaṃ nibbattitalokuttarānaṃyeva iddhipādānaṃ vasena dvattiṃsanayasahassappaṭimaṇḍitaṃ abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ kathitaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Iddhipāda)

[[#^toc-9-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva iddhipādānaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana sabbepete appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattanato appamāṇārammaṇā eva, na maggārammaṇā; sahajātahetuvasena pana maggahetukā; **na pana maggādhipatino – cattāro hi adhipatayo aññamaññaṃ garuṃ na karonti sayaṃ jeṭṭhakattā**; yathā samajātikā samavayā samathāmā samasippā cattāro rājaputtā attano attano jeṭṭhakatāya aññamaññassa apacitiṃ na karonti, evaṃ ime cattāro adhipatayo pāṭiyekkaṃ jeṭṭhakadhammatāya aññamaññaṃ garuṃ na karontīti ekanteneva na maggādhipatino. Atītādīsu ekārammaṇabhāvepi na vattabbā; nibbānassa pana bahiddhādhammattā bahiddhārammaṇā nāma honti. Evaṃ imasmiṃ pañhāpucchake nibbattitalokuttarāva iddhipādā kathitā.

## Bojjhaṅgavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-10|↑↑↑]]

Iddhipādavibhaṅgānantaraṃ bojjhaṅgavibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha "sattā"ti gaṇanaparicchedo. Bodhiyā bodhissa vā aṅgāti bojjhaṅgā – yāya satidhammavicayavīriyapītipassaddhisamādhiupekkhāsaṅkhātāya dhammasāmaggiyā lokuttaramaggakkhaṇe uppajjamānāya ariyasāvako bujjhati kilesasantānaniddāya uṭṭhahati cattāri ariyasaccāni paṭivijjhati nibbānaṃ sacchikaroti, sā dhammasāmaggī bodhi, tassā aṅgāti bojjhaṅgā jhānaṅgamaggaṅgāni viya; yo vā tāya bujjhati so ariyasāvako bodhi, tassa aṅgāti bojjhaṅgā senaṅgarathaṅgāni viya. Paṭisambhidānayena pana bodhāya saṃvattanti, bujjhanti, anubujjhanti, paṭibujjhanti, sambujjhantīti bojjhaṅgā. Satteva ca vuttā anūnā anadhikā līnuddhaccapaṭipakkhato sabbatthikato ca – dhammavicayavīriyapītiyo hi līnassa paṭipakkhā, passaddhisamādhiupekkhā uddhaccassa, sati pana loṇadhūpanaṃ viya sabbabyañjanesu sabbakammikaamacco viya ca sabbarājakiccesu sabbattha icchitabbā.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-1|↑↑↑]]

Lakkhaṇato upaṭṭhānalakkhaṇo satisambojjhaṅgo, pavicayalakkhaṇo dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, paggahalakkhaṇo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pharaṇalakkhaṇo pītisambojjhaṅgo, upasamalakkhaṇo passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, avikkhepalakkhaṇo samādhisambojjhaṅgo, paṭisaṅkhānalakkhaṇo upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo; sabbesaṃ upakārakattā sati paṭhamaṃ vuttā. Tayo cettha nayā. Paṭhamanaye satisambojjhaṅganiddese paññā saṅgahitā satiyā balavabhāvadīpanatthaṃ – yathā rājaputtaṃ gahetvā ṭhito mahāmatto attanopi rājaputtassapi tejena tejavā hoti, evaṃ paññāya saddhiṃ uppannā sati balavatī, vinā uppannā dubbalā; iti apubbaṃ acarimaṃ ekacittakkhaṇe nānārasalakkhaṇā pubbabhāgavipassanābojjhaṅgā kathitā. Dutiyanaye ajjhattabahiddhābhedena satipavicayā, kāyikacetasikabhedena vīriyaṃ, kāyacittabhedena passaddhīti satta cuddasa honti; porāṇakattherā cettha ajjhattadhammesu satipavicayaupekkhā attano khandhārammaṇattā lokiyā, appattamaggaṃ kāyikavīriyampi lokiyaṃ, avitakkaavicārā pana pītisamādhiyo kāmarūpārūpāvacarato nikkhantattā nibbattitalokuttarāva, sesā missakāti vibhajitvā dassesuṃ. Tatiyanaye bhāvanāvasena vivekanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ nirodhanissitaṃ vossaggapariṇāmiñca bhāveti – viveko ca pañcavidho tadaṅgavikkhambhanasamucchedapaṭippassaddhinissaraṇavasena, vossaggo ca duvidho pariccāgavossaggo pakkhandanavossaggoti; vipassanākkhaṇe hi tadaṅgavasena maggakkhaṇe ca samucchedavasena kilese pariccajati, tanninnabhāvena ca ārammaṇakaraṇena ca nibbānaṃ pakkhandati.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye sattapi bojjhaṅge ekato pucchitvā vissajjanassa ca pāṭiyekkaṃ pucchitvā vissajjanassa ca vasena dve nayā. Upekkhāsambojjhaṅganiddese upekkhanavasena upekkhā, upekkhanākāro upekkhanā, upekkhitabbayutte samappavatte dhamme ikkhati na codetīti upekkhā, bojjhaṅgabhāvappattiyā lokiyaupekkhanāya adhikā upekkhanā ajjhupekkhanā, abyāpārāpajjanena majjhattabhāvo majjhattatā – sā ca cittassa na sattassāti dassetuṃ "majjhattatā cittassā"ti vuttaṃ. Nayā panettha ekato pucchitvā vissajjane ekekamagge nayasahassanti cattāri nayasahassāni, pāṭiyekkaṃ pucchitvā vissajjane ekekabojjhaṅgavasena cattāri cattārīti satta catukkā aṭṭhavīsati; tāni purimehi catūhi saddhiṃ dvattiṃsa nayasahassāni kusalāneva. **Yasmā pana phalakkhaṇepi bojjhaṅgā labbhanti, tasmā vipākanayopi āraddho** – iminā sammappadhānavibhaṅgato ayaṃ viseso, vipāke ca kusalato tiguṇā nayā kātabbā.

## Maggaṅgavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-11|↑↑↑]]

Bojjhaṅgavibhaṅgānantaraṃ maggaṅgavibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha "ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo"tiādi sabbaṃ saccavibhaṅge dukkhanirodhagāminīpaṭipadāniddese vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Ayaṃ pana maggavibhaṅgo teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvāva bhājetvā dassito – suttantabhājanīye lokiyalokuttaramissakaṃ, abhidhammabhājanīye pañhāpucchake ca lokuttarameva kathitanti.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Maggaṅga)

[[#^toc-11-1|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare maggavibhaṅge "ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo"tiādi sabbaṃ saccavibhaṅge dukkhanirodhagāminīpaṭipadāniddese vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Bhāvanāvasena pāṭiyekkaṃ dassite dutiyanaye "sammādiṭṭhiṃ bhāveti vivekanissita"ntiādi sabbaṃ bojjhaṅgavibhaṅge vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ. Evamidaṃ dvinnaṃ nayānaṃ vasena suttantabhājanīyaṃ lokiyalokuttaramissakameva kathitaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha#^2-1220]]

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Maggaṅga)

[[#^toc-11-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye "ariyo"ti avatvā "aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo"ti vuttaṃ; evaṃ avuttepi ayaṃ ariyo eva, yathā muddhābhisittāya deviyā kucchismiṃ jāto putto "rājaputto"ti avuttepi rājaputtoyeva. Pañcaṅgikavārepi "aṭṭhaṅgiko"ti avuttepi aṭṭhaṅgikova veditabbo, lokuttaramaggo hi pañcaṅgiko nāma natthi; "pañcaṅgiko maggo"ti pana atirekakiccadassanatthaṃ gahitaṃ, imāni pañca kārāpakaṅgāneva micchāvācādīni pajahanti, sammāvācādayo pana sayaṃ virativasena pūrenti. Vitaṇḍavādissa vādo mahāsaḷāyatana-uparipaṇṇāsa-subhaddasuttādīhi bhinditvā "aṭṭhaṅgikova lokuttaramaggo"ti dassito. Nayā panettha aṭṭhavīsati nayasahassāni vibhattāni, vipāke pana kusalato tiguṇā nayā kātabbā.

## Jhānavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-12|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare jhānavibhaṅge sakalassa suttantabhājanīyassa paṭhamaṃ ṭhapitā mātikā pubbabhāgakaraṇīyasampadāya sampannassa sabbappakārajjhānanibbattakassa puggalassa sannissayabhūtasāsanaparidīpanaṃ, aññasāsanassa ca tathābhāvapaṭisedhanaṃ. Tattha "idhā"ti imasmiṃyeva sāsane jhānuppādako bhikkhu, na aññatra. Tena hi jhānuppādakena bhikkhunā cattāri sīlāni sodhetabbāni, indriyasaṃvarabhojanamattaññutāhi ceva jāgariyānuyogena ca jhānabhāvanāya upakārakā dhammā pariggahetabbā, satisampajaññena tesaṃ dhammānaṃ aparihānā kammaṭṭhānassa ca asammoso rakkhitabbo, sappāyasenāsanaṃ pariggahetvā jhānānurūpairiyāpathena pañca nīvaraṇe pahāya sabbajjhānānaṃ uppattikkamo sampādetabbo. Imasmiñca jhānavibhaṅge suttantabhājanīye abhidhammabhājanīye pañhāpucchake cāti tīsupi nayesu lokiyalokuttaramissakāneva jhānāni, tebhūmakadhammamissakattā teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā bhājetvā dassitāni.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Jhāna)

[[#^toc-12-1|↑↑↑]]

Mātikaṃ paṭipāṭiyā bhājento "imissā diṭṭhiyā"tiādīhi padehi sikkhattayasaṅkhātaṃ sabbaññubuddhasāsanameva dasseti. Tattha pātimokkhanti sikkhāpadasīlaṃ; yo naṃ pāti rakkhati, taṃ āpāyikādīhi dukkhehi mokkheti mocayatīti pātimokkhaṃ; tañca sīlaṃ sabbesaṃ kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ patiṭṭhā ādi ca. Yathā hi nagaravaḍḍhakī paṭhamaṃ nagaraṭṭhānaṃ sodheti, evameva yogāvacaro āditova sīlaṃ visodhetvā aparabhāge samathavipassanāmaggaphalanibbānāni sacchikaroti. So ājīvaṃ parisodhetvā anācārāgocare pahāya, aṇumattānipi vajjāni aṭṭhasaṭṭhiyojanasatasahassubbedhasinerupabbatasadisāni katvā bhayato passanto, indriyesu guttadvāro bhojane mattaññū pubbarattāpararattaṃ jāgariyānuyogamanuyutto satisampajaññena sabbakiccakārī, vivittaṃ appasaddaṃ appanigghosaṃ paṭisallānasāruppaṃ senāsanaṃ bhajitvā, pallaṅkaṃ ābhujitvā ujuṃ kāyaṃ paṇidhāya parimukhaṃ satiṃ upaṭṭhapetvā, abhijjhaṃ byāpādaṃ thinamiddhaṃ uddhaccakukkuccaṃ vicikicchañcāti ime pañca nīvaraṇe cetaso upakkilese paññāya dubbalīkaraṇe pahāya cittaṃ parisodheti. Evaṃ pahīnanīvaraṇo vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṃ savicāraṃ vivekajaṃ pītisukhaṃ paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ, tato sampasādapītisukhacittekaggatāsaṅkhātāni aṅgāni gahetvā dutiyatatiyacatutthajjhānāni upasampajja viharati; "vivicceva kāmehī"tiādīsu pana yaṃ vattabbaṃ siyā, taṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe rūpāvacaraniddese vuttanayeneva veditabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Jhāna)

[[#^toc-12-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva jhānānaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu tiṇṇaṃ jhānānaṃ nimittārammaṇattā parittārammaṇādibhāvena navattabbatā; lokuttarā pana maggakāle phalakāle vā appamāṇārammaṇā. Catutthaṃ jhānaṃ pana kusalato terasavidhaṃ — sabbatthapādaka-iddhividha-dibbasota-cetopariya-pubbenivāsa-dibbacakkhu-yathākammūpaga-anāgataṃsañāṇacatutthaṃ, catubbidhaṃ āruppacatutthaṃ, lokuttaracatutthañcāti — tesaṃ ārammaṇavatthuvasena parittamahaggatāappamāṇanavattabbārammaṇatā ceva atītānāgatapaccuppannaajjhattabahiddhārammaṇatā ca pabhedato vibhattā. Evamayaṃ jhānavibhaṅgopi sammāsambuddhena suttantabhidhammapañhāpucchakavasena teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā, sabbattha lokiyalokuttaramissakāni jhānāni dassento, bhājetvā dassito.

## Appamaññāvibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-13|↑↑↑]]

Jhānavibhaṅgānantaraṃ appamaññāvibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha catassoti gaṇanaparicchedo. Appamaññāyoti pharaṇaappamāṇavasena appamaññāyo; etā hi ārammaṇavasena appamāṇe satte pharanti, ekasattampi vā anavasesapharaṇavasena pharantīti pharaṇaappamāṇavasena appamaññāyoti vuccanti. Idha bhikkhūti imasmiṃ sāsane bhikkhu. Mettāsahagatenāti mettāya samannāgatena cetasā ekaṃ disaṃ pharitvā viharati, tatheva dutiyaṃ tatiyaṃ catutthañca. Ettha adhoti heṭṭhā, tiriyanti anudisā; iti odhiso ca anodhiso ca mettāpharaṇaṃ dassitaṃ. Sabbadhīti sabbattha, sabbattatāyāti attasamatāya vibhāgaṃ akatvā, sabbāvantaṃ lokanti sabbasattayuttaṃ sattalokaṃ. Vipulenāti pharaṇavasena vipulatā; bhūmivasena taṃ mahaggataṃ, sattārammaṇavasena appamāṇaṃ, byāpādapaccatthikappahānena averaṃ, domanassappahānato abyāpajjhanti veditabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Appamaññā)

[[#^toc-13-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye padabhājanīyaṃ dassento, yasmā idaṃ kammaṭṭhānaṃ dosacaritassa sappāyaṃ, tasmā mettāya vatthubhūtaṃ puggalaṃ dassetuṃ "seyyathāpi nāma ekaṃ puggalaṃ piyaṃ manāpa"nti vuttaṃ. Piyatāya hi byāpādassa pahānaṃ hoti, manāpatāya udāsīnatā na saṇṭhāti hirottappañca paccupaṭṭhāti, tato hirottappānupālitā mettā na parihāyati; evaṃ appanāppattāya vasībhāvaṃ upagatāya mettāya majjhattaverisaṅkhātepi sabbe satte anukkamena pharati. Karuṇāya pana duggataṃ durupetaṃ, muditāya jotibhāve ṭhitaṃ piyaṃ manāpaṃ, upekkhāya neva manāpaṃ na amanāpaṃ puggalaṃ vatthuṃ katvā dassitaṃ. Sesaṃ heṭṭhā cittuppādakaṇḍe vuttaṃ, bhāvanāvidhānampi visuddhimagge vitthārato kathitaṃ.

## Sikkhāpadavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-14|↑↑↑]]

Appamaññāvibhaṅgānantaraṃ sikkhāpadavibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha pañcāti gaṇanaparicchedo. Sikkhāpadānīti sikkhitabbapadāni, sikkhākoṭṭhāsāti attho; upari āgatā sabbepi kusalā dhammā sikkhitabbato sikkhā, pañcasu pana sīlaṅgesu yaṃkiñci aṅgaṃ tāsaṃ sikkhānaṃ patiṭṭhānaṭṭhena padanti sikkhāpadāni. Pāṇātipātāti pāṇassa ghātanā māraṇā; adinnādānāti parapariggahitassa haraṇā; kāmesu micchācārāti kilesakāmavasena lāmakācārā; musāvādāti abhūtavādato; surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhānāti yāya cetanāya taṃ majjaṃ pivanti, sā pamādakāraṇattā pamādaṭṭhānaṃ; veramaṇīti virati. Imasmiṃ pana sikkhāpadavibhaṅge abhidhammabhājanīyepi pañhāpucchakepi lokiyāneva sikkhāpadāni kathitāni; evamayaṃ vibhaṅgo dveparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvāva bhājetvā dassito.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Sikkhāpada)

[[#^toc-14-1|↑↑↑]]

Padabhājanīye yasmā na kevalaṃ viratiyeva sikkhāpadaṃ, cetanāpi sikkhāpadameva, tasmā dutiyanayo dassito; yasmā ca cetanāsampayuttā paropaṇṇāsadhammāpi sikkhitabbakoṭṭhāsato sikkhāpadameva, tasmā tatiyanayopi dassito. Duvidhañhi sikkhāpadaṃ – pariyāyasikkhāpadaṃ nippariyāyasikkhāpadañca; tattha virati nippariyāyasikkhāpadaṃ, viramanto hi tāya eva tato tato viramati, na cetanāya; vītikkamakāle pana veracetanā dussīlyaṃ nāma. Sikkhāvāre pana sabbepi catubhūmakakusalā dhammā sikkhitabbabhāvato sikkhāti dassitā.

## Paṭisambhidāvibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-15|↑↑↑]]

Sikkhāpadavibhaṅgānantaraṃ paṭisambhidāvibhaṅgo āraddho. Tattha catassoti gaṇanaparicchedo. Paṭisambhidāti pabhedā; yasmā pana "atthe ñāṇaṃ atthapaṭisambhidā"tiādimāha, tasmā ñāṇasseva pabhedāti veditabbā. Atthappabhedassa sallakkhaṇavibhāvanāvavatthānakaraṇasamatthaṃ atthe pabhedagataṃ ñāṇaṃ atthapaṭisambhidā; eseva nayo dhamme dhammapaṭisambhidāya, niruttābhilāpe niruttipaṭisambhidāya, paṭibhāne paṭibhānapaṭisambhidāya. ### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭisambhidā)

[[#^toc-15-1|↑↑↑]]

Saṅgahavāre atthoti saṅkhepato hetuphalaṃ; pabhedato pana yaṃkiñci paccayasamuppannaṃ, nibbānaṃ, bhāsitattho, vipāko, kiriyāti ime pañca dhammā attho. Dhammoti saṅkhepato paccayo; pabhedato pana yo koci phalanibbattako hetu, ariyamaggo, bhāsitaṃ, kusalaṃ, akusalanti ime pañca dhammā dhammo. ## Ñāṇavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-16|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare ñāṇavibhaṅge ekavidhena ñāṇavatthūtiādinā nayena paṭhamaṃ ekavidhādīhi dasavidhapariyosānehi dasahi paricchedehi mātikaṃ ṭhapetvā nikkhittapadānukkamena niddeso kato. Tattha aṭṭhasattatiyā padehi ekakamātikā, pañcatiṃsāya dukehi dukamātikā, aṭṭhāsītiyā tikehi tikamātikā, ekavīsatiyā catukkehi catukkamātikā, dvīhi pañcakehi pañcakamātikā, ekena chakkena chakkamātikā, ekādasahi sattakehi sattakamātikā, ekena aṭṭhakena aṭṭhakamātikā, ekena navakena navakamātikā, "dasa tathāgatassa tathāgatabalānī"tiādinā ekeneva dasakena dasakamātikā nikkhittā. Niddesavāro pana "pañcaviññāṇā na hetumevā"tiādinā nayena āraddho, avasāne ca "aññadeva dasabalañāṇaṃ, aññaṃ sabbaññutañāṇa"nti vinicchayo kato.

### Ekakamātikādi (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-1|↑↑↑]]

Ekavidhenāti ekappakārena, ekakoṭṭhāsena vā. Ñāṇañca taṃ vatthu ca nānappakārānaṃ sampattīnanti ñāṇavatthu; teneva "yāthāvakavatthuvibhāvanā paññā – evaṃ ekavidhena ñāṇavatthū"ti vuttaṃ. Pañca viññāṇāti cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca. "Na hetu ahetukā"tiādīhi dhammasaṅgahamātikāvasena, "aniccā jarābhibhūtā"tiādīhi amātikāvasenāti duvidhehi aṭṭhasattatiyā padehi ekakamātikā nikkhittā; dukānurūpehi pañcatiṃsāya dukehi dukamātikā. Tikamātikā pana "cintāmayā paññā"tiādīhi catūhi bāhirattikehi, "vipākā paññā"tiādīhi cuddasahi mātikātikehi, vitakkattike pītittike ca niyamitapaññāvasena vuttehīti aṭṭhāsītiyā tikehi nikkhittā. Catukkamātikā "kammassakatañāṇa"ntiādīhi ekavīsatiyā catukkehi, sattakamātikā "sattasattati ñāṇavatthūnī"ti saṅkhepato vuttehi ekādasahi sattakehi nikkhittā.

### Dasakamātikā (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-2|↑↑↑]]

Dasakamātikā "dasa tathāgatassa tathāgatabalānī"tiādinā ekeneva dasakena nikkhittā. Tathāgatabalānīti aññehi asādhāraṇāni tathāgatasseva balāni. Duvidhaṃ tathāgatassa balaṃ – kāyabalañca ñāṇabalañca; yaṃ dasannaṃ chaddantānaṃ balaṃ taṃ ekassa tathāgatassa kāyabalaṃ, idha pana ñāṇabalameva adhippetaṃ, ñāṇañhi akampiyaṭṭhena upatthambhakaṭṭhena ca balanti vuttaṃ. Yathā nisabho catūhi pādehi pathaviṃ uppīḷetvā acalaṭṭhānena tiṭṭhati, evaṃ tathāgato dasahi balehi samannāgato catūhi vesārajjapādehi aṭṭhaparisapathaviṃ uppīḷetvā akampiyo tiṭṭhanto āsabhaṃ ṭhānaṃ paṭijānāti, parisāsu sīhanādaṃ nadati, brahmacakkaṃ pavatteti; taṃ pana dhammacakkaṃ paṭivedhañāṇañca desanāñāṇañcāti duvidhaṃ. Ṭhānañca ṭhānatoti kāraṇañca kāraṇato; kammasamādānānaṃ gatiupadhikālapayogā vipākassa ṭhānaṃ, kammaṃ hetu; saṃkilesanti hānabhāgiyadhammaṃ, vodānanti visesabhāgiyadhammaṃ. Tathāgato paṭhamaṃ ṭhānāṭṭhānañāṇena veneyyānaṃ kilesāvaraṇābhāvaṃ passati…pe… āsavakkhayañāṇānubhāvena āsavakkhayāya dhammaṃ deseti; tasmā iminā anukkamena imāni dasabalāni vuttāni.

### Ekakaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-3|↑↑↑]]

"Pañcaviññāṇā na hetumevā"tiādinā nayena āraddhe niddesavāre na hetumevāti sādhāraṇahetupaṭikkhepaniddeso; aniccamevāti hutvā abhāvaṭṭhena aniccāyeva. Uppannavatthukā uppannārammaṇāti anāgatapaṭikkhepo, purejātavatthukā purejātārammaṇāti sahuppattipaṭikkhepo. Cakkhuviññāṇaṃ cakkhupasādameva vatthuṃ katvā rūpañca ārammaṇaṃ katvā uppajjati – nibaddhavatthu nibaddhadvāraṃ nibaddhārammaṇameva; evamete aññamaññassa gocaravisayaṃ na paccanubhonti. Na kañci dhammaṃ paṭivijānāti aññatra abhinipātamattāti cakkhuviññāṇaṃ dassanamattameva hoti; kusalākusalapaṭivijānanaṃ, iriyāpathakappanaṃ, samādhisamāpajjanaṃ, vipassanā, nibbānārammaṇatā, sabbopi panesa pabhedo manodvārikajavaneyeva labbhati, na pañcadvārikena. Supinaṃ dhātukkhobhato anubhūtapubbato devatopasaṃhārato pubbanimittato vā kapimiddhapareto passati; taṃ sekkhaputhujjanāva passanti appahīnavipallāsattā, asekkhā na passanti. Evaṃ pañcannaṃ viññāṇānaṃ yāthāvaṭṭhaṃ vatthuṃ vibhāvetīti yāthāvakavatthuvibhāvanā paññā, tassā vasena ekavidhena ñāṇavatthu.

### Dukaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-4|↑↑↑]]

Duvidhena ñāṇavatthuniddese catūsu bhūmīsu kusalapaññā attano attano bhūmipariyāpannaṃ vipākasaṅkhātaṃ atthaṃ jāpeti janeti pavattetīti atthajāpikā; arahato abhiññāsamāpattīnaṃ parikammasamaye kāmāvacarakiriyapaññāpi abhiññāsamāpattipabhedaṃ kiriyasaṅkhātaṃ atthaṃ jāpetīti atthajāpikā. Catūsu bhūmīsu vipākapaññā pana sahajātādipaccayavasena vipākatthaṃ jāpetvā ṭhitāti jāpitatthā, uppannābhiññāsamāpattīnaṃ kiriyapaññāpi tatheva; sabbāpi vā esā attano attano kāraṇehi jāpitā janitā pavattitā sayañca atthabhūtātipi jāpitatthā.

### Tikaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-5|↑↑↑]]

Tividhena ñāṇavatthuniddese yogavihitesu kammāyatanesu sippāyatanesu vijjāṭṭhānesu kammassakataṃ vā saccānulomikaṃ vā parato assutvā sayameva cintento paṭilabhati, ayaṃ cintāmayā paññā, sā pana abhiññātānaṃ mahāsattānameva uppajjati; parato sutvā paṭiladdhā sutamayā; samāpannassa antosamāpattiyaṃ pavattā bhāvanāmayā. Dānacetanāsīlacetanāsampayuttā paññā pubbacetanā muñcacetanā aparacetanāti tividhena uppajjamānā dānamayā sīlamayā nāma. Pañcasīladasasīlaṃ upādāya pātimokkhasaṃvaro adhisīlaṃ, vaṭṭapādikā aṭṭha samāpattiyo upādāya vipassanāpādikā adhicittaṃ, kammassakatapaññaṃ upādāya vipassanāmaggaphalapaññā adhipaññā, yā uppanneyeva tathāgate uppajjanti. ### Catukkaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-6|↑↑↑]]

Catubbidhena ñāṇavatthuniddese ‘idaṃ kammaṃ sakaṃ, idaṃ no saka’nti jānanaṃ kammassakatañāṇaṃ, ṭhapetvā saccānulomikaṃ vipassanāñāṇaṃ avasesā sabbāpi sāsavā kusalā paññā kammassakatañāṇameva; ekameva maggañāṇaṃ catūsu saccesu ekapaṭivedhavasena catūsu ṭhānesu saṅgahitaṃ. Maggaphalapaññā dhamme ñāṇaṃ, tena atītānāgate nayaṃ nayanaṃ anvaye ñāṇaṃ, cittaparicchedañāṇaṃ pariye ñāṇaṃ, avasesā sammutiñāṇaṃ. Kāmāvacarakusalapaññā cutipaṭisandhiṃ ācinateva, lokuttaramaggapaññā apacinateva, rūpārūpāvacarapaññā ācinati ceva vikkhambhanavasena apacinati ca. Paṭhamajjhānapaññā nibbidāya no paṭivedhāya, catutthajjhānapaññā paṭivedhāya no nibbidāya, maggapaññā ubhayāya; paṭhamajjhānapaññāva kāmasahagatasaññāmanasikāravasena hānabhāginī, nikantivasena ṭhitibhāginī, avitakkasahagatavasena visesabhāginī, nibbidāsahagatavasena nibbedhabhāginī; kilesavikkhambhanapaṭipadāya dukkhattā vipassanāparivāsassa dandhattā dukkhapaṭipadā dandhābhiññāti evamādi.

### Pañcakaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-7|↑↑↑]]

Pañcavidhena ñāṇavatthuniddese dvīsu jhānesu paññā pītipharaṇatā, tīsu jhānesu paññā sukhapharaṇatā, cetopariyapaññā cetopharaṇatā, dibbacakkhupaññā ālokapharaṇatā, paccavekkhaṇañāṇaṃ paccavekkhaṇānimittaṃ; tattha dve pādā viya, dve hatthā viya, abhiññāpādakajjhānaṃ majjhimakāyo viya, paccavekkhaṇānimittaṃ sīsaṃ viyāti bhagavā pañcaṅgikaṃ sammāsamādhiṃ aṅgapaccaṅgasampannaṃ purisaṃ viya katvā dassesi. Pañcañāṇike pana arahattaphalasamādhi adhippeto, yo paccuppannasukho āyatiñca sukhavipāko, kilesehi ārakattā ariyo, nirāmiso, akāpurisasevito, santo paṇīto paṭippassaddhiladdho ekodibhāvādhigato, na sasaṅkhāraniggayhavāritagato, sato samāpajjati sato vuṭṭhahati; evaṃ paccavekkhamānassa paccattaṃyeva aparappaccayaṃ ñāṇaṃ uppajjati.

### Chakkaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-8|↑↑↑]]

### Sattakādiniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-9|↑↑↑]]

Sattavidhena ñāṇavatthuniddese ekādasasu paṭiccasamuppādaṅgesu ekekasmiṃ paccuppanne atīte anāgate ca ñāṇadvayanti cha, tāni dhammaṭṭhitiñāṇena paccayākārañāṇena saddhiṃ satta, evaṃ sattasattati honti; tadeva ñāṇaṃ khayadhammaṃ vayadhammaṃ virāgadhammaṃ nirodhadhammanti vuttaṃ, iminā aparavipassanāya purimavipassanāsammasanaṃ, sattakkhattuṃ vipassanāpaṭivipassanā kathitā. ### Dasakaniddeso (Ñāṇa)

[[#^toc-16-10|↑↑↑]]

Dasavidhena ñāṇavatthuniddese aṭṭhānanti hetupaṭikkhepo, anavakāsoti paccayapaṭikkhepo; kāraṇañhi tadāyattavuttitāya attano phalassa ṭhānanti ca avakāsoti ca vuccati. Yaṃ yaṃ hi puthujjano niccaṃ sukhaṃ attāti gaṇhāti, taṃ taṃ ariyasāvako aniccaṃ dukkhaṃ anattāti gaṇhanto gāhaṃ viniveṭheti; mātughātādīni pañca ānantariyakammāni puthujjano karoti, na ariyasāvako; ekissā lokadhātuyā dve sammāsambuddhā apubbaṃ acarimaṃ nuppajjanti anacchariyattā desanāya visesābhāvato vivādabhāvato ca; yathā nimbabījādīni amadhurameva phalaṃ nibbattenti, evaṃ kāyaduccaritādīni amadhurameva vipākaṃ nibbattenti. Dutiyabalaniddese gatiupadhikālapayogasampattīhi paṭibāhitaṃ pāpakammaṃ vipākaṃ adatvā catasso vipattiyo āgamma deti, catūhi vipattīhi paṭibāhitaṃ kalyāṇakammaṃ catasso sampattiyo āgamma deti; idaṃ tathāgatassa kammantaravipākantarajānanañāṇaṃ akampiyaṭṭhena dutiyabalaṃ. Tatiyabalaniddese maggoti vā paṭipadāti vā kammassevetaṃ nāmaṃ; sakalagāmavāsikesu ekato ekaṃ migaṃ jīvitā voropentesupi taṃ kammaṃ āyūhanakkhaṇeyeva nānā hoti, taṃ tathāgato ‘esa niraye nibbattissati, esa tiracchānayoniyaṃ, esa pettivisaye’ti nirayagāminiṃ…pe… nibbānagāminiṃ paṭipadaṃ pajānāti. Catutthabalaniddese khandhāyatanadhātunānattaṃ, anekadhātunānādhātulokanānattañca na kevalaṃ upādinnakasaṅkhāralokassa, anupādinnakasaṅkhāralokassāpi pajānāti; sabbaññubuddhānaṃyeva hi etaṃ balaṃ, na aññesaṃ. Pañcamabalaniddese hīnādhimuttikā hīnādhimuttike, paṇītādhimuttikā paṇītādhimuttike sevanti bhajanti payirupāsanti; taṃ ajjhāsayadhātu niyāmeti. Chaṭṭhabalaniddese sattānaṃ āsayaṃ anusayaṃ caritaṃ adhimuttaṃ, apparajakkhamahārajakkhatikkhindriyamudindriyasvākāradvākārasuviññāpayaduviññāpayabhabbābhabbabhāvañca pajānāti; indriyaparopariyattiñāṇañca āsayānusayañāṇañca ekato hutvā ekaṃ balañāṇaṃ nāma jātaṃ. Sattamabalaniddese cattāro jhāyī, cattāri jhānāni, vimokkhā, savitakkasavicārādayo samādhī, samāpattiyo, hānabhāgiyo visesabhāgiyo ca dhammo, vodānampi vuṭṭhānaṃ, tamhā tamhā samādhimhā vuṭṭhānampi vuṭṭhānanti vibhattaṃ. Aṭṭhamabalaniddese anekavihitaṃ pubbenivāsaṃ, navamabalaniddese dibbena cakkhunāti sabbaṃ visuddhimagge vitthāritameva; dasamabalaniddese cetovimuttinti phalasamādhiṃ, paññāvimuttinti phalañāṇaṃ. Paravādī pana "dasabalañāṇaṃ nāma pāṭiyekkaṃ natthi, sabbaññutañāṇassevāyaṃ pabhedo"ti āha; taṃ na tathā daṭṭhabbaṃ. Dasabalañāṇañhi sakasakakiccameva jānāti – paṭhamaṃ kāraṇākāraṇameva, dutiyaṃ kammantaravipākantarameva, tatiyaṃ kammaparicchedameva, catutthaṃ dhātunānattakaraṇameva, pañcamaṃ sattānaṃ ajjhāsayādhimuttimeva, chaṭṭhaṃ indriyānaṃ tikkhamudubhāvameva, sattamaṃ jhānādīhi saddhiṃ tesaṃ saṃkilesādimeva, aṭṭhamaṃ pubbenivutthakhandhasantatimeva, navamaṃ sattānaṃ cutipaṭisandhimeva, dasamaṃ saccaparicchedameva; sabbaññutañāṇaṃ pana etehi jānitabbañca tato uttaritarañca pajānāti, etesaṃ pana kiccaṃ na sabbaṃ karoti – jhānaṃ hutvā appetuṃ, iddhi hutvā vikubbituṃ, maggo hutvā kilese khepetuṃ na sakkoti. Paṭipāṭiyā satta ñāṇāni kāmāvacarāni, tato dve rūpāvacarāni, avasāne ekaṃ lokuttaraṃ; sabbaññutañāṇaṃ pana savitakkasavicārameva kāmāvacarameva lokiyameva. Iti aññadeva dasabalañāṇaṃ, aññaṃ sabbaññutañāṇaṃ.

## Khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-17|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare khuddakavatthuvibhaṅgepi paṭhamaṃ mātikaṃ ṭhapetvā nikkhittapadānukkamena niddeso kato. Jātimadotiādayo ekakā, kodho ca upanāho cātiādayo dukā, akusalamūlādayo tikā, āsavacatukkādayo catukkā, orambhāgiyasaṃyojanādayo pañcakā, vivādamūlādayo chakkā, anusayādayo sattakā, kilesavatthuādayo aṭṭhakā, āghātavatthuādayo navakā, kilesavatthuādayo dasakā, ajjhattikassa upādāya aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritānītiādayo aṭṭhārasakā ca nikkhittāti sabbānipi etāni aṭṭha kilesasatāni nikkhittānīti veditabbāni. Avasāne ca dvāsaṭṭhi diṭṭhigatāni brahmajāle veyyākaraṇe vuttanayeneva pabhedato ca atthato ca veditabbānīti dassetvā vibhaṅgo niṭṭhāpito.

### Ekakamātikādi (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-1|↑↑↑]]

Tatrāyaṃ nikkhepaparicchedo – ādito tāva jātimadotiādayo tesattati ekakā nikkhittā, tato kodho ca upanāho cātiādayo aṭṭhārasa dukā, akusalamūlādayo pañcatiṃsa tikā, āsavacatukkādayo cuddasa catukkā, orambhāgiyasaṃyojanādayo pannarasa pañcakā, vivādamūlādayo cuddasa chakkā, anusayādayo satta sattakā, kilesavatthuādayo aṭṭha aṭṭhakā, āghātavatthuādayo nava navakā, kilesavatthuādayo satta dasakā, ajjhattikassa upādāya aṭṭhārasa taṇhāvicaritānītiādayo cha aṭṭhārasakā. Ayaṃ tāva nikkhepaparicchedo; idāni yathānikkhittāya mātikāya "tattha katamo jātimado"tiādinā nayena niddesavāro āraddho.

### Ekakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-2|↑↑↑]]

Jātiṃ paṭiccāti jātiyā sati taṃ jātiṃ nissāya uppanno majjanākārappavatto māno jātimado nāma, svāyaṃ khattiyādīnaṃ catunnampi vaṇṇānaṃ uppajjati; gottārogyayobbanajīvitalābhasakkārādīsupi ‘ahaṃ evarūpo, avasesā sattā na evarūpā’ti majjanavasena uppanno māno tena tena nāmena vutto, evaṃ savatthukaṃ mānaṃ kathetvā avatthukaṃ nibbattitamānaṃ dassetuṃ "tattha katamo mado"ti vuttaṃ. Pamādo pañcasu kāmaguṇesu sativossaggalakkhaṇo, yathā kakaṇṭako thokaṃ gantvā thokaṃ tiṭṭhati evaṃ dānādikusalakiriyāya anaṭṭhitakiriyatā; thambho cittassa uddhumātabhāvalakkhaṇo, sārambho karaṇuttariyalakkhaṇo, atricchatā sakalābhe asantuṭṭhi paralābhe ca patthanā, mahicchatā pāpicchatā ca asantaguṇasambhāvanatā paṭiggahaṇe ca amattaññutā – tayo hi pūretuṃ na sakkā, aggi upādānena, samuddo udakena, mahiccho paccayehi. Siṅgaṃ kilesasiṅgāratā, tintiṇaṃ kilesavasena khīyanākāro, cāpalyaṃ cīvarapattādimaṇḍanā, asabhāgavutti mātāpitūsu garuṭṭhānīyesu ca hirottappassa apaccupaṭṭhāpanaṃ; tandīvijambhitābhattasammadehi kilesavasena kāyālasiyaṃ, cetaso līnattena cittassa gilānākāro kathito. Kuhanā paccayapaṭisevanasāmantajappanairiyāpathavasena tividhaṃ vimhāpanaṃ, lapanā nemittikatā nippesikatā lābhena lābhaṃ nijigīsanatā ca micchājīvassa ākārā; nippesikatā hi veḷupesikā viya parassa guṇaṃ nippeseti nipuñchati. Seyyasadisahīnamānā jātiādivatthuvaseneva, seyyassa seyyohamasmītiādayo nava mānā puggalaṃ nissāya kathitā, tesu yāthāvamānā arahattamaggavajjhā, ayāthāvamānā sotāpattimaggavajjhā. Adhimāno appatte pattasaññitā, so ariyasāvakassa dussīlassa ca nuppajjati, parisuddhasīlassa pana āraddhavipassakassa dīgharattaṃ kilesasamudācāraṃ apassantassa uppajjati, uccamālaṅkavāsī mahānāgatthero viya. Asmimāno ‘ahaṃ rūpa’ntiādinā uppannamāno, micchāmāno pāpakena kammāyatanādinā; ñātijanapadavitakkā gehasitapemena uppannā, amaravitakko amaro maccho viya ekasmiṃ pakkhe asaṇṭhahanato, parānuddayatālābhasakkārasilokānavaññattipaṭisaṃyuttā ca vitakkā gehasitā eva.

### Dukaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-3|↑↑↑]]

Dukesu pubbakālaṃ kodhaṃ upanayhatīti aparakālakodho upanāho nāma, so upanandhanalakkhaṇo veraṃ appaṭinissajjanaraso, yena samannāgato puggalo ādittapūtialātaṃ viya jalateva. Makkho paraguṇamakkhanalakkhaṇo, gūthapaharaṇakaṃ gūtho viya attano kāraṇaṃ paṭhamataraṃ makkheti, parena sukatānaṃ kiriyānaṃ avacchādanapaccupaṭṭhāno; paḷāso yugaggāhalakkhaṇo paraguṇehi attano guṇānaṃ samakaraṇaraso. Māyā katapaṭicchādanalakkhaṇā, yāya samannāgato puggalo bhasmāpaṭicchanno viya aṅgāro, udakapaṭicchanno viya khāṇu hoti; sāṭheyyaṃ attano avijjamānaguṇappakāsanalakkhaṇaṃ. Anajjavena kāyavacīcittavaṅkatā kathitā, amaddavaṃ amudutāsaṅkhātā ujucittatā, akkhantiādayo khantiādipaṭipakkhato veditabbā. Ajjhattasaṃyojanaṃ kāmabhave bandhanaṃ, bahiddhāsaṃyojanaṃ rūpārūpabhavesu bandhanaṃ, yasmā kāmabhave cutipaṭisandhiyo bahukā tattha ālayopi bahu hoti; tattha ekekaṃ pañcavidhaṃ, tasmā orambhāgiyāni uddhambhāgiyānīti vuttāni.

### Tikaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-4|↑↑↑]]

Tikaniddese tīhi akusalamūlehi vaṭṭamūlasamudācāro kathito; vitakkanavasena vitakko, sañjānanavasena saññā, sabhāvaṭṭhena dhātu. Esanāsu kāmagavesanarāgo kāmesanā, bhavagavesanarāgo bhavesanā, diṭṭhigatikasammatassa brahmacariyassa gavesanā diṭṭhi brahmacariyesanā, tadekaṭṭhaṃ kammampi esanā eva. Vidhāti seyyādivasena vidahanato māno; tamoti vicikicchāsīsena avijjā, yāya atītaṃ anāgataṃ paccuppannaṃ addhānaṃ ārabbha kaṅkhati. Titthāyatanesu ‘pubbekatahetū’ti nigaṇṭhasamayo, ‘issaranimmānahetū’ti brāhmaṇasamayo, ‘ahetu appaccayā’ti ājīvakasamayo. Rāgādayo satte bandhanato kiñcanā, cittaṃ malinaṃ karaṇato malāni, pakkhalanapātahetuto visamāni, anudahanaṭṭhena aggī – daharabhikkhunī rāgena jhāyitvā kālamakāsi, manopadosikā devā dosena, khiḍḍāpadosikā mohena – kasaṭā nirojāti kasāvā. Assādadiṭṭhi sassatadiṭṭhi, attānudiṭṭhi sakkāyadiṭṭhi, micchādiṭṭhi ucchedadiṭṭhi. Upārambho paravajjānupassanalakkhaṇo, yena samannāgato puggalo tunnakāro sāṭakaṃ pasāretvā chiddameva olokento viya parassa aguṇesuyeva patiṭṭhāti; ayonisomanasikāro anicce niccanti dukkhe sukhanti pavatto uppathamanasikāralakkhaṇo, yassa vasena puggalo saccāni yāthāvato āvajjituṃ na sakkoti.

### Catukkaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-5|↑↑↑]]

Catukkaniddese cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanahetu itibhavābhavahetu ca cattāro taṇhuppādā, tesaṃ pahānatthāya paṭipāṭiyāva cattāro ariyavaṃsā desitā. Agatigamanesu yo mittasandiṭṭhañātakavasena chandena, pakativeravasena dosena, mandattā momūhattā mohena, anatthabhīto bhayena assāmikaṃ sāmikaṃ karoti, bhājiyaṭṭhāne vā ūnātirekaṃ deti, so catubbidhopi agatiṃ gacchati; ariyā etāya na gacchantīti agati. Vipariyāsā aniccādīni vatthūni niccantiādinā viparītato esanti, te saññācittadiṭṭhīnaṃ vasena dvādasa honti, tesu aṭṭha sotāpattimaggena pahīyanti, asubhe subhanti saññācittavipallāsā anāgāmimaggena, dukkhe sukhanti arahattamaggena. Adisvā ‘diṭṭhaṃ mayā’ti disvā vā ‘na diṭṭhaṃ mayā’ti anariyova vadati, tasmā evaṃ vadantassa saha saddena aṭṭha cetanā anariyavohārā. Bhayesu jātibhayādīni, rājabhayādīni, ūmikumbhīlāvaṭṭasusukābhayāni, attānuvādaparānuvādadaṇḍaduggatibhayānīti soḷasa mahābhayāni kathitāni. Diṭṭhicatukke timbarukadiṭṭhi kathitā – sayaṃkataṃ sukhadukkhanti sassatadiṭṭhi, paraṃkatanti ucchedadiṭṭhi, ubhayakatanti sassatucchedadiṭṭhi, akāraṇā evāti ahetukadiṭṭhi.

### Pañcakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-6|↑↑↑]]

Pañcakaniddese yasmā yesaṃ sakkāyadiṭṭhiādīni appahīnāni, te bhavaggepi nibbatte etāni ākaḍḍhitvā kāmabhaveyeva pātenti, tasmā orambhāgiyāni saṃyojanāni; etāni gacchantaṃ na vārenti, gataṃ pana ānenti, rūparāgādīni pana gacchantaṃ na vārenti, āgantuṃ pana na denti. Rāgādayo pañca lagganaṭṭhena saṅgā, anupaviṭṭhaṭṭhena sallā. Cetokhilā cittassa thaddhabhāvā kacavarabhāvā khāṇukabhāvā, yehi satthari dhamme saṅghe sikkhāya ca kaṅkhati, nādhimuccati, na sampasīdati; cetasovinibandhā cittaṃ bandhitvā muṭṭhiyaṃ katvā viya gaṇhanti – kāme kāye rūpe avītarāgatā, udarāvadehakaṃ bhuñjitvā seyyasukhapassasukhamiddhasukhānuyogo, ‘devo vā bhavissāmī’ti paṇidhāya brahmacariyacaraṇaṃ. Kusaladhamme āvarantīti nīvaraṇāni. Diṭṭhīsu saññīvādā asaññīvādā nevasaññīnāsaññīvādā ucchedavādā diṭṭhadhammanibbānavādā ca saṅgahitā; tattha kāmaguṇesu samappito paramadiṭṭhadhammanibbānappattoti vadanti, dutiyādivāresu pana kāmā hutvā abhāvaṭṭhena aniccā, tesaṃ vipariṇāmaññathābhāvā sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā uppajjanti, paṭhamajjhānaṃ vitakkavicārehi oḷārikaṃ sakaṇṭakaṃ viya khāyatīti uttarottaraṃ paṭikkhipanti. Akkhantiyāti anadhivāsanāya – tassā ādīnavesu puggalo bahuno janassa appiyo amanāpo verabahulo vajjabahulo hoti; ājīvakabhayaṃ asilokabhayaṃ parisasārajjabhayañca katapāpassa uppajjati.

### Chakkaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-7|↑↑↑]]

Chakkaniddese yasmā kuddho vā kodhavasena sandiṭṭhiparāmāsī vā sandiṭṭhiparāmāsitāya kalahaṃ viggahaṃ vivādaṃ āpajjati, tasmā kodhādayo vivādamūlānīti vuttā. Manāpiyesu rūpādīsu kāmagehasitā chandarāgā, amanāpiyesu virodhavatthūni. Agāravesu yo satthari dharamāne upaṭṭhānaṃ na yāti, parinibbute cetiyaṃ vandituṃ na gacchati, ayaṃ satthari agāravo; yo sakkaccaṃ dhammaṃ na suṇāti, ayaṃ dhamme agāravo; yo vuḍḍhe bhikkhū ghaṭṭento gacchati, ayaṃ saṅghe agāravo; tisso sikkhā apūrayamāno sikkhāya, appamādalakkhaṇaṃ ananubrūhayamāno appamāde, duvidhaṃ paṭisanthāraṃ akaronto paṭisanthāre agāravo nāma. Kammabhassaniddāsaṅgaṇikāsaṃsaggapapañcārāmatā parihānakarā dhammā. Cakkhunā rūpaṃ disvā somanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ domanassaṭṭhāniyaṃ upekkhāṭṭhāniyaṃ rūpaṃ upavicaratīti somanassupavicārādayo; gehasitāni kāmaguṇanissitāni, upekkhā panettha aññāṇupekkhā. ‘Atthi me attā’ti sassatadiṭṭhi, ‘natthi me attā’ti ucchedadiṭṭhi, ‘attanā vā attānaṃ sañjānāmī’tiādayopi sassatucchedadiṭṭhiyova, ‘vado vedeyyo… nicco dhuvo sassato avipariṇāmadhammo’ti sassatadiṭṭhiyā abhinivesākārā; evamayaṃ sabbāsavadiṭṭhi nāma kathitā.

### Sattakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-8|↑↑↑]]

Sattakaniddese thāmagataṭṭhena appahīnaṭṭhena ca anusentīti anusayā; vaṭṭasmiṃ satte saṃyojenti ghaṭentīti saṃyojanāni; samudācāravasena pariyuṭṭhahantīti pariyuṭṭhānāni, kāmarāgova pariyuṭṭhānaṃ kāmarāgapariyuṭṭhānaṃ. Asataṃ dhammā, lāmakaṭṭhena vā asantā dhammāti asaddhammā; rāgādīhi dosehi duṭṭhāni caritānīti duccaritāni; tena tenākārena maññantīti mānā. Diṭṭhiniddese satta ucchedavādā kathitā – paṭhamo rūpakāyasīsena cātumahābhūtikaṃ mātāpettikasambhavaṃ manussattabhāvaṃ attāti vadati, dutiyo taṃ paṭikkhipitvā dibbaṃ kāmāvacaraṃ kabaḷīkārabhakkhaṃ, tatiyo dibbaṃ rūpiṃ manomayaṃ sabbaṅgapaccaṅgiṃ ahīnindriyaṃ, tato paraṃ ākāsānañcāyatanūpagādayo arūpattabhāve attāti vadanti, sabbepi ca ‘kāyassa bhedā ucchijjati vinassatī’ti gaṇhanti.

### Aṭṭhakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-9|↑↑↑]]

Aṭṭhakaniddese kilesāyeva kilesavatthūni; kusītavatthūnīti kusītassa alasassa kosajjakāraṇāni – kammaṃ kātabbaṃ hoti, kataṃ hoti, maggo gantabbo hoti, gato hoti, piṇḍapātaṃ na labhati, labhati ‘māsācitaṃ maññe’ti garuko hoti, appamattako ābādho uppanno hoti, gilānā vuṭṭhito hoti, sabbattha ‘handāhaṃ nipajjāmī’ti osīdanaṃ, appattassa pattiyā duvidhampi vīriyaṃ nārabhati. Lokassa dhammāti lokadhammā, etehi vimutto nāma natthi, buddhānampi honti eva; lābhe sārāgo alābhe paṭivirodho, yase ayase pasaṃsāya nindāya sukhe dukkhe ca uppanno sārāgo vā virodho vā cittaṃ paṭihanatīti paṭighāto. Purisadosesu codito ‘na sarāmī’ti assatibhāvena nibbeṭheti, codakaṃyeva paṭippharati, paccāropeti, aññenāññaṃ paṭicaratīti aññena vacanena aññaṃ paṭicchādeti bahiddhā kathaṃ apanāmeti, kopañca dosañca appaccayañca pātukaroti, bāhāvikkhepakaṃ bhaṇati. Asaññīvādesu lābhino kasiṇarūpaṃ attāti gahetvā rūpīti diṭṭhi uppajjati, alābhino takkamatteneva; diṭṭhigatiko hi ummattako viya yaṃ vā taṃ vā gaṇhāti.

### Navakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-10|↑↑↑]]

Navakaniddese nava āghātavatthūni sattesu uppattivaseneva kathitāni; purisānaṃ malānīti purisamalāni; navavidhāti navappabhedā mānā. Taṇhaṃ paṭicca rūpādiārammaṇapariyesanā, pariyesanaṃ paṭicca lābho, lābhaṃ paṭicca vinicchayo – idha vitakkoyeva, lābhaṃ labhitvā hi ‘ettakaṃ mayhaṃ bhavissati, ettakaṃ parassa, ettakaṃ nidahissāmī’ti vitakkeneva vinicchināti – vinicchayaṃ paṭicca chandarāgo, chandarāgaṃ paṭicca ajjhosānaṃ ‘ahaṃ mama’nti balavasanniṭṭhānaṃ, ajjhosānaṃ paṭicca pariggaho, pariggahaṃ paṭicca macchariyaṃ parehi sādhāraṇabhāvassa asahanatā, macchariyaṃ paṭicca ārakkho, ārakkhādhikaraṇaṃ daṇḍādānasatthādānakalahaviggahavivādatuvaṃtuvapesuññamusāvādā. Iñjitādīhi sabbapadehi mānova kathito; māno hi iñjanato iñjitaṃ, maññanato maññitaṃ, phandanato phanditaṃ, papañcanato papañcitaṃ, tehi tehi kāraṇehi saṅkhatattā saṅkhatanti ca vuccati.

### Dasakaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-11|↑↑↑]]

Dasakaniddese kilesā eva kilesavatthūni. Āghātavatthūni panettha "anatthaṃ me acarī"tiādīnaṃ vasena avikopetabbe khāṇukaṇṭakādimhipi aṭṭhāne uppannāghātena saddhiṃ dasa vuttāni. Micchattesu micchāñāṇanti pāpakiriyāsu upāyacintāvasena pāpaṃ katvā ‘sukataṃ mayā’ti paccavekkhaṇākārena uppanno moho, micchāvimuttīti avimuttasseva sato vimuttasaññitā. Dasa akusalakammapathā, dasa saṃyojanāni, dasavatthukā micchādiṭṭhi, dasavatthukā antaggāhikā diṭṭhi ca heṭṭhā vuttanayeneva uttānatthā.

### Taṇhāvicaritaniddeso (Khuddaka)

[[#^toc-17-12|↑↑↑]]

Taṇhāvicaritānīti taṇhāsamudācārā taṇhāpavattiyo. Ajjhattikassa upādāyāti ajjhattikaṃ khandhapañcakaṃ upādāya; tattha kañci dhammaṃ anavakāriṃ katvā samūhato gahetvā taṇhāmānadiṭṭhivasena ‘asmī’ti hoti, tasmiṃ sati ‘khattiyo ahaṃ’ti anupanidhāya ‘itthasmī’ti, ‘yathā so khattiyo tathā aha’nti samato upanidhāya ‘evasmī’ti, ‘tato hīno vā seṭṭho vā aha’nti asamato upanidhāya ‘aññathāsmī’ti hoti; bhavissantiādīni anāgatavasena, asmi sātasmīti sassatucchedavasena, siyantiādīni saṃsayaparivitakkavasena, apāhaṃ siyantiādīni patthanākappanavasena vuttāni – dve diṭṭhisīsā, cattāro suddhasīsā, dvādasa sīsamūlakāti aṭṭhārasa. ## Dhammahadayavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-18|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tadanantare dhammahadayavibhaṅge pāḷiparicchedo tāva evaṃ veditabbo – ettha hi āditova khandhādīnaṃ dvādasannaṃ koṭṭhāsānaṃ vasena sabbasaṅgāhikavāro nāma vutto, dutiyo tesaṃyeva dhammānaṃ kāmadhātuādīsu uppattānuppattidassanavāro, tatiyo tattheva pariyāpannāpariyāpannadassanavāro, catuttho tīsu bhūmīsu uppattikkhaṇe vijjamānāvijjamānadhammadassanavāro, pañcamo tesaṃ dhammānaṃ bhūmantaravasena dassanavāro, chaṭṭho gatīsu uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇadassanavāro, sattamo abhiññeyyādivāro, aṭṭhamo sārammaṇānārammaṇavāro, navamo khandhādidhammānaṃ diṭṭhasutādivasena saṅgahetvā dassanavāro, dasamo kusalattikādivasena saṅgahetvā dassanavāroti evaṃ dasahi vārehi pāḷi paricchinnā. Yathāpucchaṃ vissajjanañhettha sabbaññubyākaraṇaṃ nāma; khandhādīnaṃ pana pabhedo khandhavibhaṅgādīsu vuttanayeneva veditabbo. Iminā ca vibhaṅgena aṭṭhārasahi vibhaṅgehi maṇḍitaṃ vibhaṅgapakaraṇaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ.

### Sabbasaṅgāhikavāro

[[#^toc-18-1|↑↑↑]]

Paṭhame sabbasaṅgāhikavāre khandhā, āyatanāni, dhātuyo, saccāni, indriyāni, hetū, āhārā, phassā, vedanā, saññā, cetanā, cittānīti dvādasa koṭṭhāsā pucchitvā vissajjitā. Tattha "avīcito yāva bhavaggaṃ etthantare kati khandhā"ti pucchite "ekoti vā…pe… cattāroti vā chāti vā avatvā pañcāti vattuṃ samattho añño natthī"ti attano ñāṇabalaṃ dīpento bhagavā "pañcakkhandhā"ti pucchānurūpaṃ vissajjanaṃ āha; yathāpucchaṃ vissajjanañhi sabbaññubyākaraṇaṃ nāmāti vuccati. "Dvādasāyatanāni, aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo, cattāri saccāni, bāvīsatindriyāni, nava hetū, cattāro āhārā, satta phassā, satta vedanā, satta saññā, satta cetanā, satta cittānī"tiādīsupi eseva nayo. Rūpakkhandhādīnaṃ pabhedo khandhavibhaṅgādīsu vuttanayeneva veditabbo.

### Uppattānuppattivāro

[[#^toc-18-2|↑↑↑]]

Dutiyavāre ye dhammā kāmabhave kāmadhātusambhūtānañca sattānaṃ uppajjanti – kāmadhātuyaṃ pariyāpannā vā apariyāpannā vā – te sabbe saṅgahetvā "kāmadhātuyā pañcakkhandhā"tiādi vuttaṃ; rūpadhātuādīsupi eseva nayo. Yasmā pana rūpadhātupariyāpannānaṃ sattānaṃ ghānāyatanādīnaṃ abhāvena gandhāyatanādīni āyatanādikiccaṃ na karonti, tasmā "rūpadhātuyā cha āyatanāni, nava dhātuyo"tiādi vuttaṃ. Yasmā ca okāsavasena vā sattuppattivasena vā apariyāpannadhātu nāma natthi, tasmā "apariyāpannadhātuyā"ti avatvā yaṃ yaṃ apariyāpannaṃ taṃ tadeva dassetuṃ "apariyāpanne kati khandhā"tiādi vuttaṃ.

### Pariyāpannāpariyāpannavāro

[[#^toc-18-3|↑↑↑]]

Tatiyavāre "pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kati kāmadhātupariyāpannā, kati na kāmadhātupariyāpannā…pe… sattannaṃ cittānaṃ kati kāmadhātupariyāpannā, kati na kāmadhātupariyāpannā"ti kāmadhātu-rūpadhātu-arūpadhātuvasena ceva pariyāpannāpariyāpannavasena ca pucchā katā. Tattha kāmadhātupariyāpannāti kāmadhātubhajanaṭṭhena pariyāpannā; taṃnissitā tadantogadhā kāmadhātutveva saṅkhaṃ gatāti attho. Sesapadesupi eseva nayo. Pariyāpannāti bhavavasena okāsavasena ca paricchinnā; apariyāpannāti tathā aparicchinnā.

### Dhammadassanavāro – Bhūmantaradassanavāro

[[#^toc-18-4|↑↑↑]]

Catutthavāre kāmadhātuyā, rūpadhātuyā, asaññasattānaṃ, arūpadhātuyā ca upapattikkhaṇe vijjamānāvijjamānā dhammā dassitā. Tattha ekādasāyatanānīti saddāyatanavajjāni; tañhi ekantena paṭisandhiyaṃ nuppajjati. Iminā nayena sabbattha attho veditabbo. Sattake "devānaṃ asurāna"nti gativasena avatvā avisesena "gabbhaseyyakāna"nti vuttaṃ; tasmā yattha yattha gabbhaseyyakā sambhavanti tattha tattha tesaṃ sattāyatanāni veditabbāni, tathā dhātuyo. Sesamettha uttānatthameva. Pañcamavāre pana tesaṃyeva dhammānaṃ kāmāvacarādibhūmantaravasena pariyāpannāpariyāpannavasena ca dassanaṃ kataṃ; tattha yaṃ vattabbaṃ taṃ dhammasaṅgahaṭṭhakathāyaṃ vuttameva.

### Uppādakakammaāyuppamāṇavāro

[[#^toc-18-5|↑↑↑]]

Chaṭṭhavāre gatīsu uppādakakammañca āyuppamāṇañca dassitaṃ – kena kammena kattha upapajjanti, upapannānañca kittakaṃ āyuppamāṇanti. Catūsu pana apāyesu bhummadevesu ca kammameva pamāṇaṃ; yāva kammaṃ na khīyati, na tāva cavanti.

#### Uppādakakammaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Uppādakakamme pañcahi kāmaguṇehi nānappakārehi vā iddhivisesehi dibbantīti devā; te sammutidevā upapattidevā visuddhidevāti tividhā. Sammutidevāti lokasammutiyā devā – rājāno deviyo kumārā; upapattidevāti devaloke uppannattā upapattiyā devā; visuddhidevāti sabbesaṃ devānaṃ pūjārahā sabbakilesavisuddhiyā devā, arahanto. Dānaṃ datvā, sīlaṃ samādiyitvā, cātuddasādīsu aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgataṃ uposathaṃ upavasitvā appekacce khattiyamahāsālādīnaṃ manussānaṃ, appekacce cātumahārājikādīnaṃ channaṃ kāmāvacaradevānaṃ sahabyataṃ upapajjanti; yasmā dānādipuññakammaṃ manussasobhagyatāya paccayo, adhimattabhāvepi ca nānappakārabhedato nānappakārassa khattiyamahāsālādibhāvassa paccayo, tasmā tassa vasena upapattibhedo dassito. Sahabyatanti sahabhāvaṃ, sabhāgā hutvā nibbattantīti attho. Tuṭṭhā pahaṭṭhāti tusitā; yathārucite bhoge nimminitvā ramantīti nimmānaratī; parehi nimmitesu bhogesu vasaṃ vattentīti paranimmitavasavattī.

#### Āyuppamāṇaṃ

[[#^toc-18-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyuppamāṇe manussānaṃ vassasataṃ appaṃ vā bhiyyoti dutiyaṃ vassasataṃ appatvā vīsāya vā tiṃsāya vā…pe… saṭṭhiyā vā vassehi adhikampi vassasataṃ. Brahmapārisajjā brahmapurohitā mahābrahmāno paṭhamajjhānabhūmiyaṃ, parittābhā appamāṇābhā ābhassarā dutiyajjhānabhūmiyaṃ, parittasubhā appamāṇasubhā subhakiṇhā tatiyajjhānabhūmiyaṃ ekatale vasanti; āyuantaraṃ pana nesaṃ nānā. Catutthajjhāne ārammaṇamanasikārachandapaṇidhiadhimokkhābhinīhārapaññānānattatāya upapattinānattaṃ hoti. Asaññasattā pana ekacce titthāyatane pabbajitvā "cittaṃ nissāya rajjanadussanamuyhanāni nāma hontī"ti citte dosaṃ disvā "acittakabhāvo nāma sobhano, diṭṭhadhammanibbānameta"nti saññāvirāgaṃ janetvā tatrūpagaṃ samāpattiṃ bhāvetvā tattha nibbattanti, pañca kappasatāni ṭhatvā kāmāvacarasaññuppādena tato cavanti. Manussesu gihibhāve ṭhitāyeva sotāpannāpi honti, sakadāgāmiphalampi anāgāmiphalampi arahattaphalampi pāpuṇanti. Aṭṭhasamāpattilābhīnaṃ paguṇajjhānaṃ, sabbesu paguṇesu patthanā, patthanāya asati maraṇasamaye samāpannā samāpatti upapattiṃ niyameti. Vehapphalā, akaniṭṭhā, nevasaññānāsaññāyatananti ime tayo devalokā seṭṭhabhavā nāma; tattha nibbattaanāgāmino tattha tattheva parinibbāyanti. Avasāne ca "natthi koci bhavo nicco"ti dīpetvā jarāmaraṇamokkhāya maggabhāvanā thomitā.

### Abhiññeyyādivāro

[[#^toc-18-6|↑↑↑]]

Sattamādīsu catūsu vāresu sattamavāre salakkhaṇapariggāhikāya abhiññāya vasena abhiññeyyatā veditabbā, ñātatīraṇapahānapariññānaṃ vasena pariññeyyatā; sā ca "rūpakkhandho abhiññeyyo pariññeyyo na pahātabbo"tiādīsu ñātatīraṇapariññāvaseneva, "samudayasaccaṃ abhiññeyyaṃ pariññeyyaṃ pahātabba"ntiādīsu pahānapariññāvasena veditabbā. Aṭṭhamavāre rūpādiārammaṇānaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādīnaṃ vasena sārammaṇānārammaṇatā veditabbā. Navamavāre khandhādidhammā diṭṭhasutamutaviññātavasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; so uttānatthoyeva. Dasamavāre kusalattikavedanāttikavipākattikaupādinnattikavitakkattikānaṃ rūpadukalokiyadukānañca vasena saṅgahetvā dassitā; tattha yaṃ vattabbaṃ siyā taṃ sabbaṃ tattha tattha pañhāpucchakavāre vuttameva. Ettāvatā dhammahadayavibhaṅgavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.
