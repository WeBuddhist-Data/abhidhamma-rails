## Uddeso

[[#^toc-1|↑↑↑]]

Vibhaṅgaṃ desayitvā tasseva anantaraṃ bhagavā dhātubhedappakāsanaṃ dhātukathaṃ adesayi. Idaṃ pakaraṇaṃ saṅgaho asaṅgahotiādīnaṃ vasena cuddasavidhena vibhattaṃ, taṃ sabbampi uddesaniddesato dvidhā ṭhitaṃ. Tattha mātikā uddeso, sā pañcavidhā – nayamātikā, abbhantaramātikā, nayamukhamātikā, lakkhaṇamātikā, bāhiramātikāti. Saṅgaho cettha jātisañjātikiriyāgaṇanavasena catubbidho; tesu "cakkhāyatanaṃ rūpakkhandhagaṇanaṃ gacchatī"ti vuttanayena gaṇanasaṅgaho idha adhippeto, tappaṭipakkhena asaṅgaho veditabbo. Ekuppādaekanirodhaekavatthukaekārammaṇatāvasena sampayogo, tappaṭipakkhato vippayogo; tesaṃ vikappato saṅgahitena asaṅgahitādīni, tadubhayasaṃsaggavikappato saṅgahitena sampayuttaṃ vippayuttantiādīni veditabbāni.

### Nayamātikā

[[#^toc-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Saṅgaho asaṅgaho…pe… vippayuttena saṅgahitaṃ asaṅgahitanti ayaṃ cuddasahi padehi nikkhittā nayamātikā nāma. Iminā saṅgahādikena nayena dhātukathā dhammā vibhattāti dassetuṃ ṭhapitattā nayamātikāti vuccati; etesaṃ padānaṃ imassa pakaraṇassa mūlabhūtattā nissayabhūtattā mūlamātikātipi vattuṃ vaṭṭati. Khandhādīnaṃ desanā nīyati pavattīyati etehi, khandhādayo eva vā nīyanti ñāyanti etehi pakārehīti nayā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-3]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-4]]

### Abbhantaramātikā

[[#^toc-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañcakkhandhā…pe… manasikāroti ayaṃ pañcavīsādhikena padasatena nikkhittā abbhantaramātikā nāma. Ayañhi saṅgahādinā nayena vibhajitabbe khandhādidhamme sarūpato dassetvā dhātukathāya abbhantareyeva ṭhapitattā abbhantaramātikāti vuccati; khandhādipadānaṃ dhammasaṅgaṇīmātikāya asaṅgahitattā pakiṇṇakamātikātipi vattuṃ vaṭṭati. Pañcakkhandhādīni khandhavibhaṅgādīsu vuttanayeneva veditabbāni; phassādayo pana sabbacittuppādasādhāraṇato vuttā, adhimokkho ca asādhāraṇesu mahāvisayattā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-4]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-8]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-5]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-13]]

### Nayamukhamātikā

[[#^toc-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Tīhi saṅgaho, tīhi asaṅgaho, catūhi sampayogo, catūhi vippayogoti ayaṃ catūhi padehi nikkhittā nayamukhamātikā nāma. Sabbesupi pañcakkhandhādīsu ceva kusalattikādīsu ca mātikādhammesu tīhi khandhāyatanadhātupadeheva saṅgaho ca asaṅgaho ca yojetabbo, tathā catūhi arūpakkhandhehi sampayogo ca vippayogo ca. Etāni saṅgahāsaṅgahādīnaṃ nayānaṃ mukhānīti dassetuṃ ṭhapitattā nayamukhamātikāti vuccati. Rūpanibbānehi vippayogo bhavantopi catūheva khandhehi hoti; na hi rūpaṃ rūpena nibbānena vā vippayuttaṃ hoti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-5]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-7]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-8]]

### Lakkhaṇamātikā

[[#^toc-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Sabhāgo, visabhāgoti ayaṃ dvīhi padehi nikkhittā lakkhaṇamātikā nāma. Sabhāgalakkhaṇehi dhammehi saṅgahanayo, visabhāgalakkhaṇehi asaṅgahanayo, tathā sampayogavippayoganayo yojetabboti sabhāgavisabhāgalakkhaṇavasena saṅgahādilakkhaṇaṃ dassetuṃ ṭhapitattā lakkhaṇamātikāti vuccati. Yena dhammā gaṇanaṃ gacchanti, so ruppanādiko samānabhāvo saṅgahe sabhāgatā, ekuppādādiko sampayoge veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-6]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-9]]

### Bāhiramātikā

[[#^toc-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Sabbāpi dhammasaṅgaṇī dhātukathāya mātikāti ayaṃ chasaṭṭhi tikapadāni dve ca dukapadasatāni saṅkhipitvā nikkhittā bāhiramātikā nāma. Ayañhi "pañcakkhandhā…pe… manasikāro"ti evaṃ dhātukathāya abbhantare avatvā "sabbāpi dhammasaṅgaṇī"ti evaṃ dhātukathāya mātikato bahi ṭhapitattā bāhiramātikāti vuccati. Evaṃ mātikāya pañcadhā ṭhitabhāvo veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-7]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-10]]

## Paṭhamanayo

[[#^toc-2|↑↑↑]]

Idāni pañcakkhandhādivasena nikkhittamātikaṃ "saṅgaho asaṅgaho"tiādīhi nayamātikāpadehi saddhiṃ yojetvā dassetuṃ niddesavāro āraddho. Tattha paṭhamo nayo saṅgahāsaṅgahapadaniddeso nāma: abbhantaramātikāya khandhādīnaṃ, tato bāhiramātikāya kusalattikādīnaṃ saṅgaho ca asaṅgaho ca tīhi khandhāyatanadhātupadeheva dassito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-19]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-20]]

### Saṅgahāsaṅgahapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Yasmā nayamātikāya "tīhi saṅgaho, tīhi asaṅgaho"ti nayamukhamātikā ṭhapitā, tasmā rūpakkhandhādīnaṃ saṅgahaṃ dassetuṃ "katihi khandhehi katihāyatanehi katihi dhātūhī"ti tīṇi khandhāyatanadhātupadāneva uddhaṭāni, "cattāri saccānī"tiādīsu ekampi na parāmaṭṭhaṃ. Yasmā ca "sabhāgo visabhāgo"ti lakkhaṇamātikā ṭhapitā, tasmā sabhāgehi saṅgaho, visabhāgehi asaṅgaho vissajjito. Khandhāyatanadhātuyo hi abhiññeyyadhammabhāvena vuttā; tasmā saṅgahāsaṅgahaviseso abhiññeyyanissito vuccamāno suviññeyyo hoti. Iminā nayena sabbapadesu saṅgahāsaṅgaho veditabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-11]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-16]]

#### Khandho

[[#^toc-2-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandho ekena khandhenāti rūpakkhandheneva; yañhi kiñci rūpaṃ rūpakkhandhasabhāgattā rūpakkhandhotveva saṅgahaṃ gacchati. Ekādasahāyatanehīti manāyatanavajjehi, sabbopi hi rūpakkhandho dasāyatanāni dhammāyatanekadeso ca hoti. Ekādasahi dhātūhīti sattaviññāṇadhātuvajjāhi; etāsu hi apariyāpannaṃ rūpaṃ nāma natthi. Asaṅgahanaye pana yasmā rūpakkhandhassa visabhāgā cattāro arūpakkhandhā, ekaṃ manāyatanaṃ, satta viññāṇadhātuyo, tasmā "catūhi khandhehi ekenāyatanena sattahi dhātūhi asaṅgahito"ti vuttaṃ. Imasmiṃ khandhaniddese ekamūlake pañca pucchā pañca vissajjanāni, rūpakkhandhamūlakāyeva dukatikacatukkā dassitā, pañcake pana bhedato ca abhedato ca dvidhā pucchāvissajjanāni katāni.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-11]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-17]]

#### Āyatanaṃ

[[#^toc-2-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyatanapadaniddese cakkhāyatanaṃ ekena rūpakkhandheneva, ekena cakkhāyataneneva, ekāya cakkhudhātuyāva saṅgahitaṃ; sotāyatanādīsupi imināva nayena saṅgahāsaṅgaho veditabbo. "Asaṅkhataṃ khandhato ṭhapetvā"ti vacanaṃ pana yasmā asaṅkhataṃ dhammāyatanaṃ nāma nibbānaṃ, tañca khandhasaṅgahaṃ na gacchati, tasmā vuttaṃ. Nibbānavajjaṃ dhammāyatanaṃ catūhi rūpavedanāsaññāsaṅkhārakkhandhehi saṅgahitaṃ, viññāṇakkhandhena pana sesāyatanadhātūhi ca na saṅgayhati. Yathā heṭṭhā rūpakkhandhamūlakā, evamidhāpi cakkhāyatanamūlakāva nayā veditabbā; dukamattameva pana pāḷiyaṃ dassetvā "dvādasāyatanānī"ti abhedatova pucchāvissajjanaṃ kataṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-12]]

#### Dhātu

[[#^toc-2-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Dhātuniddesepi āyatananiddese vuttanayova: cakkhudhātuādayo ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā saṅgahitā, visabhāgehi asaṅgahitā; dukamattameva pāḷiyaṃ dassetvā "aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo"ti abhedato pucchāvissajjanaṃ kataṃ. Rūpakkhandhassa ekādasahi dhātūhi saṅgahe sattaviññāṇadhātuvajjānaṃ vuttattā satta viññāṇadhātuyo arūpapakkhikā, sesā ekādasa dhātuyo rūpapakkhikā ceva dhammadhātuekadeso cāti veditabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-12]]

#### Saccaṃ

[[#^toc-2-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Saccaniddese sabbepi dukatikacatukkā pāḷiyaṃ dassitā. Yasmā ca dukatikesu samudayasaccasadisameva maggasaccepi vissajjanaṃ, tasmā taṃ samudayānantaraṃ vuttaṃ; dukkhasamudayadukkhamaggadukānañhi aññamaññaṃ tikena ca sadisaṃ vissajjanaṃ, tasmā maggasaccaṃ samudayānantaraṃ gahitaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-13]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-18]]

#### Indriyaṃ

[[#^toc-2-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Indriyaniddese jīvitindriyaṃ dvīhi khandhehīti rūpajīvitindriyaṃ rūpakkhandhena, arūpajīvitindriyaṃ saṅkhārakkhandhena saṅgahitaṃ. Sesaṃ vuttanayānusāreneva veditabbaṃ. Pāḷivavatthānaṃ panettha āyatanadhātuniddesasadisameva.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-14]]

#### Paṭiccasamuppādādi

[[#^toc-2-1-6|↑↑↑]]

Paṭiccasamuppādaniddese pucchaṃ anārabhitvā "avijjā ekena khandhenā"ti evaṃ vissajjanameva dassitaṃ. Saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇanti paṭisandhiyaṃ pavatte ca sabbampi vipākaviññāṇaṃ ⚑, tenāha "sattahi dhātūhi saṅgahita"nti; nāmarūpampi paṭisandhipavattivaseneva veditabbaṃ, tenevettha saddāyatanampi saṅgahetvā ekādasahāyatanehi saṅgaho dassito. Taṇhāupādānakammabhavā saṅkhārakkhandheneva saṅgahitā; bhavapadañcettha ekādasadhā vibhattaṃ, upapattibhavādayo ca upādinnakadhammāva, tasmā saddāyatanaṃ anupādinnaṃ ettha na gahitaṃ. Jāti dvīhi khandhehīti rūpajāti rūpakkhandhena, arūpajāti saṅkhārakkhandhena, jarāmaraṇesupi eseva nayo; sokadukkhadomanassāni vedanākkhandhena, paridevo rūpakkhandhena, upāyāso saṅkhārakkhandhena saṅgahito. Tato paraṃ iddhipādo saṅkhāraviññāṇakkhandhehi, jhānaṃ vedanāsaṅkhārakkhandhehi saṅgahitaṃ; appamaññādayo sadisavissajjanattā ekato niddiṭṭhā, cittaṃ pana asadisavissajjanattā pacchā gahitaṃ.

##### Divergences

- ⚑ *saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ* — the aṭṭhakathā reads it as "all resultant consciousness" (*sabbampi vipākaviññāṇaṃ*, `atthakatha #^3-15`). The mūlaṭīkā says the word *vipāka* should not restrict it here, since in the Dhātukathā wholesome and other consciousness are also included under this term (*vipākaggahaṇena visesanaṃ na kātabbaṃ*, `mulatiika #^3-19`).

#### Tikaṃ (Paṭhamanayo)

[[#^toc-2-1-7|↑↑↑]]

Evaṃ abbhantaramātikāya saṅgahaṃ dassetvā idāni bāhiramātikāya saṅgahaṃ dassetuṃ "kusalā dhammā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Vedanāttike tīhi dhātūhīti kāyaviññāṇamanoviññāṇadhammadhātūhi; sattahi dhātūhīti cakkhusotaghānajivhāviññāṇadhātūhi ceva manodhātudhammadhātumanoviññāṇadhātūhi ca. Vipākattike aṭṭhahi dhātūhīti kāyaviññāṇadhātuyā saddhiṃ tāhiyeva. Vipākadhammadhammā pana saṃkiliṭṭhasaṃkilesikehi saddhiṃ sadisavissajjanattā ekato gahitā; evaṃ sabbatikapadesu yaṃ yaṃ padaṃ yena yena padena saddhiṃ sadisavissajjanaṃ hoti, taṃ taṃ uppaṭipāṭiyāpi tena tena saddhiṃ gahetvā vissajjitaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-19]]

#### Dukaṃ (Paṭhamanayo)

[[#^toc-2-1-8|↑↑↑]]

Dukapadesupi tikapadesu vuttanayeneva saṅgahāsaṅgahanayo veditabbo: yaṃ yaṃ dukapadaṃ yena yena padena saddhiṃ sadisavissajjanaṃ hoti, taṃ taṃ uppaṭipāṭiyāpi tena tena saddhiṃ gahetvā ekato vissajjitaṃ. "Asaṅkhataṃ khandhato ṭhapetvā"ti ettha ca yasmā asaṅkhataṃ nibbānaṃ dhammāyatanadhammadhātūhi saṅgahitampi khandhasaṅgahaṃ na gacchati, tasmā tathā vuttaṃ. Ettāvatā saṅgahāsaṅgahapadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-12]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-19]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-20]]

## Dutiyanayo

[[#^toc-3|↑↑↑]]

Idāni dutiyo nayo saṅgahitenaasaṅgahitapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Paṭhamanayo saṅgāhakāsaṅgāhakanirapekkhānaṃ gaṇetabbāgaṇetabbānaṃ taṃtaṃgaṇanehi gaṇanadassanamattameva; dutiyādayo pana nayā saṅgāhakāsaṅgāhakehi saṅgahitāsaṅgahitānaṃ agaṇanādidassanāni. Imasmiñhi vāre yaṃ khandhapadena saṅgahitaṃ hutvā āyatanadhātupadehi asaṅgahitaṃ, khandhāyatanapadehi vā saṅgahitaṃ hutvā dhātupadena asaṅgahitaṃ, tassa khandhādīhi asaṅgaho pucchitvā vissajjito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-21]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-25]]

### Saṅgahitenaasaṅgahitapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-3-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandhena hi rūpakkhandhova saṅgahito, so ca aḍḍhekādasahi āyatanadhātūhi asaṅgahito nāma natthi; vedanākkhandhenapi vedanākkhandhova saṅgahito, sopi dhammāyatanadhammadhātūhi asaṅgahito nāma natthi. Evaṃ asaṅgahitatāya abhāvato manāyatanadhammāyatanādīni padāni imasmiṃ vāre na gahitāni ⚑; yāni pana padāni rūpekadesaṃ arūpena asammissaṃ, viññāṇekadesañca aññena asammissaṃ dīpenti, tāni idha gahitāni – dasāyatanā, sattarasa dhātuyo, sattindriyā, asaññābhavo ekavokārabhavo, paridevo, sanidassanasappaṭighaṃ, anidassanasappaṭighaṃ, sanidassanaṃ, sappaṭighaṃ upādā cāti. Sadisavissajjanāni samodhānetvā aṭṭha pañhā katā. Tatrāyaṃ nayo: cakkhāyatanena khandhasaṅgahena rūpakkhandho saṅgahito; tasmiṃ āyatanasaṅgahena cakkhāyatanamevekaṃ saṅgahitaṃ, sesāni dasa āyatanāni asaṅgahitāni, tāni cakkhāyatanamanāyatanehi dvīhi asaṅgahitāni; yāpi dasa dhātuyo asaṅgahitā, tā cakkhudhātuyā ceva sattahi ca viññāṇadhātūhi asaṅgahitā. Tasmā "catūhi khandhehi dvīhāyatanehi aṭṭhahi dhātūhi asaṅgahitā"ti vuttaṃ. Viññāṇadhātupañhe yāya kāyaci viññāṇadhātuyā saṅgahito viññāṇakkhandho manāyatanena asaṅgahito nāma natthi; asaññābhavaekavokārabhavesu rūpāyatanadhammāyatanavasena dveva āyatanāni saṅgahitāni. Ettāvatā saṅgahitenaasaṅgahitapadaniddeso niṭṭhito.

#### Divergences

- ⚑ *why the whole aggregates are left out of the second method* — the aṭṭhakathā gives the reason as the absence of anything excluded (*asaṅgahitatāya abhāvato*, `atthakatha #^3-21`): the aggregate of form includes only itself, and that is never excluded by bases and elements. The mūlaṭīkā says that reason does not fit, since the aggregate of form includes nothing at all; the right reason is that it is not an includer (*saṅgāhakattābhāvo evettha aggahaṇe kāraṇanti yuttaṃ*, `mulatiika #^3-29`).

## Tatiyanayo

[[#^toc-4|↑↑↑]]

Idāni tatiyo nayo asaṅgahitenasaṅgahitapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Imasmiñhi vāre yaṃ khandhapadena asaṅgahitaṃ hutvā āyatanadhātupadehi saṅgahitaṃ, tassa khandhādīhi saṅgaho pucchitvā vissajjito. Dutiyatatiyanayā tiṇṇaṃ saṅgahānaṃ saṅgahaṇāsaṅgahaṇappavattivisesena "saṅgahitena asaṅgahitaṃ, asaṅgahitena saṅgahita"nti uddiṭṭhā.

### Asaṅgahitenasaṅgahitapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-4-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandhena cattāro khandhā khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā, tesu tena ekadhammopi āyatanadhātusaṅgahena saṅgahito nāma natthi; rūpakkhandhato hi sukhumarūpamattaṃ dhammāyatanaṃ bhajati ⚑. Viññāṇakkhandhenapi itare cattāro khandhā asaṅgahitā, tesu tena ekopi āyatanadhātusaṅgahena saṅgahito natthi. Evaṃ saṅgahitatāya abhāvato rūpakkhandhaviññāṇakkhandhacakkhāyatanādīni padāni imasmiṃ vāre na gahitāni; yāni pana padāni viññāṇena vā oḷārikarūpena vā asammissaṃ dhammāyatanekadesaṃ dīpenti, tāni idha gahitāni – tayo khandhā, tathā saccā, soḷasa indriyāni, paccayākāre cuddasa padāni, upari cuddasa, gocchakesu samatiṃsa, dve cūḷantaradukā, aṭṭha mahantaradukāti. Sadisavissajjanāni ekato katvā dvādasa pañhā vuttā; tesu khandhavibhāgova hoti, āyatanadhātūsu pana bhedo natthi, sabbattha dhammāyatanadhammadhātuvaseneva veditabbā. Tatrāyaṃ nayo: vedanākkhandhena nibbānañca sukhumarūpasaññāsaṅkhārā ca khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā hutvā āyatanadhātusaṅgahena saṅgahitā; tesu nibbānaṃ khandhasaṅgahaṃ na gacchati, āyatanadhātusaṅgahaṃ pana gacchateva. Tena vuttaṃ "asaṅkhataṃ khandhato ṭhapetvā tīhi khandhehi ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā saṅgahitā"ti. Jīvitindriye vedanāsaññāviññāṇakkhandhā khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā, tesu pana vedanāsaññāva āyatanadhātusaṅgahena saṅgahitā; iminā upāyena sabbattha khandhabhedo veditabbo.

#### Divergences

- ⚑ *"ye dhammāyatanena saṅgahitā"* — the aṭṭhakathā argues that states "included by the mind-object base" are not thereby included by the aggregate of form (`atthakatha #^3-33`). The mūlaṭīkā says this cannot be said (*taṃ na sakkā vattuṃ*): the mind-object base includes no state by any inclusion, being a mixed group of unlike aggregates and *nibbāna*; the phrase must be read as "counted by the count of the mind-object base" (*dhammāyatanagaṇanena gaṇitāti attho*, `mulatiika #^3-33`).

## Catutthanayo

[[#^toc-5|↑↑↑]]

Idāni catuttho nayo saṅgahitenasaṅgahitapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Tattha yaṃ khandhādīhi saṅgahitena khandhādivasena saṅgahitaṃ, puna tasseva khandhādīhi saṅgaho pucchitvā vissajjito. Catutthapañcamā nayā na dutiyatatiyā viya saṅgahaṇāsaṅgahaṇappavattivisesena uddiṭṭhā: saṅgahaṇappavattiyā eva saṅgahitena saṅgahitaṃ, asaṅgahaṇappavattiyā eva asaṅgahitena asaṅgahitaṃ uddiṭṭhaṃ.

### Saṅgahitenasaṅgahitapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Taṃ khandhāyatanadhātūsu ekampi sakalakoṭṭhāsaṃ gahetvā ṭhitapadesu na yujjati; sakalena hi khandhādipadena aññaṃ khandhādivasena saṅgahitaṃ nāma natthi, yaṃ attano saṅgāhakaṃ saṅgaṇhitvā puna teneva saṅgahaṃ gaccheyya. Yāni pana padāni saṅkhārekadesaṃ vā vedanekadesaṃ vā sukhumarūpaṃ vā saddekadesaṃ vā aññena asammissaṃ dīpenti, tāni idha gahitāni – dve saccā, pannarasa indriyāni, ekādasa paṭiccapadāni, upari ekādasa, gocchakapadāni tiṃsāti. Pañhā panettha dveyeva honti. Tatrāyaṃ nayo: samudayasaccena taṇhāvajjā sesā saṅkhārā khandhādisaṅgahena saṅgahitā, puna tehi taṇhāva saṅgahitā, sā taṇhā puna saṅkhāreheva saṅgahitā; tasmā "ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā saṅgahitā"ti vuttaṃ ⚑. Arūpadhammapucchāsu saṅkhārakkhandho vā vedanākkhandho vā eko khandho nāma, rūpadhammapucchāsu rūpakkhandho; paridevapucchāya saddāyatanaṃ ekaṃ āyatanaṃ, saddadhātu ekā dhātu, sesaṭṭhānesu dhammāyatanadhammadhātuvaseneva attho veditabbo.

#### Divergences

- ⚑ *"ekena khandhena"* — the aṭṭhakathā reads the answer as naming the states by which the raised term is included (*te dhamme sandhāya*, `atthakatha #^3-46`). The mūlaṭīkā says this cannot be said: what is asked here is simply inclusion, not inclusion by the includer, so "one aggregate" means one aggregate-count, not an includer (*ekena khandhagaṇanenāti ayamevettha attho, na saṅgāhakenāti*, `mulatiika #^3-36`).

## Pañcamanayo

[[#^toc-6|↑↑↑]]

Idāni pañcamo nayo asaṅgahitenaasaṅgahitapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Tattha yaṃ khandhādīhi asaṅgahitena khandhādivasena asaṅgahitaṃ, puna tasseva khandhādīhi asaṅgaho pucchitvā vissajjito. Ayaṃ saṅgahāsaṅgahanayānaṃ pariyosānaṃ.

### Asaṅgahitenaasaṅgahitapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-1|↑↑↑]]

Taṃ pañcakkhandhaggāhakesu dukkhasaccādīsu, viññāṇena saddhiṃ sukhumarūpaggāhakesu anidassanaappaṭighādīsu ca padesu na yujjati ⚑; yāni pana pañcakkhandhe viññāṇañca sukhumarūpena saddhiṃ ekato na dīpenti, tāni idha gahitāni. Sadisavissajjanānaṃ vasena samodhānetvā katehi saddhiṃ sabbepi catuttiṃsa pañhā honti. Tatrāyaṃ nayo: rūpakkhandhena cattāro khandhā nibbānañca khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā; āyatanadhātusaṅgahena pana ṭhapetvā viññāṇaṃ avasesā saṅgahitāti viññāṇameva tīhipi saṅgahehi asaṅgahitaṃ. Puna tena viññāṇena saddhiṃ nibbānena cattāro khandhā asaṅgahitā, te sabbepi puna viññāṇeneva asaṅgahitā. Viññāṇañca khandhato eko viññāṇakkhandho, āyatanato ekaṃ manāyatanaṃ, dhātuto satta viññāṇadhātuyo; tasmā "ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena sattahi dhātūhi asaṅgahitā"ti vuttaṃ. Dutiyapañhe rūpaviññāṇānaṃ vasena, tatiyapañhe viññāṇassa asaṅgāhakānaṃ rūpādīnaṃ catunnaṃ vasena, catutthapañhe cakkhāyatanassa asaṅgāhakānaṃ vedanādīnaṃ catunnaṃ vasena khandhādayo veditabbā. Iminā upāyena sabbattha yaṃ pucchāya uddhaṭaṃ padaṃ, tadeva yehi dhammehi khandhādivasena asaṅgahitaṃ, tesaṃ vasena khandhādayo veditabbā.

#### Divergences

- ⚑ *why the dukkhasacca-type terms are left out* — the aṭṭhakathā says that with such a term *nibbāna* alone would fail of aggregate-inclusion, and nothing else would be unincluded (`atthakatha #^3-48`). The mūlaṭīkā limits that reason to the truth of suffering: for the invisible-without-impact type of term, *nibbāna* is itself inside the excluding group, and a thing is not its own excluder (*taṃ dukkhasaccaṃ sandhāya vuttaṃ*, `mulatiika #^3-39`).

## Chaṭṭhanayo

[[#^toc-7|↑↑↑]]

Idāni chaṭṭho nayo sampayogavippayogapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. "Catūhi sampayogo, catūhi vippayogo; sabhāgo visabhāgo"ti vacanato catūhi arūpakkhandheheva sabhāgānaṃ ekasantānasmiṃ ekakkhaṇe uppannānaṃ arūpakkhandhānaṃyeva aññamaññaṃ sampayogo labbhati. Ayaṃ visabhāgatā saṅgahanaye natthi, gaṇanūpagamattañhi saṅgahaṭṭho; sampayoganaye pana atthi, ekuppādatādilakkhaṇañhi sampayogaṭṭho.

### Sampayogavippayogapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Tattha yaṃ labbhati yañca na labbhati, taṃ sabbaṃ pucchāya gahitaṃ; vissajjane pana yaṃ na labbhati, taṃ "natthī"ti paṭikkhittaṃ. Rūpadhammānaṃ rūpena nibbānena vā, nibbānassa ca rūpena saddhiṃ sampayogo nāma natthi, tathā rūpanibbānānaṃ arūpakkhandhehi; bhinnasantānehi nānākkhaṇikehi arūpadhammehipi saddhiṃ natthiyeva. Yāni pana padāni sattasu viññāṇadhātūsu ekāyapi avippayutte rūpena nibbānena vā missakadhamme dīpenti, tāni sabbathāpi idha na yujjantīti na gahitāni – dhammāyatanaṃ, dhammadhātu, dukkhasaccaṃ, jīvitaṃ, saḷāyatanaṃ, nāmarūpaṃ, cattāro mahābhavā, jātijarāmaraṇaṃ, tikesu ekūnavīsati, gocchakesu paññāsa, cūḷantare aṭṭha, mahantare pannarasa, tato pare aṭṭhārasāti tevīsapadasataṃ ⚑. Sesāni khandhādīni yujjantīti tāni gahetvā ekekavasena ca samodhānena ca pañhavissajjanaṃ kataṃ. Ādipañhasmiṃ sarūpato dassetvā parato pāḷi saṃkhittā.

#### Saccādi

[[#^toc-7-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Samudayamaggasaccapañhe tīhi khandhehīti saṅkhārakkhandhaṃ ṭhapetvā sesehi; ekāya dhātuyāti manoviññāṇadhātuyā, samudayamaggānañhi aññāya dhātuyā sampayogo natthi; ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyāti saṅkhārakkhandhadhammāyatanadhammadhātūhi, etesu hi taṃ saccadvayaṃ kehici sampayuttaṃ. Vibhāgarahitehi vedanākkhandhādīhi vippayogavacanaṃ na yuttanti samudayasaccaṃ tehi sampayuttattena vuttaṃ, na vippayuttattena. Sukhindriyādipañhe tīhīti saññāsaṅkhāraviññāṇehi; ekāya dhātuyāti kāyaviññāṇadhātuyā manoviññāṇadhātuyā ca. Rūpabhavapañhe sabbesampi arūpakkhandhānaṃ arūpāyatanānañca atthitāya "na kehicī"ti vuttaṃ, ghānajivhākāyaviññāṇadhātūnaṃ pana natthitāya "tīhi dhātūhi vippayutto"ti. Adhimokkhapañhe dvīhi dhātūhīti manodhātumanoviññāṇadhātūhi sampayutto, pannarasahīti sesāhi dasahi rūpadhātūhi pañcahi ca cakkhuviññāṇādīhi vippayutto.

#### Tikaṃ (Chaṭṭhanayo)

[[#^toc-7-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Kusalapañhe kusalehi catunnampi khandhānaṃ gahitattā sampayogo paṭikkhitto. Vedanāttikapañhe ekena khandhenāti vedanākkhandheneva; pannarasahīti cakkhusotaghānajivhāviññāṇadhātumanodhātūhi ceva rūpadhātūhi ca; ekādasahīti kāyaviññāṇadhātuyā saddhiṃ rūpadhātūhi. Avitakkavicāramattapañhe dutiyajjhānavicāraṃ ṭhapetvā sesā avitakkavicāramattā nāma, pītiṃ ṭhapetvā sesā pītisahagatā; vicāro vicārena, pīti ca pītiyā na sampayuttāti saṅkhārakkhandhadhammāyatanadhammadhātūsu kehici sampayuttā nāma ⚑. Parittārammaṇaṃ vipākadhammasadisaṃ; mahaggatārammaṇādayo kusalasadisāva. Anuppannesu pañcahi dhātūhīti cakkhuviññāṇādīhi, tāni hi ekantena uppādidhammabhūtāneva uppannakoṭṭhāsampi bhajanti; paccuppannārammaṇādayo parittārammaṇasadisāva.

##### Divergences

- ⚑ *avitakkavicāramattā* — the aṭṭhakathā takes the term to mean the states of the second jhāna-type apart from applied thought itself (*dutiyajjhānavicārañhi ṭhapetvā sesā*, `atthakatha #^3-71`). The mūlaṭīkā accepts this but adds that even if the term were taken to include applied-thought states, the whole group could still be called associated with *vicāra*, since none of it is unassociated with sustained thought (`mulatiika #^3-48`).

#### Dukaṃ (Chaṭṭhanayo)

[[#^toc-7-1-6|↑↑↑]]

Dukapadesu hetuādayo samudayasadisāva; sahetukā ceva na ca hetūpi pītisahagatasadisāva, tathā parāmāsasampayuttā; anupādinnā anuppannasadisāva. Sesaṃ sabbattha uttānatthameva. Dassanenapahātabbahetukesu pana keci saṅkhārakkhandhekadesena mohena sampayuttā, keci asampayuttāti na samudāyo tena sampayutto, nāpi añño koci dhammo atthi yena so samudāyo sampayutto siyā; tasmā "dassanenapahātabbahetukā dhammā sampayuttāti natthī"ti vuttaṃ. Ettāvatā sampayogavippayogapadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.

## Sattamanayo

[[#^toc-8|↑↑↑]]

Idāni sattamo nayo sampayuttenavippayuttapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Imasmiñhi vāre pucchāya uddhaṭapadena ye dhammā sampayuttā, tehi ye dhammā vippayuttā, tesaṃ khandhādīhi vippayogo pucchitvā vissajjito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-75]]

### Sampayuttenavippayuttapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-8-1|↑↑↑]]

Taṃ rūpakkhandhādīsu na yujjati, rūpakkhandhena hi sampayuttā nāma natthi; samudayasaccādīni pana satipi tehi sampayutte sampayuttenavippayuttānaṃ khandhādīhi vippayogābhāvato na gahitāni ⚑. Yāni pana padāni dhammadhātuyā sampayutte dhamme viññāṇañca aññena asammissaṃ dīpenti, tāni idha gahitāni – cattāro khandhā, ekaṃ manāyatanaṃ, dve indriyāni, satta dhātupadāni, tayo paṭiccapadā, phassasattakaṃ, tike tayo, mahantare satta, ekaṃ savitakkaṃ, ekaṃ savicāraṃ, upekkhāya yuttaṃ ekanti. Sadisavissajjanāni uppaṭipāṭiyāpi samodhānetvā vedanākkhandhādikā pañhā katā. Vedanākkhandhādipañhe ekenāti manāyatanena, sattahīti viññāṇadhātūhi, kehicīti dhammāyatane vedanādīhi. Viññāṇadhātupañhe te dhammā pucchāya uddhaṭaṃ viññāṇadhātuṃ ṭhapetvā sesā cha viññāṇadhātudhammā, rūpaṃ, nibbānañca; tehi sabbesaṃ khandhāyatanānaṃ saṅgahitattā na kehici khandhehi āyatanehi vā vippayuttā, ekāya dhātuyāti yā yā pucchāya uddhaṭā hoti tāya tāya. Upekkhindriyapañhe pañcahīti upekkhāsampayuttāhi cakkhuviññāṇadhātuādīhi. Iminā nayena sabbattha pucchāya uddhaṭapadeneva saddhiṃ vippayuttānaṃ vasena attho veditabbo.

#### Divergences

- ⚑ *which terms are left out of the seventh method* — the aṭṭhakathā names the aggregate of form and "others of this kind" (*evarūpāni*), on the ground that nothing is associated with them (`atthakatha #^3-75`). The mūlaṭīkā reads *evarūpāni* as "terms where the answer does not fit", not "terms with nothing associated", because the truth of origin and the like do have associated states yet are still left out; it adds that for the terms with sustained thought only (*avitakkavicāramatta*) it sees no reason for their omission (*aggahaṇe kāraṇaṃ na dissati*), and records an older reading of the uddāna (`mulatiika #^3-50`, `#^3-51`).

## Aṭṭhamanayo

[[#^toc-9|↑↑↑]]

Idāni aṭṭhamo nayo vippayuttenasampayuttapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Tattha sabbāpi pucchā moghapucchāva.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-81]]

### Vippayuttenasampayuttapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-9-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandhena hi vippayuttā nāma cattāro khandhā, tesaṃ aññehi sampayogo natthi; vedanākkhandhena vippayuttaṃ rūpaṃ nibbānañca, tassa ca kenaci sampayogo natthi. Evaṃ sabbapadesu vippayuttānaṃ sampayogābhāvo veditabbo. Tasmā pāḷiyaṃ "rūpakkhandhena ye dhammā vippayuttā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā sampayuttā"ti avatvā "rūpakkhandhena ye dhammā vippayuttā, te dhammā katihi khandhehi…pe… sampayuttāti natthī"ti vuttaṃ: yathāniddhāritaṃ vippayuttenasampayuttameva natthi, kuto tassa puna khandhādīhi sampayuttatā. Iti pucchāya moghattā sabbavissajjanesu "natthi natthi" icceva vuttaṃ.

## Navamanayo

[[#^toc-10|↑↑↑]]

Idāni navamo nayo sampayuttenasampayuttapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Tattha yaṃ khandhādivasena sampayuttaṃ, puna tasseva khandhādīhi sampayogo pucchitvā vissajjito: yaṃ rūpakkhandhādīsu araṇantesu yena vedanākkhandhādinā sampayuttaṃ, puna tasseva vedanākkhandhādino khandhādīhi sampayogo pucchito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-83]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-56]]

### Sampayuttenasampayuttapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-10-1|↑↑↑]]

Taṃ rūpena vā rūpamissakehi vā sabbarūpakkhandhasaṅgāhakehi vā padehi saddhiṃ na yujjati: rūpena hi rūpamissakena vā aññesaṃ sampayogo natthi; sabbārūpakkhandhasaṅgāhakehi sabbesaṃ sampayogārahānaṃ gahitattā aññaṃyeva natthi, yaṃ tena saha sampayogaṃ gaccheyya. Yāni pana padāni rūpena asammissaṃ arūpekadesaṃ dīpenti, tāni idha gahitāni – cattāro arūpakkhandhā, manāyatanaṃ, satta viññāṇadhātuyo, dve saccā, cuddasa indriyāni, paccaye dvādasa padāni, tato upari soḷasa, tikesu aṭṭha, gocchake tecattālīsa, mahantaraduke satta, piṭṭhidukesu chāti. Sabbapañhesu ye dhammā pucchāya uddhaṭā, te yehi sampayuttā honti, tesaṃ vasena khandhādibhedo veditabbo. Vedanākkhandhena hi itare tayo khandhā sampayuttā, puna tehi vedanākkhandho sampayutto; so tehi saññādīhi tīhi khandhehi, ekena manāyatanena, sattahi viññāṇadhātūhi, ekasmiṃ dhammāyatane dhammadhātuyā ca kehici saññāsaṅkhāreheva sampayutto. Eseva nayo sabbattha.

## Dasamanayo

[[#^toc-11|↑↑↑]]

Idāni dasamo nayo vippayuttenavippayuttapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Tattha ye sampayogavippayogapadaniddese rūpakkhandhādayo dhammā uddhaṭā, sabbapucchāsu teyeva uddhaṭā; sadisavissajjanānaṃ pana ekato gahitattā padāni aññāya paṭipāṭiyā āgatāni.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-89]]

### Vippayuttenavippayuttapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-11-1|↑↑↑]]

Tattha yaṃ padaṃ pucchāya uddhaṭaṃ, taṃ yehi dhammehi vippayuttaṃ, tesaṃ vasena khandhādivibhāgo veditabbo. Rūpakkhandhena hi vedanādayo vippayuttā, tehi ca rūpakkhandho vippayutto; nibbānaṃ pana sukhumarūpagatikameva. So rūpakkhandho catūhi khandhehi, ekena manāyatanena, sattahi viññāṇadhātūhi, dhammāyatanadhammadhātūsu kehici vedanādīhi dhammeheva vippayutto. Eseva nayo sabbattha. Aggahitesu pana dhammāyatanādidhammā anārammaṇamissakasabbacittuppādagatadhammabhāvato tehi vippayuttassa abhāvā na gahitā; dukkhasaccacatumahābhavaabyākatādidhammā tehi vippayuttehi vippayuttānaṃ khandhādīhi vippayogābhāvato na gahitā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-89]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-58]]

#### Tikaṃ (Dasamanayo)

[[#^toc-11-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Tikapadesupi sampayogavippayogapadaniddese uddhaṭāneva padāni sadisavissajjanānaṃ vasena ekato gahitāni. "Kusalehi dhammehi ye dhammā vippayuttā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā vippayuttā"ti ettha kusalā dhammā akusalābyākatehi vibhattā, te ca kusalehi; na tesaṃ samudāyekadesabhāvo, tasmā khandhādīni anāmasitvā vippayuttatāmattena kusalehi itaresaṃ, itarehi ca kusalānaṃ visabhāgatāsabbhāvato aññamaññavippayuttatā vuttā. Esa nayo sabbesu evarūpesu. Avitakkavicāramattesu pana tehi vippayuttehi vippayuttānaṃ soḷasahi dhātūhi vippayogo vutto.

#### Dukaṃ (Dasamanayo)

[[#^toc-11-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Dukapadesupi eseva nayo: yaṃ padaṃ pucchāya uddhaṭaṃ, taṃ yehi dhammehi vippayuttaṃ, tesaṃ vasena khandhādivibhāgo veditabbo, sadisavissajjanāni ca ekato gahitāni. Dukkhasaccasadisā pana anārammaṇamissakā samudāyā tehi vippayuttehi vippayuttānaṃ khandhādīhi vippayogābhāvato na gahitā. Ettāvatā vippayuttenavippayuttapadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.
