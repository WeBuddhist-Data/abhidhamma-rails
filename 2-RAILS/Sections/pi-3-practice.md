## Uddeso

[[#^toc-1|↑↑↑]]

Dhātukathāyaṃ sabbe dhammā saṅgahāsaṅgahasampayogavippayogavasena vibhattā. Saṅgaho cettha gaṇanasaṅgaho: "cakkhāyatanaṃ rūpakkhandhagaṇanaṃ gacchatī"ti yathā sabhāgadhammā ekagaṇanaṃ gacchanti, evaṃ yoginā attano cittasantāne uppannaṃ yaṃ kiñci dhammaṃ "ayaṃ katamakkhandhagaṇanaṃ gacchati, katamāyatanagaṇanaṃ, katamadhātugaṇana"nti gaṇetvā sallakkhetabbaṃ. Ekuppādaekanirodhaekavatthukaekārammaṇatāvasena sampayogo; tasmā yena cittena saddhiṃ ye dhammā ekato uppajjanti nirujjhanti, te sampayuttāti jānitvā akusalasampayutte dhamme pajahitvā kusalasampayutte bhāvetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-8]]

### Nayamātikā

[[#^toc-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Nayamātikāya cuddasahi padehi khandhādīnaṃ desanā nīyati, khandhādayo eva vā ñāyanti; ime nayā imassa pakaraṇassa mūlabhūtā nissayabhūtā. Tasmā yoginā "ayaṃ dhammo kena saṅgahito, kena asaṅgahito, kena sampayutto, kena vippayutto"ti imehi nayehi dhamme upaparikkhantena sattattābhāvena dhammamattameva passitabbaṃ, evaṃ passanto akusalaṃ na karoti, kusalaṃ vaḍḍheti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-3]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-4]]

### Abbhantaramātikā

[[#^toc-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Abbhantaramātikāyaṃ pañcakkhandhā dvādasāyatanāni aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo cattāri saccāni bāvīsatindriyāni paṭiccasamuppādo ca, satipaṭṭhānasammappadhānaiddhipādajhānaappamaññāindriyabalabojjhaṅgamaggā ca, phassavedanāsaññācetanācittaadhimokkhamanasikārā ca sarūpato dassitā. Phassādayo sabbacittuppādasādhāraṇato vuttā; tasmā yoginā yaṃ kiñci cittaṃ uppajjati, taṃ phassena vedanāya saññāya cetanāya manasikārena saddhiṃyeva uppajjatīti sallakkhetvā satipaṭṭhānādayo bodhipakkhiyadhamme bhāvetabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-4]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-8]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-13]]

### Nayamukhamātikā

[[#^toc-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Tīhi khandhāyatanadhātupadeheva saṅgaho ca asaṅgaho ca yojetabbo, catūhi arūpakkhandheheva sampayogo ca vippayogo ca. Na hi rūpaṃ rūpena nibbānena vā vippayuttaṃ hoti; sampayogo vippayogo ca catūhi nāmakkhandhehi eva hoti. Tasmā yoginā nāmadhammā aññamaññaṃ sampayujjanti, rūpaṃ pana tehi vippayuttanti nāmarūpaṃ vavatthapetvā, cittaṃ sampayuttadhammehi saha uppajjitvā saha nirujjhatīti passantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-5]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-7]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-8]]

### Lakkhaṇamātikā

[[#^toc-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Sabhāgalakkhaṇehi dhammehi saṅgaho, visabhāgalakkhaṇehi asaṅgaho; ruppanādiko samānabhāvo saṅgahe sabhāgatā, ekuppādādiko sampayoge. Tasmā yoginā dhamme salakkhaṇato pariggaṇhantena "ayaṃ ruppanalakkhaṇo, ayaṃ vediyanalakkhaṇo"ti sabhāgavisabhāgavasena vavatthapetabbaṃ; evaṃ salakkhaṇato pariggahitesu dhammesu attasaññā na patiṭṭhāti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-6]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-9]]

### Bāhiramātikā

[[#^toc-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Sabbāpi dhammasaṅgaṇī dhātukathāya mātikā: kusalattikādīni chasaṭṭhi tikapadāni dve ca dukapadasatāni imehi saṅgahādinayehi vibhajitabbāni. Tasmā yoginā dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ vuttā kusalākusalābyākatādayo sabbepi dhammā khandhāyatanadhātūhi saṅgahetvā passitabbā; evaṃ passanto akusalaṃ pajahati, kusalaṃ upasampādeti, sabbaṃ dhammaṃ khandhādimattato passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpeti.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-7]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-10]]

## Paṭhamanayo

[[#^toc-2|↑↑↑]]

Paṭhamanaye khandhādayo kusalādayo ca sabbe dhammā tīhi khandhāyatanadhātupadehi saṅgahitā asaṅgahitā ca dassitā. Tasmā yoginā yaṃ kiñci rūpaṃ vā arūpaṃ vā "idaṃ ettakehi khandhehi āyatanehi dhātūhi saṅgahitaṃ, ettakehi asaṅgahita"nti gaṇetvā, gaṇanamatte ṭhitaṃ dhammaṃ "mama"nti na gahetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-19]]

### Saṅgahāsaṅgahapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Khandhāyatanadhātuyo abhiññeyyadhammabhāvena vuttā; saccāni pariññeyyatādivisesadassanatthaṃ, indriyādīni adhipatiyādikiccavisesadassanatthaṃ. Tasmā saṅgahāsaṅgahaviseso abhiññeyyanissito suviññeyyo hoti. Yoginā paṭhamaṃ khandhāyatanadhātuvasena dhamme abhijānitvā, tato dukkhaṃ parijānitvā, samudayaṃ pajahitvā, maggaṃ bhāvetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-16]]

#### Khandho

[[#^toc-2-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Yaṃ kiñci rūpaṃ rūpakkhandhasabhāgattā rūpakkhandhotveva saṅgahaṃ gacchati; ekādasasu dhātūsu apariyāpannaṃ rūpaṃ nāma natthi. Rūpakkhandhassa pana cattāro arūpakkhandhā, manāyatanaṃ, satta viññāṇadhātuyo visabhāgā. Tasmā yoginā attano kāye yaṃ kiñci rūpaṃ "rūpakkhandhagaṇanaṃ gacchati, na attagaṇana"nti, vedanādayo ca "tena tena khandhena gaṇitā"ti passantena khandhamattato atirittaṃ attānaṃ apassantena kāyacittesu ahaṅkāramamaṅkāro pajahitabbo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-11]]

#### Āyatanaṃ

[[#^toc-2-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Cakkhāyatanaṃ ekena rūpakkhandhena, ekena cakkhāyatanena, ekāya cakkhudhātuyā saṅgahitaṃ, sesehi asaṅgahitaṃ. Asaṅkhataṃ pana dhammāyatanaṃ nibbānaṃ khandhasaṅgahaṃ na gacchati. Tasmā yoginā cakkhādīni dvārāni "idaṃ ekāyatanamattaṃ, na attā"ti passantena dvāresu saṃvaraṃ upaṭṭhapetvā, saṅkhatesu āyatanesu anabhiratena asaṅkhataṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-12]]

#### Dhātu

[[#^toc-2-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Dhātuniddesepi āyatananiddese vuttanayova: ekekā dhātu ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā saṅgahitā, visabhāgehi asaṅgahitā. Ekādasa dhātuyo rūpapakkhikā, satta viññāṇadhātuyo arūpapakkhikā. Tasmā yoginā cakkhudhātuādayo aṭṭhārasa dhātuyo paccekaṃ vavatthapetvā "dhātumattaṃ idaṃ, na satto"ti passantena sattasaññā pajahitabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-10]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-12]]

#### Saccaṃ

[[#^toc-2-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Saccaniddese samudayasaccaṃ maggasaccañca sadisavissajjanāni, tasmā maggasaccaṃ samudayānantaraṃ vuttaṃ. Saccāni pana khandhādīsu abhiññātesu pariññeyyatādivisesadassanatthaṃ vuttāni. Tasmā yoginā khandhāyatanadhātuvasena abhiññātaṃ dukkhaṃ parijānitabbaṃ, samudayo pahātabbo, maggo bhāvetabbo; evaṃ samudayaṃ pajahitvā maggaṃ bhāventena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-13]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-16]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-18]]

#### Indriyaṃ

[[#^toc-2-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Indriyāni adhipatiyādikiccavisesadassanatthaṃ vuttāni. Jīvitindriyaṃ dvīhi khandhehi saṅgahitaṃ: rūpajīvitindriyaṃ rūpakkhandhena, arūpajīvitindriyaṃ saṅkhārakkhandhena. Tasmā yoginā jīvitampi khandhadvayamattaṃ, na attāti passantena saddhādīni indriyāni attano attano kicce adhipatibhāvena bhāvetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-14]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-16]]

#### Paṭiccasamuppādādi

[[#^toc-2-1-6|↑↑↑]]

Paṭiccasamuppādaniddese avijjā ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā saṅgahitā, taṇhā upādānaṃ kammabhavo ca saṅkhārakkhandheneva; sokadukkhadomanassāni vedanākkhandhena, paridevo rūpakkhandhena, upāyāso saṅkhārakkhandhena. Jātijarāmaraṇāni nissattaṭṭhena dhammāyatanadhammadhātudhammehi sabhāgāni. Tasmā yoginā sokaparidevādike uppanne "ayaṃ vedanākkhandho, ayaṃ saṅkhārakkhandho"ti khandhamattato passitvā, jātijarāmaraṇāni nissattāni dhammamattānīti jānantena avijjātaṇhāupādānāni pajahitabbāni; iddhipādajhānaappamaññādayo pana kusalā dhammā khandhato pariggahetvā bhāvetabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-15]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-17]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-18]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-20]]

#### Tikaṃ (Paṭhamanayo)

[[#^toc-2-1-7|↑↑↑]]

Bāhiramātikāya kusalattikādīni khandhāyatanadhātūhi saṅgahitāni; sadisavissajjanāni padāni uppaṭipāṭiyāpi ekato gahitāni. Tasmā yoginā kusalampi akusalampi abyākatampi khandhāyatanadhātumattato passitabbaṃ: akusalaṃ khandhamattanti ñatvā tattha anabhiratena pajahitabbaṃ, kusalaṃ khandhamattanti ñatvā tattha mānaṃ akatvā bhāvetabbaṃ, evaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-19]]

#### Dukaṃ (Paṭhamanayo)

[[#^toc-2-1-8|↑↑↑]]

Dukapadesupi tikapadesu vuttanayeneva saṅgahāsaṅgaho veditabbo. Asaṅkhataṃ nibbānaṃ khandhasaṅgahaṃ na gacchati, tasmā "asaṅkhataṃ khandhato ṭhapetvā" vuttaṃ. Tasmā yoginā sabbe saṅkhatadhamme khandhapariyāpanne passitvā, khandhehi vimuttaṃ asaṅkhataṃ nibbānaṃ ārammaṇaṃ katvā, saṅkhatesu nikantiṃ akarontena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.
