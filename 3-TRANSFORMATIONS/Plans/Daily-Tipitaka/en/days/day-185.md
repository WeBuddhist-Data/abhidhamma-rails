# Day 185 — A Whole Mind Turned to Good

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today finishes the sixth method. The text now takes the groups of three and the pairs from the matrix (*mātikā*) of the first book. For each one it asks: what is this associated with, and what is it dissociated from?

The first answer may surprise you. Wholesome (*kusala*) states are associated with nothing. Why? The word "wholesome" already takes in all four mental heaps, so nothing is left outside to join with. The same is true of unwholesome states. Then come states with pleasant, painful, and neutral feeling. After them come states with thinking and rapture, states with roots (*hetu*), and taints, fetters, and defilements. At the end, a short verse lists the many terms that cannot be asked about at all, because they mix mind with matter.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which of today's moments were wholesome, all the way through?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### KUSALA — Wholesome

**Pronunciation:** *ku-sa-la* — three short syllables. Say "KU-sa-la".

**In today's passage:** The first answer today is "None": wholesome (*kusala*) states are associated with nothing. The teachers explain that "wholesome" already covers all four mental heaps at once. Feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness can all be wholesome together.

**Why it matters:** Wholesome is not just one good act or one good thought. It can be the whole mind in a moment: a kind feeling, a clear view, a good intention, a bright awareness. Today, try to make one moment wholesome all the way through.
