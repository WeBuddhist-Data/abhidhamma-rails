# Day 186 — An Empty Question

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today covers two methods. The seventh asks a two-step question. Take what is associated with feeling. Then take what is dissociated from *those*. What are they dissociated from? The answer: from four aggregates, the mind base, and the seven elements of consciousness. In short, from the mind.

The eighth method is the shortest of all. It asks: what is dissociated from form, and what is *that* associated with? But what stands apart from form are the four mental heaps, and no partner is left for them. What stands apart from feeling is form and *nibbāna*, which never join with anything. So every answer is just "None." The teachers call this an empty question (*moghapucchā*). The text asks it anyway, and answers it honestly.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What question do I keep asking that has no real answer?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### MOGHAPUCCHĀ — An Empty Question

**Pronunciation:** *mo-gha-puc-chā* — four syllables; "gh" is a breathy "g", and "cch" is like the "ch" in "church". Say "MO-gha-PUCH-chaa".

**In today's passage:** The teachers say every question in the eighth method is an empty question (*moghapucchā*). Nothing that stands apart from form can join with anything else. So the text answers "None" each time, and moves on.

**Why it matters:** The mind asks many empty questions. "Why does this always happen to me?" "What if it all goes wrong?" Some questions have no answer worth chasing. Notice when a question is empty. Give it a calm "None," and come back to what is here.
