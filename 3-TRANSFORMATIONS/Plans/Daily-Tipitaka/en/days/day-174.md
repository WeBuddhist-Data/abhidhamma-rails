# Day 174 — A New Book: Sorting Everything

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today a new book begins: the Dhātukathā, the Discussion of Elements. It is the third book of the Abhidhamma. The book before it, the Vibhaṅga, took each topic apart. This book asks a new question. Where does each thing belong? It sorts every phenomenon into three lists: the five aggregates, the twelve bases, and the eighteen elements.

Today's five verses are the book's matrix (its list of contents). First come fourteen methods. The first is inclusion and exclusion (*saṅgaha*, *asaṅgaha*): which list counts a thing in, and which leaves it out. Later methods ask what is associated (*sampayoga*): what arises and ends together in one mind. Next comes the inner list of topics, from the five aggregates to contact and attention. Then come three ways to count and two marks: same kind and different kind. Last, the whole Dhammasaṅgaṇī, the first book, is named as a matrix too.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *If I sorted this moment into parts, would I find a "me" among them?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### NAYA — Method

**Pronunciation:** *na-ya* — two short syllables. Say "NAH-yah".

**In today's passage:** The book opens with fourteen methods (*naya*). The teachers say a method is a way that carries the teaching forward, and a way that lets us know things. These fourteen are the root of the whole book.

**Why it matters:** A method is a way of looking. Ask of any thought: what kind is it? What arises with it? Seen this way, a thought is just a thought, not a self. The teachers say one who looks like this does less harm and grows more good. Try it once today when worry comes.
