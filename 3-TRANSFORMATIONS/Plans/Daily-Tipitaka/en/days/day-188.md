# Day 188 — Good and Harm Never Share a Moment

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

The tenth method asks the last question of this set. Take what is dissociated from a thing. Then take what is dissociated from *that*. Where does it end?

Start with material form. Feeling and the other mental heaps stand apart from form. And form, in turn, stands apart from them. So the answer comes back to form: dissociated from four aggregates, one base, and seven elements. The teachers add that *nibbāna* is treated here like subtle matter. The verses go through the aggregates, bases, faculties, links of dependent origination, and kinds of becoming. Then come the groups of three. Wholesome states stand apart from unwholesome ones, and unwholesome from wholesome. They never arise in the same moment of mind.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What wholesome act can I bring in today, to leave less room for harm?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VIPPAYOGA — Dissociation

**Pronunciation:** *vip-pa-yo-ga* — four syllables; hold the double "pp" a little. Say "vip-pa-YO-ga".

**In today's passage:** Dissociation (*vippayoga*) means not arising together in one mind. Today, form and the mental heaps stand apart from each other. In the groups of three, wholesome and unwholesome states stand apart too. The teachers say they are different kinds, so they never share one moment.

**Why it matters:** We cannot push a bad state out by force. But in a moment of kindness, ill will is simply not there. In a moment of giving, greed is absent. Each wholesome moment is a moment free of harm. Make many of them.
