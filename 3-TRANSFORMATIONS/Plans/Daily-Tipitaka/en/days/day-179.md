# Day 179 — What Is Never Counted

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today ends the first method. The pairs keep coming. States with an object and states without one. Consciousness and mental factors. Things born of mind and things not. Defilements and states free of them. Last come the sense sphere, the fine-material sphere, the formless sphere, and states that lead out.

Listen for one phrase that keeps coming back: "leaving the unconstructed out of the aggregate count." The unconstructed (*asaṅkhata*) is *nibbāna*. It counts as a base and an element, but never as an aggregate. Everything else falls into the five heaps: every feeling, every thought, every body. With this, the first of the fourteen methods is complete.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What am I holding on to that is only part of the five heaps?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### ASAṄKHATA — The Unconstructed

**Pronunciation:** *a-saṅ-kha-ta* — four syllables; "ṅ" is the "ng" in "sing". Say "a-SUNG-kha-ta".

**In today's passage:** The unconstructed (*asaṅkhata*) is a name for *nibbāna*. It is never counted in the aggregates. It counts only as the mind-object base and the mind-object element. That is why so many of today's verses say "leaving the unconstructed out."

**Why it matters:** Everything we build, from body to feelings to plans, sits in the five heaps. The teaching asks us to see this and not to cling to it. Freedom is not one more thing in the heap. Let go of one small grasping today, and rest for a moment in that freedom.
