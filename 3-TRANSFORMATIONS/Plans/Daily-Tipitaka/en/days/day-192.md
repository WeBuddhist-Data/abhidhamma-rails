# Day 192 — Apart from the Body

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

The thirteenth method joins exclusion and association. It takes what is excluded (*asaṅgahita*) from a term, left out of its aggregate, base, and element. Then it asks what those states are associated with and dissociated from.

Start with the aggregate of form. What is left out of it by all three counts? Only consciousness. Consciousness is associated with feeling, perception, and formations. It is dissociated from material form and from nibbāna. The verses repeat this for many terms, from the life faculty to birth, aging, and death. The teaching adds that states can stand apart in four ways: by kind, like wholesome and unwholesome; by plane, like the sense sphere and the fine-material sphere; by time, like past and future; and by person, inner and outer.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Is my mind wholesome or unwholesome right now? It cannot be both.*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VIÑÑĀṆA — Consciousness

**Pronunciation:** *viñ-ñā-ṇa* — three syllables. Say "vin-NYAA-na"; the "ññ" sounds like the "ny" in "canyon".

**In today's passage:** Consciousness (*viññāṇa*) is bare knowing. Today's first verse asks what is left out of the aggregate of form by every count. Only consciousness. It is joined with feeling, perception, and formations, but it stands apart from form.

**Why it matters:** Body and mind work together, but they are not the same thing. The knowing of a pain is not the knee that hurts. Seeing this gives you a little space. In that space you can choose a kinder response, and know your mind a little better.
