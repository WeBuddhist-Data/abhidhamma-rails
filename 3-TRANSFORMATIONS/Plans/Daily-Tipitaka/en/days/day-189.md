# Day 189 — Check the Root

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today closes the tenth method. The text takes the pairs from the matrix (*mātikā*) of the first book. For each one it asks: what stands apart from this, and what stands apart from *that*?

The first pairs are about roots (*hetu*). Greed, hate, and delusion are the unwholesome roots. Non-greed, non-hate, and non-delusion are the wholesome roots. The verses go on to taints, fetters, and defilements, to states with thinking and rapture, and to the sense sphere and beyond. The last verse repeats the list of terms that cannot be asked about, since they mix mind with matter. Then the method ends. Four methods remain in this book.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which root is feeding my mind right now: greed, hate, delusion, or their opposites?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### HETU — Root

**Pronunciation:** *he-tu* — two syllables. Say "HAY-tu".

**In today's passage:** A root (*hetu*) is what holds a mind steady and feeds it, like the root of a tree. Today's first verse asks about states that are roots and states that have roots. Some minds have roots; some have none.

**Why it matters:** Every unwholesome mind grows from greed, hate, or delusion. Every wholesome mind grows from their opposites. You cannot always stop a thought, but you can check its root. Ask, "What is feeding this?" Then grow the good roots, and let the others weaken.
