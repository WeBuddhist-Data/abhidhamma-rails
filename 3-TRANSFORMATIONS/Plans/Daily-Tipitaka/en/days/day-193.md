# Day 193 — The Last Method Begins

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today opens the fourteenth and last method of the Discussion of Elements (*Dhātukathā*). It asks: what is dissociated (*vippayutta*) from a term? Then: where are those states included, and where are they excluded?

The verses begin with the aggregates, bases, and elements. Then they go on to the truths, the faculties, and the kinds of becoming. Some terms are left out of this method. The mind-object base is one: it holds both mind and matter, so nothing can be dissociated from it. Today's last verse is striking. What is dissociated from formless becoming, sorrow, or the path is included in every aggregate, base, and element, setting the unconstructed apart. It is excluded from none.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can a mind be generous and greedy in the same moment?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### BHAVA — Becoming

**Pronunciation:** *bha-va* — two short syllables. Say "BUH-vuh", with a breath after the b.

**In today's passage:** Becoming (*bhava*) is existence as it keeps going: life in the sense sphere, the fine-material sphere, or the formless sphere. Today's verses ask what is dissociated from each kind of becoming, and where those states are counted.

**Why it matters:** Every kind of becoming is something that arises and passes. None of it is a fixed home. Seeing this, we can hold our plans and our lives a little more lightly. We can do a little less harm while we chase them, and a little more good.
