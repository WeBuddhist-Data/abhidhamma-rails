# Day 187 — Partners in One Moment

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

The ninth method asks: take what is associated with a thing, then what is associated with *that*. Where do we end up?

Start with feeling. Feeling arises with perception, formations, and consciousness. And those, in turn, arise with feeling. So the answer comes back to the mind: three aggregates, one base, and seven elements. The verses then go through consciousness, the truths, the faculties, the links of dependent origination, *jhāna* (meditative absorption), contact, volition, and attention. Then come the groups of three and the pairs. Only the mental side appears in this method. Material form has no partner, so it is left out.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *When a feeling arises in me today, what thoughts and urges come with it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### CETASIKA — Mental Factor

**Pronunciation:** *ce-ta-si-ka* — four syllables; "c" is like the "ch" in "chair". Say "CHAY-ta-si-ka".

**In today's passage:** Feeling, perception, and the formations are mental factors (*cetasika*), the qualities that arise with consciousness. Today each one is shown arising with the others, and with consciousness, in the same moment.

**Why it matters:** The mind is not one solid thing. Each moment is a team: consciousness and its mental factors. Some teams are wholesome; some are not. When you notice a good factor at work, like patience or care, give it room. It brings good partners with it.
