# Day 195 — The Discussion of Elements Ends

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today we finish the Discussion of Elements (*Dhātukathā*), the third book of the Abhidhamma. The last method goes on through the pairs of the matrix (*mātikā*): fetters, knots, floods, and hindrances; clinging and defilements; the sense sphere and beyond. For each one it asks what stands apart from it, and where those states are counted.

A closing verse names forty-seven terms that do not fit this method. To ask about them would be an empty question (*moghapucchā*). Then the book ends. Across fourteen methods it has sorted every state into aggregates, bases, and elements. It has shown what is included and what is left out, what arises together and what stands apart.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which fetter or defilement visits my mind most often?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SAṂYOJANA — Fetter

**Pronunciation:** *saṃ-yo-ja-na* — four syllables. Say "sum-YO-juh-nuh".

**In today's passage:** A fetter (*saṃyojana*) is a bond that ties beings to the round of rebirth. Today's first verse begins with fetters, then knots, floods, yokes, and hindrances. It asks what stands apart from each, and where those states are counted.

**Why it matters:** Fetters and wholesome states cannot share one moment of mind. So each kind thought, each patient breath, is a moment the fetters are not working. That is how the long work of freedom happens, one moment at a time.
