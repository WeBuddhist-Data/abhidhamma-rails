# Day 190 — What Rides with Craving

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today begins the eleventh of the fourteen methods (*naya*). It joins two earlier questions. First: what is included (*saṅgahita*) with a term, counted in the same aggregate, base, and element? Then: what are those states associated with, and what are they dissociated from?

The first verse starts with the truth of the origin. The teaching says this is craving (*taṇhā*), which belongs to the aggregate of formations. In one mind, craving arises with feeling, perception, and consciousness. It never mixes with material form, and it stays apart from nibbāna. The verses then go through the faculties, from pleasure and pain to faith and wisdom. They end with sorrow, lamentation, and despair, and a short verse that counts the terms. The whole method fits in one day.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *When craving comes up, what else arrives with it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### TAṆHĀ — Craving

**Pronunciation:** *taṇ-hā* — two syllables. Say "TUN-haa"; the dot under the n makes it a little heavier.

**In today's passage:** Today's first verse asks about the truth of the origin. The teaching names it craving (*taṇhā*) and counts it in the aggregate of formations. In one mind, it is joined with feeling, perception, and consciousness. But it is apart from material form and apart from nibbāna.

**Why it matters:** Craving never comes alone. It brings a feeling and a way of seeing with it. When you notice wanting, look for the feeling under it, and let that kind of mind go. Nibbāna is free of craving. Turning toward it makes the mind a little cleaner.
