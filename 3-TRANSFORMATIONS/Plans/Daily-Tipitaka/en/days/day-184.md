# Day 184 — What Arises Together

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today a new question begins. The sixth method is about association (*sampayoga*). Two things are associated when they arise together, end together, share one base, and take one object. Only the four mental heaps can do this: feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness. They arise together in one mind, in one moment.

So when the text asks what material form is associated with, the answer is "None." Form is only dissociated from (*vippayutta*, not joined with) the mind. The same goes for the eye, the ear, and the other sense doors. Then the verses go through the elements of consciousness, the truths, the faculties, the links of dependent origination, the kinds of becoming, and the factors of the path. Each time the text asks two things. What does it arise with? What does it stand apart from?

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What arose together with my last angry thought, or my last kind one?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SAMPAYOGA — Association

**Pronunciation:** *sam-pa-yo-ga* — four syllables. Say "sum-pa-YO-ga".

**In today's passage:** Association (*sampayoga*) means arising together in one mind. Feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness come up in the same moment, take the same object, and end together. Material form and *nibbāna* never do this. That is why many answers today are simply "None."

**Why it matters:** A thought never comes alone. It comes with a feeling, a label, and an urge. Anger brings its own companions; kindness brings different ones. When you notice one of them, look for the others. Knowing what arises together helps you choose what to feed.
