# Day 194 — Present in Every Mind

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today the last method reaches a group of seven: contact, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness, attention, and decision. For the first six, what is dissociated from them is only material form, setting the unconstructed apart. In other words, these six are found in every mind. Decision is different: some states of mind are found without it.

Then the verses walk through the groups of three from the matrix (*mātikā*): wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate; past, present, and future; inner and outer. They end on the first pairs: roots, taints, and states free of taints. Each time the question is the same. What stands apart from this, and where is it counted?

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Contact, feeling, perception, volition, attention: can I notice them in this very moment?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### MANASIKĀRA — Attention

**Pronunciation:** *ma-na-si-kā-ra* — five syllables. Say "muh-nuh-si-KAA-ruh".

**In today's passage:** Attention (*manasikāra*) is the mind turning toward its object. Today's first verse names it with contact, feeling, perception, volition, and consciousness. Whatever is dissociated from these six is only material form and the unconstructed. So attention is present in every mind.

**Why it matters:** You are always paying attention to something. The question is what. Attention to a grudge feeds the grudge. Attention to someone's kindness feeds gratitude. Choosing where you place attention is one of the simplest ways to do a little more good.
