# Day 176 — The Truths, the Faculties, and Ignorance

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today finishes the eighteen elements. Then it sorts the four noble truths and the twenty-two faculties. The same three questions come each time. Which aggregates, bases, and elements include it? Which leave it out?

Some answers are worth noticing. The truth of suffering is included in all five aggregates, all twelve bases, and all eighteen elements. The truth of the ending is in no aggregate at all. The truths of origin and of the path get the same answer, so the text puts them side by side. The life faculty (*jīvitindriya*) sits in two aggregates: one part is bodily, one part is mental. In the last verse a new list begins: dependent origination. Its first link is ignorance. It fits in just one aggregate, one base, and one element.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Where did not-knowing steer me today?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### AVIJJĀ — Ignorance

**Pronunciation:** *a-vij-jā* — three syllables; hold the double "jj" a moment. Say "a-VIJ-jaa".

**In today's passage:** Ignorance (*avijjā*) is the first link of dependent origination. Yet the book counts it like anything else: one aggregate, one base, one element. The teachers note that here the text skips the question and goes straight to the answer.

**Why it matters:** Ignorance can feel huge, like a fog over our whole life. Here it is one small item on a list. It arises, and it can be seen. The teaching says to let it go. When you catch yourself not knowing what you feel today, pause and name it.
