# Day 178 — Even the Good Is Counted

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

Today finishes the groups of three and begins the groups of two. The last threes are about time and place: past, future, and present states; inner, outer, and both. Then the pairs begin. Roots and not roots. Constructed and unconstructed. Material and immaterial. Mundane (*lokiya*, of this world) and supramundane (*lokuttara*, above the world). Then come the taints, the fetters, and misapprehension (*parāmāsa*, wrong grasping).

Every one of them is counted the same way. Good or bad, of this world or beyond it, each is just so many aggregates, bases, and elements. Nothing gets a special place outside the count, except *nibbāna*, which is never an aggregate.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I do good today without needing credit for it?*

**v. 98.** States with a sublime object… states with a measureless object… inferior states… states fixed in wrongness… states fixed in rightness… states with the path as object… states with the path as root… states in which the path has predominance are included in four aggregates, two bases and two elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten bases and sixteen elements.

**v. 132.** Misapprehended states… states that are misapprehended but not misapprehensions… states dissociated from misapprehension but misapprehended are included in five aggregates, twelve bases and eighteen elements. In how many are they excluded? They are not excluded from any aggregate, any base or any element.

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### KUSALA — Wholesome

**Pronunciation:** *ku-sa-la* — three short syllables. Say "KU-sa-la".

**In today's passage:** The groups of three began with wholesome (*kusala*) states. Today's verses carry that list to its end. Every state in it is counted in aggregates, bases, and elements. The good is counted just like the bad.

**Why it matters:** The teaching says: see the unwholesome as only aggregates, and let it go. See the wholesome as only aggregates too, and grow it without pride. Good deeds still matter. But they need no "me" to puff up. Do one kind thing today, and let it be just that.
