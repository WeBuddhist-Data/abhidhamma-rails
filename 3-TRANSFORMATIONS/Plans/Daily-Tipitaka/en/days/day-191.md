# Day 191 — Feeling Has Company

## 1. 🧭 Today’s Chanting Guide

The twelfth method turns the last question around. First it asks what is associated (*sampayutta*) with a term: what arises and ends with it in one mind. Then it asks where those partners are counted. In which aggregates, bases, and elements are they included, and from which are they excluded?

The first verse starts with feeling (*vedanā*). Its partners are perception, formations, and consciousness. They fill three aggregates, two bases, and eight elements. Everything else is left out. The verses go on to consciousness, the truths, the faculties, and the links of dependent origination. Then they walk through the pairs of the matrix (*mātikā*): roots, taints, fetters, and defilements. A closing verse lists the terms this method can use, and the method ends.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *When I feel something, what else is in my mind at that moment?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VEDANĀ — Feeling

**Pronunciation:** *ve-da-nā* — three syllables. Say "VAY-duh-naa", with a long last "aa".

**In today's passage:** Feeling (*vedanā*) is the pleasant, painful, or neutral tone of an experience. It is not the same as emotion. Today's first verse asks what is joined with feeling. The answer is perception, formations, and consciousness. Together they make one moment of mind.

**Why it matters:** A feeling is never alone. It comes with a way of seeing and a way of reacting. When you feel something strongly, look at the whole bundle, not just the feeling. Then it is easier to hold the feeling lightly, and the mind grows a little clearer.
