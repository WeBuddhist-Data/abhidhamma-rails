## 5. Departures from the English termbases (pi-3 … pi-6) and from the pi-3 zero-shot wording

sammāsaṅkappa / saṅkappa = right thought / thought (termbases and the other zero-shots: right intention / intention). Reason: cetanā = intention stands in the same list (^1-1), so the two must differ.
mano (in the citta string) = intellect. Reason: citta = mind and nāma = mentality are already taken; the string needs distinct words.
upādāniya = subject to clinging (pi-3 zero-shot: objects of clinging). Reason: one pattern for all the '-niya' items (subject to taints, fetters, ties, floods, yokes, hindrances, clinging, defilement).
saṃkilesika = subject to defilement (pi-3 zero-shot: lead to defilement; pi-5 termbase agrees with 'subject to'). Same reason.
saṃyojaniya / ganthaniya / oghaniya / yoganiya / nīvaraṇiya = subject to fetters / ties / floods / yokes / hindrances (pi-3/pi-5 termbases give only the bare noun).
upādānā (plural items) = kinds of clinging (pi-3 zero-shot: 'states that are clinging'). Reason: 'clinging states' would be misread as an adjective.
arūpāvacara = of the immaterial sphere (pi-5/pi-6 termbases: formless sphere). Reason: arūpino dhammā = immaterial states in the same matrix; pi-3 zero-shot also says immaterial.
arūpino = immaterial (pi-5 termbase: non-material). Same reason.
lokuttara = that transcends the world (pi-4/pi-5 termbases: supramundane). Reason: plain reader; follows pi-3 zero-shot.
adosa = non-hate (pi-5 termbase) kept; English 'hate' also for dosa.
kāya in kāyapassaddhi etc. = the mental body. Reason: the text itself defines it as the feeling, perception and mental-formations aggregates; plain 'body' would be read as the physical body. Minor clarification by wording.
kenaci viññeyyā = that some kinds of consciousness can know. Reason: bare 'knowable by something' is opaque; the Nikkhepa definitions are by kinds of consciousness. Clarification by wording; pi-3 zero-shot had a bracketed '[kind of mind]'.
pariyāpanna = included in the spheres of existence (pi-3 termbase: contained; pi-3 zero-shot used a bracket). Reason: bare 'included states' is meaningless to a plain reader.
niyyānika = that leads to liberation (pi-3/pi-5 termbases: lead / leads out). Same reason.
samāpatti in the hirī/ottappa strings = engaging in (pi-4 termbase also 'engaging').
sampajañña = clear awareness (pi-4 termbase gave 'lack of clear [comprehension]'; pi-4 zero-shot says clear awareness).
bhavadiṭṭhi = the view of continued existence, to match bhavataṇhā = craving for continued existence (pi-5 termbase).
na hetū kho pana dhammā sahetukāpi / ahetukāpi (and the 6th pair of every group) = both lines are written out in full: 'States that are not roots but have roots.' / 'States that are not roots and are without roots.' (the Pāli (b) line is elliptical). Reason: chapters 4 and 5 name these items in full.
