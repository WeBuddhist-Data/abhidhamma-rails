## Frame
Katamaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ X? = What is the material form that X?
… – idaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ X. = … – this is the material form that X.
Idaṃ taṃ rūpaṃ upādā. / no upādā. (closing lines) = This is the material form that is derived. / This is the material form that is not derived.
Two classification terms together = "X and Y" ("is internal and derived", "makes impact and is a faculty"); "is" is shared only when both are adjectives and the first is positive; a term that already contains and/but is joined with ", and" ("is internal, and is produced by past action and subject to clinging").
