### The Chapter on Material Form ^1-2-0

#### The Opening Statement ^1-2-1-0

What states are indeterminate? The results of wholesome and unwholesome states, of the sense sphere, of the fine-material sphere, of the immaterial sphere, and not included in the spheres of existence: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness; the states that are functional, neither wholesome nor unwholesome nor results of action; all material form; and the unconditioned element – these states are indeterminate. ^1-583

##### The Sets of One ^1-2-2-1-0

Among these, what is all material form? The four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements – this is called all material form. All material form is not a root, is without roots, is dissociated from roots, has conditions, is conditioned, is material [variant: rūpiyaṃ (Sī.)], is worldly, is subject to taints, is subject to fetters, is subject to ties, is subject to floods, is subject to yokes, is subject to hindrances, is misapprehended, is subject to clinging, is subject to defilement, is indeterminate, is without an object, is not a mental factor, is dissociated from mind, neither is a result nor produces results, is undefiled but subject to defilement, is not with applied thought and sustained thought, is not without applied thought but with sustained thought only, is without applied thought and without sustained thought, is not accompanied by rapture, is not accompanied by happiness, is not accompanied by equanimity, is to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development, is among the states whose roots are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development, leads neither to nor away from accumulation, is of one neither in training nor beyond training, is limited, is of the sense sphere, is not of the fine-material sphere, is not of the immaterial sphere, is included in the spheres of existence, is not among the states not included in the spheres of existence, is not fixed, does not lead to liberation, is arisen, is knowable by the six kinds of consciousness, is impermanent, is overcome by ageing.
Thus material form is classified in one way.
