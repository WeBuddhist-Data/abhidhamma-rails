Sources: the Pāli, the termbase / decisions, the zero-shot and the final pi-1 rails only (no Burmese translation, nissaya or web source; language-priority/ not opened; no Marathi wording).

Rule for a termbase that disagrees with itself: where an undecided compound / inflected entry keeps the zero-shot form and a decided entry of the same word (decisions-A.tsv) says otherwise, the rails follow the decided entry (pi-1 rails precedent, doubt 1); those rows are ARTEFACT «termbase conflict» and listed under the doubts. Where a compound has its own entry and no decided member entry speaks for it, the compound entry stands (pi-1 precedent «compound with its own entry»).
