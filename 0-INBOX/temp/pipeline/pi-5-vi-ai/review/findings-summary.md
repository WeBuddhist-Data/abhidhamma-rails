# pi-5 Vietnamese rails review: summary for the user's decisions (2026-11-22)

Five reviewers (findings-r1 … r5.md, our own words, no Tịnh Sự text). Totals: CRITICAL 2, MAJOR 18, MINOR about 70. No reviewer found a polarity error, an Āmantā / Na hevaṃ swap or a lost negation; frames, pentads and lists are faithful; the Tịnh Sự comparison found more errors on its side than ours.
