## Left out: same Pāli word, different meaning (or a difference the pi-6 conventions intend)

- rūpaṃ, saṅkhārā, saṅkhāre (bare words in the word-checking pairs) = रूप, रचनाएँ vs prior भौतिक रूप / दृश्य रूप / मानसिक रचनाएँ: the bare word is wider than the aggregate (terms file; senses conventions §7). rūpaṃ in 'matter' = भौतिक रूप as before.
- kāyasaṅkhāro, vacīsaṅkhāro (breathing; thought before speech) = शरीर की रचना, वाणी की रचना vs pi-5 शरीर की कर्म-रचना, वाणी की कर्म-रचना (the volitional sense; pi-2 and pi-5 also have शरीर की रचना).
- anulomaṃ, paccanīkaṃ = सकारात्मक, नकारात्मक vs pi-5 सीधा क्रम, उलटा क्रम (order of argument in the Kathāvatthu).
- cakkhundriyaṃ, manindriyaṃ, indriyaṃ … vs pi-1, pi-2, pi-4 X की इंद्रिय / इंद्रिय: the senses to be guarded (core terms keep इंद्रिय there).
- kāmadhātu vs pi-2 'the element of sensual thought'; rūpadhātu (visible-form element) vs pi-2, pi-5 realm; dhātu (bare) vs लोक (realm); sotadhātu vs pi-5 श्रवण-शक्ति (the divine ear).
- uppajjati vs pi-2 जन्म लेना (said of beings); upapajjati vs pi-5 अगला जन्म (next existence).
