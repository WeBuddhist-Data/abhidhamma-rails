## Presence-check exceptions added
Heading genitives (Tika, Catukka … Dasaka: «Матрица троек / четвёрок …», per segment); verb stem alternations (samāpajjati, vuṭṭhāti, paṭivijānāti, nadati, vipaccanti, gāmī, anuseti, ūpage, yathākammūpage, samāpajjāmi, vuṭṭhahāmī); past / future stems (uppannāya, abbhaññaṃsu, abhijānissanti, lābhiṃ, vuṭṭhitassa, nibbatteyya, upasampajja); short forms (vītarāgo, vīta- citta, Sassato, antavā, anantavā, kusalo); fleeting vowels (bhūmīsu, bhūmako, cittena); viññeyyameva (as viññeyyā); nirayaṃ (ад ~ в аду). The inherited sīha and tika keyword exceptions are not used in this chapter.
