Where the Pāli starts "Ayaṃ/Ime vuccati…" as a new sentence (no dash), so does the Russian: «Это называется чувством, находящимся вблизи.»
Comma before the dash only when it closes a participial phrase or clause (MAHABHUTA, FEELINGS3, FAR_OTHER …); none after a plain noun list.
