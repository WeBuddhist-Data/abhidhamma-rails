# pi-2 Russian senses: conventions added by the parts (coordinator notes, binding for later parts)

## Part 1 (2026-11-17)
- Merged as one sense (grammatical/count variants): жажда / виды жажды; виды самомнения / самомнение; виды поиска / поиск; скрытые склонности / скрытая склонность; конечный / конечность (and бесконечный); жизнь / срок жизни; всё / весь; внутренний / внутри себя.
- Kept pi-1 splits: Sīla нравственность vs обряд; bahiddhā внешний / вовне vs вне ("ito bahiddhā"); āyatanāni сферы восприятия vs сферы (titthāyatanāni); purisa человек (purisadosā, purisamalāni) vs мужественность (purisindriya).
- New splits: vatthūni основы (ñāṇa-, kilesavatthūni) vs поводы (kusīta-, āghāta-, virodhavatthūni); vādā учения vs ложь (musāvādā); vatthukā предмет (dasavatthukā) vs имеющий … основу (uppannavatthukā); vipatti испорченность vs неудача (2-828); vitakkā направленная мысль vs мысли (minor topics); Kāmadhātu элемент чувственных желаний (six elements) vs мир чувственных желаний; Rūpadhātu элемент видимых форм vs мир форм; dhammā явления vs объекты ума.
- Kāma: «чувственные желания» (sense desires, and the sense sphere named after them: kāmadhātu, kāmavitakka) vs чувственные удовольствия / чувственный / чувственные блага (kāmarāga, kāmāsava, kāmataṇhā, kāmaguṇa, kāmacchanda; the pi-1 tag).
- Tag families:
  - X-anupassanā: `contemplation of X, Nth foundation of mindfulness (stem and heading member)`; Xniddeso: `exposition of contemplation of X (heading)`
  - X-iddhipādo: `the base of supernormal power consisting in X (nominative sg.)`
  - X-samphassa: `contact of the X, X contact (compound member)`; X-samphassajā: `arising from contact of the X (feminine nominative sg.)`
  - "vuccati/vuccanti X": `'is called X', close of the definition (verb phrase)`
  - noun + adjective phrases: `X liable to the taints`, `X free from the taints`, `conjoined X, in the dyad predicates`, `ethically indeterminate X`, `X not yet come, future X`, each `(phrase, nominative sg./pl.)`
  - instrumental phrases (ābyākatāhi saññāhi …): `… (instrumental pl. phrase)`
  - Xvāro: `the section on X, of the analytical knowledges (heading)`; dassanavāro titles: `the section showing … (heading)`
  - …ñca: `and X, with ca (neuter sg.)`
  - minor-topic plural lists: `X, the three of the minor topics (nominative pl.)`
  - ādī / ādīni: `and the rest, and so on, as in … (compound final)`
  - Mātikā: `the matrix, the schedule of topics a chapter expounds (heading and heading member)`

## Part 2 (2026-11-17)
- N-vidhena headwords (Ekavidhena … Dasavidhena, Ekādasa-, Catuvīsati-, Tiṃsa-/Tiṃsati-, Bahuvidhena): one sense, tag `into N kinds, the N-fold division of a topic (instrumental)`; Ekavidhena `one by one, the single-kind division of a topic (instrumental)`. Never "classification" in these tags (overrides pi-1's Catubbidhena/Chabbidhena tags). `dasavidhena/bahuvidhena X-kkhandho` = `the aggregate of X divided into ten/many kinds (phrase, nominative sg.)`.
- Masculine -o predicates of the question sections: `… (masculine nominative sg.)`, e.g. `liable to the X`, `not liable to the X`, `conjoined with X`, `dissociated from X`, `dissociated from the X but liable to them`, `having a past/limited/… object`.
- "X ceva X-sampayutta": `being a X and also conjoined with Xs (phrase, masculine nominative sg. / nominative pl. / neuter sg.)`.
- Indriya family: `the controlling faculty of the eye/ear/nose/tongue/body (nominative sg.)`; stem form `(stem and compound member)`.
- Splits: hetuko/hetukaṃ имеющий корни vs имеющий причиной (dependent-origination tetrads); sukhasahagataṃ счастье vs удовольствие (body-consciousness); sukha счастье / удовольствие / лёгкий; mano ум vs разум (citta synonym string); kusalo благой vs искусный (samādhikusalo); hīno низший vs хуже; pasādo ясная вера vs чувствительность; dukkhaṃ страдание vs боль; vaṇṇa внешность / красота vs похвала vs цвет; sutaṃ услышанный vs учёность; yoga ярмо vs умение; also Sotaṃ, mutta, vāta, taca, phala, patta, mūla, kasāvo, raso, parittā, cetasikaṃ, cittaṃ (вид ума), hetū, dhātūnaṃ, sārammaṇā.
- One Russian value, one sense even if the Pāli has two uses: hadayaṃ «сердце» (organ and mind-synonym, two-part tag); mūla «корни» (plant roots and kusalamūla).
- Names for the sense organ in the base definitions (paṇḍara, khetta, netta, tīra, samuddo, dvārā): `…, a name for the sense organ in the base definitions`.
- Earth-element list: sajjhaṃ «серебро», rajataṃ «монеты» (the translation keeps the two silver words apart; correct).
