### Analysis of the Path Factors ^2-11-0

#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-11-1-0

486. The noble eightfold path, namely: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration. ^2-486

487. Therein, what is right view? Knowledge of suffering, knowledge of the origin of suffering, knowledge of the cessation of suffering, knowledge of the way leading to the cessation of suffering — this is called "right view".
Therein, what is right intention? The intention of renunciation, the intention of non-ill-will, the intention of harmlessness — this is called "right intention".
Therein, what is right speech? Abstaining from false speech, abstaining from divisive speech, abstaining from harsh speech, abstaining from idle chatter — this is called "right speech".
Therein, what is right action? Abstaining from killing living beings, abstaining from taking what is not given, abstaining from sexual misconduct — this is called "right action".
Therein, what is right livelihood? Here a noble disciple, having abandoned wrong livelihood, makes his living by right livelihood — this is called "right livelihood".
Therein, what is right effort? Here a monk generates desire, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the non-arising of evil, unwholesome things that have not yet arisen; he generates desire, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the abandoning of evil, unwholesome things that have arisen; he generates desire, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the arising of wholesome things that have not yet arisen; he generates desire, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the persistence of wholesome things that have arisen, for their non-confusion, their increase, their expansion, their development, their perfection — this is called "right effort".
Therein, what is right mindfulness? Here a monk dwells contemplating the body in the body — ardent, clearly comprehending, mindful, having removed covetousness and grief with regard to the world; he dwells contemplating feelings in feelings — ardent, clearly comprehending, mindful, having removed covetousness and grief with regard to the world; he dwells contemplating the mind in the mind — ardent, clearly comprehending, mindful, having removed covetousness and grief with regard to the world; he dwells contemplating mental objects in mental objects — ardent, clearly comprehending, mindful, having removed covetousness and grief with regard to the world — this is called "right mindfulness".
Therein, what is right concentration? Here a monk, secluded from sense pleasures, secluded from unwholesome things, enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption, which is with thought and with examination, with joy and happiness born of seclusion; with the stilling of thought and examination, he enters and dwells in the second meditative absorption, which has inner confidence and unification of mind, is without thought and without examination, with joy and happiness born of concentration; with the fading away of joy he dwells equanimous, mindful and clearly comprehending, and experiences happiness with the body — that of which the noble ones declare, "equanimous, mindful, one who dwells happily" — he enters and dwells in the third meditative absorption; with the abandoning of happiness and the abandoning of pain, and with the earlier disappearance of joy and grief, he enters and dwells in the fourth meditative absorption, which is neither painful nor pleasant and has purity of mindfulness through equanimity — this is called "right concentration". ^2-487

488. The noble eightfold path, namely: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration. ^2-488

489. Therein, what is right view? Here a monk develops right view, based on seclusion, based on dispassion, based on cessation, ripening in release; he develops right intention… he develops right speech… he develops right action… he develops right livelihood… he develops right effort… he develops right mindfulness… he develops right concentration, based on seclusion, based on dispassion, based on cessation, ripening in release.
The Classification According to the Discourses is finished. ^2-489

#### Classification According to the Abhidhamma ^2-11-2-0

490. The eightfold path: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration. ^2-490

491. Therein, what is the eightfold path? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the meditative absorption that is beyond the world, leading out, leading to dismantling, for the abandoning of views, for reaching the first stage — secluded from sense pleasures… he enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption, of difficult practice and slow direct knowledge — on that occasion there is the eightfold path: right view… right concentration. ^2-491

492. Therein, what is right view? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view, the awakening factor of investigation of things, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right view".
Therein, what is right intention? The thinking, thought, intention, absorption, full absorption, application of the mind, right intention, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right intention".
Therein, what is right speech? The refraining from the four kinds of verbal misconduct, abstaining, desisting, abstinence, not doing, not committing, not transgressing, not overstepping the limit, destroying the bridge to them — right speech, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right speech".
Therein, what is right action? The refraining from the three kinds of bodily misconduct, abstaining, desisting, abstinence, not doing, not committing, not transgressing, not overstepping the limit, destroying the bridge to them — right action, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right action".
Therein, what is right livelihood? The refraining from wrong livelihood, abstaining, desisting, abstinence, not doing, not committing, not transgressing, not overstepping the limit, destroying the bridge to it — right livelihood, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right livelihood".
Therein, what is right effort? The mental initiation of energy… right effort, the awakening factor of energy, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right effort".
Therein, what is right mindfulness? The mindfulness, recollection… right mindfulness, the awakening factor of mindfulness, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right mindfulness".
Therein, what is right concentration? The steadiness of the mind… right concentration, the awakening factor of concentration, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "right concentration". This is called "the eightfold path". The remaining things are associated with the eightfold path. ^2-492

504. The noble eightfold path, namely: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration. ^2-504

505. Of the eight path factors, how many are wholesome, how many unwholesome, how many neutral… how many involve conflict, how many are free from conflict? ^2-505

##### The Triads ^2-11-3-1-0

506. They may be wholesome; they may be neutral. Right intention is associated with pleasant feeling; seven path factors may be associated with pleasant feeling or may be associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. They may be results; they may be things subject to result. They are not taken up at birth and not able to be clung to. They are undefiled and not connected with defilement. Right intention is without thought but with examination only; seven path factors may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, or may be without thought and without examination. Right intention is accompanied by joy and accompanied by happiness, not accompanied by equanimity; seven path factors may be accompanied by joy, may be accompanied by happiness, or may be accompanied by equanimity. They are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. Their causes are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. They may be leading to dismantling; they may be leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling. They may belong to one in training; they may belong to one beyond training. They are measureless. They have measureless objects. They are refined. They may be fixed in rightness; they may be unfixed. They do not have the path as their object; they may have the path as their cause; they may have the path as their dominant factor; they may not be describable either as having the path as their cause or as having the path as their dominant factor. They may be arisen; they may be not arisen; they may be due to arise. They may be past; they may be future; they may be present. They are not describable as having a past object, a future object, or a present object. They may be internal; they may be external; they may be both internal and external. They have external objects. They are invisible and offer no resistance. ^2-506

##### The Pairs ^2-11-3-2-0

507. Right view is a root; seven path factors are not roots. They have roots. They are associated with roots. Right view is both a root and has roots; seven path factors are not describable as being both roots and having roots; they have roots but are not roots. Right view is both a root and associated with roots; seven path factors are not describable as being both roots and associated with roots; they are associated with roots but are not roots. Seven path factors are not roots yet have roots; right view is not describable either as being a non-root that has roots or as being a non-root without roots.
They have conditions. They are conditioned. They are invisible. They offer no resistance. They are immaterial. They are beyond the world. They can be known by one means; they cannot be known by another.
They are not mental taints. They are without mental taints. They are dissociated from mental taints. They are not describable either as being mental taints and subject to mental taints, or as being subject to mental taints but not mental taints. They are not describable either as being mental taints and associated with mental taints, or as being associated with mental taints but not mental taints. They are dissociated from mental taints. They are not fetters… they are not ties… they are not floods… they are not yokes… they are not hindrances… they are not wrong grasping… they have objects. They are not minds. They are mental factors. They are associated with mind. They are conjoined with mind. They are originated by mind. They arise together with mind. They follow along with mind. They are conjoined with and originated by mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and arise together with mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and follow along with mind. They are external. They are not derived. They are not taken up at birth.
They are not clinging… they are not defilements… they are not to be abandoned by seeing. They are not to be abandoned by meditative development. Their causes are not to be abandoned by seeing.
