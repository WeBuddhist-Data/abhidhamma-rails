#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-3-1-0

172. There are six elements – the earth element, the water element, the fire element, the air element, the space element, and the consciousness element. ^2-172

173. Therein, what is the earth element? The earth element is twofold: there is internal and there is external. Therein, what is the internal earth element? Whatever internally, in oneself, is hard, solid, hardness, the state of being hard, internal and taken up at birth – namely: head hair, body hair, nails, teeth, skin, flesh, sinews, bones, bone marrow, kidneys, heart, liver, membranes, spleen, lungs, intestines, mesentery, stomach contents, and excrement; or whatever else internally, in oneself, is hard, solid, hardness, the state of being hard, internal and taken up at birth – this is called 'the internal earth element'.
Therein, what is the external earth element? Whatever externally is hard, solid, hardness, the state of being hard, external and not taken up – namely: iron, copper, tin, lead, silver ore, pearl, gems, cat's-eye, conch shell, stone, coral, silver, gold, ruby, emerald, grass, wood, gravel, potsherds, earth, rock, and mountains; or whatever else externally is hard, solid, hardness, the state of being hard, external and not taken up – this is called 'the external earth element'. The internal earth element and the external earth element, gathered and grouped together: this is called "the earth element". ^2-173

174. Therein, what is the water element? The water element is twofold: there is internal and there is external. Therein, what is the internal water element? Whatever internally, in oneself, is water, watery, moisture, moistness, the cohesion of material form, internal and taken up at birth – namely: bile, phlegm, pus, blood, sweat, fat, tears, grease, saliva, mucus, joint fluid, and urine; or whatever else internally, in oneself, is water, watery, moisture, moistness, the cohesion of material form, internal and taken up at birth – this is called 'the internal water element'.
Therein, what is the external water element? Whatever externally is water, watery, moisture, moistness, the cohesion of material form, external and not taken up – namely: the juice of roots, the juice of stems, the juice of bark, the juice of leaves, the juice of flowers, the juice of fruits, milk, curds, ghee, butter, oil, honey, molasses, and the waters of the earth or of the sky; or whatever else externally is water, watery, moisture, moistness, the cohesion of material form, external and not taken up – this is called 'the external water element'. The internal water element and the external water element, gathered and grouped together: this is called "the water element". ^2-174

175. Therein, what is the fire element? The fire element is twofold: there is internal and there is external. Therein, what is the internal fire element? Whatever internally, in oneself, is fire, fiery, heat, heatedness, warmth, warmness, internal and taken up at birth – namely: that by which one is warmed, that by which one ages, that by which one is burned, and that by which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted is fully digested; or whatever else internally, in oneself, is fire, fiery, heat, heatedness, warmth, warmness, internal and taken up at birth – this is called 'the internal fire element'.
Therein, what is the external fire element? Whatever externally is fire, fiery, heat, heatedness, warmth, warmness, external and not taken up – namely: fire of wood, fire of straw, fire of grass, fire of cow dung, fire of husks, fire of rubbish, lightning fire, the heat of fire, the heat of the sun, the heat of a heap of wood, the heat of a heap of grass, the heat of a heap of grain, the heat of a heap of goods; or whatever else externally is fire, fiery, heat, heatedness, warmth, warmness, external and not taken up – this is called 'the external fire element'. The internal fire element and the external fire element, gathered and grouped together: this is called "the fire element". ^2-175

176. Therein, what is the air element? The air element is twofold: there is internal and there is external. Therein, what is the internal air element? Whatever internally, in oneself, is air, airy, the distension of material form, internal and taken up at birth – namely: winds going upward, winds going downward, winds in the belly, winds in the bowels, winds coursing through the limbs, cutting winds, piercing winds, throbbing winds, breathing in and breathing out; or whatever else internally, in oneself, is air, airy, the distension of material form, internal and taken up at birth – this is called 'the internal air element'.
Therein, what is the external air element? Whatever externally is air, airy, the distension of material form, external and not taken up – namely: east winds, west winds, north winds, south winds, dusty winds, dust-free winds, cold winds, hot winds, gentle winds, strong winds, black winds, high-altitude winds, winds from birds' wings, winds from a supaṇṇa bird, winds from palm-leaf fans, winds from a winnowing fan; or whatever else externally is air, airy, the distension of material form, external and not taken up – this is called 'the external air element'. The internal air element and the external air element, gathered and grouped together: this is called "the air element". ^2-176

177. Therein, what is the space element? The space element is twofold: there is internal and there is external. Therein, what is the internal space element? Whatever internally, in oneself, is space, spatial, a void, a hollow, an opening, a gap, not touched by flesh and blood, internal and taken up at birth – namely: the opening of the ear, the opening of the nose, the door of the mouth, that by which one swallows what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted, that in which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted stays, and that by which what is eaten, drunk, chewed, and tasted passes out below; or whatever else internally, in oneself, is space, spatial, a void, a hollow, an opening, a gap, not touched by flesh and blood, internal and taken up at birth – this is called 'the internal space element'.
Therein, what is the external space element? Whatever externally is space, spatial, a void, a hollow, an opening, a gap, not touched by the four great elements, external and not taken up – this is called 'the external space element'. The internal space element and the external space element, gathered and grouped together: this is called "the space element". ^2-177

178. Therein, what is the consciousness element? The eye-consciousness element, the ear-consciousness element, the nose-consciousness element, the tongue-consciousness element, the body-consciousness element, and the mind-consciousness element – this is called "the consciousness element".
These are the six elements. ^2-178

179. There are another six elements – the pleasure element, the pain element, the gladness element, the sadness element, the equanimity element, and the ignorance element. ^2-179

180. Therein, what is the pleasure element? Bodily comfort, bodily pleasure; the comfortable, pleasant experience born of body-contact; the comfortable, pleasant feeling born of body-contact – this is called "the pleasure element".
Therein, what is the pain element? Bodily discomfort, bodily pain; the uncomfortable, painful experience born of body-contact; the uncomfortable, painful feeling born of body-contact – this is called "the pain element".
Therein, what is the gladness element? Mental comfort, mental pleasure; the comfortable, pleasant experience born of mind-contact; the comfortable, pleasant feeling born of mind-contact – this is called "the gladness element".
Therein, what is the sadness element? Mental discomfort, mental pain; the uncomfortable, painful experience born of mind-contact; the uncomfortable, painful feeling born of mind-contact – this is called "the sadness element".
Therein, what is the equanimity element? What is mentally neither comfortable nor uncomfortable; the experience born of mind-contact that is neither painful nor pleasant; the feeling born of mind-contact that is neither painful nor pleasant – this is called "the equanimity element".
Therein, what is the ignorance element? Not knowing, not seeing, not comprehending, not awakening, not fully awakening, not penetrating, not grasping, not fathoming, not weighing evenly, not reflecting, not seeing directly; poor discernment, foolishness, lack of clear understanding; delusion, bewilderment, complete confusion; ignorance, the flood of ignorance, the yoke of ignorance, the underlying tendency to ignorance, the obsession of ignorance, the barrier of ignorance; delusion, the root of the unwholesome – this is called "the ignorance element".
These are the six elements. ^2-180

181. There are another six elements – the element of sense desire, the element of ill will, the element of cruelty, the element of renunciation, the element of goodwill, and the element of harmlessness. ^2-181

182. Therein, what is the element of sense desire? Thinking connected with sense desire – thought, intention, fixing, focusing, directing the mind, wrong intention – this is called the element of sense desire. Taking the Avīci hell as the lower limit and the gods who wield power over others' creations as the upper limit, whatever within this range moves here and is included here – the aggregates, elements, and sense-fields, material form, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness – this is called "the element of sense desire".
Therein, what is the element of ill will? Thinking connected with ill will – thought… wrong intention – this is called "the element of ill will". Or the mind's irritation over the ten grounds for resentment: hostility, resistance, opposition; anger, rage, fury; hatred, ill feeling, malice; the mind's vexation, mental resentment; anger, being angry, the state of being angry; hatred, hating, the state of hating; vexation, being vexed; opposition, hostility; ferocity, harshness, and the mind's displeasure – this is called "the element of ill will".
Therein, what is the element of cruelty? Thinking connected with cruelty – thought… wrong intention – this is called "the element of cruelty". Here someone harms living beings with a hand, a clod, a stick, a knife, a rope, or with one thing or another; such tormenting, harassing, hurting, injuring, provoking, and attacking of others – this is called "the element of cruelty".
Therein, what is the element of renunciation? Thinking connected with renunciation – thought… right intention – this is called "the element of renunciation". Indeed, all wholesome states are "the element of renunciation".
Therein, what is the element of goodwill? Thinking connected with goodwill – thought… right intention – this is called "the element of goodwill". Loving-kindness towards living beings, the act of loving, the state of loving, the liberation of mind through loving-kindness – this is called "the element of goodwill".
Therein, what is the element of harmlessness? Thinking connected with harmlessness – thought, intention, fixing, focusing, directing the mind, right intention – this is called "the element of harmlessness". Compassion towards living beings, the act of compassion, the state of compassion, the liberation of mind through compassion – this is called "the element of harmlessness".
These are the six elements.
Thus these three sets of six, gathered and grouped together, make eighteen elements.
The Classification According to the Discourses is finished. ^2-182

#### Classification According to the Abhidhamma ^2-3-2-0

There are eighteen elements – the eye element, the element of sights, the eye-consciousness element; the ear element, the element of sounds, the ear-consciousness element; the nose element, the element of smells, the nose-consciousness element; the tongue element, the element of tastes, the tongue-consciousness element; the body element, the element of things touched, the body-consciousness element; the mind element, the element of mental objects, and the mind-consciousness element. ^2-183

184. Therein, what is the eye element? The eye that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the eye element".
Therein, what is the element of sights? The sight that is the shining of colour derived from the four great elements… this is the element of sights – this is called "the element of sights".
Therein, what is the eye-consciousness element? Therein, what is the nose element? The nose that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the nose element".
Therein, what is the element of smells? The smell that is derived from the four great elements, invisible and offering resistance… this is the element of smells – this is called "the element of smells".

Depending on the nose and smells there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding nose-consciousness element – this is called "the nose-consciousness element".
Therein, what is the tongue element? The tongue that is a sensitive surface derived from the four great elements… this is an empty village – this is called "the tongue element".
Therein, what is the element of tastes? The taste that is derived from the four great elements, invisible and offering resistance… this is the element of tastes – this is called "the element of tastes".

Depending on the tongue and tastes there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding tongue-consciousness element – this is called "the tongue-consciousness element".

Therein, what is the element of things touched? The earth element… this is the element of things touched – this is called "the element of things touched".
Therein, what is the body-consciousness element? Depending on the body and things touched there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding body-consciousness element – this is called "the body-consciousness element".
Therein, what is the mind element? Immediately after the arising and ceasing of the eye-consciousness element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind element; immediately after the arising and ceasing of the ear-consciousness element… of the nose-consciousness element… of the tongue-consciousness element… of the body-consciousness element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind element; or, at the first turning of attention towards any object whatever there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind element – this is called "the mind element".
Therein, what is the element of mental objects? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the material form that is invisible and offers no resistance and is included in the sense-field of mental objects, and the unconditioned element.
Therein, what is the aggregate of feeling? As a single class, the aggregate of feeling is joined with contact. As a twofold class: there is feeling with a root cause, and feeling without one. As a threefold class: there is wholesome feeling, unwholesome feeling, and neutral feeling… thus the aggregate of feeling is classified up to ten ways… thus the aggregate of feeling is classified in many ways. This is called "the aggregate of feeling".
Therein, what is the aggregate of perception? As a single class, the aggregate of perception is joined with contact. As a twofold class: there is perception with a root cause, and perception without one. As a threefold class: there is wholesome perception, unwholesome perception, and neutral perception… thus the aggregate of perception is classified up to ten ways… thus the aggregate of perception is classified in many ways. This is called "the aggregate of perception".
Therein, what is the aggregate of mental formations? As a single class, the aggregate of mental formations is joined with mind. As a twofold class: there are formations that are root causes, and formations that are not. As a threefold class: there are wholesome formations, unwholesome formations, and neutral formations… thus the aggregate of mental formations is classified up to ten ways… thus the aggregate of mental formations is classified in many ways. This is called "the aggregate of mental formations".
Therein, what is the material form that is invisible, offers no resistance, and is included in the sense-field of mental objects? The faculty of femininity… physical food – this is called the material form that is "invisible, offers no resistance, and is included in the sense-field of mental objects".
Therein, what is the unconditioned element? The ending of desire, the ending of hatred, and the ending of delusion – this is called "the unconditioned element". This is called "the element of mental objects".
Therein, what is the mind-consciousness element? Immediately after the arising and ceasing of the eye-consciousness element there arises the mind element, and immediately after the arising and ceasing of the mind element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity… and the corresponding mind-consciousness element; immediately after the arising and ceasing of the ear-consciousness element… of the nose-consciousness element… of the tongue-consciousness element… of the body-consciousness element there arises the mind element, and immediately after the arising and ceasing of that mind element there arises mind, mentality, mental activity… and the corresponding mind-consciousness element; and depending on the mind and mental objects there arises mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, and the corresponding mind-consciousness element – this is called "the mind-consciousness element".
The Classification According to the Abhidhamma is finished. ^2-184
