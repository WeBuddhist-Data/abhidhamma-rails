### Analysis of the Aggregates ^2-1-0

#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-1-1-0

1. There are five aggregates – the aggregate of material form, the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness. ^2-1

##### The Aggregate of Material Form ^2-1-1-1-0

2. Therein, what is the aggregate of material form? Whatever material form there is – past, future, or present; one's own or another's; coarse or subtle; inferior or superior; far or near – all of it gathered and grouped together: this is called the aggregate of material form. ^2-2

3. Therein, what is past material form? Material form that is past, ceased, gone, changed, ended, completely ended, that arose and then disappeared, that is past and counted as belonging to the past – that is, the four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements: this is called past material form.
Therein, what is future material form? Material form that is not yet born, not yet come to be, not yet come about, not yet produced, not yet brought forth, not yet appeared, not yet arisen, not yet come up, not yet emerged, not yet come into view, that is future and counted as belonging to the future – that is, the four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements: this is called future material form.
Therein, what is present material form? Material form that is born, come to be, come about, produced, brought forth, appeared, arisen, come up, emerged, come into view, that is present and counted as belonging to the present – that is, the four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements: this is called present material form. ^2-3

4. Therein, what is one's own material form? Material form that, for this or that being, is internal, personal, one's own, individual, and taken up at birth – that is, the four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements: this is called one's own material form.
Therein, what is another's material form? Material form that, for this or that other being, this or that other person, is internal, personal, their own, individual, and taken up at birth – that is, the four great elements and the material form derived from the four great elements: this is called another's material form. ^2-4

5. Therein, what is coarse material form? The eye faculty… the sense-field of touch: this is called coarse material form.
Therein, what is subtle material form? The faculty of femininity… physical food: this is called subtle material form. ^2-5

6. Therein, what is inferior material form? Material form that, for this or that being, is looked down on, despised, scorned, treated with contempt, disregarded, considered low, thought of as low, agreed to be low, unwanted, unattractive, disagreeable – that is, sights, sounds, smells, tastes, and things touched: this is called inferior material form.
Therein, what is superior material form? Material form that, for this or that being, is not looked down on, not despised, not scorned, not treated with contempt, but regarded highly, considered excellent, thought of as excellent, agreed to be excellent, wanted, attractive, agreeable – that is, sights, sounds, smells, tastes, and things touched: this is called superior material form. But material form should also be understood as inferior or superior only relative to this or that other material form. ^2-6

7. Therein, what is material form that is far? The faculty of femininity… physical food; or whatever other material form there is that is not close, not nearby, far off, not near: this is called material form that is far.
Therein, what is material form that is near? The eye sense-field… the sense-field of touch; or whatever other material form there is that is close, nearby, not far off, near: this is called material form that is near. But material form should also be understood as far or near only relative to this or that other material form. ^2-7

#### Classification According to the Abhidhamma ^2-1-2-0

##### The Aggregate of Material Form ^2-1-2-1-0

33. Therein, what is the aggregate of material form? As a single class, the aggregate of material form is this: all material form is not a root cause, is without root causes, is dissociated from root causes; it exists with conditions, is compounded, is material; it is worldly, subject to the mental taints, able to bind with the fetters, able to tie with the ties, able to sweep away with the floods, able to yoke with the yokes, able to obstruct with the hindrances, able to be wrongly grasped, able to be clung to, open to defilement; it is neutral, without an object, not a mental factor, dissociated from mind; it is neither a result nor something that produces results; it is undefiled yet open to defilement; it is not accompanied by both thought and examination, not accompanied by examination alone, but without both thought and examination; it is not accompanied by joy, not accompanied by happiness, not accompanied by equanimity; it is to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development, and its root causes are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development; it neither leads to further accumulation nor to dismantling; it belongs neither to trainees nor to those beyond training; it is limited, belongs to the sense sphere, does not belong to the fine-material sphere, does not belong to the immaterial sphere; it is included in the round of existence, not outside it; it is not fixed in its outcome, does not lead out of the round; it is arisen, and can be known by the six kinds of consciousness; it is impermanent and overcome by aging. This is the aggregate of material form as a single class.
As a twofold class, the aggregate of material form is this: there is material form that is derived, and material form that is not derived. There is material form that is taken up at birth, and material form that is not. There is material form that is taken up and can be clung to, and material form that is not taken up but can be clung to. There is material form that is visible, and material form that is invisible. There is material form that offers resistance, and material form that does not. There is material form that is a faculty, and material form that is not. There is material form that is a great element, and material form that is not. There is material form that is bodily or verbal expression, and material form that is not. There is material form produced by mind, and material form not produced by mind. There is material form that arises together with mind, and material form that does not. There is material form that follows along with mind, and material form that does not. There is material form that is internal, and material form that is external. There is material form that is coarse, and material form that is subtle. There is material form that is far, and material form that is near… there is material form that is physical food, and material form that is not physical food. This is the aggregate of material form as a twofold class.
(As this was analysed in the Chapter on Material Form, so it should be analysed here.)
As a threefold class, the aggregate of material form is this: material form that is internal is derived; material form that is external may be derived or not derived. Material form that is internal is taken up at birth; material form that is external may be taken up or not taken up. Material form that is internal is taken up and can be clung to; material form that is external may be taken up and clung to, or not taken up but able to be clung to… material form that is internal is not physical food; material form that is external may be physical food or not physical food. This is the aggregate of material form as a threefold class.
As a fourfold class, the aggregate of material form is this: material form that is derived may be taken up at birth or not taken up; material form that is not derived may be taken up or not taken up. Material form that is derived may be taken up and clung to, or not taken up but able to be clung to; material form that is not derived may be taken up and clung to, or not taken up but able to be clung to. Material form that is derived may offer resistance or not; material form that is not derived may offer resistance or not. Material form that is derived may be coarse or subtle; material form that is not derived may be coarse or subtle. Material form that is derived may be far or near; material form that is not derived may be far or near… and there is material form that is seen, heard, sensed, and cognized. This is the aggregate of material form as a fourfold class.
As a fivefold class, the aggregate of material form is this: the earth element, the water element, the fire element, the air element, and the material form derived from them. This is the aggregate of material form as a fivefold class.
As a sixfold class, the aggregate of material form is this: material form knowable by the eye, knowable by the ear, knowable by the nose, knowable by the tongue, knowable by the body, and knowable by the mind. This is the aggregate of material form as a sixfold class.
