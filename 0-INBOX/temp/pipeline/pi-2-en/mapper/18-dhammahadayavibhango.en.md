#### The Section Showing the Different Planes ^2-18-5-0

1020. There are things of the sense sphere and things not of the sense sphere; things of the fine-material sphere and things not of the fine-material sphere; things of the immaterial sphere and things not of the immaterial sphere; things included in a sphere and things not included in any sphere.
