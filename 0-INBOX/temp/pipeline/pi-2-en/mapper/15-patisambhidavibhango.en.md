### Analysis of the Analytical Knowledges ^2-15-0

#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-15-1-0

##### The Summary Section ^2-15-1-1-0

718. There are four analytical knowledges: the analytical knowledge of meaning, the analytical knowledge of principles, the analytical knowledge of language, the analytical knowledge of ready understanding. Knowledge with regard to meaning is the analytical knowledge of meaning; knowledge with regard to principles is the analytical knowledge of principles; knowledge with regard to the expression of the language of those principles is the analytical knowledge of language; knowledge with regard to knowledges is the analytical knowledge of ready understanding. This is the summary section. ^2-718

##### The Scripture Section ^2-15-1-6-0

724. There are four analytical knowledges: the analytical knowledge of meaning, the analytical knowledge of principles, the analytical knowledge of language, the analytical knowledge of ready understanding.
Therein, what is the analytical knowledge of principles? Here a monk knows the teaching — the discourses, the mixed prose-and-verse, the expositions, the verses, the inspired utterances, the "thus it was said" texts, the birth stories, the marvellous accounts, the question-and-answer texts. This is called "the analytical knowledge of principles". He knows the meaning of each thing that has been spoken — "this is the meaning of this saying, this is the meaning of this saying". This is called "the analytical knowledge of meaning". Knowledge with regard to the expression of the language of those principles is the analytical knowledge of language. Knowledge with regard to knowledges is the analytical knowledge of ready understanding. This is the scripture section.
The Classification According to the Discourses is finished. ^2-724

##### The Triads ^2-15-3-1-0

749. They may be wholesome; they may be neutral. They may be associated with pleasant feeling; they may be associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. Three analytical knowledges may be things subject to result, or may be neither results nor things subject to result; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be a result, may be a thing subject to result, or may be neither a result nor a thing subject to result. Three analytical knowledges are not taken up at birth but able to be clung to; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be not taken up at birth but able to be clung to, or may be not taken up at birth and not able to be clung to. Three analytical knowledges are undefiled but connected with defilement; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be undefiled but connected with defilement, or may be undefiled and not connected with defilement.
Three analytical knowledges are with thought and with examination; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, or may be without thought and without examination. They may be accompanied by joy; they may be accompanied by happiness; they may be accompanied by equanimity. They are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. Their causes are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development.
Three analytical knowledges may be leading to accumulation, or may be leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be leading to accumulation, may be leading to dismantling, or may be leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling.
Three analytical knowledges belong to one neither in training nor beyond training; the analytical knowledge of meaning may belong to one in training, may belong to one beyond training, or may belong to one neither in training nor beyond training. Three analytical knowledges are limited; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be limited, or may be measureless. The analytical knowledge of language has a limited object; three analytical knowledges may have limited objects, may have expanded objects, or may have measureless objects.
Three analytical knowledges are middling; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be middling, or may be refined. Three analytical knowledges are unfixed; the analytical knowledge of meaning may be fixed in rightness, or may be unfixed. The analytical knowledge of language is not describable as having the path as its object, as having the path as its cause, or as having the path as its dominant factor; the analytical knowledge of meaning does not have the path as its object, may have the path as its cause, may have the path as its dominant factor, or may not be describable either as having the path as its cause or as having the path as its dominant factor; two analytical knowledges may have the path as their object, do not have the path as their cause, may have the path as their dominant factor, or may not be describable either as having the path as their object or as having the path as their dominant factor.
