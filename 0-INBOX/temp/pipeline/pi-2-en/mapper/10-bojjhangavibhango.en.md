### Analysis of the Awakening Factors ^2-10-0

#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-10-1-0

466. There are seven awakening factors: the awakening factor of mindfulness, the awakening factor of investigation of things, the awakening factor of energy, the awakening factor of joy, the awakening factor of tranquillity, the awakening factor of concentration, the awakening factor of equanimity. ^2-466

467. Therein, what is the awakening factor of mindfulness? Here a monk is mindful, endowed with the highest mindfulness and care; he remembers and recalls what was done long ago and said long ago — this is called "the awakening factor of mindfulness".
Dwelling thus mindful, he investigates that teaching with wisdom, examines it, and enters into a full inquiry of it — this is called "the awakening factor of investigation of things".
As he investigates that teaching with wisdom, examines it, and enters into a full inquiry of it, energy is initiated in him without slackening — this is called "the awakening factor of energy".
In one whose energy is initiated, unworldly joy arises — this is called "the awakening factor of joy".
In one whose mind is joyful, the body becomes tranquil and the mind becomes tranquil — this is called "the awakening factor of tranquillity".
In one whose body is tranquil and who is happy, the mind becomes concentrated — this is called "the awakening factor of concentration".
He is one who watches well over the mind thus concentrated, with equanimity — this is called "the awakening factor of equanimity". ^2-467

#### Classification According to the Abhidhamma ^2-10-2-0

473. Therein, what are the seven awakening factors? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the meditative absorption that is beyond the world, leading out, leading to dismantling, for the abandoning of views, for reaching the first stage — secluded from sense pleasures… he enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption, of difficult practice and slow direct knowledge — on that occasion there are seven awakening factors: the awakening factor of mindfulness… the awakening factor of equanimity. ^2-473

474. Therein, what is the awakening factor of mindfulness? The mindfulness, recollection… right mindfulness, the awakening factor of mindfulness, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of mindfulness".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of investigation of things? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view, the awakening factor of investigation of things, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of investigation of things".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of energy? The mental initiation of energy… right effort, the awakening factor of energy, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of energy".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of joy? The joy, gladness, rejoicing, delight, mirth, merriment, felicity, elation, satisfaction of the mind, the awakening factor of joy — this is called "the awakening factor of joy".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of tranquillity? The tranquillity of the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness, their calming down, becoming tranquil, calming, state of being calmed, the awakening factor of tranquillity — this is called "the awakening factor of tranquillity".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of concentration? The steadiness of the mind… right concentration, the awakening factor of concentration, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of concentration".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of equanimity? The equanimity, being equanimous, watching over with equanimity, neutrality of the mind, the awakening factor of equanimity — this is called "the awakening factor of equanimity". These are called the seven awakening factors. The remaining things are associated with the seven awakening factors. ^2-474

#### The Section of Questions and Answers ^2-10-3-0

482. There are seven awakening factors: the awakening factor of mindfulness, the awakening factor of investigation of things, the awakening factor of energy, the awakening factor of joy, the awakening factor of tranquillity, the awakening factor of concentration, the awakening factor of equanimity. ^2-482

483. Of the seven awakening factors, how many are wholesome, how many unwholesome, how many neutral… how many involve conflict, how many are free from conflict? ^2-483

##### The Triads ^2-10-3-1-0

484. They may be wholesome; they may be neutral. The awakening factor of joy is associated with pleasant feeling; six awakening factors may be associated with pleasant feeling or may be associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. They may be results; they may be things subject to result. They are not taken up at birth and not able to be clung to. They are undefiled and not connected with defilement. They may be with thought and with examination; they may be without thought but with examination only; they may be without thought and without examination. The awakening factor of joy is not accompanied by joy, is accompanied by happiness, and is not accompanied by equanimity; six awakening factors may be accompanied by joy, may be accompanied by happiness, or may be accompanied by equanimity. They are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. Their causes are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. They may be leading to dismantling; they may be leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling. They may belong to one in training; they may belong to one beyond training. They are measureless. They have measureless objects. They are refined. They may be fixed in rightness; they may be unfixed. They do not have the path as their object; they may have the path as their cause; they may have the path as their dominant factor; they may not be describable either as having the path as their cause or as having the path as their dominant factor. They may be arisen; they may be not arisen; they may be due to arise. They may be past; they may be future; they may be present. They are not describable as having a past object, a future object, or a present object. They may be internal; they may be external; they may be both internal and external. They have external objects. They are invisible and offer no resistance. ^2-484

##### The Pairs ^2-10-3-2-0

485. The awakening factor of investigation of things is a root; six awakening factors are associated with roots. The awakening factor of investigation of things is both a root and has roots; six awakening factors are not describable as being both roots and having roots; they have roots but are not roots. The awakening factor of investigation of things is both a root and associated with roots; six awakening factors are not describable as being both roots and associated with roots; they are associated with roots but are not roots. Six awakening factors are not roots yet have roots; the awakening factor of investigation of things is not describable either as being a non-root that has roots or as being a non-root without roots. They have conditions. They are conditioned. They are invisible. They offer no resistance. They are immaterial. They are beyond the world. They can be known by one means; they cannot be known by another. They are not mental taints. They are without mental taints and dissociated from mental taints. They are not describable either as being mental taints and subject to mental taints, or as being subject to mental taints but not mental taints. Nor as being mental taints and associated with mental taints, or as being associated with mental taints but not mental taints. They are dissociated from mental taints. They are without mental taints. They are not fetters… they are not ties… they are not floods… they are not yokes… they are not hindrances… they are not wrong grasping… they have objects. They are not minds. They are mental factors. They are associated with mind. They are conjoined with mind. They are originated by mind. They arise together with mind. They follow along with mind. They are conjoined with and originated by mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and arise together with mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and follow along with mind. They are external. They are not derived. They are not taken up at birth. They are not clinging… they are not defilements… they are not to be abandoned by seeing. They are not to be abandoned by meditative development. Their causes are not to be abandoned by seeing. Their causes are not to be abandoned by meditative development. They may be with thought; they may be without thought. They may be with examination; they may be without examination.
The awakening factor of joy is without joy; six awakening factors may be with joy or may be without joy. The awakening factor of joy is accompanied by happiness; six awakening factors may be accompanied by happiness or may be not accompanied by happiness. They do not belong to the fine-material sphere. They do not belong to the immaterial sphere. They are not included in any sphere. They may be leading out; they may be not leading out. They may be fixed; they may be unfixed. They are unsurpassed. They are free from conflict.
