#### Classification According to the Discourses ^2-13-1-0

642. There are four measureless states. Here a monk dwells pervading one direction with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness, likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth. So above, below, across, everywhere, regarding all as himself, he dwells pervading the entire world with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness — abundant, expanded, measureless, without hostility, without ill will. He dwells pervading one direction with a mind accompanied by compassion, likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth. ##### Loving-Kindness ^2-13-1-1-0

643. And how does a monk dwell pervading one direction with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness? Just as, on seeing one person who is dear and pleasing, he would feel loving-kindness, in the same way he pervades all beings with loving-kindness.
Therein, what is loving-kindness? The friendliness towards beings, being friendly, the state of friendliness, the liberation of mind through loving-kindness — this is called "loving-kindness".
Therein, what is the mind? The mind, mentality, mental activity, heart, the bright one, mind, the mind sense-field, the mind faculty, consciousness, the aggregate of consciousness, the corresponding mind-consciousness element — this is called "the mind". This mind is accompanied by this loving-kindness, arisen together with it, conjoined with it, associated with it. Therefore it is said, "with a mind accompanied by loving-kindness". ^2-643

##### The Triads ^2-13-3-1-0

701. They may be wholesome; they may be neutral. Three measureless states are associated with pleasant feeling; equanimity is associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. They may be results; they may be things subject to result; they may be neither results nor things subject to result. They may be taken up at birth and able to be clung to; they may be not taken up at birth but able to be clung to. They are undefiled but connected with defilement. Three measureless states may be with thought and with examination, may be without thought but with examination only, or may be without thought and without examination; equanimity is without thought and without examination. Three measureless states may be accompanied by joy, may be accompanied by happiness, are not accompanied by equanimity, or may not be describable as accompanied by joy; equanimity is accompanied by equanimity. They are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. Their causes are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. They may be leading to accumulation; they may be leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling. They belong to one neither in training nor beyond training. They are expanded. They are not describable as having limited objects, expanded objects, or measureless objects. They are middling. They are unfixed. They are not describable as having the path as their object, as having the path as their cause, or as having the path as their dominant factor. They may be arisen; they may be not arisen; they may be due to arise. They may be past; they may be future; they may be present. They are not describable as having a past object, a future object, or a present object. They may be internal; they may be external; they may be both internal and external. They have external objects. They are invisible and offer no resistance. ^2-701

##### The Pairs ^2-13-3-2-0

702. Loving-kindness is a root; three measureless states are not roots; they have roots; they are associated with roots. Loving-kindness is both a root and has roots; three measureless states are not describable as being both roots and having roots; they have roots but are not roots. Loving-kindness is both a root and associated with roots; three measureless states are not describable as being both roots and associated with roots; they are associated with roots but are not roots. Three measureless states are not roots yet have roots; loving-kindness is not describable either as being a non-root that has roots or as being a non-root without roots.
They have conditions; they are conditioned; they are invisible; they offer no resistance; they are immaterial; they are worldly; they can be known by one means; they cannot be known by another. They are not mental taints; they are subject to mental taints; they are dissociated from mental taints; they are not describable as being both mental taints and subject to mental taints; they are subject to mental taints but not mental taints; they are not describable either as being mental taints and associated with mental taints, or as being associated with mental taints but not mental taints. They are dissociated from mental taints but subject to mental taints.
They are not fetters… they are not ties… they are not floods… they are not yokes… they are not hindrances… they are not wrong grasping… they have objects; they are not minds; they are mental factors; they are associated with mind; they are conjoined with mind; they are originated by mind; they arise together with mind; they follow along with mind; they are conjoined with and originated by mind; they are conjoined with, originated by, and arise together with mind; they are conjoined with, originated by, and follow along with mind; they are external; they are not derived; they may be taken up at birth; they may be not taken up at birth.
They are not clinging… they are not defilements… they are not to be abandoned by seeing; they are not to be abandoned by meditative development; their causes are not to be abandoned by seeing; their causes are not to be abandoned by meditative development. Three measureless states may be with thought or may be without thought; equanimity is without thought. Three measureless states may be with examination or may be without examination; equanimity is without examination. Three measureless states may be with joy or may be without joy; equanimity is without joy. Three measureless states may be accompanied by joy or may be not accompanied by joy; equanimity is not accompanied by joy. Three measureless states are accompanied by happiness; equanimity is not accompanied by happiness. Equanimity is accompanied by equanimity; three measureless states are not accompanied by equanimity. They do not belong to the sense sphere; they belong to the fine-material sphere; they do not belong to the immaterial sphere; they are included in a sphere; they are not leading out; they are unfixed; they are surpassable; they are free from conflict.
