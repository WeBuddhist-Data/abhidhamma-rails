##### The Triads ^2-7-3-1-0

388. They may be wholesome; they may be neutral. They may be associated with pleasant feeling; they may be associated with neither-painful-nor-pleasant feeling. They may be results; they may be things subject to result. They are not taken up at birth and not able to be clung to. They are undefiled and not connected with defilement. They may be with thought and with examination; they may be without thought but with examination only; they may be without thought and without examination. They may be accompanied by joy; they may be accompanied by happiness; they may be accompanied by equanimity. They are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. Their causes are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by meditative development. They may be leading to dismantling; they may be leading neither to accumulation nor to dismantling. They may belong to one in training; they may belong to one beyond training. They are measureless. They have measureless objects. They are refined. They may be fixed in rightness; they may be unfixed.
