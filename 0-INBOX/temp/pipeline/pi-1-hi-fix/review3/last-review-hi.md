# Last review: corrected Hindi pi-1 (Dhammasaṅgaṇī), audience and scholar

**What I checked:** all 62 kinds of change in `changes.jsonl`, and all 140 blocks in `sample.jsonl` (65 changed by the fixes, 75 not), each read as whole sentences against the Pāli. I also searched the whole current book file (`AI_translation/hindi/pi-1-hindi-plain.md`) for old wording that might be left over and for agreement errors, and checked disputed terms in `1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhammasangani-atthakatha.md`. I did not read the earlier reviews.

**Transliteration:** this is the plain text, not a scholar version, so I did not ask for Pāli transliteration in the running text.

## 1. Verdicts

**Audience (plain.md): satisfied with corrections (1 minor).**
- The changes make the text clearer for a reader with no Buddhist background. Words with misleading everyday meanings are gone (मूर्छा, अशिक्षित, निडरता, आक्रोश, अमानवीय, दुःखद, सेवन, "permanent self"). The new wording (उपदेश से अनजान, रोष, मदहोशी, कष्टदायक प्रगति, मनुष्येतर ध्वनि, बुराई से न डरने की वृत्ति) is plain, modern Hindi.
- «आँख / कान … मन से होने वाला विज्ञान» prevents the reading "science of the eye" or "psychology". In the lists it reads naturally.
- One remaining fault misleads this reader: in the four-element definitions (^1-967–^1-970), *bahiddhā* is rendered «दूसरों से संबंधित», which says the elements exist only in people.

**Scholar check: satisfied with corrections (2 minor, one shared with the audience).**
- The changes are accurate, and the commentary supports them:
  - *paṭiccasamuppanna* is no longer "mutual".
  - *idappaccayatā* and *paṭiccasamuppannesu dhammesu* are two objects.
  - *siyā* is modal.
  - *nāma* includes the unconditioned.
  - *appaṇihita* is "without aspiration" (aṭṭhakathā: *rāgadosamohapaṇidhayo natthi*).
  - The *neva dassanena na bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukā* reading is right.
- Terminology is consistent: no old wording is left anywhere in the book, and I found no agreement errors with विज्ञान.
- Structure and display are faithful: (क)/(ख)/(ग) numbering, «…» for …pe…, variant brackets «[पाठभेद / वर्तनी-भेद (Sī./Syā.): …]», and «यानी» only before closed lists (open example lists keep «जैसे»).
- One inconsistency is left: the *upādiṇṇa* correction made at ^1-1050/1051 was not carried to ^1-967–^1-970, where the aṭṭhakathā gives the same note.

## 2. Necessary corrections

| ref | perspective | severity | current | corrected | reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ^1-967, ^1-968, ^1-969, ^1-970 | scholar | minor | «चाहे कर्म से उत्पन्न हो या कर्म से उत्पन्न न हो» | «चाहे ग्रहण किया हुआ हो या ग्रहण न किया हुआ हो» | The aṭṭhakathā on 967 says: *"Upādiṇṇanti na kammasamuṭṭhānameva. Avisesena pana sarīraṭṭhakassetaṃ gahaṇaṃ. Sarīraṭṭhakañhi upādiṇṇaṃ vā hotu anupādiṇṇaṃ vā, ādinnagahitaparāmaṭṭhavasena sabbaṃ upādiṇṇameva nāma."* That is the same note on which ^1-1050/1051 was changed to «ग्रहण की हुई». As it stands, the same word in the same sense has two renderings, one of them the rejected "kamma-born". This is wrong terminology, not a style choice. (The meaning of the whole clause barely changes, because it is an either/or.) |
| ^1-967, ^1-968, ^1-969, ^1-970 | audience + scholar | minor | «चाहे स्वयं में हो या दूसरों से संबंधित हो» | «चाहे स्वयं में हो या बाहर हो» | Misleading. «दूसरों से संबंधित» reads as "belonging to other people", so a stone's earth or the wind would be excluded. The aṭṭhakathā on 967 has *"Ajjhattanti niyakajjhattaṃ. Bahiddhāti bāhiraṃ"*, and it counts the earth-*kasiṇa* as *bahiddhā* (*"niyakajjhattato bahiddhābhāvena bahiddhābhūtāni pathavīkasiṇādīni"*). The termbase already has the sense बाहर for *bahiddhā*. «दूसरों से संबंधित» stays right in the *ajjhatta* triad (^1-1051, where the text itself says *parasattānaṃ*). |

Combined, the clause in all four blocks would read: «…, चाहे स्वयं में हो या बाहर हो, चाहे ग्रहण किया हुआ हो या ग्रहण न किया हुआ हो – …». The subject (पृथ्वी / जल / अग्नि / वायु तत्व) is masculine, so the forms are किया हुआ.

  - 0 instances of चेतना are left. Agreement is right everywhere I searched (no «होने वाली विज्ञान», «की विज्ञान», «विज्ञान … होती / हो चुकी / सकती»).

  - ^1-1388/^1-1404: «शरीर से होने वाला वह विज्ञान जो दुःख के साथ होता है» is clear and reads as one item.

  - *māna* (^1-1239): «अपने को कुछ मानना, अपने को कुछ मान बैठना».
- **Single terms:**
  - *mucchā* मदहोशी matches the aṭṭhakathā (*mucchanti etāya pāṇino*).
  - *khārika* क्षारीय is distinct from नमकीन.
  - *āsevanā* निरंतर आचरण.
  - *nevasaññānāsaññā* «न पहचान, न पहचान के अभाव».

- **Other changed blocks:**
  - *upādiṇṇa* as «कर्म से उत्पन्न» is right where the text defines it by kamma (^1-653, ^1-748, ^1-915–^1-933, ^1-1217, ^1-1393, ^1-1551).

  - The title, ^1-0 and the colophon carry the book's name.
- **Unchanged sample blocks** (the Mātikā lines, cetasika definitions, rūpa sections, the dyad answers 1-1475–1-1579): accurate, consistent and natural.
- **«दान देना व्यर्थ है» (^1-1369):** this follows the aṭṭhakathā on 1221 (*dinnassa pana phalaṃ vipāko natthīti gaṇhāti*). It is an allowed clarification, not an addition.

## 4. For the human reviewers

- **Stray «ः» in the Pāli source:** it is in 11 blocks, not only the 2 the fixes log names: ^1-1106, ^1-1123, ^1-1128, ^1-1143, ^1-1144, ^1-1168, ^1-1181, ^1-1240, ^1-1241, ^1-1369, ^1-1371. The Hindi renders it correctly as «;».
- **«काया की गाँठ» (*kāyagantha*, ^1-1140 and following):** the aṭṭhakathā reads *nāmakāyaṃ gantheti*. A plain reader may take काया as the physical body. Elsewhere the text uses «मानसिक काया» for this *kāya* (*kāyapassaddhi* etc.). This is a literal rendering, the same kind of decision as the third-jhāna *kāyena*, so I did not count it as a correction.
- **Termbase, if the corrections are applied:** `upādiṇṇaṃ` (termbase line 916) needs the second sense «ग्रहण किया हुआ» for ^1-967–^1-970. The `bahiddhā` entry already has «बाहर», and its second-sense note should say it is used for the triad only.
- **«स्वयं सीमित, पर अपरिमित विषय वाला»:** «पर» is a light contrast word that is not in the Pāli. It is acceptable under plain.md, but a scholar may notice it.
