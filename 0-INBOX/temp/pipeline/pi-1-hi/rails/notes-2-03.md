## Terms the termbase lacks, built from members
- maggapariyāpannaṃ: magga + pariyāpanna (the termbase has only the masculine/feminine "समाई हुई / समाए हुए" forms with «अस्तित्व के लोकों में»).
- sammappadhānaṃ: sammā + padhānaṃ = सही प्रयास. The same Hindi as sammāvāyāmo; pi-5 has «सही प्रयत्न», but the pi-1 termbase entry padhānaṃ = प्रयास wins.
- rūparāga / arūparāga: rūpa (fine-material sphere) and Arūpa (immaterial sphere) + rāga.
- anavasesappahānāya: avasesa = शेष + pahāna = छोड़ना: «बिना कुछ शेष रखे छोड़ने के लिए».
- mahānayā: mahā (as in mahāvipākā, बड़ा) + nayā = विधि.
- diṭṭhānaṃ / pattānaṃ: built on the model of ñātānaṃ and viditānaṃ.
- sammākammanto: sammā + kamma = सही कर्म (as in pi-5).

## Decisions and source notes
- bhāveti has no expressed subject in Pāli. Hindi needs one, so the formula has «जिस समय कोई …».
- Hindi «चुकना» does not take a negative, so the negated forms in ^1-296 read «जानी नहीं गई हैं». Adherence therefore reports viditānaṃ and sacchikatānaṃ at ^1-296 as false misses.
- Source slip: a stray space before …pe… at ^1-342 and ^1-359 («vūpasamā …pe…», «jhānaṃ …pe…»). Treated as if absent, so the Hindi is the same as in the neighbouring segments.
- Source oddity: ^1-364 has a comma, not a dash, before «idaṃ tasmiṃ samaye aññindriyaṃ hoti». Kept (no dash added).
- Remaining adherence misses are inflection only: viharati / upasampajja viharati (रहता है), kathinatā and jimhatā (the approved strings «कड़ेपन», «टेढ़ेपन»), sacchikatānaṃ at ^1-364 (feminine «की जा चुकी हैं»).
