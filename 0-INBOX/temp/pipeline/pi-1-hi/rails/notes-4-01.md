## Termbase misfits and gaps

- bhūtā (^1-1041, ^1-1046; abhūtā ^1-1042, ^1-1045): termbase has only 'great elements = महाभूत'; here 'become, come to be'. Wrote «हो चुकी».
- titthāyatanaṃ (every diṭṭhi list): termbase āyatana is only इंद्रिय-क्षेत्र; here 'ground of sectarian views'. Wrote «मतवादों का आधार» (tittha = मतवाद).
- samuppannā (^1-1041, ^1-1046; asamuppannā ^1-1042, ^1-1045): termbase gives «उत्पन्न», the same as the adjacent uppannā. Wrote «भली-भाँति उत्पन्न» to keep the two apart, matching sañjātā, samuṭṭhitā.
- Terms absent from the termbase, built here: ajjhosānaṃ «चिपके रहना»; māra- «मार»; lābha «लाभ»; saṅgha «संघ»; pubbante «अतीत» / aparante «भविष्य»; saṅgahitā «वर्गीकृत» (saṅgaho = वर्गीकरण); atthaṅgatā «अस्त हो चुकी»; maccheraṃ «कंजूसी»; asassato «अशाश्वत»; seyyo «श्रेष्ठ»; appiya «अप्रिय»; atthaṃ «हित».
- paṭipadāya in dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya: termbase «प्रगति का ढंग» used as locked; a plain reader may prefer another word.
