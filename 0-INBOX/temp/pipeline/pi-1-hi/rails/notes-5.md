## Recasts to avoid a word next to its own repetition

- X … uppajjati: the locus comes first, so «लोभ, लोभ के साथ…» and «दृष्टि ग़लत दृष्टि से…» do not arise.

- dukkhasahagata (^1-1388, ^1-1404): compound member gives दुःखद, which cannot stand before «के साथ». Wrote दुःख के साथ होने वाली (same as 2-05).
