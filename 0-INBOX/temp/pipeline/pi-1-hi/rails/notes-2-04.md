## Termbase misfit

- paṭighasaññānaṃ (^1-501). Termbase has paṭigha only as «चिढ़» (aversion, resentment). In this attainment formula it is the perception of sensory impact, which is the sense of sappaṭigha / appaṭigha (टकराव वाला / टकराव से रहित). Wrote «टकराव की पहचानों». 2-05 does the same.

## Built from members (no termbase entry)

- titthāyatanaṃ: tittha = मतवाद + āyatana sense «आधार» (as in abhibhāyatanāni): «मतवादों का आधार».
- avijjogho, avijjāyogo, avijjānusayo, avijjāpariyuṭṭhānaṃ, avijjālaṅgī: अज्ञान + ogho / yogo / anusayo / pariyuṭṭhānaṃ / laṅgī.
- diṭṭhigahanaṃ, diṭṭhikantāro, diṭṭhivisūkāyikaṃ, diṭṭhivipphanditaṃ: दृष्टि + the member term.
- lubbhitattaṃ, sārajjitattaṃ, byāpajjitattaṃ, kaṅkhāyitattaṃ: modelled on dussitattaṃ (द्वेष से भरा होना).
- domanassindriyaṃ, sukhindriyaṃ: member + क्षमता.
- pañcindriyāni, tīṇindriyāni: numeral + indriyāni.
- nevasaññānāsaññāyatana: नेवसञ्ञा = न पहचान, nāsaññā = न अ-पहचान + क्षेत्र.

## Decisions and source notes

- thinaṃ and middhaṃ do not occur in this chapter: the prompted minds (^1-399, 402, 409, 412, 421) abbreviate the factor list with …pe…, so that synonym string is not set here.
- Source slip: ^1-377 ends with «?» after a statement. Kept, as the sample keeps ^1-33.
- Source slip: ^1-473 has «.» after the question «Katamā … cetanā hoti». The Hindi has «?», since it is a question.
- Source slip: stray space before …pe… at ^1-429 and ^1-498. Treated as absent.
- Source oddity: ^1-500 orders the abbreviated jhānas dutiyaṃ, tatiyaṃ, catutthaṃ, paṭhamaṃ, pañcamaṃ. Kept (also at 2-05).
- «Tasseva … vipākaṃ … viharati, tasmiṃ samaye» has no relative in Pāli. Hindi needs a correlative for «उस समय», so it has «जिस समय वह …».
- 2-05 words the count clause «एक शरीर की चेतना का तत्व / एक मन के विषयों का इंद्रिय-क्षेत्र»; this chapter and 2-03 have «शरीर की चेतना का एक तत्व / मन के विषयों का एक इंद्रिय-क्षेत्र» (identical Pāli at ^1-453, 467, 482, 496). 2-01, 2-02 and 2-05 open that line «और उस समय»; 2-03 has «उस समय».
- Adherence: the only misses are inflection (ārabbha in «विषय बनाए», katattā in «किए जाने», viharati in «रहता है») and ñāṇaṃ at ^1-390, where the keyword is aññāṇaṃ (न जानना).
