## Source notes (translated as written)
- ^1-1310: «ime dhammā sukkā?» ends with "?" (all parallels end with "."); kept as «?». Looks like a source slip.
- ^1-1168, ^1-1181, ^1-1240, ^1-1241, ^1-1369, ^1-1371: the Pāli uses the Devanāgarī visarga «ः» where a colon/semicolon is meant («aññāṇaṃः yaṃ», «vāः yā», «pavedentītiः yā»); rendered as «;».
- ^1-1189: «rūpāvacarā arūpāvacarā» lacks the comma of every parallel; rendered with the standard frame.
- ^1-1222 vs ^1-1264/^1-1271: punctuation of «sīlena suddhi vatena suddhi» varies (commas or none); one Hindi rendering.
- ^1-1301: «Tīṇi akusalamūlāni lobho, doso, moho» has no dash (^1-1249 has one); kept without (colon).
- ^1-1285 / ^1-1286: equanimity is listed for kāma, rūpa, arūpa, apariyāpanna in the "with" item but only kāma, rūpa, apariyāpanna in the "without" item (the reverse of the other pairs); translated as written.
- ^1-1283 / ^1-1285: the associated aggregates start from saññākkhandho (feeling itself is the factor excluded); translated as written.
