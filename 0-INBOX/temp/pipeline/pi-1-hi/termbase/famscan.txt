== āhāra (2)
  āhāraṃ	आहार	nutriment, a sustaining condition of mind (contact, volition, consciousness)
  āhāraṃ	भोजन	edible food, as in kabaḷīkāro āhāro, in the moderation-in-eating passage
== ālok (2)
  āloko	आलोक	light, as a figure for wisdom (nominative sg.)
  āloko	प्रकाश	daylight as a visible form, in the list of visible objects
== ārammaṇa (2)
  ārammaṇaṃ	विषय	object of the mind, what the mind takes up (noun, nominative/accusative sg.)
  ārammaṇaṃ	विषय वाला	having such-and-such an object, as in parittārammaṇaṃ (compound final)
