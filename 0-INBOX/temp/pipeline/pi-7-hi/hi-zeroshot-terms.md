## 4. paccaya and the 24 conditions (all forms)

paccaya = कारण [pi-6 paccayo, pi-1/pi-2 paccayā]
paccayo (bare predicate, "is a condition") = कारण होता/होती है [new]
na … paccayo = कारण नहीं होता/होती [new]
Rule: name = "<base>-कारण"; ablative (frames, fixed conditions in counts) = "<name> से"; negative ablative = "<name> के बिना"; instrumental "…paccayena paccayo" = "<name> के रूप में कारण होता है"; locative in counts = "<name> में"; negative locative = "<name> के अभाव में"; negative name (heading) = "<name> का अभाव"; before a postposition "<name> के अभाव …".
Chains of ablatives are joined with ", " (F.CH): hetupaccayā naārammaṇapaccayā = मूल-कारण से, विषय-कारण के बिना.
