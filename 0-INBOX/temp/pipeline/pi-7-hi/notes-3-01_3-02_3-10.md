## Source slips and oddities (translated as written)
- 3-01 count line "Nahetupaccayā ārammaṇe cattāri, ādhipatiyā cattāri…": "ādhipatiyā" is a spelling slip for adhipatiyā (rendered प्रधानता-कारण में).
- 3-02 "lokuttarasa" (pañhā frame) = spelling slip for lokuttarassa.
- 3-10 ^7-33-95 "cittasamuṭṭhānānañca rūpaṃ" (gen. pl. + nom. sg.): agreement slip, rendered मन से उत्पन्न भौतिक रूप.
- "Upādānañca noupādānaṃ ca dhammaṃ" (3-10): odd form of the joint accusative; rendered as the joint item.
- "sahetukassa ceva na ca hetussa ca", "upādāniyassa ceva no ca upādānassa ca", "upādānasampayuttassa ceva no ca upādānassa ca" used for a single item (extra "ca"): rendered as the single item.
- Missing "–" in the Pāli, kept without a dash: "… dhammā … dhammassa sahajātaṃ" (3-01 ^7-24-198 and parallel lines in 3-02), "Sahajātādhipati nahetu adhipati …" (3-01 ^7-24-22 area), "purejātaṃ vatthu ahetukānaṃ …" (3-01), "sappaṭigho dhammo appaṭighassa dhammassa atthipaccayena paccayo sahajātaṃ" (3-02).
