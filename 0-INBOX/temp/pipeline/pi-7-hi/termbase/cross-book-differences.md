## Left out: same Pāli word, different meaning (or a difference the pi-7 conventions intend)

- paṭicca = निर्भर होकर vs pi-1/2/3 परस्पर-आश्रित (that is the paṭiccasamuppāda compound member; mapper conventions).
- Saṃkhittaṃ संक्षिप्त 'abbreviated' vs pi-2/5/6 सिमटा हुआ (a shrunken mind); Tikaṃ, Catukkaṃ, Navakaṃ … (combinations of N conditions) vs pi-2/pi-4 "N-N के वर्गीकरण / प्रकार" (the classifications by N); Suddhaṃ, Ekamūlakaṃ, kāraṇena, sadde, byañjanena, Saraṇaṃ, doso, Sotaṃ, Upādā, ṭhānesu: homonyms.
- saṅkhāre (in 'saṅkhāre aniccato vipassati') = निर्मित परिघटना, as pi-5 'conditioned things in general'; pi-1/2 मानसिक रचनाएँ is the fourth aggregate.
- uppajjati vs pi-2 जन्म लेना (said of beings); dhamma / dhammo / dhammā vs मन के विषय / शिक्षा; rūpaṃ (matter vs visible form); cakkhundriyaṃ vs pi-2/pi-4 आँख की इंद्रिय (the senses to be guarded, core terms keep इंद्रिय there); āhāro / āhāraṃ भोजन (edible food) vs pi-1/2 आहार (the mental nutriment); kāyassa vs pi-1/2 मानसिक काया; Ajjhattaṃ, Bahiddhā (triad items) vs pi-1/2/4 स्वयं में, बाहर (the adverb 'internally / outside').
- Label and frame words: sahajātaṃ / purejātaṃ / sahajāto (labels and the Sahajātavāra frame) vs pi-1/2/5 participles 'born together / before'.
- Plural-only and head-noun-only differences: kileso / kilese मन के क्लेश vs pi-1/5 मन का क्लेश, āsavo मन के मैल vs pi-1 मन का मैल; kiriyābyākata- vs pi-1/2 «… मन» and pi-5 «केवल क्रिया के रूप में …»; vipākadhammadhammo, Apariyāpannaṃ, kabaḷīkāraṃ, vuṭṭhahantassa (pi-1/pi-5 add a noun); dictionary-form-only differences (vuṭṭhahitvā उठकर vs pi-5 उठना, vodānaṃ शुद्धि vs pi-2/5 शुद्ध होना, niruddhe, pahātabbaṃ, viññeyyaṃ, pahīne, uppajjeyyuṃ, bhaṇitvā); spelling महत्व / महत्त्व.
- Within pi-7, kept on purpose: nevavipākanavipākadhammadhamma- = «न कर्म-फल रूपी न कर्म-फल उत्पन्न करने वाला» (pi-2's term for the triad item, unchanged) although the bare adjective vipāka is now कर्म के फल रूपी (row 2).
