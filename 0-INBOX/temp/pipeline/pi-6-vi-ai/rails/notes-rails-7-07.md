## Strings that cannot align with 2-02 (family E), and why

- «no ca so …» = «, nhưng người ấy vẫn …»: 2-02's no-ca frame has no pronoun because its Pāli has none; here the Pāli has so / tato / so tato, rendered with family C's pronouns (7-03 / 7-06). Everything else (verb forms, «vẫn», both-frame, «không V B, cũng không V C», ṭhapetvā head, yo_q) is asserted equal.
- the both-frame with tadekaṭṭhaṃ («V A, cũng V B, vốn cùng hiện diện với nó») has no 2-02 counterpart (no tadekaṭṭha in the Khandhayamaka); it is 2-02's / 7-05's both-frame plus 7-05's TAD_JOIN.
