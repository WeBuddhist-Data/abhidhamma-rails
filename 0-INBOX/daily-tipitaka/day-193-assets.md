## Cuddasamanayo

[[#^toc-15|↑↑↑]]

Idāni cuddasamo nayo vippayuttenasaṅgahitāsaṅgahitapadaniddeso nāma āraddho, so ca dhātukathāya pariyosānanayo. Tattha yesaṃ padānaṃ vippayogo na ruhati, tāni imasmiṃ vāre na gahitāni. Kāni pana tānīti? Dhammāyatanādīni. Dhammāyatanassa hi khandhādīsu ekenāpi vippayogo na ruhati; dhammadhātuādīsupi eseva nayo.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-100]]

### Vippayuttenasaṅgahitāsaṅgahitapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-15-1|↑↑↑]]

Tesaṃ aggahitānaṃ idamuddānaṃ: "Dhammāyatanaṃ dhammadhātu, jīvitindriyameva ca; nāmarūpapadañceva, saḷāyatanameva ca. Jātiādittayaṃ ekaṃ, padaṃ vīsatime tike; tikāvasānikaṃ ekaṃ, satta cūḷantare padā. Daseva gocchake honti, mahantaramhi cuddasa; cha padāni tato uddhaṃ sabbānipi samāsato; padāni ca na labbhanti, cattālīsañca satta cā"ti. Pariyosāne ca "dhammāyatanaṃ dhammadhātū"ti pāḷigāthāpi imamevatthaṃ dīpeti; tattha "samucchede na labbhantī"ti pariyosāne naye na labbhantīti attho. Dhammāyatanādīnaṃ anārammaṇamissakasabbacittuppādagatadhammabhāvato vippayogassa aruhaṇaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ; jātiādittayassa pana dhammasabhāvamattattā na kathañci sampayogo vippayogo ca ruhati. Dukkhasaccādidhammāva idha tehi vippayuttānaṃ saṅgahāsaṅgahasabbhāvā gahitā. Imāni pana ṭhapetvā sesāni sabbānipi labbhanti; tesu khandhādivibhāgo vuttanayānusāreneva veditabbo. Ettāvatā vippayuttenasaṅgahitāsaṅgahitapadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā, dhātukathāpakaraṇañca niṭṭhitaṃ.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-3-practice.md`)

## Cuddasamanayo

[[#^toc-15|↑↑↑]]

Cuddasamanaye vippayuttena saṅgahitaṃ asaṅgahitañca pucchitaṃ; yesaṃ pana vippayogo na ruhati, dhammāyatanādīni, tāni na gahitāni. Tasmā yoginā dhamme vavatthapentena "ayaṃ dhammo kehici vippayutto, ayaṃ na kenaci vippayujjatī"ti yathābhūtaṃ jānitabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-100]]

### Vippayuttenasaṅgahitāsaṅgahitapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-15-1|↑↑↑]]

Jātiādittayassa dhammasabhāvamattattā na kathañci sampayogo vippayogo ca ruhati: jāti jarā maraṇañca saṅkhatadhammānaṃ sabhāvamattaṃ, na tehi visuṃ kiñci. Tasmā yoginā sabbe saṅkhatadhamme jātijarāmaraṇasabhāve passantena tesu ālayo pajahitabbo; imāni ṭhapetvā sesāni sabbānipi labbhantīti dhātukathāya pariyosāne sabbadhamme khandhāyatanadhātuvasena vavatthapetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/pi-3-english-plain.md`)

The states that are dissociated from the aggregate of form: in how many aggregates, bases and elements are those states included? Those states are included in four aggregates, two bases and eight elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten sense bases and ten elements. ^3-456

The states that are dissociated from the aggregate of feeling… from the aggregate of perception… from the aggregate of formations… from the aggregate of consciousness… from the mind base… from the mind faculty: in how many aggregates, bases and elements are those states included? Leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, those states are included in one aggregate, eleven sense bases and eleven elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from four aggregates, one base and seven elements. ^3-457

The states that are dissociated from the eye base… from the touch base… from the eye element… from the touch element… those states are included in four aggregates, two bases and eight elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten sense bases and ten elements. ^3-458

The states that are dissociated from the eye-consciousness element… from the ear-consciousness element… from the nose-consciousness element… from the tongue-consciousness element… from the body-consciousness element… from the mind-element… from the mind-consciousness element… leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, those states are included in five aggregates, twelve sense bases and seventeen elements. In how many are they excluded? They are not excluded from any aggregate or any base, but are excluded from one element. ^3-459
