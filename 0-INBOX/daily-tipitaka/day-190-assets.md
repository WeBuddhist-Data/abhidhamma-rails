## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-3-summaries.md`)

## Ekādasamanayo

[[#^toc-12|↑↑↑]]

Idāni ekādasamo nayo saṅgahitenasampayuttavippayuttapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. Tattha ye saṅgahitenasaṅgahitapadaniddese samudayasaccādayova dhammā uddhaṭā, sabbapucchāsu teyeva uddhaṭā; sadisavissajjanānaṃ pana ekato gahitattā padāni aññāya paṭipāṭiyā āgatāni.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-91]]

### Saṅgahitenasampayuttavippayuttapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-12-1|↑↑↑]]

Tattha ye dhammā pucchāya uddhaṭapadena khandhādisaṅgahena saṅgahitā, tesaṃ yehi sampayogo vā vippayogo vā hoti, tesaṃ vasena khandhādivibhāgo veditabbo. Samudayasaccena tāva saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannā dhammā khandhādisaṅgahena saṅgahitā. Te ca sesehi tīhi khandhehi, ekena manāyatanena, sattahi viññāṇadhātūhi sampayuttā; saṅkhārakkhandhe dhammāyatanadhammadhātūsu ca ṭhapetvā taṇhaṃ sesehi sampayuttattā kehici sampayuttā nāma. Ekena pana rūpakkhandhena, dasahi rūpāyatanehi rūpadhātūhi ca vippayuttā; ekasmiṃ dhammāyatane dhammadhātuyā ca rūpanibbānehi vippayuttattā kehici vippayuttā nāma. Iminā upāyena sabbattha attho veditabbo. Ettāvatā saṅgahitenasampayuttavippayuttapadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā.

## Ekādasamanayo

[[#^toc-12|↑↑↑]]

Ekādasamanaye saṅgahitena sampayuttaṃ vippayuttañca pucchitaṃ: yaṃ khandhādisaṅgahena ekato saṅgahitaṃ, tampi kehici sampayuttaṃ kehici vippayuttaṃ hoti. Tasmā yoginā ekasmiṃ koṭṭhāse saṅgahitānampi dhammānaṃ nānāsampayogaṃ passantena dhammā yathāsabhāvato vavatthapetabbā.

### Saṅgahitenasampayuttavippayuttapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-12-1|↑↑↑]]

Samudayasaccena saṅgahitā saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannā dhammā sesehi tīhi khandhehi ekena manāyatanena sattahi viññāṇadhātūhi sampayuttā, rūpakkhandhena pana rūpanibbānehi ca vippayuttā. Tasmā yoginā "taṇhā uppajjamānā vedanāsaññāviññāṇehi saha cittena sampayuttā uppajjatī"ti sallakkhetvā taṇhāsampayuttaṃ cittaṃ pajahitabbaṃ; nibbānaṃ pana taṇhāya vippayuttanti jānantena tadabhimukhaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## English translation (from `3-TRANSFORMATIONS/Translations/en-Contemporary-English-Abhidhamma/pi-3-english-plain.md`)

Take the states that are included with the truth of the origin… with the truth of the path in terms of aggregates, included in terms of bases and included in terms of elements: with how many aggregates, bases and elements are those states associated? Those states are associated with three aggregates, one base and seven elements, and partly associated with one aggregate, one base and one element. From how many are they dissociated? They are dissociated from one aggregate, ten sense bases and ten elements, and partly dissociated from one base and one element. ^3-409

Take the states that are included with the femininity faculty… with the masculinity faculty in terms of aggregates, included in terms of bases and included in terms of elements: with how many aggregates, bases and elements are those states associated? None. From how many are they dissociated? They are dissociated from four aggregates, one base and seven elements, and partly dissociated from one base and one element. ^3-410
