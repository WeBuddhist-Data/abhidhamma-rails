## Chaṭṭhanayo

[[#^toc-7|↑↑↑]]

Idāni chaṭṭho nayo sampayogavippayogapadaniddeso nāma āraddho. "Catūhi sampayogo, catūhi vippayogo; sabhāgo visabhāgo"ti vacanato catūhi arūpakkhandheheva sabhāgānaṃ ekasantānasmiṃ ekakkhaṇe uppannānaṃ arūpakkhandhānaṃyeva aññamaññaṃ sampayogo labbhati. Ayaṃ visabhāgatā saṅgahanaye natthi, gaṇanūpagamattañhi saṅgahaṭṭho; sampayoganaye pana atthi, ekuppādatādilakkhaṇañhi sampayogaṭṭho.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-57]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-43]]

### Sampayogavippayogapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Tattha yaṃ labbhati yañca na labbhati, taṃ sabbaṃ pucchāya gahitaṃ; vissajjane pana yaṃ na labbhati, taṃ "natthī"ti paṭikkhittaṃ. Rūpadhammānaṃ rūpena nibbānena vā, nibbānassa ca rūpena saddhiṃ sampayogo nāma natthi, tathā rūpanibbānānaṃ arūpakkhandhehi; bhinnasantānehi nānākkhaṇikehi arūpadhammehipi saddhiṃ natthiyeva. Yāni pana padāni sattasu viññāṇadhātūsu ekāyapi avippayutte rūpena nibbānena vā missakadhamme dīpenti, tāni sabbathāpi idha na yujjantīti na gahitāni – dhammāyatanaṃ, dhammadhātu, dukkhasaccaṃ, jīvitaṃ, saḷāyatanaṃ, nāmarūpaṃ, cattāro mahābhavā, jātijarāmaraṇaṃ, tikesu ekūnavīsati, gocchakesu paññāsa, cūḷantare aṭṭha, mahantare pannarasa, tato pare aṭṭhārasāti tevīsapadasataṃ ⚑. Dhammāyatanañhi rūpanibbānamissakattā viññāṇena sampayuttanti vattuṃ na sakkā; yasmā panettha vedanādayo viññāṇena sampayuttā, tasmā vippayuttantipi na sakkā vattuṃ. Sesāni khandhādīni yujjantīti tāni gahetvā ekekavasena ca samodhānena ca pañhavissajjanaṃ kataṃ. Ādipañhasmiṃ sarūpato dassetvā parato pāḷi saṃkhittā.

#### Khandho (Chaṭṭhanayo)

[[#^toc-7-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandho "katihi sampayutto"ti pucchito "natthī"ti paṭikkhitto, rūpassa hi kenaci sampayogo natthi; vippayogo panassa catūhi khandhehi ekenāyatanena sattahi dhātūhi dassito. Paṭhamapañhe ekenāyatanenāti manāyatanena; kehicīti dhammāyatanadhammadhātupariyāpannehi vedanāsaññāsaṅkhārehi. Dutiyapañhe tīhīti pucchitaṃ pucchitaṃ ṭhapetvā sesehi: vedanākkhandho saññāsaṅkhārehi kehici sampayutto, itarepi attānaṃ ṭhapetvā itarehi; kehici vippayuttoti rūpanibbānehi. Evaṃ sabbattha rūpassa vippayoge dhammāyatanadhammadhātūsu arūpaṃ, arūpassa vippayoge rūpaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ. Tatiyapañho uttānatthova.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-61]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-62]]

#### Āyatanaṃ (Chaṭṭhanayo)

[[#^toc-7-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyatanapañhe "katihi khandhehī"tiādiṃ avatvā "sampayuttanti natthī"ti vuttaṃ; taṃ pana khandhādīnaṃyeva vasena veditabbaṃ. Paratopi evarūpesu pañhesu eseva nayo: ādipañhasmiñhi sarūpato dassetvā parato pāḷi saṃkhittā, iminā nayena sabbattha atthayojanā veditabbā.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-63]]

#### Dhātu (Chaṭṭhanayo)

[[#^toc-7-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Dhātupañhe viññāṇadhātūnaṃ "soḷasahi dhātūhi vippayuttā"ti vuttaṃ: cakkhuviññāṇadhātu tāva attānaṃ ṭhapetvā chahi viññāṇadhātūhi dasahi ca rūpadhātūhi vippayuttā; sesāsupi eseva nayo. Khandhāyatanavibhāgavirahitampi viññāṇaṃ dhātuvibhāgena vibhattameva vuttanti "cakkhuviññāṇadhātu…pe… manoviññāṇadhātu soḷasahi dhātūhi vippayuttā"ti vuttaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-64]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-46]]

#### Saccādi

[[#^toc-7-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Samudayamaggasaccapañhe tīhi khandhehīti saṅkhārakkhandhaṃ ṭhapetvā sesehi; ekāya dhātuyāti manoviññāṇadhātuyā, samudayamaggānañhi aññāya dhātuyā sampayogo natthi; ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyāti saṅkhārakkhandhadhammāyatanadhammadhātūhi, etesu hi taṃ saccadvayaṃ kehici sampayuttaṃ. Vibhāgarahitehi vedanākkhandhādīhi vippayogavacanaṃ na yuttanti samudayasaccaṃ tehi sampayuttattena vuttaṃ, na vippayuttattena. Sukhindriyādipañhe tīhīti saññāsaṅkhāraviññāṇehi; ekāya dhātuyāti kāyaviññāṇadhātuyā manoviññāṇadhātuyā ca. Rūpabhavapañhe sabbesampi arūpakkhandhānaṃ arūpāyatanānañca atthitāya "na kehicī"ti vuttaṃ, ghānajivhākāyaviññāṇadhātūnaṃ pana natthitāya "tīhi dhātūhi vippayutto"ti. Adhimokkhapañhe dvīhi dhātūhīti manodhātumanoviññāṇadhātūhi sampayutto, pannarasahīti sesāhi dasahi rūpadhātūhi pañcahi ca cakkhuviññāṇādīhi vippayutto.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-3-practice.md`)

## Chaṭṭhanayo

[[#^toc-7|↑↑↑]]

Chaṭṭhanaye sampayogo vippayogo ca dassito: ekasantānasmiṃ ekakkhaṇe uppannānaṃ arūpakkhandhānaṃyeva aññamaññaṃ sampayogo labbhati, ekuppādatādilakkhaṇañhi sampayogaṭṭho. Tasmā yoginā cittuppāde vedanāsaññāsaṅkhāraviññāṇāni ekato uppajjitvā ekato nirujjhantānīti passantena "cittena saha uppannā ime dhammā cittaṃ kilesenti vā vodapenti vā"ti sallakkhetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-mulatiika#^3-43]]

### Sampayogavippayogapadaniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpadhammānaṃ rūpena nibbānena vā, nibbānassa ca rūpena saddhiṃ sampayogo nāma natthi; bhinnasantānehi nānākkhaṇikehi arūpadhammehipi saddhiṃ natthiyeva. Tasmā yoginā "parassa cittaṃ mama cittena na sampayuttaṃ, atītaṃ cittaṃ paccuppannena na sampayutta"nti passantena atītaṃ nānusocitabbaṃ, paresaṃ cittaṃ attano cittanti na gahetabbaṃ; paccuppannacitte eva uppannā dhammā upaparikkhitabbā.

#### Khandho (Chaṭṭhanayo)

[[#^toc-7-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandho kenaci sampayutto natthi, catūhi khandhehi ekenāyatanena sattahi dhātūhi vippayutto; vedanākkhandho saññāsaṅkhāraviññāṇehi sampayutto, rūpanibbānehi vippayutto. Tasmā yoginā kāyaṃ cittena vippayuttaṃ, vedanaṃ pana cittena sampayuttanti jānantena kāyikadukkhe uppanne "kāyo na cittaṃ"ti sallakkhetvā cittaṃ domanassena na anuvidhāyitabbaṃ; vedanaṃ vedanāmattato passitvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-61]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-dhatukatha-atthakatha#^3-62]]

#### Āyatanaṃ (Chaṭṭhanayo)

[[#^toc-7-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Cakkhāyatanādīnaṃ rūpāyatanānaṃ "sampayuttanti natthī"ti vuttaṃ; rūpassa hi kenaci sampayogo natthi. Tasmā yoginā cakkhādīni dvārāni rūpamattāni, cittena asampayuttānīti passitvā, dvāresu uppajjamānaṃ cittameva rakkhitabbaṃ: dvārasaṃvarena akusalaṃ na kātabbaṃ.

Cakkhuviññāṇadhātu attānaṃ ṭhapetvā chahi viññāṇadhātūhi dasahi ca rūpadhātūhi vippayuttā; sesāsupi eseva nayo. Tasmā yoginā ekasmiṃ khaṇe ekameva viññāṇaṃ uppajjatīti jānantena "passanto na suṇāti, suṇanto na passatī"ti dvāravasena viññāṇāni paccekaṃ vavatthapetvā ekattasaññā pajahitabbā, yasmiṃ dvāre cittaṃ pavattati tattheva sati upaṭṭhapetabbā.

#### Saccādi

[[#^toc-7-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Samudayamaggānaṃ manoviññāṇadhātuyā eva sampayogo, aññāya dhātuyā sampayogo natthi; sukhindriyaṃ kāyaviññāṇadhātuyā manoviññāṇadhātuyā ca sampayuttaṃ; adhimokkho manodhātumanoviññāṇadhātūhi. Tasmā yoginā taṇhā ca maggo ca manodvāreyeva uppajjantīti jānantena manodvāre sati visesato upaṭṭhapetabbā: tattha uppannā taṇhā pahātabbā, maggo bhāvetabbo, evaṃ cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.
