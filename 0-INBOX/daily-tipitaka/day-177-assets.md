#### Paṭiccasamuppādādi

[[#^toc-2-1-6|↑↑↑]]

Paṭiccasamuppādaniddese pucchaṃ anārabhitvā "avijjā ekena khandhenā"ti evaṃ vissajjanameva dassitaṃ. Saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇanti paṭisandhiyaṃ pavatte ca sabbampi vipākaviññāṇaṃ ⚑, tenāha "sattahi dhātūhi saṅgahita"nti; nāmarūpampi paṭisandhipavattivaseneva veditabbaṃ, tenevettha saddāyatanampi saṅgahetvā ekādasahāyatanehi saṅgaho dassito. Taṇhāupādānakammabhavā saṅkhārakkhandheneva saṅgahitā; bhavapadañcettha ekādasadhā vibhattaṃ, upapattibhavādayo ca upādinnakadhammāva, tasmā saddāyatanaṃ anupādinnaṃ ettha na gahitaṃ. Jāti dvīhi khandhehīti rūpajāti rūpakkhandhena, arūpajāti saṅkhārakkhandhena, jarāmaraṇesupi eseva nayo; sokadukkhadomanassāni vedanākkhandhena, paridevo rūpakkhandhena, upāyāso saṅkhārakkhandhena saṅgahito. Tato paraṃ iddhipādo saṅkhāraviññāṇakkhandhehi, jhānaṃ vedanāsaṅkhārakkhandhehi saṅgahitaṃ; appamaññādayo sadisavissajjanattā ekato niddiṭṭhā, cittaṃ pana asadisavissajjanattā pacchā gahitaṃ.

##### Divergences

- ⚑ *saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ* — the aṭṭhakathā reads it as "all resultant consciousness" (*sabbampi vipākaviññāṇaṃ*, `atthakatha #^3-15`). The mūlaṭīkā says the word *vipāka* should not restrict it here, since in the Dhātukathā wholesome and other consciousness are also included under this term (*vipākaggahaṇena visesanaṃ na kātabbaṃ*, `mulatiika #^3-19`).

#### Tikaṃ (Paṭhamanayo)

[[#^toc-2-1-7|↑↑↑]]

Evaṃ abbhantaramātikāya saṅgahaṃ dassetvā idāni bāhiramātikāya saṅgahaṃ dassetuṃ "kusalā dhammā"tiādi āraddhaṃ. Vedanāttike tīhi dhātūhīti kāyaviññāṇamanoviññāṇadhammadhātūhi; sattahi dhātūhīti cakkhusotaghānajivhāviññāṇadhātūhi ceva manodhātudhammadhātumanoviññāṇadhātūhi ca. Vipākattike aṭṭhahi dhātūhīti kāyaviññāṇadhātuyā saddhiṃ tāhiyeva. Vipākadhammadhammā pana saṃkiliṭṭhasaṃkilesikehi saddhiṃ sadisavissajjanattā ekato gahitā; evaṃ sabbatikapadesu yaṃ yaṃ padaṃ yena yena padena saddhiṃ sadisavissajjanaṃ hoti, taṃ taṃ uppaṭipāṭiyāpi tena tena saddhiṃ gahetvā vissajjitaṃ.

#### Paṭiccasamuppādādi

[[#^toc-2-1-6|↑↑↑]]

Paṭiccasamuppādaniddese avijjā ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā saṅgahitā, taṇhā upādānaṃ kammabhavo ca saṅkhārakkhandheneva; sokadukkhadomanassāni vedanākkhandhena, paridevo rūpakkhandhena, upāyāso saṅkhārakkhandhena. Jātijarāmaraṇāni nissattaṭṭhena dhammāyatanadhammadhātudhammehi sabhāgāni. #### Tikaṃ (Paṭhamanayo)

[[#^toc-2-1-7|↑↑↑]]

Bāhiramātikāya kusalattikādīni khandhāyatanadhātūhi saṅgahitāni; sadisavissajjanāni padāni uppaṭipāṭiyāpi ekato gahitāni. States that are undefiled but subject to defilement are included in five aggregates, twelve sense bases and eighteen elements. In how many are they excluded? They are not excluded from any aggregate, any base or any element. ^3-87

States with thinking and with holding the thought are included in four aggregates, two bases and three elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten sense bases and fifteen elements. ^3-88

States without thinking, with only holding the thought… states accompanied by rapture are included in four aggregates, two bases and two elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten sense bases and sixteen elements. ^3-89

Leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, states without thinking and without holding the thought are included in five aggregates, twelve sense bases and seventeen elements. In how many are they excluded? They are not excluded from any aggregate or any base, but are excluded from one element. ^3-90

States accompanied by happiness are included in three aggregates, two bases and three elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from two aggregates, ten sense bases and fifteen elements. ^3-91

States accompanied by equanimity are included in three aggregates, two bases and seven elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from two aggregates, ten sense bases and eleven elements. ^3-92

States to be abandoned by seeing… states to be abandoned by development… states whose roots are to be abandoned by seeing… states whose roots are to be abandoned by development… states leading to accumulation… states leading to dispersal… learner states… adept states… exalted states are included in four aggregates, two bases and two elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten sense bases and sixteen elements. ^3-93

States to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development… states whose roots are to be abandoned neither by seeing nor by development… states leading neither to accumulation nor to dispersal… states that are neither learner nor adept: leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, these are included in five aggregates, twelve sense bases and eighteen elements. In how many are they excluded? They are not excluded from any aggregate, any base or any element. ^3-94

Limited states are included in five aggregates, twelve sense bases and eighteen elements. In how many are they excluded? They are not excluded from any aggregate, any base or any element. ^3-95

Immeasurable states… superior states: leaving the unconditioned out of the aggregate count, these are included in four aggregates, two bases and two elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten sense bases and sixteen elements. ^3-96

States with a limited object [spelling variant: parittāramaṇā (?)] are included in four aggregates, two bases and eight elements. In how many are they excluded? They are excluded from one aggregate, ten sense bases and ten elements. ^3-97
